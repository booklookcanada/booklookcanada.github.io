What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

WE OPERATE A THRIFT STORE (CLOTHING & HOUSEHOLD ITEMS ARE PROVIDED TO THOSE IN NEED). WE PROVIDE AN INCOME TAX SERVICE WITH THE CVITP PROGRAM; WE DONATE FUNDS TO ADVANCE OVERSEAS MISSIONS THROUGH DENOMINATION HEADQUARTERS. WE RUN A FOOD BANK AND A LUNCH/SNACK PROGRAM IS ALSO PROVIDED IN THE LOCAL SCHOOLS FOR CHILDREN IN NEED. WE HOST A SENIOR SOUPER LUNCH TWICE A MONTH EXCEPT IN THE SUMMER. WE PROVIDE FUNDING WHICH ENABLES CHILDREN TO ATTEND SUMMER CAMPS.
