Who runs it — the people it pays

It reported 77 full-time positions and 42 part-time employees (year ended Dec 31, 2024). Total pay for all its positions: $3,253,966, as filed. The highest pay band it reported was “$80,000–$119,999”. 3 employees were reported in that band. Pay is reported in bands — ranges, not exact salaries. The top band is the highest range with anyone in it: it is not one person's salary, and it is not what everyone there is paid.

What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

NISA FOUNDATION IS A COMMUNITY-BASED ORGANIZATION THAT HAS PROMOTED SOCIAL AND MENTAL WELLNESS FOR RACIALIZE WOMEN ACROSS CANADA. THE ORGANIZATION PROVIDES A TOLL FREE PEER-TO-PEER COUNSELING SERVICE (12 HOURS A DAY 7 DAYS A WEEK) FAICLITATES EDUCATIONAL SEMINARS AND PROVIDES REFERRALS TO RESOURSES SUCH AS SHELTERS EXPERT COUNSELING LEGAL ADVICE AND FINANCIAL AID AMONG OTHERS. OPRERATE TRANSITIONAL HOUSES FOR WOMEN AND CHILDREN IN MUSLIM CANADIAN COMMUNITY WHO ARE HOMELESS POOR OF LOW INCOME AND/OR VICTIMS OF DOMESTIC ABUSE AND TO PROVIDE COUNSELING AND OUTREACH PROGRAMS TO ITS RESIDENTS
