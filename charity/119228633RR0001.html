What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Aug 31, 2024)

The objectives of the 'Corporation' are for charitable purposes: A) To advance the knowledge and appreciation of and to stimulate interest in the arts generally and music in particular by performance and otherwise; B) to advance the development of the performing arts in Canada; C) to provide opportunities for Canadian artistic talent; and D) to provide own and operate facilities and services for the purposes referred to in the above including but not limited to the halls known as Massey Hall and Roy Thomson Hall. The Corporation owns and operates two premier concert halls in Canada - Massey Hall and Roy Thomson Hall. The halls are used to host concerts of the Toronto Symphony Orchestra as well as a vast cross-section of Canadian and international music and spoken-artists from all genres. The Corporation supports the arts through a number of special programs including free concerts Share the Music for students and the Roy Thomson Hall Award.
