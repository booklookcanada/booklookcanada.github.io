What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

THE SANFORD UNITED CHURCH (SUC) HOLDS REGULAR WEEKLY SPIRITUAL WORSHIP OF THE CHRISTIAN FAITH FOR ABOUT 20 PARISHIONERS. THROUGH MAINLY MONETARY DONATIONS IN ADDITION TO PAYING THE CHURCH OPERATING AND MAINTENANCE COSTS THE SUC MAKES FINANCIAL DONATIONS TO A LOCAL FOOD BANK THE MISSION & SERVICE FUND OF THE UNITED CHURCH OF CANADA AND OTHER LOCAL CHARITABLE ORGANIZATIONS ALL IN SUPPORT OF HUMANITARIAN RELIEF AND SOCIAL DEVELOPMENT PROGRAMS.
The Sandford United Church was amalgamated with the Zephyr United Church and the new Church The Sandford Zephyr United Church began operations on November 1 2024. The amalgamating letter was already sent to CRA and CRA went ahead and amalgamated the churches. Technically this return is for The Sandford United Church and is for the period Jan 1 to Oct 31 2024.
