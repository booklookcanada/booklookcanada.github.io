WE DONATE FUNDS TO DENOMINATION HEADQUARTERS FOR OVERSEAS MISSIONARY ACTIVITIES AND EXPANSION AND SUPPORT OF CHURCHES IN CANADA 4. WE DONATE FUNDS FOR WORLDWIDE RELIEF ENDEAVORS THROUGH CANADIAN BAPTIST MINISTRIES AND BAPTIST WOMEN'S MINISTRY 5.
