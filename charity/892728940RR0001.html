What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

The Foundation operates from two locations - Wellspring Westerkirk House at Sunnybrook and Wellspring Birmingham Gilgan House as well as offering online programs and some programs in local hospitals. The Foundation offers support programs and services for individuals and their families living with cancer. Opportunities for development and self-help skills leading to enhancing quality of life of persons living with cancer. Access to information and education for health care. Main support and services programs include group support lecture series peer support coping skills nutrition pain and stress management programs and exercise programs.
