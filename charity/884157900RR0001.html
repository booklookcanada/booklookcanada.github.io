What it says it does

The 2024 Festival was an all in-person Festival and Academy and hosted concerts and events at: Koerner Hall Walter Hall and Heliconian Hall. These included ticketed concerts free programming artist training and our Community Program for amateur musicians. To keep an online presence all reGENERATION concerts were recorded and excerpts of these concerts can be found on our website and YouTube pages. Our Community Showcase marathon concert was livestreamed free to the public.
