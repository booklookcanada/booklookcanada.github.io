What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Jun 30, 2024)

THE ORGANIZATION UNDERTAKES PROGRAMS AND ACTIVITIES FOR THE RELIEF OF POVERTY AND TO PROVIDE SUPPORT TO THE POOR AND HOMELESS. THIS SUPPORT TAKES MANY FORMS INCLUDING MEALS LIFE SKILLS SPIRITUAL GUIDANCE AND RESPITE PET CARE RESOURCE SHELTER AND AFFORDABLE HOUSING PROGRAMS THAT PROVIDE ESSENTIAL SERVICES SUCH AS RESTING SPACES MEALS ON-SITE ACCESS TO CASE WORKERS AND REFERRALS TO SUPPORT SERVICES. THE MINISTRY ALSO PROVIDES ACCESS TO TECHNOLOGY FOR CLIENTS AND MAKES PERSONAL ITEMS AVAILABLE.
