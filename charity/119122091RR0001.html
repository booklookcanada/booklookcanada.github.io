Who runs it — the people it pays

It reported 38 full-time positions and 57 part-time employees (year ended Jul 31, 2024). Total pay for all its positions: $4,977,034, as filed. The highest pay band it reported was “$120,000–$159,999”. 3 employees were reported in that band. Pay is reported in bands — ranges, not exact salaries. The top band is the highest range with anyone in it: it is not one person's salary, and it is not what everyone there is paid.
