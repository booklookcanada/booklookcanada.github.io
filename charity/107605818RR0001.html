What it says it does

OUR ORGANIZATION OFFERS PROGRAMS AND SERVICES TO HELP CHILDREN/YOUTH WITH A LEARNING DISABILITY AND/OR ADHD TO BE SUCCESSFUL. OUR CATCHMENT AREA IS WITHIN THE CITY OF LONDON AND IN MIDDLESEX ELGIN OXFORD HURON AND PERTH COUNTIES IN ONTARIO.
