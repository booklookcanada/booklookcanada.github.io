What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

PAL OTTAWA PROVIDES A COMMUNITY OF SUPPORT AND VOLUNTEER SUPPORT SERVICES TO VULNERABLE SENIOR MEMBERS OF THE OTTAWA-GATINEAU ARTS COMMUNITY. ONE OF OUR MAIN PROGRAMS IS SUPPORTING CAST A CORPS OF TRAINED VOLUNTEERS WHO ASSIST ARTISTS IN NEED WITH THE TASKS OF DAILY LIVING AND HELO THEM TO RETAIN THEIR INDEPENDENCE IN THEIR CURRENT HOME. OUR LONG-TERM GOAL IS TO CREATE A CENTRE FOR ARTS PROFESSIONAL IN ALL DISCIPLINES FROM ACROSS THE CAPITAL REGION. THIS CENTRE WILL PROVIDE HOUSING AND OTHER AMENITIES SUCH AS STUDIE REHEARSAL AND PERFORMANCE SPACE BRINGING ARTISTS TOGETHER IN FELLOWSHIP TO CONTINUE THEIR JOURNEY AS CREATORS PERFORMERS TEACHERS AND MENTORS.
