What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Sep 30, 2024)

We run residential & day camps that focus on furthering Christianity by showing God's love & teaching God's principles from the Bible. We run leadership development programs to train young adults in Christian character and leadership. We run school programs to promote mental emotional physical & Christian spiritual growth. We provide retreats for groups to experience God through creation and receive Christian teaching. We have many volunteers throughout the year who help with the above programs and preparing of the physical property.
