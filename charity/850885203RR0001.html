What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Aug 31, 2024)

THE ORGANIZATION IS AN ONTARIO MINISTRY OF EDUCATION INSPECTED PRIVATE SPECIAL EDUCATION SCHOOL FOR STUDENTS WITH DISABILITIES AND WEAKNESSES. THERE ARE THREE DISTINCT PROGRAMS WITHIN THE SCHOOL: 1) A TRADITIONAL PRIVATE HIGH SCHOOL FOR STUDENTS WHO ARE AVERAGE IN TERMS OF INTELLIGENCE BUT CANNOT FUNCTION IN A TRADITIONAL SCHOOL SETTING. FOR EXAMPLE DYSLEXIA ATTENTION DEFICIT DISORDER ASPERGER'S SYNDROME LEARNING DISABILITIES HEAD INJURIES PSYCHIATRIC ISSUES AND THOSE WHO NEED SPECIFIC DIRECTION BUT DO NOT HAVE A DIAGNOSIS; 2) INDIVIDUAL HIGH SCHOOL CREDITS FOR STUDENTS WHO ARE ATTENDING OTHER PRIVATE OR PUBLIC SCHOOLS WHO WISH TO IMPROVE THEIR PERFORMANCE IN A SPECIFIC SUBJECT THROUGH SMALL GROUP INSTRUCTION; 3) AFTER SCHOOL TUTORING PROGRAM FOR STUDENTS IN GRADES 1-12 WHO RECEIVE TUTORING IN SPECIFIC SUBJECTS OR ATTEND FOR ASSISTANCE WITH BASIC LIFE SKILLS.
