What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

PELLETIER YOUTH IN TRANSITION IMPLEMENTS PROGRAMS DIRECTLY OR WITH PARTNERS TO ADDRESS AND PREVENT PROBLEMS FACED BY DISADVANTAGED YOUTH BY ASSISTING IN COMMUNITY PROGRAMS AND CRISIS INTERVENTION DESIGNED TO EDUCATE MENTOR AND SUPPORT YOUNG PEOPLE IN LIVING INDEPENDENTLY AND LEADING RESPONSIBLE AND LOCATING SUITABLE HOUSING FOR DISADVANTAGED YOUTH; ADVANCE EDUCATION BY PROVIDING SCHOLARSHIPS AND OTHER FORMS OF FINANCIAL ASSISTANCE TO DISADVANTAGED YOUTH TO BE USED BY SUCH YOUTH FOR POST SECONDARY RELIEVE POVERTY AND PROVIDE EMERGENCY FINANCIAL ASSISTANCE FOR DISADVANTAGED YOUTH BY OPERATING AN EMERGENCY FUND TO BE USED BY SUCH YOUTH
