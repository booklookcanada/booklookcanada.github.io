What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

PEMBROKE REGIONAL HOSPITAL FOUNDATION SUPPORTS PROGRAMS AND SERVICES INTEGRAL TO THE ADVANCEMENT OF HEALTH CARE AND THE TOTAL WELL-BEING OF THE CITIZENS OF PEMBROKE AND THE SURROUNDING COMMUNITIEIS BY THE RAISING MANAGING AND DISTRIBUTION OF FUNDS TO THE PEMBROKE REGIONAL HOSPITAL. AN ONGOING AREA OF FOCUS IS TO PROVIDE SUPPORT FOR EQUIPMENT CURRENTLY NOT FUNDED THROUGH THE MINISTRY. PROGRAMS INCLUDE TARGETED CAMPAIGNS TO INDIVIDUALS BUISNESSES AND SERVICE ORGANIZATIONS.
