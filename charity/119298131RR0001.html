WILDLIFE HABITAT CANADA WORKS TO CONSERVE RESTORE AND ENHANCE WILDLIFE HABITAT IN CANADA. NEW PROGRAMS EFFECTIVE APRIL 1 2019 WILDLIFE HABITAT CANADA AS AN INDEPENDANT CONTRACTOR SIGNED A SERVICE AGREEMENT WITH THE NATURE CONSERVANCY OF CANADA TO ADMNISTER AND COORDINATE THE NATUREL HERITAGE CONSERVATION PROGRAM A LAND CONSERVATION INITIATIVE INTENDED TO SECURE ECOLOGICALLY SENSITIVE LAND AT A MAXIMUM RATE OF $500 000 IN A FISCAL PEROID FOR FOUR YEARS. STARTING LAST YEAR AND FOR 3 YEARS A NEW GRANT PROGRAM CALLED COMMUNITY CONSERVATION ACTION PROGRAM WAS LAUNCHED TO HELP SMALL ORGANIZATIONS TO RECEIVE GRANTS FOR CONSERVATION PROJECTS; A TOTAL OF $160 000 IN FUNDING WAS APPROVED OVER THOSE 3 YEARS.
THE NATURE CONSERVANCY OF CANADA DUCKS UNLIMITED CANADA WILDLIFE HABITAT CANADA AND A VARIETY OF REGIONAL AND LOCAL LAND TRUST REPRESENTED BY THE ALLIANCE OF CANADIAN LAND TRUST ARE WORKING TOGETHER UNDER THE NATURAL HERITAGE CONSERVATION PROGRAM TO SUPPORT THESE GOALS WITH A COMMITMENT TO PROTECT AN ADDITIONAL 15 000 HECTARES OF HABITAT FOR SPECIES AT RISK. WILDLIFE HABITAT CANADA IS PROVIDING GRANT ADMINISTRATION SERVICES FOR THE LAND TRUST CONSERVATION FUND WHICH IS A COMPONENT OF THE NATURAL HERITAGE CONSERCATION PROGRAM.
