What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

THE FOUNDATION WORKS TO IMPROVE LITERACY AND TO FOSTER LOVE OF READING AND LEARNING FOR CHILDREN IN CANADA BY PROVIDING FUNDS TO SUPPORT THE PURCHASED OF NEW BOOKS AND EDUCATIONAL RESOURCES FOR THE LIBRARIES OF HIGH-NEEDS ELEMENTARY SCHOOLS. THIS PAST YEAR WE CONTINUTED TO IMPLEMENT THE LITERACY FUND GRANT AND ADOPT-A-SCHOOL PROGRAMS.
OVER THE PAST YEAR THE FOUNDATION ENGAGED IN A NEW INITIATIVE SURROUNDING SOCIAL EMOTIONAL LEARNING WHEREBY STUDENTS AND EDUCATORS FROM ACROSS CANADA COULD ACCESS CURATED BOOK LISTS AND EDUCATIONAL RESOURCES TO LEARN MORE EMPATHY AND EMOTIONS THROUGH ACCESS AND DIVERSE BOOKS.
