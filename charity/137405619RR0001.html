WE ARE A FAITH BASED ORGANIZATION PARTNERING WITH NATIONAL CHRISTIAN LEADERS. TOGETHER WE DEVELOP SUSTAINABLE PROJECTS THAT ADDRESS THE CYCLE OF POVERTY. OUR EFFORTS INCLUDE THE CONSTRUCTION OF A HOSPITAL IN GUATEMALA; TWO CHILDREN HOMES IN PERU EDUCATION EFFORTS IN GUATEMALA KENYA MALAWI AND HAITI; A BIRTHING CENTER IN OAXACA MEXICO; FEEDING PROGRAMS AND PASTORAL SUPPORT IN A VARIETY OF COUNTRIES
