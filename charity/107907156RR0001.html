What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

THE PURPOSE OF ROCK MILLS FELLOWSHIP BAPTIST CHURCH IS 'TO GLORIFY GOD AS WE FOLLOW GOD'S WORD GROW IN CHRIST AND PRESENT HIM TO OUR WORLD.' WE PROVIDE A WORSHIP SERVICE AND A SUNDAY SCHOOL PROGRAMME OPEN TO ALL AGES INCLUDING ADULTS EACH SUNDAY MORNING ALONG WITH WEEKLY PRAYER MEETINGS. THROUGH PROGRAMMES FOR TEENS MEN AND WOMEN WE GIVE ENCOURAGEMENT AND FELLOWSHIP WITH INSTRUCTION AND DISCUSSION ON HOW TO HANDLE LIFE'S CHALLENGES FROM A CHRISTIAN PERSPECTIVE. ROCK MILLS ALSO HAS AN ACTIVE MISSIONS PROGRAMME AND WE SUPPORT THROUGH FINANCIAL GIFTS TO REGISTERED MISSIONS LETTERS AND PRAYERS A VARIETY OF MISSIONARIES INVOLVED IN BRINGING CHRIST'S MESSAGE OF HOPE TO CANADA AND TO DIFFERENT PARTS OF THE WORLD. WE ALSO SEEK TO BE A LIGHT TO OUR LOCAL COMMUNITY CONTRIBUTING TO COMMUNITY PROJECTS SUCH AS THE FLESHERTON AND COMMUNITY FOOD BANK. EACH SUMMER WE PROVIDE A VACATION BIBLE SCHOOL PROGRAMME FOR CHILDREN. OUR PASTOR VISITS THE SICK AND ELDERLY AND CONDUCTS SERVICES AT THE LOCAL NURSING HOME. HE OFFERS PASTORAL CARE AND COUNSEL WHEN REQUESTED.
OUR SUNDAY SCHOOL PROGRAMME AND OUR JUNIOR CHURCH PROGRAMME ON SUNDAY MORNINGS HAVE SEEN A LARGE INCREASE IN THE NUMBER OF CHILDREN WE ARE SERVING. WE CONTINUE TO STRIVE TO TEACH THESE YOUNG PEOPLE IIN UNDERSTANDING THE NATURE OF GOD AND TO NURTURE THE DEVELOPMENT OF CHRISTIAN VALUES AN USE IN DAILY LIFE.
