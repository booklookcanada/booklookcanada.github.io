What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Jun 30, 2024)

Ashbury College Foundation's objectives are to invest and manage the endowment fund contributions for the benefit of the College pursuant to the investment policy guidelines; to increase the endowment fund from year to year; to utilize endowment funds to encourage academic excellence by establishing scholarships bursaries and awards that reward academic achievement and assist deserving students; and to enhance the financial sustainability and reputation of the College.
