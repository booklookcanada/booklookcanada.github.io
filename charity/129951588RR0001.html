What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

THE CHARITY PROVIDES ACCOMMODATION COUNSELLING SUPPORT SERVICES AND SAFETY FOR WOMEN AND CHILDREN INVOLVED IN SITUATIONS OF FAMILY VIOLENCE OR ABUSE IN THE COUNTY OF SIMCOE AND SURROUNDING AREA. THE CHARITY ALSO PROVIDES INSTRUCTION INFORMATION AND A COMMUNITY PRESENCE IN ORDER TO HEIGHTEN PUBLIC AWARENESS OF FAMILY VIOLENCE AND ABUSE
