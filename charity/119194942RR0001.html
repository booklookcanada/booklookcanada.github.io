What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Jun 30, 2024)

St. Paul's Bloor Street is the largest historic parish church in Canada dedicated to nurturing disciples of Jesus Christ. Our mission centers on a comprehensive discipleship covenant that encourages active engagement in worship ministry stewardship prayer and evangelism. We offer a vibrant schedule of worship services every Sunday and throughout the week fostering a strong community of faith. Our programs include Bible study groups Christian education courses and a range of activities for children and youth designed to cultivate spiritual growth and community connection. Additionally we engage in various outreach initiatives and parish life ministries the serve both our congregation and the broader community. Through these efforts we aim to inspire equip and empower individuals to live out their faith in meaningful ways.
