What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

The Maltby Centre is a not-for-profit lead agency in Kingston Ontario responsible for delivering children and youth mental health care and autism programs for its catchment area in eastern Ontario covering Kingston Frontenac and Lennox & Addington (KFL&A). These wide ranging programs cover all age groups including young adults up to age 24 years. In addition to our Kingston site Maltby delivers programs in four community outreach centres: North Brook Napanee Sharbot Lake and Sydenham.
Maltby has expanded its specialist service provision adding Indigenous and French-speaking mental health therapists and introducing a new in-person CBT-based group for 2SLGBTQIA+ named AFFIRM. Maltby also continues to grow its new autism service delivery model and collaborate with partners across Ontario on new initiatives such as Youth Wellness Hubs Provincial Training Initiatives and the CARIBOU depression research project.
