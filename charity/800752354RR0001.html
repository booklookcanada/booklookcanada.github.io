Who runs it — the people it pays

It reported 42 full-time positions and 74 part-time employees (year ended Mar 31, 2024). Total pay for all its positions: $4,062,293, as filed. The highest pay band it reported was “$120,000–$159,999”. 1 employee was reported in that band. Pay is reported in bands — ranges, not exact salaries. The top band is the highest range with anyone in it: it is not one person's salary, and it is not what everyone there is paid.

Ontario Hepatitis Nursing Program - to provide specialized assessment and treatment for Hepatitis C for the Guelph and Waterloo region in Ontario and to coordinate access to allied health and other needed services. Consumption & Treatment Services - to provide staffing services for the CTS site in downtown Kitchener.
