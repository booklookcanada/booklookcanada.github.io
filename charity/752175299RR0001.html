The highest pay band it reported was “$120,000–$159,999”. 2 employees were reported in that band. Pay is reported in bands — ranges, not exact salaries. What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

Commissioning producing and presenting installations: working with professional artists to create rotating multi-artist exhibitions across the site that invite artists to adapt existing and/or create new work - Commissioning producing and presenting performance projects: working with professional artists to develop new outdoor presentations in dance theatre music etc. often site-specific in nature - Artist residency: an annual program to advance site-specific experimentation and artist development via a long-term engagement with our space - Signature public art projects: larger-scale artistic interventions commissioned produced and created with artists - Incubation program: empowering and supporting emerging-level organizations curators collectives and artists to develop projects for the site - Tours talks and workshops: engaging external speakers and experts (including artists community leaders and curators) with audiences to explore our seasonal thematic and artworks - A variety of free recreation fitness and community activities such as operations of a winter ice skating trail
Expansion of Bentway programming to other sites under the Gardiner Expressway; out into the local neighborhood; and at a new facility next to Canoe Landing Park
