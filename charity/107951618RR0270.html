What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

WE PREACH AND ADVANCE THE SPIRITUAL TEACHINGS OF THE SALVATION ARMY BY PRACTICING THE RELIGIOUS OBSERVANCES TENETS AND DOCTRINES ASSOCIATED WITH THAT FAITH. WE HOLD WEEKLY WORSHIP SERVICES AND PROVIDE PASTORAL CARE. WE DONATE FUNDS TO THE DENOMINATIONAL HEADQUARTERS FOR OVERSEAS MISSIONARY ACTIVITIES. WE OPERATE INSTRUMENTAL AND VOCAL MUSIC PROGRAMS AND PROVIDE MINISTRIES FOR MEN WOMEN YOUTH AND CHILDREN WEEKLY. WE PROVIDE AND DISTRIBUTE FREE MEALS AND CLOTHING TO THOSE IN NEED ON THE STREETS OF LONDON ON A WEEKLY BASIS. ANNUALLY WE OPERATE AN IN HOUSE SUMMER CAMP EMPHASIZING RELIGIOUS MUSICAL AND RECREATIONAL PROGRAMS
