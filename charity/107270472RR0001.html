What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

To provide the counseling and assistance of all kinds to the youth of Dunnville through the establishment of of ongoing programs and Centre to accomplish the objectives. Current programs include: Youth Co-Ed Sports (Volleyball Basketball and Tennis) Young Adult Independent Training program After School Groups Automotive Training Culinary & Baking Program Summer Camps & Outings Community Breakfast/brunch programs and mental health checks. We have volunteers for each of these programs that work with our staff or run the programs themselves 2-3 volunteers per day 4-5 days a week throughout year for 3-5 hours. Summer programs include: Day Camp Walking outings within the community and youth day trips outside of the community and sponsoring students to attend overnight summer camps within Ontario.
