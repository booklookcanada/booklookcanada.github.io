What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

KIDS LUNCH CLUB ELEMENTARY SCHOOL CHILDREN FROM VARIOUS PRIORITY SCHOOLS FROM BOTH CO-TERMINUS BOARDS WILL BENEFIT FROM KIDS LUNCH CLUB PROGRAMS. PRIORITY SCHOOLS HAVE CHILDREN WHO SUFFER FROM FOOD INSECURITIES AND DO NOT HAVE A SOURCE OF NOURISHMENT DURING THE SCHOOL DAY. THE KIDS LUNCH CLUB IS CURRENTLY OPERATIONAL IN SEVEN (7) ELEMENTARY SCHOOLS. THE KIDS LUNCH CLUB PROGRAM SUPPORTS VULNERABLE CHILDREN BY PROVIDING NUTRITIONAL EDUCATION AND ACCESS TO NUTRITIOUS FOOD. RECIPIENTS OF THE KIDS LUNCH CLUB PROGRAM ARE THE POPULATION SUFFERING FROM FOOD INSECURITIES AND ARE THE ONES WHO DO NOT HAVE A SOURCE OF NOURISHMENT DURING THE SCHOOL DAY.

What this page can't tell you

  • Whether its work works. A return shows money, not results.

  • Anything after Dec 31, 2024, its newest return in the published data.
  • Who its directors are. The published data does not include them.
