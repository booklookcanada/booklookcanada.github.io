What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

IN 2024 FINANCIAL ASSISTANCE WAS PROVIDED FOR THE EDUCATION OF IMPOVERISHED AND VULNERABLE CHILDREN AND UNIVERSITIES IN INDIA THROUGH SIKH HUMAN DEVELOPMENT FOUNDATION (CANADA) - 'SHDF' (CANADIAN REGISTERED CHARITY). WE COULD NOT PROVIDE HELP TO WIDOW CHILDREN IN INDIA THROUGH C.W.E.F. BECAUSE THE GOVERNMENT OF INDIA RESTRICTIONS ON C.W.E.F. REMITTANCE FROM CANADA TO INDIA. HOWEVER WE PROVIDED SCHOLARSHIPS TO NEEDED AND BRIGHT STUDENTS IN COUPLE OF SCHOOLS INCLUDING DR. AMBEDKAR SOCIETY SCHOOLS IN NORTHERN INDIA RURAL AREA.. WE PROVIDE OURSELVES TO THE EXTENT OF BELOW $4600 WE ALSO PROVIDED HELP THROUGH VARIOUS INDIAN CHARITIES AND TORONTO METROPOLITAN UNIVERSITY (REGISTERED CHARITIES IN CANADA) (SEE ATTATCHED T1236)
AS MENTIONED ABOVE IN 2024 WE PROVIDED EDUCATION ASSISTANCE TO COLLEGES AND UNIVERSITIES NEEDY STUDENTS THROUGH SGDF CANADA. ALSO WE DONATED TO VARIOUS CANADIAN CHARITIES AND TORONTO METROPOLITAN UNIVERSITY. (SEE ATTACHED T1236 FOR FURTHER INFORMATION) WE DONATED $25 000 TO TORONTO METROPOLITAN UNIVERSITY FOR HELPING INTERNATIONAL NEEDY BRIGHT STUDENTS EFFICTIVE 2024 ONWARDS. ALSO WE DONATED $14 000 TO NISHKAM WELFARE ORG. TO HELP NEEDY BUT BRIGHT STUDENT IN NORTHERN INDIA FOR FURTHER EDUCATION. ALSO DONATED $25 000 IN DEC.. 2023 TO SHDF CANADA FOR HELPING NORTH INDIAN COLLEGE AND UNIVERSITY STUDENTS FOR PROFESSIONAL COURSES AND PROVIDING ONGOING FINANCIAL HELP IN 2024 AND BEYOND.
