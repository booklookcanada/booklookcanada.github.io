What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

THE ORGANIZATION UNDERTAKES ACTIVITIES THAT SUPPORT LITERACY AND BUSINESS SKILLS SERICE PROVIDERS IN GREY BRUCE HURON PERTH AND SOUTH GEORGIAN BAY (NORTHEAST SIMCOE COUNTY). SUCH ACTIVITIES INCLUDE: PARTNERING WITH COMMUNITY STAKEHOLDERS TO BUILD CAPACITY FOR INTEGRATED LEARNING AND EMPLOYMENT PROGRAMS....THROUGH PROFESSIONAL DEVELOPMENT RESEARCH AND COMMUNITY SERVICE PLANS. OBJECTIVES INCLUDE: COORDINATING LITERACY PLANNING WITH SERVICE PROVIDERS AND THE COMMUNITY DEVELOPING COMMON STRATEGIES FOR ADDRESSING LITERACY ISSUES DEVELOPING AND MANAGING COMMUNICATION AND INFORMATION SYSTEMS FOR USE BY COMMUNITY MEMBERS PARTNERS AND THE LITERACY FIELD ACTING AS A REGIONAL LITERACY RESOURCE CENTRE RAISING AWARENESS AND EDUCATING THE PUBLIC ABOUT LITERACY NEEDS AND ORGANIZING AND PROVIDING TRAINING FOR LITERACTY PRACTITIONERS.
