What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

MAINTENANCE OF MISSION PARISHES WITHIN THE DIOCESE OF ALGOMA INCLUDING A MISSION STATION FOR SEAFARERS IN THUNDER BAY. PARTICIPATE AND CONTRIBUTE TO THE GENERAL SYNOD OF THE ANGLICAN CHURCH OF CANADA FOR MISSIONARY PURPOSES. DIOCESAN PROGRAMS FOR THE ADVANCEMENT OF RELIGION EDUCATION AND SOCIAL ACTION. COMPANION RELATIONSHIP WITH THE DIOCESE OF TARIME TANZANIA. WORK ON PROJECTS TO PURCHASE MUSICAL INSTRUMENTS MOTORCYCLES VEHICLES BUILDING OF CHRCHES AND TRAINING CENTRES.
