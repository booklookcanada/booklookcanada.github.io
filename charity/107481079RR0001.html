HOGHLAND YOUTH FOR CHRIST OFFERS A VARIETY OF YOUTH-FOCUSED PROGRAMS IN THE 10 DIFFERENT COMMUNITIES WE SERVE. THIS INCLUDES OPERATING YOUTH CENTRES WHICH PROVIDE 'DROP-IN' AND OTHER SMALL GROUP PROGRAMS. IN PARTNERSHIP WITH PUBLIC SCHOOLS WE PROVIDE STRUCTURED PROGRAMS SUCH AS CLUBS AND SPECIAL INTEREST GROUPS FOR JUNIOR HIGH AND HIGH SCHOOL YOUTH.
