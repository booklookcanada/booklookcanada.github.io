OWN AND MAINTAIN A BUILDING WHERE WE OPERATE A USED CLOTHING STORE STAFFED BY VOLUNTEERS AND PART-TIME PAID EMPLOYEES PROFITS DONATED TO THE COMMUNITY OF MISSISSIPPI MILLS AND SURROUNDING AREAS. 2.
