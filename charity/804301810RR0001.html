What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

THE COMPASSION SOCIETY RESPONDS TO THE NEEDS OF THE COMMUNITY BY PROVIDING DONATED FOOD CLOTHING EVERYDAY HOUSEHOLD ARTICLES AND REFERRALS TO LOCAL RESOURCES. THREE TIMES WEEKLY THE COMPASSION SOCIETY OPENS ITS DOORS TO PROVIDE ECONOMICALLY DISADVANTAGED COMMUNITY MEMBERS WITH FOOD CLOTHING HOUSEHOLD ITEMS AND TOYS WITHOUT CHARGE. IT ALSO PROVIDES GUIDANCE TO ITS CLIENTS ON PUBLICITY AVAILABLE PROGRAMS THAT CAN PROVIDE THEM WITH NEEDED ASSISTANCE. THEMED PROGRAMS ARE ALSO FUN EACH MONTH TO COINCIDE WITH SIGNIFICANT EVENTS (MOTHER'S DAY FATHER'S DAY CANADA DAY BACK TO SCHOOL CHRISTMAS).
