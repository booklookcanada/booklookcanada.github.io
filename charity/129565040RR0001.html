What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

TO EDUCATE & PROVIDE TO THE ABORIGINAL COMMUNITY AND TO OTHER CITIZENS IN THE GREATER TORONTO AREA AND WHERE REQUIRED ELSEWHERE IN ONT. & CANADA ON THE LEGAL RIGHTS OF ABORIGINAL PEOPLES. TO PROVIDE ABORIGINAL CONTROLLED AND CULTURALLY BASED JUSTICE ALTERNATIVES TO PROVIDE SUPPORT SERVICES FOR THE PURPOSES OF ENSURING PROPER LEGAL REPRESENTATION OF ABORIGINAL PEOPLES AND TO PROVIDE CULTURALLY SENSITIVE COUNSELLING SERVICES WITH THE GOAL OF BETTER EQUIPPING THE ABORIGINAL COMMUNITY IN THE GREATER TORONTO AREA AND WHERE REQUIRED ELSEWHERE IN ONT. & CANADA IN DEALING WITH ISSUES RELATED TO THE JUSTICE SYSTEM
