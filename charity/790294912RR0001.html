What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

To preach and advance the spiritual teachings of the Pushti Margiya sect of Hinduism. To establish maintain and support a Place of Worship with services conducted in accordance with the tenets and doctrines of Pushti Marg and to celebrate religious festivals and cultural and community events by following the Pushti Marg traditions. To establish Museum on Vraj culture and heritage and organize activities which can promote culture and tourism. To organize major festivals and religious events which promote arts culture and heritage. To establish and maintain a cultural and religious school of instruction for children and youth and to involve them in community services. To provide information and essential settlement services to newcomers. To organize seminars and information sessions on health wellness nutrition diet and art of living. To teach and promote character and moral building and reverence for all. To foster and promote social and cultural integration of children youth and seniors to Canadian mainstream and to organize similar activities. To set up a resource center (library) and to distribute the resources literature and information in paper and electronic forms. To promote interfaith harmony and cooperate with other organizations for this purpose. To help assist and render relief to all distress caused by natural and other calamities and to support humanitarian causes.
