What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

Educate the Children continues to raise funds for their school building project in rural Nigeria. In the year 2024 Educate the Children organized a fundraiser to raise money for their projects. Educate the Children organized and participated in a few awareness building events to promote and increase donor engagement. We continue to reach out to the community for donations of school supplies for the students in Ugbele Akah. We also continued to build relationships and partnerships with local business to support our project. We shipped school supplies to the student during this fiscal period and was able to provide school supplies to over 300 students. We continue to communicate with the teachers to provide instructional support when needed and requested by them. We provide students with graduation incentives talk to families to encourage them to choose education for their children especially female children. We provided support for the inter-house sports to encourage the students to be active.
In May of 2024 our chair of the board went to the school in Ugbele Akah to work with the classroom teachers provide professional development and ran some arts and craft sessions for the teachers to provide new ways of working with their students in the areas of arts crafts and self reliance. This was a very welcomed venture that yielded a great result.
