What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

THE MISSION OF LUMENUS IS TO CREATE A SEAMLESS INTEGRATED AND GUIDED NETWORK OF MENTAL HEALTH DEVELOPMENTAL AND COMMUNITY SERVICES TO REDUCE SYSTEMIC BARRIERS CLOSE GAPS BETWEEN SERVICES AND PROGRAMS REGARDLESS OF ORIGIN AND AS A DIRECT RESULT EASE ACCESS FOR ALL. LUMENUS’ VISION IS TO ENSURE THAT EVERY INFANT CHILD YOUTH INDIVIDUAL AND FAMILY IN TORONTO GETS PERSONALIZED GUIDANCE AND THE MENTAL HEALTH DEVELOPMENTAL AND COMMUNITY SERVICES EXPERTISE THEY NEED TO BE SEEN BE HEARD AND BE WELL. LUMENUS MEETS GUIDES AND SERVES OVER 16 000 CLIENTS AND PROGRAM PARTICIPANTS PER YEAR AND WORKS WITH OVER 100 PARTNER AGENCIES AND ORGANIZATIONS TO DELIVERY SERVICE AND PROGRAMS TO THOSE IN NEED. OVER 650 EMPLOYEES ARE COMPASSIONATE KNOWLEDGEABLE AND ACCOUNTABLE GUIDES EXPERTS IN MENTAL HEALTH DEVELOPMENTAL AND COMMUNITY SERVICES AND ARE COMMITTED TO HELPING OUR CLIENTS NAVIGATE THE SYSTEM PROGRAMS AND SERVICE THEY NEED. LUMENUS’S PROGRAMS AND SERVICES INCLUDE INDIVIDUAL FAMILY AND CAREGIVER SUPPORTS ACROSS THE LIFESPAN IN THE AREAS OF EARLY YEARS MENTAL HEALTH DEVELOPMENTAL AND AUTISM SERVICES. LUMENUS IS INCORPORATED BY LETTERS PATENT UNDER THE CANADA NOT-FOR-PROFIT CORPORATIONS ACT AS AN ORGANIZATION WITHOUT SHARE CAPITAL AND IS A REGISTERED CHARITY UNDER THE INCOME TAX ACT (CANADA)
