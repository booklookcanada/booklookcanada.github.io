What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

We provide administrative and support services leadership development and training programs for our individual local churches across Canada. We plant new churches and redevelop existing congregations in Canada. We fund students for ministry training. We participate in foreign mission programs. We operate a compassion ministry sponsoring children in several foreign countries.
