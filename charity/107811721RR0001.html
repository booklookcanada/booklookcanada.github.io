What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

Calvanist Cadet Corps: a program designed for boys aged 8 to 13 based on Christian principles. GEMS Girl's Club: a relationship building club program created especially for girls. Friendship Ministries: to share God's love with people who have cognitive impairments. Coffee Break Ministries: an out reach group oriented small Bible study for women. You Group: a time of teaching sharing and fellowship for high school aged students. Sunday School: for all ages to promote biblical knowledge. Children's Worship: a program designed for children aged 4 - 8 years of age to encourage and initiate a relationship with God. Little Lambs: an introductory program designed for 2 & 3 year old children based on Christian Principles. Cooking for Diabetics: A program that is aimed at educating and practicing good eating habits. Cooking for one: a program aimed at equipping singles to establish and maintain healthy eating habits.
