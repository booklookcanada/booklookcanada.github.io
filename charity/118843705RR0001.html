What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

Worship and prayer services. Bible studies. Provide Christian education and activities for all ages. Donate funds to assist local national and international charities. Provide pastoral care including performing weddings funerals and baby dedications. Provide benevolent assistance to individuals and community organizations in need. Provide weekly youth and children's programs. Provide summer day camps and youth programs. Participate in community events. Participate in and host monthly community meal programs.
