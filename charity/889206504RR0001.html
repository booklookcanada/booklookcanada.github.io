What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

RIWOCHE TIBETAN BUDDHIST TEMPLE IS LOCATED IN TORONTO CANADA. ACTIVITIES FOCUS ON THE SPIRITUAL DEVELOPMENT OF ALL SENTIENT BEINGS THROUGH TEACHING MEDITATION PRACTICE AND OTHER MEANS. JUST AS A SKILLED PHYSICIAN MAKES USE OF VARIOUS REMEDIES ACCORDING TO THE NEEDS OF EACH PATIENT SO ALSO ARE VARIOUS METHODS USED TO GUIDE STUDENTS TOWARD ENLIGHTENMENT AND TO BENEFIT ALL BEINGS. DRAWING UPON THE WISDOM OF ALL SCHOOLS OF BUDDHISM TEACHINGS AT RIWOCHE TIBETAN BUDDHIST TEMPLE EMPHASIZE TANTRIC OR VAJRAYANA BUDDHISM AN UNIQUE AND EFFECTIVE COLLECTION OF TEACHINGS AND PRACTICES PARTICULARLY APPROPRIATE TO THE PRESENT AGE.
