What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

The Foundation aims to be a catalyst for social change by leveraging the power of sport to improve the lives of children and youth. The Foundation raises capital through donation receipts and fundraising activities (including electronic raffles during sports games) and expends its capital by making charitable donations to qualified donees and delivering its own programs.
