What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

The purposes of the organization are: 1. To deliver environmental programs and services including household and community action designed to promote: a) sustainable resource use renewable energy energy efficiency b) clean air water soil c) healthy ecosystems 2. to educate and increase the public's understanding of the environment and its importance through workshops participation in or organization of public events and presentations to community groups and schools 3. to conduct research relating to the environment and disseminate the results of such research
