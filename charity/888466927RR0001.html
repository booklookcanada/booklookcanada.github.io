What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Aug 31, 2024)

PROMOTE AND ADVANCE THE SPIRITUAL TEACHINGS OF THE JEWISH FAITH BY PRACTICING THE RELIGIOUS OBSERVANCES TENETS & DOCTRINES ASSOCIATED WITH THAT FAITH. THE CHARITY MAINTAINS RELIGIOUS AND EDUCATIONAL FACILITIES ACCORDING TO THE CHABAD HASIDIC PHILOSOPHY FOR BOTH CHILDREN AND ADULTS. THE ORGANIZATION USES PROGRAMS PUBLICATIONS AND SOCIAL EVENTS AS A CHANNEL TO DRAW INDIVIDUALS TOWARD THE PATH OF ITS MOVEMENT. TO RELIEVE POVERTY BY PROVIDING BASIC NECESSITIES OF LIFE INCLUDING FOOD CLEAN WATER CLOTHING AND/OR SHELTER TO MEMBERS OF THE JEWISH COMMUNITY WHO ARE POOR OR LOW INCOME OR IN NEED.
