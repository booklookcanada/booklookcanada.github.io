What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

Investigate allegations that children may be in need of protection/provide guidance counselling and other services to families for protection of children and for the prevention of circumstances requiring protection of children. Protect children when necessary/supervise children in their own home when they have been assigned by the Family Court to the Society's supervision as a child in need of protection under the Child and Family Services Act/care for children committed under the Child and Family's Services Act to the Society's care/place children for adoption. Provide extended care for youth ages 18+ as needed/provide a supervised access for families and children in receipt of agency services/record disclosure/provide where feasible emergency assistance to families/provide financial awards to certain children in the agency's care undertaking post-secondary school education/Christmas cheer for families receiving services from the agency/counselling to teen parents other parents children with special needs.
