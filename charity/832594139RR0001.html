What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

GLOBAL BRIGADES HEALTH AND DEVELOPMENT ( THE 'ORGANIZATION') IS NOT-FOR-PROFIT ORGANIZATION INCORPORATED WITHOUT SHARE CAPITAL UNDER THE SOCIETIES ACT OF ONTARIO. THE ORGANIZATION IS A REGISTERED CHARITY AND IS EXEMT FROM THE PAYMENT OF INCOME TAXES UNDER THE INCOME TAX ACT. THE ORGANIZATION THE CANADIAN REPRESENTATIVE OF GLOBAL BRIGADES ASSOCIATION ( INTERNATIONAL) RAISES FUNDS AND PROVIDES STUDENT AND PROFESSIONAL VOLUNTEERS FOR CHARITABLE PROGRAMS AND SPEICAL PROJECTS IN DEVELOPING COUNTRIES. CHARITABLE WORK AND FUNDRAISING IS CONDUCTED THROUGH A NETWORK OF UNIVERSITY CLUBS AND INCLUDES INITIATIVES IN PUBLIC-HEALTH DENTAL SERVICES MEDICINE FINANCE ENGINEERING AND WATER PROJECTS.
