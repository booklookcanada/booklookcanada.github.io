Who runs it — the people it pays

It reported 197 full-time positions and 78 part-time employees (year ended Jun 30, 2024). Total pay for all its positions: $4,591,467, as filed. The highest pay band it reported was “$40,000–$79,999”. 10 employees were reported in that band. Pay is reported in bands — ranges, not exact salaries. The top band is the highest range with anyone in it: it is not one person's salary, and it is not what everyone there is paid.
