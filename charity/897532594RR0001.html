What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

Multicultural Religious Programs: • Church Service on Sunday mornings • Prayer meetings on Sunday mornings • Adults virtual Bible study and social meets on Tuesday evenings • Musical Worship Practice on Wednesday evenings • We hold mixed age group Bible Studies on Wednesday Evenings Multicultural Community Programs: • Provide sponsorship and resettlement support to global refugees in their resettlement to our community. • Provide community outreach and support to vulnerable members of our society such as newcomers single women single mothers at-risk youth seniors persons with disabilities or the unemployed. • Operate a secondhand service providing free or affordable clothes furnishings and other household items to vulnerable members of the community. • Offer community volunteer experience and hours to high school students to help them graduate. We also provide job referrals to students who complete their 40 hours community service with us. • Offer volunteer work to newcomers along with referrals to help them gain Canadian work experience enabling them to obtain job opportunities in Canada. • Provide community space rentals to the city and the neighbourhood. • Provide lunch and afternoon activities or workshops through our monthly Community Sunday program (i.e. heart and stroke workshop community safety workshop stress management workshop). • Run and host seasonal community markets (i.e. Christmas Market Valentine's Day Market etc.) with local vendors and makers • Provide art and cultural events such as movie nights or art workshops on a seasonal basis. • Host seasonal community outings for those in need of connection and help people form new relationships with those in the community (i.e. bowling billiards breakfast lunch BBQ days beach days). • Operate seasonal community clean up events to promote teamwork and teach the skills to properly maintain and care for our environment. • Operate seasonal community gardening events to promote the benefits of attending our premises and its green space environment.
