What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

PFLAG Canada is a national network of over 60 chapters and local contacts which provide peer support and other resources to their communities regarding sexual orientation and gender identity. Support and information is also offered through a toll free telephone line and the website. PFLAG develops and distributes educational materials such as posters and brochures to schools health authorities community centers and social service agencies. PFLAG also participates in workshops public forums and partnerships which promote awareness and education on diversity sexual orientation gender identity and expression.
