What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

Holy Trinity Armenian Church has been organizing Sunday Masses cultural events and heritage perseverance programs for members of the community. HTAC has become as vibrant as ever. Armenians from all over the world residing in Toronto and locals from different backgrounds have been benefiting from our services provided by the Parish Priest the Council and Committees working under the umbrella of Holy Trinity Armenian Church. Here are a summary of our services: Humanitarian & Community Culture & Heritage Sunday School Junior & Senior Choir Senior Citizens Children's Summer Camp
