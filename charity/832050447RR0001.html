What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

THE FOUNDATION WAS CREATED TO RECEIVE DONATIONS AND OPERATE FUND RAISING ACTIVITIES AND TO MAINTAIN A FUND TO APPLY ALL OR PART OF THE PRINCIPAL THERE FROM TO QUALIFIED DONEES THAT ARE INVOLVED IN: (A) DEVELOPMENT AND PROVISION OF METHODS SCREENING TESTS AND TOOLS TO ASSIST IN IDENTIFYING AND TREATING PERSONS WITH AUTISM SPECTRUM DISORDERS ('ASD') (B) THE EDUCATION OF THE GENERAL PUBLIC AS WELL AS CAREGIVERS FOR THE FAMILY MEMBERS OF PERSONS WITH ASD ABOUT THE CAUSES DIAGNOSIS AND TREATMENT OF AUTISM (C) THE PROVISION OF SUPPORT SERVICES FOR PEOPLE WITH ASD AND THEIR CAREGIVERS AND FAMILY MEMBERS; AND (D) THE ASSISTANCE OF YOUNG ADULTS ON THE AUTISM SPECTRUM AND OUTREACH PROGRAMS THAT FACILITATE THEIR INTEGRATION INTO THEIR COMMUNITIES
