What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

THE CHARITY (HPCFC) PROVIDES AN ONGOING SUPPORT TO INDIVIDUALS NEEDING EMOTIONAL PRACTICAL AND SPIRITUAL SUPPORT AS IT RELATES TO UNEXPECTED OR PROBLEM PREGNANCIES MISCARRIAGE EARLY INFANT LOSS ABORTION AND SEXUAL HEALTH. HPCFC CONTINUES TO OFFER SUPPORT EMOTIONALLY PRACTICALLY AND SPIRITUALLY TO CLIENTS PRE AND POST-NATAL. HPCFC OFFERS PARENTING PROGRAMS TO MOMS DADS MOMS SUPPORT GROUPS DADS SUPPORT GRANDMAS RAISING THEIR GRANDCHILDREN SUPPORT GROUP AND ONE ON ONE SUPPORT RELATIONSHIPS CO-PARENTING PROGRAM HOMEMAKING SCHOOLS AND EDUCATION THROUGH OUR SEXUAL HEALTH PROGRAMS AND SUMMER CAMP FOR GIRLS TO BUILD SELF ESTEEM. OUR VOLUNTEERS HELP TO SUPPORT THESE GROUPS SO THAT WE CAN OFFER CHILDCARE DURING OUR MOMS GROUPS AND FOR SOME ONE ON ONE SUPPORT TOO. WE CURRENTLY HAVE 56 VOLUNTEERS AND OVER 1050 HOURS WERE GIVEN IN 2024
