What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Aug 31, 2024)

Let’s Talk Science is a national charitable organization that helps children and youth fulfill their potential and prepare for their future careers and role as citizens in a rapidly changing world by supporting their learning and engagement through science technology engineering and mathematics ('STEM'). The organization offers many programs services and resources that help youth develop positive attitudes critical skills and career awareness and it connects the STEM and education communities to strengthen learning. Let’s Talk Science engages diverse audiences including preschool through high school youth and educators post-secondary and industry-based volunteers and community organizations to make STEM engagement relevant. Programming is available in English and French in person and online in alignment with education curricula across Canada. The organization is supported by universities colleges governments industry foundations and individuals.
