What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

LENNOX AND ADDINGTON INTERVAL HOUSE OPERATES A FULL RANGE OF SERVICES FOR WOMEN AND CHILDREN IMPACTED BY GENDER BASED VOLENCE. THESE SERVICES INCLUDE AN EMERGENCY SHELTER CRISIS LINE TRANSITIONAL HOUSING AND OUTREACH SERVICES. WE OPERATE A VARIETY OF TRANSITIONAL HOUSING PROGRAMS WHICH INCLUDE A FAMILY PROGRAM FOR MOTHERS AND THEIR CHILDREN A PROGRAM FOR WOMEN WITHOUT CHILDREN AND SPECIFIC YOUTH PROGRAM FOR YOUTH WHO HAVE LEFT HOME PREMETURELY AS THE RESULT OF GENDER BASED CONFLICT IN THEIR FAMILY HOMES.
