What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

WE OFFER PROGRAMS FOR EVERY AGE GROUP: BOYS AND GIRLS CLUBS YOUTH GROUP BIBLE STUDIES OUTREACH. AND SUNDAY MORNING WORSHIP SERVICES WHERE OUR PASTOR PREACHES THE WORD OF GOD. WITH HELP FROM OTHER LOCAL CHURCHES AND COMMUNITY MEMBERS WE OFFER COFFEE BREAK WHICH IS A LADIES BIBLE STUDY A ONE WEEK VACATION BIBLE SCHOOL TO CHILDREN IN THE COMMUNITY AND A ONE WEEK SPORTS CAMP. OUR CHURCH COLLECTS DONATIONS EACH SUNDAY TO GIVE TO OTHER CHARITABLE ORGANIZATIONS. WE HELP THOSE IN NEED WITHIN OUR OWN MEMBERS AS WELL AS IN THE COMMUNITY EITHER FINANCIALLY PHYSICALLY OR MENTALLY. WE SUPPORT THE LOCAL FOODBANK. WE ALSO COLLECT GENTLY USED LOTHING SORT AND PACK THE CLOTHING IN BOXES AND SEND TO AN INDIAN RESERVE IN NORTHERN ONTARIO.
