What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

To preach the Gospel of Jesus Christ to the people by providing worship services and Christian education classes to the congregation. To edify the existing congregation members of the church by providing fellowship meetings prayer meeting evangelism meetings and visitations. To reach out to non-Christians and support other mission organizations which may carry out missionary work in different parts of Canada and around the world.
