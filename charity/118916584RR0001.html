What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

OUR MISSION IS TO KNOW LOVE AND FOLLOW JESUS. WE ACCOMPLISH THIS THROUGH SUNDAY SERVICES MID WEEK YOUTH GROUPS SMALL GROUP BIBLE STUDIES MEN AND WOMEN'S GROUPS OUTREACH TO OUR COMMUNITY THROUGH CHILDREN AND YOUTH GROUPS PUBLIC EVENTS SUCH AS GARAGE SALES AND TEDDY BEAR PLAY EVENT FOR THE COMMUNITY AND SUPPORT OF OTHER MISSION CHARITABLE ORGANIZATIONS IN CANADA AND AROUND THE WORLD. WE ENCOURAGE MISSION TEAMS TO SHOW CHRIST IN CANADA BOLIVIA OR OTHER COUNTRIES BY ASSISTING THE DOWNTRODDEN POOR AND LONELY. WE ENCOURAGE YOUTH TO ATTEND CHRIST FOCUSED CAMPS. THROUGH EMPLOYEES AND VOLUNTEERS WE REACH OUT TO BAPTISTS AND OTHER FOLLOWERS OF CHRIST IN BOLIVIA TO TRAIN AND ENCOURAGE THEM ACCORDING TO OUR MISSION. WE COLLECT FOOD AND CLOTHING FOR THE LOCAL FOOD BANK AND HOMELESS PROGRAMS. WE ASSIST YOUNG SINGLE MOTHERS THROUGH RAMOTH LIFE CENTRE AND YOUTH FOR CHRIST. OUR LOCAL SCHOOLS' NUTRITION PROGRAM BENEFITS FROM OUR HELP. ANNUALLY WE GET TOGETHER AT A CHRISTMAS BANQUET AND SEVERAL TIMES DURING THE YEAR AT 'SOUPER' SUNDAYS TO DEVELOP RELATIONSHIPS WITH MEMBERS AND ADHERENTS AND TO INTRODUCE OURSELVES TO VISITORS OR OTHERS FROM THE COMMUNITY THAT ARE INVITED/ENCOURAGED TO ATTEND. OUR SUNDAY SERVICES ARE STREAMED ONLINE.
