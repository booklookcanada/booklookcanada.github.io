THE ORGANIZATION OPERATES TO PROVIDE CULTURALLY AND SPIRITUALLY SENSITIVE HUMANITARIAN AND SOCIAL SERVICES TO THE MUSLIMS AND NON-MUSLIMS COMMUNITIES OF THE WATERLOO REGION. ONGOING PROGRAMS INCLUDE: -IMMIGRATION PARTNERSHIP COUNCIL: TO HELP TO EASE THOSE TRANSITIONS TO HELP IMMIGRANTS AND REFUGEES GET THE BEST START TO THEIR NEW LIFE IN WATERLOO REGION. -REGIONAL MUNICIPALITY OF WATERLOO: EQUITY AND DIVERSITY -CAREGIVER/TEEN CONFLICT COACHING -HEAR ME OUT: FOCUSES ON TO ADDRESS SOCIAL ISOLATION AS WELL AS FOSTER RESILIENCY AND COMMNUNITY CONNECTEDNESS AMONG VISIBLE MINORITY YOUTH WHO IDENTIFY AS MUSLIM IN THE KITCHENER-WATERLOO AREA. -ONTARIO TRILLIUM FOUNDATION: RESILIENCE THROUGH MENTAL HEALTH SUPPORT PEER SUPPORT NET WORKING EVENTS. -COMMUNITY GATHERINGS -SENIOR SEMINAR/WORKSHOPS AND OUTDOOR GATHERINGS -COUNSELING SUPPORT: MSS CLIENTS RECEIVE YEAR-ROUND IN-PERSON ONLINE AND PHONE COUNSELLING SUPPORT -MENTAL HEALTH WORKSHOPS: MSS ORGANIZES SPIRITUALLY INTEGRATED AND FAITH-BASED MENTAL HEALTH PROMOTION AND SUPPORT WORKSHOPS.
MSS REMAINS COMMITTED TO SUPPORTING THE COMMUNITY THROUGH THESE ONGOING PROGRAMS FOSTERING RESILIENCE MENTAL HEALTH COMMUNITY INTEGRATION AND WELL-BEING
