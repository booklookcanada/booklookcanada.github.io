What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

We bring together church partners and local leaders through long-term transformational development programs in which lives and communities move towards the fullness of life characterized by dignity justice hope and peace. In the communities where we work we provide for the necessities of life for the most marginalized and we ensure access to education by providing school fees teacher training pastoral training vocational training and income generation opportunities. We provide opportunities for our church partners to engage with our programs through involvement in impact teams internships and long-term mission work.
