What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

South Bruce Grey Health Centre (SBGHC) is a dynamic multi-site health care organization serving four rural communities in southern Grey and Bruce Counties. Each of our hospital sites offer acute care services a 24-7 emergency department and a full complement of diagnostic and laboratory services.
