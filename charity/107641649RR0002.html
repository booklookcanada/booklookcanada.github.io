Who runs it — the people it pays

It reported 713 full-time positions and 1526 part-time employees (year ended Dec 31, 2024). Total pay for all its positions: $60,662,778, as filed. The highest pay band it reported was “$250,000–$299,999”. 1 employee was reported in that band. Pay is reported in bands — ranges, not exact salaries. The top band is the highest range with anyone in it: it is not one person's salary, and it is not what everyone there is paid.

What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

We are a charity dedicated to strengthening the foundations of communities. We do this by nurturing the potential of children teens and young adults; promoting healthy living and fostering social responsibility. Our focus in inclusiveness and accessibility means we serve people of all ages backgrounds and abilities through all stages of life. Through YMCA financial assistance programs the YMCA is accessible to all. Our core offerings include: Child care Camping Aquatics Health & Fitness Education Services Volunteer Development Community Initiatives and Opportunities to Give.
