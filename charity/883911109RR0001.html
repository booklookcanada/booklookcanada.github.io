What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Sep 30, 2024)

Eagle's Nest Association of Waterdown (Eagles Nest) is a local organization committed to providing affordable mental health services for adults youth and children. Eagles Nest offers both partial and full subsidies to ensure that care is accessible to everyone. To further reduce barriers services are available both in person and virtually. Eagles Nest welcomes individuals from all backgrounds regardless of religion ethnicity race gender or sexuality. The mental health services offered include therapy coaching and support groups. In addition Eagles Nest operates Rescued & Restored a thrift store that sells donated items such as furniture housewares and clothing from the community. The proceeds from the store help fund the organization's charitable activities and allow Eagles Nest to distribute practical items to clients and other agencies at little or no cost. A dedicated team of over 35 volunteers support various areas of the organization including programs events administration and the thrift store. Annually volunteers contribute over 3 000 hours bringing strength to Eagles Nest with diverse skills community connections and fundraising efforts.
