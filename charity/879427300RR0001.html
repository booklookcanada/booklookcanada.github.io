What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended May 31, 2024)

Provide support for local congregations & ministries for crisis management web development educational resources financial management and hiring. Provide continuing education workshops for local leaders and clergy. Provide financial assistance for disaster relief through Week of Compassion & other relief agencies. Participate in both the Canadian Council of Churches & the World Council of Churches through volunteer members appointed by the Board of Directors.
Assist in programming at Disciples & partner camps and youth conferences across Canada. Provide pastoral care for clergy and congregations without a Pastor. Assist low-income ministries with pooled resources.
