What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

We hold daily worship services in the church Tuesdays through Sundays. We offer pastoral care in local hospitals long-term care facilities & community members' homes. We celebrate Masses at the long-term care facility in our parish boundary. We provide various ministries and prayer groups for women men youth and children. We offer Children's Liturgy on a monthly basis at Sunday Mass. We operate a food distribution program for the less fortunate in the parish community. We celebrate Masses with our local elementary schools at least twice per year either in their school gymnasiums or in our worship space. We offer live-streaming of our Sunday Mass for those unable to attend. We donate funds to our Diocese for missionary activities and to charitable organizations in our community. We utilized social media platforms to communicate and evangelize to our parishioners and community.
Weekly sorting of recycling items brought in by community members.
