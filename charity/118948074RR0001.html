What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

WE PROVIDE PASTORAL CARE VISIT THE SICK AND ELDERLY DO CHARITABLE WORK FOR VARIOUS CHARITABLE ORGANIZATIONS. WE ARE INVOLVED IN PARISH MINISTRIES. WE PROVIDE WORKSHOPS AND SUPPORT VARIOUS EDUCATIONAL OPPORTUNITIES. WE DONATE FUNDS TO OTHER REGISTERED CHARITIES INCLUDING FOR RELIEF FROM NATURAL DISASTERS. LITURGIES AT OUR CARITAS RESIDENCE ARE OPEN TO THE PUBLIC. WE MINISTER TO THE POOR THE SICK AND ELDERLY EDUCATE YOUTH AND PROVIDE PASTORAL CARE TO THOSE IN NEED IN THE DOMINICAN REPUBLIC THROUGH OUR SISTERS IN CONSUELO AND YAMASA. WE PROVIDE SUPPORT FOR THE MEMBERS OF THE CONGREGATION OF THE GREY SISTERS OF THE IMMACULATE CONCEPTION THROUGH ACCOMMODATIONS ON-GOING EDUCATION AND OTHER PERSONAL NEEDS.
