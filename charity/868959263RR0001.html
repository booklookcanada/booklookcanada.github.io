What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

To promote and advance the activities beliefs and religious teachings of the orthodox Jewish movement known as Chabad Lubavitch. To publish and disseminate the educational and spiritual litterature of the Chabad Lubavitch movement and to establish libraries where such material may be obtained and studied. To provide financial assistance to the needy and to establish scholarships for eligible persons seeking to study the teachings of Chabad Lubavitch. To organize and take part in charitable community service programs including visiting the sick and the elderly providing meals for the needy and volunteer work for existing charitable organizations.
