What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

PATHWAYS TO EDUCATION CANADA IS A NATIONAL CHARITABLE ORGANIZATION BREAKING THE CYCLE OF POVERTY THROUGH EDUCATION. ITS AWARD-WINNING PROGRAM CREATES POSITIVE SOCIAL CHANGE BY SUPPORTING YOUTH LIVING IN LOW-INCOME COMMUNITIES TO OVERCOME BARRIERS TO EDUCATION GRADUATE FROM HIGH SCHOOL AND BUILD THE FOUDNATION FOR A SUCCESSFUL FUTURE. HIGH SCHOOL DROPOUT RATES IN LOW-INCOME COMMUNITIES ACROSS CANADA CAN BE MORE THAN DOUBLE THE NATIONAL AVERAGE. YOUTH LIVING IN THESE COMMUNITIES FACE INCREASED BARRIERS TO EDUCATION THAT CAN STAND IN THE WAY OF GRADUATING RESULTING IN A WIDENING ACHIEVEMENT GAP THAT CAN LAST A LIFETIME. THE PATHWAYS PROGRAM PROVIDES YOUTH LIVING IN LOW-INCOME COMMUNITIES WITH A COMBINATION OF ACADEMIC FINANCIAL SOCIAL AND ONE-ON-ONE SUPPORTS THAT IS PROVEN TO REMOVE BARRIERS TO HIGH SCHOOL GRADUATION AND HELP THEM TRANSITION TO POST-SECONDARY EDUCATION TRAINING OR EMPLOYEMENT WHEN STUDENTS LIVING IN LOW-INCOME COMMUNITIES GRADUATE FROM HIGH SCHOOL THEY DON'T JUST CREATE BETTER LIVES FOR THEMSELVES. HIGH SCHOOL GRADUATES ARE MORE LIKELY TO VOTE VOLUNTEER DONATE TO CAUSES AND GIVE BACK TO THE PLACES THEY CALL HOME - CREATING A BETTER FUTURE FOR ALL OF US.
PATHWAYS TO EDUCATION HAS A PROVEN TRACK RECORD OF DESIGNING AND DELIVERING PROGRESSIVE EDUCATIONAL PROGRAMMING TO YOUTH ACROSS CANADA. WE ARE COMMITTED TO EVOLVING OUR METHODS OF PROGRAM DELIVERY BY WORKING TOGETHER WITH COMMUNITIES TO CONVEIVE DEVELOP DELIVER AND SCALE NEW APPROACHES TO SUPPORT THE CHANGING NEEDS OF STUDENTS. PATHWAYS TO EDUCATION LEVERAGES EMERGING BUSINESS PRACTICES NEW TECHNOLOGIES AND STRATEGIC PARTNERSHIPS TO ENSURE WE ARE WELL-POSITIONED SCALE FOR IMPACT AND REACH MORE YOUTH ACROSS THE COUNTRY.
