What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

TO IMPROVE PEDIATRIC CARDIAC CARE FOR CHILDREN LIVING IN DEVELOPING COUNTRIES BY: PROVIDING PEDIATRIC HEART SURGERY AND FOLLOW UP CARE FOR CHILDREN IN NEED; PROVIDING MEDICAL EDUCATION FOR DOCTORS AND OTHER HEALTH PROFESSIONALS FROM DEVELOPING COUNTRIES AND FROM ISRAEL; AND PROVIDING MEDICAL EQUIPMENT TO AID CHILDREN SUFFERING FROM HEART DISEASE. BUDGET DERIVED FROM DONATIONS PROVIDED BY PATRONS FOUNDATIONS AND CONTRIBUTORS.
