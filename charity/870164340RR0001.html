What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Jul 14, 2024)

Awarding scholarships to students studying geophysics at Canadian universities and Canadian students studying geophysics at foreign universities. - Providing financial assistance to students to attend geophysics seminars and related educational events. - Organizing geophysical symposia and invited lectures at Canadian university geophysical departments. - Fund-raising efforts related to the preceding activities. - Managing endowment funds.
Canadian students studying geophysics at foreign universities are now eligible for scholarships.
