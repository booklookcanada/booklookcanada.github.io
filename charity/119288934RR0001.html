WATERCAN HELPS CITIZENS IN DEVELOPING COUNTRIES BUILD SUSTAINABLE WATER SUPPLY AND SANITATION SERVICES AND PROMOTES PROPER HYGIENE. WATERCAN ENCOURAGES CANADIANS TO LEAD SUPPORT.
