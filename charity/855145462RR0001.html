What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

Provide legal services to low-income persons in judicial proceedings in Canada to establish that a wrongful conviction has occurred and the wrongly convicted in exonerated. The Foundation provides financial assistance to those receiving legal services for accommodation food clothing and transportation. The Foundation seeks to educate the public on matters of criminal law and wrongful conviction through courses seminars events and meetings and publications.
