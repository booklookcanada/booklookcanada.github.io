What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

### Annual Report on Access to Education for Disabled Children in Ethiopia **Introduction** This report outlines the successful activities undertaken to improve access to education for blind and visually impaired children in Ethiopia. The initiative was a collaborative effort between local and international partners including W Ross Macdonald Boarding School and the Canadian National Institute for the Blind (CNIB). The goal was to provide essential educational tools to support the academic development of children with disabilities. --- **Objectives** - To provide blind and visually impaired students in Ethiopia with the necessary educational tools and technology. - To collaborate with institutions and individuals to gather resources that can significantly enhance learning opportunities for these students. - To secure funding to ensure the safe and timely shipment of materials to Ethiopia. --- **Activities Undertaken** 1. **Collection of Educational Materials** A range of specialized tools and resources were collected to aid the education of visually impaired children in Ethiopia. These included: - **White canes** for mobility and independence. - **Audio devices** for auditory learning. - **Laptops** with assistive software for enhanced educational access. - **Braille printers** to allow students to read and write in Braille. - **Talking watches** to support time management and independence. - A variety of **other educational supplementary items** specifically designed for blind and visually impaired students. 2. **Collaboration with Key Partners** The project was made possible through strategic partnerships: - **W Ross Macdonald Boarding School**: A school specializing in education for the blind and visually impaired which provided significant support in sourcing materials. - **Canadian National Institute for the Blind (CNIB)**: An organization dedicated to helping individuals with vision loss which also contributed resources and expertise. 3. **Fundraising for Shipping** A fundraising campaign was launched to cover the costs associated with shipping the collected materials to Ethiopia. Contributions were received from several individuals enabling the safe and timely transportation of the supplies. --- **Outcome** All educational materials were shipped and received safely by the intended recipients in Ethiopia.
