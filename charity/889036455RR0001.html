What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Jul 31, 2024)

Financial administration fundraising and program development for: 1) Student Nutrition Programs serving the Toronto area; 2) Beyond 3:30 initiative to support inner city communities that provide programming focused on healthy living arts sports and other learning opportunities for students in the Toronto District school Board; 3) Vision hearing and medical clinics provided to students in the Toronto District School Board; 4) Other fundraising opportunities that support the Toronto District School Board.
