What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

Preach and advance the teachings of the Islamic faith and the religious tenets doctrines observances and culture associated with that faith. Daily five times Prayer and Friday Jumuah Prayer. Daily Maktab ( Religious Class) from 5.30 PM to 7.30 PM. and weekend Religious class on Saturday from 10 AM to 2.00 PM . Bi-Weekly Tafsir Al-Quran and DailyHadith and Maswara Session. Monthly Mahfil/Halaqah/seminar. Monthly Youth Program and Bi-Weekly sisters Halaqah Program. Yearly Ramadan program and Yearly Eid program. Weekly Da'Wah ((invite people and teach them religious program. Tabligi Jamaat ( visit door to door to invite people for gathering for teaching important Islamic program)). March break camp for kids to learn Islam art and other activities. Summer Camp Monday to Thursday from 10.30 to 2.30 PM to learn about Hadith Dua art painting and go out side field trip.
The future programs are: Counselling for families child care Supports services for elderly people and funeral services. Also support services for immigrants and refugees in need including language proficiency employment training job searches translation and information on Canadian culture and life and Health & Safety. Help home less tree planting cleaning road and parks for better environment. Support flood victim fire earthquake hospital and many disaster in Canada.
