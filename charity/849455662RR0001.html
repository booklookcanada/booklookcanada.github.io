What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

PROVIDE THE ORGANIZATIONAL ELEMENT THAT ENABLES CHARITABLE GIVING THROUGH CARRIER-BILLED DONATIONS ACROSS WIRELESS CARRIER PLATFORMS. OVERSEE RESPONSIBILITY FOR COMPLINCE WITH ALL FEDERAL AND PROVINCIAL LAWS AND REGULATIONS. DEVELOP MANAGE AND KEEP STANDARDS FOR PARTICIPATION. CERTIFY REGISTERED CHARITIES PARTICIPATION AND GIVING CAMPAIGNS. MANAGE MESSAGE DELIVERY AND BILLING SOLUTIONS ACROSS CARRIERS. MANAGE ASSIGNED SHORT CODES TO BE UTILIZED BY CHARITIES FOR MOBILE GIVING. ACT AS BILLING SETTLEMENT AND RECORDS CLEARINGHOUSE BETWEEN CARRIERS AND PARTICIPATING CHARITIES. MANAGE ONGOING TECHNOLOGICAL DEVELOPMENT TO SUPPORT ENHANCED PROGRAM OFFERINGS AND INCREASED FUNCTIONALITY. CREATION AND INTEGRATION OF NEW CUSTOMIZABLE THERMOMETER WIDGET (TO DISPLAY ON SCREEN OR A CHARITY'S WEBSITE TO MEASURE DONATIONS RAISED IN REAL-TIME). IMPLEMENTED DYNAMIC QR CODE FEATURE TO TRACK SCAN STATISTICS.
