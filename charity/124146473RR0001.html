In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

1. Social scientific research about economic social and environmental public policy issues of relevance to Canadian society. 2. Analysis of and commentary about public policy decisions and proposals. 3. Educational activities including disseminating information and opinions to the public through educational reports studies commentaries fact sheets infographics newsletters and a periodical covering issues of relevance to public policy development and debates. 4. Public policy development advocacy activities and making submissions to governments.
