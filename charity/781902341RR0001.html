What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

1) TO ADVANCE RELIGION BY PREACHING THE TEACHINGS AND RELIGIOUS TENETS DOCTRINES AND OBSERVANCES ASSOCIATED WITH JUDAISM. 2) TO RELIEVE POVERTY BY PROVIDING FOOD CLOTHING AND OTHER BASIC NECESSITIES OF LIFE TO INDIVIDUALS OR FAMILIES IN NEED. 3) TO ADVANCE EDUCATION BY PROVIDING PUBLICLY AVAILABLE SCHOLARSHIPS BURSARIES AND OTHER FORMS OF FINANCIAL ASSISTANCE TO STUDENTS IN NEED TO BE USED FOR ELEMENTARY SECONDARY AND POST EDUCATION. 4) TO UNDERTAKE ACTIVITIES ANCILLIARY AND INCIDENTAL TO THE ATTAINMENT OF THE ABOVE CHARITABLE PURPOSES. 5) TO RECEIVE AND MAINTAIN FUND OR FUNDS AND TO APPLY ALL OR PART OF THE PRINCIPAL AND INCOME THERE FROM TIME TO TIME TO QUALIFIED DONEES AS DEFINED IN SUBSECTION 149.1(1) OF THE INCOME TAX ACT (CANADA.
