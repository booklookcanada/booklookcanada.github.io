What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Jul 31, 2024)

John Knox Christian School Society is an educational community serving Grades JK to 8. It provides the children a Christian learning environment infused with Christian beliefs and values. Every subject taught and every school activity at John Knox is with a Christian perspective. We are committed to a Christ-centred education in a nurturing environment and teach our students discernment to equip them for a life of responsive discipleship. John Knox engages and equips its students to interact in a Christ-like fashion with the world we live in fully acknowledging that God is in control. We also run a Before and After School Program and a Special Education program for children with special needs.
