What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

KINGSTON INTERVAL HOUSE IS COMMITTED TO SUPPORTING WOMEN CHILDREN AND YOUTH EXPERIENCING VIOLENCE AND WORKING COLLABORATIVELY WITH THE COMMUNITY TO ELIMINATE ALL FORMS OF VIOLENCE AND OPPRESSION. KINGSTON INTERVAL HOUSE AS A FEMINIST ORGANIZATION IS COMMITTED TO THE PROMOTION OF EQUALITY AND THE END OF VIOLENCE IN OUR WORLD. TO THIS END KINGSTON INTERVAL HOUSE AS ONE PART OF A NETWORK OF SERVICES FOR WOMEN AND THEIR CHILDREN/YOUTH WHO HAVE BEEN VICTIMIZED BY VIOLENCE WILL: - PROVIDE SAFE TEMPORARY DHELTER TO WOMEN AND THEIR CHILDREN/YOUTH WITHIN A RESPECTFUL WELCOMING ENVIRONMENT - PROVIDE INFORMATION RESOURCES REFERRALS ADVOCACY AND NON JUDGEMENTAL SUPPORTIVE COUNSELLING FROM A FEMINIST PERSPECTIVE THESE CONFIDENTIAL SERVICES ARE AVAILABLE TO WOMEN AND THEIR CHILDREN/YOUTH RESIDING AT VISITING OR TELEPHONING THE SHELTER - ADVOCATE ON BEHALF OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN/YOUTH - EDUCATE THE PUBLIC ABOUT VIOLENCE AGAINSED WOMEN AND CHILDREN/YOUTH
