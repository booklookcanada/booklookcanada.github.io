Who runs it — the people it pays

It reported 522 full-time positions and 295 part-time employees (year ended Mar 31, 2024). Total pay for all its positions: $83,608,532, as filed. The highest pay band it reported was “$250,000–$299,999”. 1 employee was reported in that band. Pay is reported in bands — ranges, not exact salaries. The top band is the highest range with anyone in it: it is not one person's salary, and it is not what everyone there is paid.

What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

USKOKA ALGONQUIN HEALTHCARE PROVIDES EMERGENCY HEALTH CARE SERVICES AND INPATIENT CARE AT TWO ACUTE CARE SITES IN HUNTSVILLE AND BRACEBRIDGE. PATIENT CARE IS ALSO SUPPORTED THROUGH A NUMBER OF OUTPATIENT PROGRAMS AT BOTH HOSPITAL SITES AND AT THE ALMAGUIN HIGHLANDS HEALTH CENTRE IN BURK'S FALLS. BOTH OF OUR HOSPITALS IN HUNTSVILLE AND BRACEBRIDGE HAVE AN EMERGENCY DEPARTMENT FOR 24/7 EMERGENCY SERVICES.
