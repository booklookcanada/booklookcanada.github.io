What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

We hold face-to-face and online worship services every Sunday and provide leadership training. We also hold cell group meetings throughout the weekly Ongoing programs: • We held face-to-face worship services every Sunday. It’s a church gathering every Sunday at 3 pm-6 pm for 60 Regular Attendees to worship and observe the Sermon. • Providing leadership face-to-face training every Tuesday – it's a weekly mentoring session exclusively for church leaders by coaching encouraging and building a strong church body of Christ in Mississauga led by the Senior Pastor • Bible Study Groups every Wednesday in our six (6) locations. It’s a mid-week gathering of church members led by individual church leaders in a household to study the word of God. • Weekly prayer meeting – face to face – intercessory meet up every Saturday night to pray for the congregation prayer requests and prayer for the country. • PEPSOL Training – it's an online training every Friday for PESOL Students. A school of leader mentorship to strengthen the foundation of a believer of Christ after the Encounter Retreat. It will equip the students to become disciples of Jesus. Annual Programs: • Twice a year in April & October we organize a three-day Encounter God Retreat for our members' spiritual growth which will help new believers experience the wonderful work of God and most of the delegates experienced Fire Freedom and Focus on the journey as a Christian. • Annual Anniversary Specials – We celebrate our church anniversary. An annual commitment where we invite Guest Speakers from the Philippines and a wonderful opportunity to invite people to the church their extended family friends classmates and workmates to celebrate with us. eek.
• Week of Fire – a special gathering every end of the month that falls on Thursday to rekindle the fire of every church leader & member led by the Senior Pastor. • Couples Night in February – we organize an activity for married couples to ignite relationships imparting the importance of a God-centered family. • Family Summer Camps – all church members are invited to experience outdoor activities on summer days with their families. There are team-building games and once a year the congregation will participate in an outdoor fellowship.
