What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Apr 30, 2024)

TRINITY COLLEGE PROVIDES EDUCATIONAL INSTRUCTION OT UNDERGRADUATE STUDENTS IN THE FACULTY OF ARTS & SCIENCE AT THE UNIVERSITY OF TORONTO AND TO BASIC AND ADVANCED DEGREE STUDENTS IN THE FACULTY OF DIVINITY TRINITY COLLEGE. ALL OF THE STUDENTS ARE ENROLLED IN COURSES PROCEEDING TOWARDS A RECOGNIZED DEGREE. THE COLLEGE ALSO PROVIDES AT COST ACCOMODATION AND MEALS TO STUDENTS LIVING IN COLLEGE OWENED RESIDENCES DURING THE ACADEMIC YEAR.
