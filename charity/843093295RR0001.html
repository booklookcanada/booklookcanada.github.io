What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

THE SAMARA CENTRE FOR DEMODRACY IS A NON-PARTISAN REGISTERED CHARITY DEDICATED TO REALIZING A RESILIENT DEMOCRACY WITH AN ENGAGED PUBLIC AND RESPONSIVE INSTITUTIONS. OUR RESEARCH AND PROGRAMS MAKE US A GO-TO RESOURCE FOR ACTIVE CITIZENS EDUCATORS PUBLIC LEADERS AND THE MEDIA. OUR ONGOING PROGRAMS EXAMINE TECHNOLOGY'S INFLUENCE ON OUR DEMOCRACY AND THE LIVED EXPERIENCE OF POLITICS IN CANADA. WE ALSO CONTINUE TO SUPPORT PUBLIC ENGAGEMENT INITIATIVES AIMED AT SECURING A VIBRANT CULTURE OF CIVIC ENGAGEMENT.
