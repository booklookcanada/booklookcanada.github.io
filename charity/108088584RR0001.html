What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

TRCA works to protect conserve and restore natural resources within its jurisdiction for the benefit of current and future generations. TRCA provides programs and services that support healthy rivers and shorelines sustainable communities regional biodiversity and the conservation of greenspace. Activities include watershed planning and management flood risk management habitat restoration environmental education community engagement and research to inform evidence-based decision-making.
