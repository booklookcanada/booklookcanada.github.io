What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Jun 30, 2024)

THE CHOIR MET WEEKLY FROM SEPTEMBER TO DECEMBER 2023 AND FROM JANUARY TO MAY 2024 TO REHEARSE THE TWO CONCERTS. ADDITIONAL REHEARSALS WERE ALSO HELD. THE CHRISTMAS CONCERT 'I'LL BE HOME FOR CHRISTMAS' WAS PRESENTED ON DECEMBER 7TH AND 8TH THE SPRING CONCERT 'WELCOME SPRING' WAS PRESENTED ON MAY 2ND AND 3RD 2024 GUEST SOLISTS WERE PRESENT AT ALL CONCERTS. THERE ARE RECEPTIONS FOR THE PATRONS HELD AFTER EVERY CONCERT. THE CHOIR HAS A VERY ACTIVE EXECUTIVE AND BOARD AND REGULAR MEETINGS ARE HELD THROUGHOUT OUT THE ENTIRE YEAR.
THE CHOIR PERFORMED FOR THE KIWANIS MUSIC FESTIVAL ON OCTOBER 21ST FOR THE 75TH ANNIVERSARY. THE CHOIR PERFORMED MOZART'S REQUIEM WITH THE SUDBURY SYMPHONY ON MARCH 24TH. THIS INCLUDED 6 ADDITIONAL REHEARSALS.
