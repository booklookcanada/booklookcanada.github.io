What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

TO ADVANCE AND TEACH THE RELIGIOUS TENETS DOCTRINES OBSERVANCE AND CULTURE ASSOCIATED WITH THE ISLAMIC FAITH BY DISTRIBUTING PUBLISHING TRANSLATIONS OF THE QURAN AND OTHER RELATED EDUCATIONAL MATERIAL ABOUT ISLAM IN ENGLISH AND FRENCH AND IN OTHER LANGUAGES AS APPROPRIATE IN SUCH PLACES AS HOTELS AT EVENTS AND IN OTHER PUBLIC PLACES AND VENUES FOR THE BENEFIT OF THE PUBLIC. THE ORGANIZATION ALSO SELLS TRANSLATED COPIES OF THE QURAN TO THOSE MEMBERS OF THE PUBLIC WHO ARE INTERESTED AT A SUBSIDIZED COST AS PART OF ITS MAIN ACTIVITIES. FURTHER THE ORGANIZATION WILL MAKE USE OF ITS WEBSITE AND DISTRIBUTE BROCHURES / FLYERS TO PROMOTE ISLAM AND GENERATE AWARENESS OF AND INTEREST IN THE QURAN AND RELATED EDUCATIONAL MATERIALS ABOUT ISLAM.
