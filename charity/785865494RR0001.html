What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended May 31, 2024)

THE FOUNDATION'S PROGRAM IMPROVES THE EFFECTIVENESS OF QUALIFIED DONEES AND NON-PROFIT ORGANIZATIONS BY PROVIDING THEM WITH TICKETS TO ENTERTAINMENT CULTURAL ARTS AND SPORTING EVENTS IN ORDER TO ADVANCE THEIR CHARITABLE PURPOSES OF RELIEVING POVERTY AND SOCIAL ISOLATION AMONG POOR CHILDREN AND YOUTH.
THE ROAD TO COMMUNITY PROGRAM REDUCES ADDITIONAL BARRIERS BY PROVIDING FREE TRANSPORTATION TO EVENTS FOR TICKET RECIPIENTS.
