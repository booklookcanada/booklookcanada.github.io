What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

In person services were conducted weekly. Masks were worn by those choosing to do so. In addition we are posting the sermon weekly to YouTube in order to reach a wider circle.. All visitation was done by telephone with some limited in person visitation to the members and adherents of the congregation. Outdoor Gospel concerts were held for 7 times in our park in back of the church - they were well-attended. When a few children attended the worship service they are taken to a classroom for more age appropriate programming. No large dinners were held for the entire year although our ladies group did meet for small fellowship lunches. The men held two fellowship breakfasts one in April one in November. Both financial and food and clothing donations are made to the local food bank and Friendship House in Brantford to minister to the local community.
No new programs were started.
