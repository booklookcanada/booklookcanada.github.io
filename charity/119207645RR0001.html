What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

We provide public worship services and pastoral care to our congregation and the community. We provide free meals groceries and clothing to those in need. We also provide financial support to The United Church of Canada the Brantford Food Bank and other charitable causes as listed in report of Qualified Donnees. We provide free space for Senior's exercise classes and low rental space for other community groups.
