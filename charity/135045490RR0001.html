What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

Community Food Share continues to provide short-term and emergency food to residents in the North Dundas South Dundas and North Stormont. This is primarily achieved through Food Banks in Winchester and Morrisburg and satellite locations in Williamsburg and Finch. We also run a number of other programs that benefit our clients including a Community Garden Tax Program Community Advocate. Finally we collaborate with other organizations to offer programs that clients benefit from.
There were no new programs added in 2024; however all programs were enhanced and improved.
