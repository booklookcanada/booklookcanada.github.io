What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Aug 31, 2024)

TO ADVANCE EDUCATION THE BOARD MAINTAINS ELEMENTARY SCHOOLS IN SIOUX LOOKOUT DRYDEN FORT FRANCES ATIKOKAN AND STRATTON; HIRES AND TRAINS TEACHING AND SUPPORT STAFF; PROVIDES EDUCATIONAL SUPPORT SERVICES TO ITS STUDENTS; PROVIDES FACILITIES THAT ENCOURAGE ACHIEVEMENT AND WELLBEING SUCH AS ADEQUATE PLAYGROUND FACILITIES AND PROVIDES ENRICHMENT OPPORTUNITIES FOR ITS STUDENT; INCLUDING FIELD TRIPS ACADEMIC RESOURCES AND ACADEMIC PROGRAMMING SUCH AS MUSIC ARTS AND LANGUAGE PROGRAMS.
