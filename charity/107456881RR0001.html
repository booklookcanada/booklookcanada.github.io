The highest pay band it reported was “$350,000 and over”. 1 employee was reported in that band. Pay is reported in bands — ranges, not exact salaries. The top band is the highest range with anyone in it: it is not one person's salary, and it is not what everyone there is paid.

What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

THE GUELPH COMMUNITY HEALTH CENTRE DELIVERS A RANGE OF PROGRAMS AND SERVICES FOCUSED ON IMPROVING THE HEALTH AND WELL-BEING OF OUR COMMUNITY BY PROVIDING SERVICES THAT ELIMINATE BARRIERS TO GOOD HEALTH FOR THE PRIORITIZED POPULATIONS WE SERVE; USING A HEALTH EQUITY APPROACH TO CARE. THE EQUITY-DESERVING POPULATIONS WE SERVE INCLUDE HOMELESS/ UNDER HOUSED THOSE WITH MODERATE TO SERVE MENTAL HEALTH AND ADDICTIONS NEW CANADIANS AND REFUGEES LOW INCOME LGBTQ+ COMMUNITY MEMBERS INDIGENOUS COMMUNITY MEMBERS AND VULNERABLE CHILDREN AND THEIR FAMILIES. OUR CORE PROGRAM IS OUR DELIVERY OF WRAPAROUND PRIMARY HEALTH CARE SERVICES INCLUDING PHYSICIAN AND NURSE PRACTITIONER-LED PATIENT CARE WITH COMPREHENSIVE INTERDISCIPLINARY SUPPORTS INCLUDING SOCIAL WORK DIETITIANS AND CLIENT NAVIGATION. COMMUNITY-BASED SERVICES INCLUDE OUR FOOD SECURITY-FOCUSED SEED PROGRAM PARENT OUTREACH WORKER SERVICES WITHIN PRIORITY NEIGHBOURHOODS OUR PEER-LED BREASTFEEDING PROGRAMMING CHRONIC DISEASE SELF-MANAGEMENT PROGRAMS AND PEER-LEADER PROGRAMS AS WELL AS OUT STREET OUTREACH SERVICES( INCLUDING SPECIALIZED OUTREACH SERVICES AND WELCOMING STREETS ALONG WITH OUTREACH NURSING INTEGRATED WITHIN THE SHELTER SYSTEM). THE GUELPH CHC IS ALSO A SERVICE DELIVERY PARTNER FOR EARLYON SERVICES WHICH INCLUDE DROP-IN PLAY-BASED LEARNING OPPORTUNITIES FOCUSING ON EARLY CHILDHOOD DEVELOPMENT AND CAREGIVER SUPPORTS
