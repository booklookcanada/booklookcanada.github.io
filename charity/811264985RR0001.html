What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

Delivery of projects outside of Canada is through an affiliated Muslim Hands Organization. Together with local partners and country offices Muslim Hands is working in the world's poorest countries to tackle the root causes of poverty and respond to emergencies disasters and conflicts. Muslim Hands Canada has chosen to focus its work outside of Canada in Yemen Turkey (Syrian Refugee Camps) Niger Mali Pakistan Bangladesh Sudan Gambia India Senegal and Somalia and conduct programs and activities in 4 key areas. They provide programs such as education emergency environment food general health livelihoods Orphans Shelter and Wash.
