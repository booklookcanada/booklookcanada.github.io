What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

ACTUA'S MANDATE IS TO SUPPORT DELIVER AND ADVOCATE FOR THE ENGAGEMENT OF YOUTH IN STEM PROGRAMMING THAT BUILDS THEIR CONFIDENCE AND SKILLS. ACTUA'S NETWORK OF MEMBER ORGANIZATIONS FOCUSES ON REACHING YOUTH FROM UNDER-SERVED COMMUNITIES INCLUDING GIRLS AND YOUNG WOMEN INDIGENOUS YOUTH YOUTH IN REMOTE AND RURAL COMMUNITIES RACIALIZED YOUTH AND YOUTH FACING OTHER SOCIAL OR ECONOMIC BARRIERS. WE ENGAGE AND TRAIN EDUCATORS ACROSS THE COUNTRY ON INCLUSIVE APPROACHES TO STEM ENGAGEMENT EQUIPPING THEM TO REACH AND SUPPORT ALL YOUTH. WE ALSO CONDUCT AND SUPPORT RESEARCH THAT INFORMS AND IMPROVES OUR STEM PROGRAMMING WHILE PROVIDING VALUABLE EVIDENCE TO STRENGTHEN YOUTH STEM EDUCATION AND ADVOCACY AROUND EQUITABLE ACCESS TO STEM OP-PORTUNITIES FOR ALL YOUTH IN CANADA.
