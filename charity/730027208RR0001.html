What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

The organization will encourage religious instruction and the performance of religious activities associated with The Christianfaith. This will include the religious education of adults youth and children the training of religious leadership the formation ofsmall groups for religious support and teaching and the maintenance of a library of religious material for the use of religiousteachings. The organization will conduct Christian worship services and perform religious activities to promote The Christian faith.These activities include worship services and instruction for children youth and adults. The organization will conduct preachingand teaching in order to introduce The Christian faith to others in the community. We will organize Christian outreach activitiesfor children youth and adults to educate and inform the community about the religious tenets doctrines and observancesassociated with the Christian faith. We will also provide pastoral services to the community especially to the many people in thecommunity who are weak in body or spirit and in need of help. We will provide support for the weak through community visits and support their families through spiritual support groups to advance the Christian faith. These activities will take place at 4898Sixteenth Avenue Markham ON and at other facilities where volunteers can conveniently meet. These activities will be conducted365 days a year and will be carried out by pastoral staff and volunteers. The organization will conduct meetings and provide instruction so as to encourage support of and participation in the missionsand missionary activities. This will include inviting missionaries teach and share their experience with children youth and adultsin such a way as to encourage propagation of the Christian faith. These activities will take place at 4898 Sixteenth AvenueMarkham ON and at other facilities where volunteers can conveniently meet. These activities will be conducted 365 days a yearand will be carried out by pastoral staff and volunteers. The organization may from time to time run such other programs or activities that are ancillary to the attainment of the abovecharitable purposes. These activities will be conducted 365 days a year and will be carried out by pastoral staff andvolunteers.
