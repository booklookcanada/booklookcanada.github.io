IN EUROPE: ASSIST EXISTING EVANGELICAL CHURCHES AND MEMBERS OF THE BODY OF CHRIST. ESTABLISH EVANGELICAL CHURCHES IN EUROPE; ESTABLISH TRAINING CENTRES IN EUROPE FOR THE PREPARATION OF NATIONAL PASTORS AND LAY LEADERS.
