What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended May 31, 2024)

CONSTRUCTING SCHOOLS IN CAMEROON AND ALSO PROVIDING WATER PROJECTS SUCH A LATRINES AND WATER TOWERS FOR VILLAGES IN CAMEROON. SOME OF OUR OTHER PROJECTS INCLUDE PROVIDING UNIFORMS TEXTBOOKS AND ADDITIONAL FUNDS FOR TEACHER SALARIES FOR THE SCHOOLS. WE HAVE ALSO PROVIDED FUNDS FOR INTERNALLY DISPLACED STUDENTS IN CAMEROON TO HELP PAY FOR THEIR SCHOOL FEES.
OUR NEW PROGRAM IS CALLED THE DMOBILIZATION DISARMAMENT AND REINTEGRATION (DDR) WHICH FOCUSES ON THE REINTEGRATION OF EX-COMBATANTS INTO CIVILIAN LIFE. THE PROGRAM USES EDUCATION AS WELL AS PARTNERING WITH LOCAL BUSINESSES TO PROVIDE EMPLOYMENT FOR EX-COMBATANTS.
