What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

Centre In the Square's charitable work is to advance the knowledge and appreciation of and stimulate interest in the arts through music drama etc. by providing performances and educational experiences in the Waterloo Region. Centre In the Square accomplishes this by attracting artists for live performances for approximately 150-175 days per year. Centre In The Square includes space for the Kitchener-Waterloo Art Gallery. Centre In The Square operates and maintains the facilities for the purposes mentioned above. Centre In The Square's mission is to create memorable experiences.
Centre In The Square has begun working with specific organizations that offer arts and cultural experiences to particular community members. These organizations are specifically chosen as they represent a specific part of the community that we feel may not have the opportunity to experience a memorable experience in arts and/or culture.
