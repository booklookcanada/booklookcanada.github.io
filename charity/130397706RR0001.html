What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Jun 30, 2024)

We provide: 1) A minimum of 3 religious services each day 2) Wedding and funeral services 3) A wide variety of adult education programs 4) Afternoon and Sunday religious schooling 5) Training to young people to enable them to celebrate Bar/Bat Mitzvah 6) Training for volunteers in areas of visits to the sick shut-ins & houses of mourning 7) Scholarships to students in disciplines related to Judaism 8) Youth activities to a wide range of age groups
