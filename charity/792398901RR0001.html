What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

THE FOUNDATION HAS ONE DONOR THE CHAPNIK FAMILY. ASIDE FROM ONE DONATION TO A US CHARITY THE FOUNDATION DISTRIBUTES FUNDS ONLY TO OTHER REGISTERED CHARITIES IN CANADA AND DONATES TO VARIOUS CAUSES RELATING TO POVERTY RESEARCH SOCIAL WELFARE EDUCATION SANITATION ETC. THE FOUNDATION HAS NO VOLUNTEERS AND RAISES NO MONEY ASIDE FROM THE DONATIONS RECEIVED FROM THE CHAPNIK FAMILY.
THERE ARE NO NEW PROGRAMS PER SE; ONLY TO CONTINUE TO FUND AND SUPPORT DONEES WHO FOCUS ON WORTHY AND JUST CAUSES FOR THE BETTERMENT TO HUMANITY.
