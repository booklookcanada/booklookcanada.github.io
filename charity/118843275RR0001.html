What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

One Sunday worship service per week with the option of having Sunday worship services provided on-line (virtual) through U-tube. Pastoral Care provided when required to shut-ins the sick and elderly. On going support to the National Denomination (The United Church of Canada) Mission and Service activities. Ongoing support to the Canadian Food Grains Bank through a local food growing project in partnership with two other local United Church Congregations. Ongoing support to local registered Charities.
Started a Coffee & Dessert drop-in Social Hour once a month to offer support to older and young families in the local community and also started a Soup & Sandwich lunch once a month to support seniors and local residents in the community. Some of the proceeds from these two new programs are being given to a local Food Bank which supports low income persons.
