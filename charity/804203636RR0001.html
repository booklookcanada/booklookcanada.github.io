What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Jun 30, 2024)

Bravo Niagara! presents concerts featuring Canadian and international artists in various venues and public spaces throughout the Niagara region including Niagara-on-the-Lake and St. Catharines. The programming is supported by 15 community volunteers who collectively donated approximately 3 000 hours per year. Bravo Niagara! provides opportunities for young emerging artists to participate in educational performance opportunities master classes and workshops with renowned artists. Bravo Niagara! produces performing arts festivals and concert series in the Niagara region’s shoulder seasons which provides further opportunities for the public to access high quality professional artistic experiences. The programming spans a wide range of musical genres including classical chamber opera jazz and pop. Bravo Niagara! is a culturally diverse organization and is committed to presenting diverse artists and artistic programming. Through its various presentations Bravo Niagara! offers free educational outreach opportunities and affordably priced concerts. Bravo Niagara! also produces and commissions new multidisciplinary works featuring original music and ballet.
