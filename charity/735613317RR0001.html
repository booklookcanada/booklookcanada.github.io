What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

Ongoing programs: (1) We hold regular/weekly Christian faith worship services every Sunday. (2) We hold weekly Bible study on Fridays. (3) We hold prayer meetings twice a week on Wednesdays and Saturdays to support members emotionally and spiritually. (4) We hold monthly evangelism outreaches. (5) We do food donation to Heron Emergency Food Centre. (6) We do cash donation to other qualified donee. (7) We do yearly Big Give event in Ottawa - where free items are donated to people. (8) We give free cooked hot meals to the student members twice a month (9) We do quarterly MFH - academy to provide supports to members seeking job opportunities (experts are brought to give seminars on resume writing job search strategy financial smartness etc. (10) We do a yearly back to school BBQ in our community where we donnate school bags and supplies to students (11) We offer women and children ministries. (12) We organize yearly free summer camp for the children. 13) We offer pastoral care support and counselling. 14) We do weekly youth hangout event and quarterly night of worship
We started regular weekly youth services We modified giving free cooked hot meals to the student members to a weekly event We started the she leads conference - to equip our young ladies to take leadership positions in future We started drive to clean up garbage along the road in our community.
