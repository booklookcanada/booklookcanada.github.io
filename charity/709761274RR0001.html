What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

The purposes of the Church are: a)To preach and advance the teachings of the Christian faith and the religious tenets doctrines observances and culture associated with that faith. b) To establish maintain and support a house of worship with services conducted in accordance with the tenets and doctrines of the Christian faith. c)To support and maintain missions and missionaries in order to propagate the Christian faith. One component of fulfilling these objects is the weekly Sunday morning public worship services which comprise of worship music and singing to God followed by either scriptural reading and discussion or a sermon delivered by a Minister based on a portion of the Holy Bible to educate and encourage individuals to apply the scriptural principles to life. Such principles include establishing a relationship with God growing in that relationship love of fellow man forgiveness and trusting the scriptural promises of God. Prayer and communal gathering are also key components of weekly services. In addition to the Sunday worship services similar midweek programs are available periodically. In addition described below are weekly and/or special events tailored for different age or interest groups. The Well Ministries has a specialized focus on Youth Services. Youth that identifies with TWM will be able to take part in the Sunday worship gatherings but the tailored youth programs aimed at connecting with the youth in the Blind River and surrounding communities will be carried out on a regular weekly basis. At the TWM location a youth center is open after school hours 5 times a week. This is a community connection activity where TWM leaders and trained volunteers can connect with youth. It is a place for youth to 'hang out' have a snack and do homework to establish connections and rapport and also consists of skill-building workshops and discussion groups. In some activities TWM partners with other community services to provide special workshops at the TWM facility but such programs will be under the direction and control of TWM. We want to give supervised recreation as an alternative to other risk behaviors. Two years ago The Well added a Food Stability Program for youth.
The Well’s Food Stability Program has continued to grow and serve community members besides youth. We want to increase the nutritional value of our clients' diets ease financial stress and also introduce new types of foods. Similar to the Youth Center we continue doing Camps for ages 6-12 on days that parents need childcare such as PD Days a week in summer and March Break. We have started to do a midweek discussion group. This group fosters deeper relationships in the TWM faith community as well as reaching out into the community to form relationships by which to share the hope of the Gospel and is available to all ages. The Well is present in community events such as fairs and festivals and has opened ‘by donation’ family events such as movie nights and open mic nights. We also have ‘by donation’ holiday meals for those in need on holidays such as Christmas and Easter.
