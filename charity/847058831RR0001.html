What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Jun 30, 2024)

THE TORONTO WRITERS' COLLECTIVE OBJECTIVES ARE: (A) TO PROMOTE DEVELOP AND FACILITATE EXPRESSIVE WRITING SKILLS AND OTHER RELATED FORMS OF ARTISTIC EXPRESSION FOR MEMBERS OF AT-RISK MARGINALIZED AND VULNERABLE COMMUNITIES THROUGH PROGRAMS TRAINING AND WORKSHOPS; (B) TO PROMOTE INDIVIDUAL AND COMMUNITY WELL-BEING IN THE GREATER TORONTO AREA AND OTHER URBAN CENTRES ACROSS CANADA BY PROVIDING THE OPPORTUNITY FOR MEMEBRS OF AT-RISK MARGINALIZED AND VULNERABLE COMMUNITIES TO USE EXPRESSIVE WRITING TECHNIQUES AND OTHER RELATED FORMS OF ARTISTIC EXPRESSION TO DEVELOP THEIR OWN VOICES; (C) TO MAKE AVAIALBLE TO THE GENERAL PUBLIC THE SHORT STORIES POEMS PERFORMANCES AND OTHER FORMS OF LITERARY AND ARTISTIC EXPRESSION THAT ARE PRODUCED THROUGH SUCH PROGRAMS TRAINING AND WORKSHOPS; AND (D) TO UNDERTAKE ACTIVITIES ANCILLARY AND INCIDENTAL TO THE ATTAINMENT OF THE ABOVE CHARITABLE PURPOSES
