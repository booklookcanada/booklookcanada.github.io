What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

Reporting period ending: 2024-12-31 Ongoing Programs and activities 1. Sunday services: We had regular Sunday worship service every week which includes singing preaching praying and sharing and we also had Sunday school for both adults and children. 2. Fellowship groups: We had 10 fellowship groups some held online others met physically. Fellowship group meetings usually involved hymn singing Bible study and sharing. 3. Online Prayer meeting on Wednesday evenings and Saturday morning: We had weekly prayer meeting. Usually we started with hymn singing and study of God's word then we prayed together. 4. We had a joint English service together with West Toronto Chinese Bible Church at Don Valley Bible Chapel (25 Axsmith Crescent) on Saturday evenings. There are about 20 youth attending those meetings from 7-9pm. 5. During the Canada Day weekend our whole church joined the Discipleship camp organized by Christian Communication Inc. of Canada as our retreat of the year. 6. On Saturday Aug 10 2024 we had our church outing day at Sunnybrook Park with food singing and message sports etc. 7. We sent out teams or individuals to do mission trip to Cambodia. 8. Baptism service: We had a baptism service during the Easter Weekend. 9. Holiday Celebration: We had our celebration events on Feb 10 2024 for Chinese New Year on Mar 31 for Easter on May 12 for Mother’s day on June 16 for Father’s day on Sep 15 for Chinese Moon Festival Oct 13 for Thanksgiving on Dec 22 for Christmas. 10. We have been participating the Community Drop-in Meal program with Don Valley Bible Chapel on the last Saturday of every month in 2024.
We started a new fellowship for youth from grade 7 to 12 on Thursday night we prepared dinner organized activities and talked about gospel.
