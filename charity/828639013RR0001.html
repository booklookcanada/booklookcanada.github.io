1) HOLD SUNDAY MORNING WORSHIP GATHERINS THAT INCLUDE MUSIC DRAMA & CREATIVE MEDIA PRESENTATIONS. 2) PROGRAM RUN SUNDAY AND MID-WEEK WORSHIP & EDUCATIONAL EXPERIENCES FOR CHILDREN. 3) CARE FOR THE LESS FORTUNATE IN BENEVOLANCE PROGRAMS AND PARTICIPATION IN DOWNTOWN CHURCH. 4) PROVIDE COUNSELLING AND SUPPORT FOR THOSE STRUGGLING WITH ADDICTIONS AND RELATIONSHIP BREAKDOWNS. 5) LEARN ENCOURAGE AND CARE FOR ONE ANOTHER IN SMALL GROUP GATHERINGS. 6) RUN RECREATIONAL SPORTS PROGRAMS FOR THE CHURCH FAMILY AND COMMUNITY. 7) PARTICIPATE IN WORLD MISSIONS.
