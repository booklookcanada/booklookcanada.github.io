What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

THE JULIE PATEL FOUNDATION HAS BEEN FOUNDED TO MAINTAIN A FUND OR FUNDS AND TO APPLY ALL OR PART THEREOF AND THE INCOME THEREFROM FOR EDUCATION OR CAREER PURPOSES AND IN PARTICULAR TO SUPPORT CHARITABLE ORGANIZATIONS THAT ARE QUALIFIED DONEES IN ACTIVITIES WHICH ADVANCE INDIVIDUALS IN THE AREAS OF BUSINESS EDUCATION LEADERSHIP AND HEALTH. THIS IS ACCOMPLISHED BY PROVIDING TUITION SUPPORT WITH THE EMPHASIS ON STUDENTS WHO WITHOUT FINANCIAL ASSISTANCE MIGHT NOT OTHERWISE BE ABLE TO CONTINUE THEIR EDUCATION. THE FOUNDATION ALSO SUPPORTS CHARITABLE ORGANIZATIONS THAT ARE QUALIFIED DONEES IN ACTIVITIES THAT ADVANCE INDIVIDUALS OR GROUPS OF PEOPLES IN THE AREAS OF EDUCATION BUSINESS AND HEALTH.
