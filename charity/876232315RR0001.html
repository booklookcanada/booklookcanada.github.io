What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

THE COMMUNITY FOUNDATION OF GREATER PETERBOROUGH STRENGTHENS THE ENVIRONMENTAL SOCIAL AND CULTURAL SUSTAINABILITY OF OUR COMMUNITY THROUGH CHARITABLE MEANS. WE DO THIS BY INSPIRING GIVING MAKING GRANTS BUILDING AND MAINTAINING FUNDS PROVIDING PHILANTHROPIC SERVICES SUPPORTING THE FUNDRAISING AND VOLUNTEERING CAPACITY OF LOCAL CHARITIES AND FOSTERING RECONCILIATION WITH INDIGENOUS PEOPLES. IN 2024 THE COMMUNITY FOUNDATION'S CHARITABLE ACTIVITIES WERE GRANTMAKING AND CAPACITY BUILDING ACTIVITIES. GRANTS WERE MADE THANKS TO GENEROUS COMMUNITY DONORS AND FROM THE EARNINGS ON INVESTED ASSETS.
