What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

The Land Conservancy for Kingston Frontenac and Lennox & Addington (LC-KFL&A) owns 13 properties (one property newly acquired in 2022) and 2 conservation easements totalling just over 450 hectares of protected land providing habitat for 18 species at risk.
Three new properties have been acquired in 2025 plus one additional conservation easement. Two more properties are in the early stages of acquisition and another additional conservation easement is being considered.
