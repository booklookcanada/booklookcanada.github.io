What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

THE UNITED WAY OF BRUCE GREY IS A NON-PROFIT NON-PARTISAN VOLUNTEER DRIVEN ORGANIZATIONS THAT ENGAGES IN AN EFFORT TO MOBILIZE RESOURCES TO SUPPORT A BROAD RANGE OF NON-PROFIT HUMAN CARE SERVICES. IT EXIST TO IMPROVE THE QUALITY OF LIFE OF ALL PEOPLE IN COUNTIES OF BRUCE AND GREY BY RAISING AND INVESTING MONEY IN SERVICES AND PROGRAMS THAT HELP MEET HUMAN NEEDS IN OUR COMMUNITIES. DURING THE YEAR THE UNITED WAY OF BRUCE GREY CAMPAIGNED FUNDS AND DONATIONS WHICH WERE ALLOCATED TO REGISTERED CHARITIES TO OPERATE CHARITABLE PROGRAMS.
