What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Sep 30, 2024)

MAF CANADA carries out its charitable purposes: By joint ministries with foreign and domestic agencies such as MAF US MAF International Prairie College and the Lutheran Assoc. of Missionaries and Pilots. Through these joint ministries MAF is assisting nationals in remote and isolated communities throughout the world. MAF Canada also carries out its charitable purpose with its own program and activities in Canada and Angola where we are responsible for the flying operations providing training for mission staff humanitarian assistance as well as ministering to the spiritual needs of those we serve and work with.
