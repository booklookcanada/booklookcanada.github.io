What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

WE ARE A CHARITY DEDICATED TO STRENGTHENING THE FOUNDATIONS OF COMMUNITIES. WE DO THIS BY NURTURING THE POTENTIAL OF CHILDREN TEENS AND YOUNG ADULTS; PROMOTING HEALTHY LIVING AND FOSTERING SOCIAL RESPONSIBILITY. OUR FOCUS ON INCLUSIVENESS AND ACCESSIBILITY MEANS WE SERVE PEOPLE OF ALL AGES BACKGROUNDS AND ABILITIES THROUGH ALL STAGES OF LIFE. THROUGH THE YMCA FINANCIAL ASSISTANCE PROGRAMS THE YMCA IS ACCESSIBLE TO ALL. OUR CORE OFFERINGS INCLUDE: CHILDCARE CAMPS HEALTH FITNESS AND AQUATIC PROGRAMS YOUTH LEADERSHIP EMPLOYMENT SERVICES LOCAL COMMUNITY AND GLOBAL INITIATIVES AND OPPORTUNITIES TO GIVE.
