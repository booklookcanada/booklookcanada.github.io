What it says it does

SEW ON FIRE'S NUMEROUS VOLUNTEERS RECORD MANAGE AND COORDINATE THE RECEIPT OF SORTING AND STORAGE OF DONATED MATERIALS AND SUPPLIES. VOLUNTEERS SORT PACKAGE AND ASSEMBLE CUSTOM GIFT BAGS FOR DISTRIBUTION IN CANADA AND GLOBALLY THROUGH CANADIAN REGISTERED CHARITIES. THE ACTIVITIES OF SEW ON FIRE ARE PROMOTED THROUGH SPEAKING ENGAGEMENTS FUNDRAISING AND PRINTED MATERIALS TO HELP RELIEVE POVERTY IN CANADA AND THE WORLD. THE CHARITY IS 100% VOLUNTEER.
