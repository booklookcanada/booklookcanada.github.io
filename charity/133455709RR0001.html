What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

Addiction and Mental Health Services - KFLA (AMHS-KFLA) provides services to individuals with a mental health and/or addiction concern in Kingston Frontenac and Lennox & Addington counties. We provide supportive housing crisis intervention court support case management community outreach services assertive community treatment (ACT) addiction services - including problem gambling long term mental health counseling and rehabilitation with an Occupational Therapist addiction and stabilization beds and transitional services.
