What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

EMPLOYMENT - PROVIDES EMPLOYMENT SKILLS TRAINING TO YOUNG PEOPLE AND ADULTS WHO ARE INVESTED IN INCREASING THEIR EMPLOYABILITY IN AN ENVIRONMENT THAT PROMOTES COMMUNITY ENGAGEMENT AND PARTNERSHIP HARD WORK AND CORE EMPLOYMENT SKILLS ENHANCEMENT. INCLUDES SEASONAL HORTICULTURAL OUTDOOR WORKER PROGRAM YOUTH GARDEN PROGRAM AND ACADEMIC YEAR PROGRAM. OUTREACH - ENGAGES WITH VARIOUS COMMUNITIES THROUGHOUT THUNDER BAY USING FOOD AND GROWING AS COMMON DENOMINATORS.INCLUDES COMMUNITY GROWER PROGRAM COOKING PROGRAMS AND SUPERIOR SEED PRODUCERS. EDUCATION - PROVIDES EDUCATIONAL PROGRAMS TO LOCAL SCHOOLS THROUGH COOKING PROGRAMS IN FOODS CLASSROOMS CULTIVATING SCHOOL GARDENS WITH STUDENTS AND PROVIDING FRESHLY PREPARED REGIONALLY SOURCED SCHOOL MEALS TO STUDENTS EITHER MONTHLY OR IN THE CASE OF THE GET FRESH CAFE DAILY. AGRICULTURAL ACTIVITIES TO ENGAGE YOUTHS. FOOD ACCESS - PROGRAMS THAT ADDRESS FOOD INSECURITY FOR PEOPLE EXPERIENCING POVERTY AND BARRIERS TO FOOD. COMMUNITY FOOD CENTRE
