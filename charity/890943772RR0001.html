What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

Food for Life is a regional leader in food rescuing and sharing good surplus food to charities and direct to neighbours ensuring everyone has access to good food. Built on a model of sustainability our unique supply chain works with all levels of the food system to support local food programs Good Food fridges community dinners food delivery programs. Focused research is aimed at using the power of rescued food as a lever to improve the health and economic situation of people and the planet.
