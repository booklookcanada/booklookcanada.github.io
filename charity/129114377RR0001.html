Who runs it — the people it pays

It reported 273 full-time positions and 66 part-time employees (year ended Mar 31, 2024). Total pay for all its positions: $11,258,732, as filed. The highest pay band it reported was “$120,000–$159,999”. 1 employee was reported in that band. Pay is reported in bands — ranges, not exact salaries. The top band is the highest range with anyone in it: it is not one person's salary, and it is not what everyone there is paid.
