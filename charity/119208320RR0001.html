TAFFELMUSIK CANADA'S ORCHESTRA ON PERIOD INSTRUMENTS HAS ACHIEVED INTERNATIONAL RECOGNITION FOR ITS CONCERTS AND RECORDINGS. THIS WAS TAFELMUSIK'S FIRST SEASON OF ALL LIVE PERFORMANCES SINCE THE COVID-19 PANDEMIC.
