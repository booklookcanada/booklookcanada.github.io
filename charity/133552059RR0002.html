What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

THE CHARITY THROUGH VOLUNTEER MEMBERS VISIT THE LOW INCOME TO ASSESS THEIR NEEDS AND TO PROVIDE APPROPRIATE ASSISTANCE. ASSISTANCE MAY INCLUDE FOOD CLOTHING MEDICAL AND FINANCIAL HELP OR A COMBINATION. WE ALSO OPERATE TWO USED GOODS STORES STAFFED BY VOLUNTEERS WHERE WE ACCEPT DONATIONS OF USED CLOTHING AND FURNITURE. THE STORES PROVIDES REASONABLY PRICED USED ITEMS TO THE COMMUNITY. EITHER THE USED GOODS DONATED OR THE FUNDS GENERATED THROUGH SALES ARE USED TO ASSIST THOSE IN NEED. FOODBANK AND A SOUP KITCHEN IS MAINTAINED AT THE STORE. OUTREACH PROGRAMS INCLUDE CHRISTMAS DINNER AND A CARBON MONOXIDE ALARM PROGRAM IN PARTERSHIP WITH THE LOCAL FIRE DEPARTMENT.
