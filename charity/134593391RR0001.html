What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

THE CHARITY'S PRIMARY PURPOSE IS TO CONDUCT SOCIAL WELFARE PROGRAMS IN CANADA AND ISRAEL. FOR THE MOST PART THESE PROGRAMS INCLUDE RESETTLEMENT OF NEW IMMIGRANTS STUDENT SCHOLARSHIPS COMMUNITY REHABILITATION PROJECTS SERVICES FOR THE NEEDY AND INFIRM COMMUNITY CENTRES DAYCARE CENTRES AND PROGRAMS FOR SENIOR CITIZENS.
