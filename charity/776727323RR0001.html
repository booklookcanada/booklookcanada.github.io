What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

The CAGP Foundation supports and strengthens strategic philanthropy in Canada by providing learning and professional development opportunities to charitable fundraisers and professional advisors in the area of planned giving. We conduct research to increase knowledge of the issues and trends in philanthropic giving. Through public education we raise awareness about philanthropy and show Canadians the enormous potential to changing lives through a gift to a charity in the will.
