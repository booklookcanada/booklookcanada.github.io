What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

GRIEF AND BEREAVEMENT FOR INDIVIDUALS WHO HAVE LOST A LOVED ONE THROUGH DEATH. At Norfolk & Haldimand Hospice we believe in embracing every moment of life with compassion dignity and support. As a beacon of comfort in our community we offer specialized care and services to individuals and families navigating life-limiting illnesses. Our Vision: To offer comprehensive hospice palliative care support for communities of Norfolk and Haldimand. Our Services: Our hospice provides a range of services tailored to meet the diverse needs of our patients and their families. We are committed to walking alongside our community members every step of their journey. Our Values: At the heart of our hospice is a commitment to respect integrity empathy and collaboration. We believe in upholding the dignity and worth of every individual fostering meaningful connections and empowering individuals to live their lives to the fullest even in the face of illness. How You Can Support Us: Join us in our mission to provide compassionate care by volunteering donating or spreading awareness about the vital services we offer. Together we can make a difference in the lives of those facing life’s most challenging moments. No matter where you are in your journey The Norfolk Haldimand Hospice is here to provide comfort support and understanding every step of the way. Reach out to us today to learn more about how we can help.
