What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

THE ENAGB YOUTH PROGRAM PLANS ORGANIZES AND IMPLEMENTS PROGRAMMING BASED ON THE GUIDANCE OF OUR YOUTH AND STAFF TO ENSURE A COLLABORATIVE EFFORT FOR EMOTIONAL PHYSICAL SPIRITUAL AND MENTAL WELLBEING. EARLYON OFFERS FREE PROGRAMS TO PARENTS/CAREGIVERS AND THEIR FAMILIES FROM BIRTH TO SIX YEARS OF AGE. THESE CENTERS WELCOME ALL FAMILIES TO PARTICIPATE IN QUALITY PROGRAMS THAT. 1) HELP STRENGTHEN ADULT-CHILD RELATIONSHIPS; 2) SUPPORT PARENT EDUCATION; 3) FOSTER HEALTHY CHILD DEVELOPMENT.
