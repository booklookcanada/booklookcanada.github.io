What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Aug 31, 2024)

Toronto Dance Theatre (TDT) is a professional contemporary dance company dedicated to the research creation and production of original Canadian choreography. TDT bridges connections between artists and audiences in Toronto and beyond through live dance productions artist residency opportunities and public outreach.
