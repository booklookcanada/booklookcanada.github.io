What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

We carry out the work of the UCC I Cornwall Ontario. We conduct weekly worship and religious education. A trained team of pastoral care volunteers provide visitation in homes hospitals and nursing homes. We established the Feed My Sheep program in conjunction with the Agape Center providing hot meals and fellowship on Statutory holidays. We support the Agape Kids Community Kitchen with donations of food for children. We donate funds to Mission and Service which provides help across the world. We are a founding member of Cornwall Interfaith Partnership a group of multi-faiths who share our church space. We provide safe space for Centennial Choir Wigs for Cancer Comfort Quilts and Swahili Faith Community. We also provide safe space for Encore Seniors who conduct education seminars. We donate money to several qualified donees.
