Who runs it — the people it pays

It reported 29 full-time positions and 12 part-time employees (year ended Dec 31, 2024). Total pay for all its positions: $2,090,901, as filed. The highest pay band it reported was “$120,000–$159,999”. 1 employee was reported in that band. Pay is reported in bands — ranges, not exact salaries. The top band is the highest range with anyone in it: it is not one person's salary, and it is not what everyone there is paid.

What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

THE CHARITY FACILITATES AND PROVIDES FOR THE PREVENTION OF CRUELTY TO ANIMALS AND THEIR PROTECTION. IT PROVIDES SHELTER FOR STRAY INJURED AND ABANDONED ANIMALS INVESTIGATES REPORTS OF CRUELTY TO ANIMALS PUBLIC ENDANGERMENT BY ANIMALS AND PROVIDES ANIMAL POUND SERVICES FOR THE CITY OF PETERBOROUGH.
