What it says it does

(A) TO PROMOTE RACIAL EQUALITY BY CONDUCTING AND PUBLISHING RESEARCH ON THE SUBJECT OF COMBATTING RACISM AND ANTISEMITISM. (B) TO PROMOTE RACIAL EQUALITY BY HOLDING ACADEMIC CONFERENCES ON SUBJECT OF COMBATTING RACISM AND ANTISEMITISM.
