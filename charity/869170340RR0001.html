What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Aug 31, 2024)

The Charity operates an orthodox Jewish high school for girls devoted to the study of the laws tenets and doctrines of the Jewish faith as well as general academic studies. The Charity carries on edu- cational programs to provide teachers with the necessary skills to successfully educate their stud- ents in the laws beliefs and practices of the jewish religion. It organizes and takes part in cha- ritable community service programs including visiting the sick and elderly providing meals for the needy and volunteer work for existing charitable organizations.
