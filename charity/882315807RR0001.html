What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended May 31, 2024)

EACH SPRING THE FOUNDATION SEEKS APPLICATIONS FOR BURSARIES FROM GRADE 12 STUDENTS IN WINDSOR AND/OR ESSEX COUNTY ONTARIO WHO ARE FINANCIALLY CHALLENGED DUE TO THE DEATH OF ONE OR MORE PARENTS AND YET WISH TO ENROLL IN THEIR FIRST YEAR AT A POST-SECONDARY COLLEGE OR UNIVERSITY. THE FOUNDATION THEN AWARDS ONE OR MORE BURSARIES EACH YEAR WHICH AMOUNTS ARE THEN PAID TO THE POST-SECONDARY SCHOOL CHOSEN BY THE APPLICANT WITH THE PAYMENT MADE BEFORE THE SCHOOL YEAR COMMENCES.
IN THE FISCAL YEAR RUNNING FROM JUNE 1 2023 AND ENDING MAY 31 2024 THE FOUNDATION WAS ACTIVE IN THAT IN JUNE 2023 THE FOUNDATION AWARDED A BURSARY OF $2 000 TO (...) A GRADE 12 STUDENT AT ST. ANNE HS. SHE IS NOW ATTENDING THE UNIVERSITY OF WINDSOR. THE FOUNDATION ALSO WAS ACTIVE IN THE FISCAL YEAR IN THE SENSE IT INVESTED ITS ASSETS WITH TD DIRECT INVESTING IN ORDER FOR THE FOUNDATION TO MAXIMIZE ITS FUTURE GIVING ACTIVITIES.

What this page can't tell you

  • Whether its work works. A return shows money, not results.
  • Whether anyone audited these figures. The CRA publishes returns as filed, without checking them. Some charities also post audited statements — BookLook compared the largest 200 charities' published statements with their returns (see “How to read this site”).
  • Anything after May 31, 2024, its newest return in the published data.
  • Who its directors are. The published data does not include them.
  • Questions worth asking it directly: Do you publish audited financial statements? Who sits on your board this year? What did last year's programs achieve, and how do you know?
