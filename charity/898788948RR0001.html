What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

The Hospice for Eating Disorders of Toronto operating as Sheena's Place is a support centre for individuals families and friends affected by eating disorders. Sheena's Place provides a wide range of professionally facilitated groups (both online and in person) in many different areas such as support skill building nutritional body image & movement expressive arts webinars workshops and recreational all free of charge. Sheena's Place is committed to providing accessible timely support and education to increase awareness and understanding of eating disorders while fostering a sense of community and connection.
