What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

There are online and in-person options for our weekly Sunday service. Sunday school programming weekly for kids. Pastoral care provided for members and non members. Bible studies and prayer meetings are available. There is a mid week children's program for community kids supper provided every other week free of charge. Each summer we run a soccer program for community children targeted to those who cannot afford to play in the city league. Money available for those who cannot afford to pay. Our Deacons run a Benevolence Ministry for people in the congregation and community that need help with groceries etc. Twice weekly we run an exercise program for seniors free of charge. Once a month a seniors lunch and speaker focused on topics for seniors.
