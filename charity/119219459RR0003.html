What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

CNIB is a registered charity passionately providing community-based support knowledge and a national voice to ensure Canadians who are blind or partially sighted have the confidence skills and opportunities to fully participate in life. To do that our dedicated specialists work with people of all ages in their home communities or local CNIB office - providing the personalized support they need to see beyond vision loss build their independence and lead the lives they want. In addition to our community-based services we strive to eliminate avoidable sight loss with world-class research and by promoting the importance of vision health through public education.
