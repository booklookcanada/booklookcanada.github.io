It reported 14 full-time positions and 5 part-time employees (year ended Dec 31, 2024). Total pay for all its positions: $1,034,301, as filed. The highest pay band it reported was “$120,000–$159,999”. 1 employee was reported in that band. Pay is reported in bands — ranges, not exact salaries. The top band is the highest range with anyone in it: it is not one person's salary, and it is not what everyone there is paid.

What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

WE ENVISION A FUTURE WHERE THE KAWARTHA REGION IS CHARACTERIZED BY NATURAL SPACES AND CONNECTIONS THAT SUPPORT HEALTHY AND REPRESENTATIVE ECOCSYSTEMS AND LANDSCAPES. OUR MISSION IS 'CARING FOR LAND ENTRUSTED TO US AND HELPING OTHERS PROTECT THE LAND THEY LOVE IN THE KAWARTHAS'. BY FULFILLING OUR MISSION WE DESIRE AND EXPECT THAT KLT WILL BE RECOGNIZED AND TRUSTED AS A LEADER IN CONSERVATION IN THE KAWARTHAS AND WILL ACTIVELY WORK WITH OTHERS TO ACHIEVE OUR VISION.
