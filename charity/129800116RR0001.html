What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

1. TO OPERATE A PREGNANCY RESOURCE CENTRE THAT WILL PROVIDE PRACTICAL ASSISTANCE. SUCH AS THE PROVISION OF MATERNITY AND INFANT CLOTHING AND ASSISTANCE IN FINDING ACCOMMODATION AND PARENTING AND MENTORING PROGRAMS. TO ANYONE WHO IS IN DISTRESS AT THE TIME OF A PREGNANCY OR WHO IS CARING FOR AN INFANT. AND/OR IS WITHOUT THE EMOTIONAL OR SPIRITUAL MEANS TO COPE. 2. TO OFFER LIMITED HEALTH CARE SERVICES INCLUDING MEDICAL CONFIRMATION OF PREGNANCY AND/OR ULTRASOUND AND SCREENING ASSESSMENT AND TREATMENT FOR SEXUALLY TRANSMITTED INFECTIONS (STIS). 3. TO EDUCATE INDIVIDUALS ABOUT SEXUAL HEALTH AND HEALTHY RELATIONSHIPS THROUGH PRESENTATIONS AND WORKSHOPS.
