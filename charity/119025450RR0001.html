What it says it does

THE CHARITY HOLDS WORSHIP SERVICES DAILY OPERATES YOUTH GROUPS CONDUCTS ADULT EDUCATION SEMINARS AS WELL AS RUNS A SUMMER CAMP FOR RECREATION AND RELIGIOUS PROGRAMS.
