What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Apr 30, 2024)

Conrad Grebel University College is founded by the Mennonite church and affiliated with the University of Waterloo. The mission and programs of Grebel are rooted in and and inspired by its Christian identity and its Anabaptist/Mennonite heritage. Its programs include residential teaching research and community activities. The College teaches courses in Arts History Music Peace and Conflict Studies Philosophy Religious Studies and Sociology. The College also provides residence accommodation for university students.
