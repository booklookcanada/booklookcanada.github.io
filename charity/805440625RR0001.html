What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

WE PROVIDE A HOUSING TO FAMILIES AND INDIVIDUALS IN NEED IN THE DOMINICAN REPUBLIC. WE ESTABLISH AND PROVIDE A VARIETY OF ONGOING SUPPORT FOR SCHOOLS MEDICAL CLINICS CHURCHES AND COMMUNITY CENTRES IN THE DOMINICAN REPUBLIC. WE ADVANCE EDUCATION BY PROVIDING VOCATIONAL TRAINING FOR ADULTS IN THE DOMINICAN REPUBLIC. WE ARE ENCOURAGING HEALTH EDUCATION AND CARE FOR CHILDREN AND EXPANDING THE MEDICAL CLINIC PROGRAM TO INCLUDE A SMALL PEDIATRIC MEDICAL CENTRE.
