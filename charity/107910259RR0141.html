What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

THE PARISH IS DEDICATED TO FOSTERING SPIRITUAL GROWTH THROUGH THE ADVANCEMENT OF ROMAN CATHOLIC RELIGIOUS WORSHIP INSTRUCTIONS AND MINISTRY TO BOTH PARISHIONERS AND THE COMMUNITY AT LARGE. IN THIS RESPECT THE PARISH: HOLDS WORSHIP SERVICES BOTH DAILY AND ON WEEKENDS PROVIDES CATECHESIS AND SACRAMENTAL PREPARATION PROVIDES PASTORAL CARE TO THE SICK DYING AND THOSE IN GRIEF COUNSELS INDIVIDUALS AND FAMILIES IN CRISIS ORGANIZES DAYS FOR PARISH RETREATS AND ACTIVITIES AND ASSISTS THE NEEDY AND HOMELESS IN ITS NEIGHBOURHOOD BY PROVIDING PASTORAL CARE FOOD VOUCHERS AND CLOTHING.
