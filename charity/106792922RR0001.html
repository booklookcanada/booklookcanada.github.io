What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

PROMOTE GROWTH AND DEVELOPMENT OF CHILDREN BY PROVIDING THE OPPORTUNITY FOR ONE-TO-ONE FRIENDSHIPS WITH ADULTS. TRADITIONAL MATCHES ARE ONE CHILD TO ONE ADULT IN A WEEKLY MEETING SETTING. OCCASIONAL BIG BUNCH PROGRAM TAKES CHILDREN FROM WAITING LIST TO AN EVENT ARRANGED BY BBBS PETERBOROUGH. THE IN-SCHOOL-MENTORING PROGRAM MATCHES AN ADULT AND A CHILD FOR ONE HOUR A WEEK IN-SCHOOLS. THE GO-GIRLS PROGRAM MATCHES A YOUNG FEMALE ADULT WITH A SMALL GROUP OF 5-8 SENIOR FEMALE ELEMENTARY SCHOOL STUDENTS IN A SCHOOL SETTING TO PROVIDE 'HEALTHY-BODY-HEALTHY-MIND' PROGRAMMING OVER A TEN WEEK PERIOD. THE GAME-ON PROGRAM MATCHES A YOUNG MALE ADULT WITH A SMALL GROUP OF 5-8 SENIOR MALE ELEMENTARY SCHOOL STUDENTS IN A SCHOOL SETTING TO PROVIDE 'HEALTHY-BODY-HEALTHY-MIND' PROGRAMMING OVER A TEN WEEK PERIOD. THE CAMP-PONACKA PROGRAM IS DESIGNED TO COVER THE COST OF SENDING CURRENT 'LITTLES' TO A CAMP-PROGRAM SPONSORED BY BBBS ALUMNI (PREVIOUS 'LITTLES')
