What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

Provide places of worship & mediation in Canada 365 days a year. Operates full-time approved daily & weekend School up to grade 8. Provide family counseling services and seminars in education welfare volunteer work health and community. Provide a venue and forum for youth to engage in youth events sporting educational and cultural activities. Provide emergency relief and services to people in need within Canada and internationally. Cooperate with Government of Canada in international relief efforts. Operate ladies wing to provide support and counseling for ladies. Operate and administer food banks across Canada.
