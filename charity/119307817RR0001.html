What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

YOUTH WITHOUT SHELTER IS A 53 BED RESIDENCE FOR HOMELESS YOUTH AGED 16-24. IN ADDITION TO SHELTER AND FOOD THE FOLLOWING SERVICES ARE PROVIDED: MENTAL HEALTH COUNSELLING LIFE SKILLS TRAINING EMPLOYMENT SERVICES PERSONAL CLOTHING AND HYGIENE NEEDS AND HOUSING AND AFTER DISCHARGE CARE. TWENTY OF THE BEDS ARE RESERVED FOR A STAY IN SCHOOL PROGRAM PROVIDING SPECIALIZED CARE FOR YOUTH TO ENABLE THEM TO CONTINUE THEIR EDUCATION. ANOTHER 5 BEDS AT A SATELLITE PROPERTY ACCOMMODATE ADDITIONAL RESIDENTS IN THE STAY IN SCHOOL PROGRAM.
