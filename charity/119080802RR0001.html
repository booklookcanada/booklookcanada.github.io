What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

PARKMINSTER UNITED IS AN OPEN AND AFFIRMING/INCLUSIVE CONGREGATION OF THE UNITED CHURCHH OF CANADA. WE HOLD WEEKLY WORSHIP SERVICES AND PROVIDE PASTORAL CARE TO MEMBERS AND NON MEMBERS IN THE COMMUNITY. WE OFFER A CHRISTIAN MINISTRY TO ADULTS YOUTH AND CHILDREN THROUGH A VARIETY OF CHRISTIAN EDUCATION AND OTHER PROGRAMS. WE DONATE TO CHARITABLE CAUSES LOCALLY NATIONALLY AND ABROAD INCLUDING REFUGEE SPONSORSHIPS THE UNITED CHURCH OF CANADA MISSION AND SERVICE FUND INDIGENOUS CAUSES ORGANIZATIONS SUPPORTING THE HOMELESS THE FOOD BANK ETC. WE HAVE A LABYRINTH ON OUR PROPERTY OPEN TO THE COMMUNITY.
