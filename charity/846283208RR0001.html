What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Jun 30, 2024)

ShelterBox Canada provides shelter essential items and technical assistance to help some of the world’s most vulnerable people recover and rebuild their homes after disaster. ShelterBox teams listen and adapt our support to the needs of each community working together with those affected by disaster alongside our supporters and partners. We specialize in emergency shelter ranging from tents to shelter kits and semi-permanent structures and also provide affected communities with vital supplies like water filters solar lights cooking equipment mosquito nets mattresses and blankets and more - all customized to each situation. ShelterBox Canada is a registered charity and it is one of 14 ShelterBox Affiliates around the world. ShelterBox Canada has 88 volunteers across the country who raise funds and awareness for the organization.
