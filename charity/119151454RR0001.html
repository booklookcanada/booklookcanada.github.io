What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

THE COMMUNITY OWNS AND OPERATES:AN ELEMENTARY SCHOOL AND ONE NURSING HOME AS WELL AS A SENIOR RESIDENCE IN MANITOBA. THE MEMBER OF THE COMMUNITY ARE ALSO INVOLVED IN: TEACHING PRESCHOOLERS AND CHILDREN WHO ARE MENTALLY CHALLENGED; PROVIDING RELIGIOUS INSTRUCTIONS PUBLICATIONS AND MUSIC FROM PRESCHOOLERS TO ADULTS SUMMER CAMPS FROM AGE 7-16 COUNSELLING/GRIEF COUNSELLING/SPIRITUAL DIRECTION PASTORAL CARE VISITING AND/OR ASSISTING WITH NUMEROUS ASPECTS OF LIFE FOR THE SICK ELDERLY THE POOR AND THE IMMIGRANT. THE SISTERS ACTIVELY PARICIPATE IN THE VARIOUS PROGRAMS AND THE LIFE OF THE PARISHES ACROSS CANADA. MOST OF THESE ACTIVITIES ARE PERFORMED ON A VOLUNTARY BASIS.
