What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

During the fiscal period Naseeha Mental Health independently operated several charitable programs. These included: Helpline Operation: Naseeha ran a helpline available 24 hours a day 7 days a week throughout the year providing essential support to individuals in need of mental health assistance. Education Programs: The organization developed and delivered educational programs targeted at young people across Canada aiming to raise awareness and provide guidance on mental health issues. Promotional Material Development: Naseeha created and distributed promotional materials to inform individuals struggling with mental health issues about the available help and resources they can access through the organization. Web Therapy Program: Naseeha offered a free web therapy program as well as a subsidized therapy option to make mental health support more accessible and affordable. These services provided professional cost-effective therapy to individuals seeking more structured support These activities were carried out by Naseeha through its own employees and volunteers ensuring direct and impactful service delivery.
