What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

ARMAGH IS A NON-PROFIT SECOND-STAGE SUPPORTIVE TRANSITIONAL HOUSING PROGRAM FOR WOMEN AND THEIR CHILDREN WHO HAVE EXPERIENCED VIOLENCE OR ABUSE. ARMAGH ACCEPTS REFERRALS FROM THE LOCAL CRISIS SHELTERS AND THE PEEL CHILDREN'S AID DOMESTIC VIOLENCE TEAM. SERVICES INCLUDE SUPPORTIVE TRANSITIONAL HOUSING FOR UP TO TWO (2) YEARS; INDIVIDUAL AND GROUP COUNSELLING; LEGAL SUPPORT HOUSING SUPPORT SELF-ESTEEM PARENTING LIFE SKILLS AND SUPPORT PROGRAMS FOR BOTH WOMEN AND CHILDREN ADVOCACY REFERRALS ABOUT COMMUNITY RESOURCES AND OUTREACH FOLLOW UP FOR WOMEN AND THEIR CHILDREN FOR UP TO ONE MORE YEAR WHEN THEY LEAVE. ARMAGH HAS 20 UNITS/APARTMENTS AND CAN ACCOMMODATE 65 RESIDENTS
