The highest pay band it reported was “$200,000–$249,999”. 1 employee was reported in that band. Pay is reported in bands — ranges, not exact salaries. What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Jul 31, 2024)

HEALTHPARTNERS CANADA (THE ORGANIZATION) IS AN ASSOCIATION OF NATIONAL HEALTH CHARITIES INCORPORATED UNDER THE CANADA NOT-FOR-PROFIT CORPORATIONS ACT TO SOLICIT DONATIONS FROM EMPLOYEES OF VARIOUS CORPORATIONS AND FEDERAL AND PROVINCIAL GOVERNMENTS FOR DISTRIBUTION TO ITS MEMBERS. ITS MISSION IS TO PROMOTE HEALTH AND WELL-BEING BY ENGAGING AND CONNECTING CANADIANS TO OUR LEADING HEALTH CHARITIES. THE ORGANIZATION THAT WORKS TO ASSIST SEVERAL HEALTH AND DISEASE-RELATED CHARITY ORGANIZATIONS ACROSS CANADA. IT DOES SO BY HELPING EMPLOYERS RUN WORKPLACE FUNDRAISERS AND CHARITABLE CAMPAIGNS AND DISTRIBUTING THE DONATION MONEY TO ITS 17 MEMBERS.
