What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

THE MARIANHILL FOUNDATION IS A PUBLIC FOUNDATION WITH THE OBJECTIVE OF RAISING FUNDS TO SUPPORT MARIANHILL INC. TO PROVIDE CARE TO THE FRAIL ELDERLY IN RENFREW COUNTY. IN ADDITION TO OPERATING 194 BEDS MARIANHILL PROVIDES A NUMBER OF PROGRAMS INCLUDING ADULT DAY PROGRAMS IN PEMBROKE GOLDEN LAKE BARRY'S BAY PETAWAWA AND ALMONTE IN-HOME RESPITE SUPPORTIVE HOUSING HOMEMAKING OVERNIGHT RESPITE AND A SUPPORT GROUP FOR CLIENTS WITH PARKINSON'S DISEASE. MARIANHILL INC. IS A CHARITABLE ORGANIZATION THAT WAS STARTED BY THE GREY SISTERS OF THE IMMACULATE CONCEPTION. MARIANHILL INC. RELIES ON DONATIONS AND FUNDRAISING TO HELP FUND PROGRAMS AND TO PURCHASE EQUIPMENT FOR CLIENTS AND PROVIDE IMPROVEMENTS TO THE BUILDING.
