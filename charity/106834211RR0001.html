What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

At Community Living Cambridge we partner with individuals who have developmental disabilities and their community to live fulfilling lives. We do so by operating 26 group homes and apartments through independent living supports in the community home sharing arrangements employment supports recreational and community participation activities as well as respite and passport to the community programs. The person centered approach to our supports strives to be inclusive respectful innovative and accountable at all times.
