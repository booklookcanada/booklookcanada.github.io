What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Apr 30, 2024)

JUMP MATH IS AN AWARD-WINNING CHARITABLE ORGANIZATION THAT IS COMMITTED TO BUILDING A MORE EQUITABLE SOCIETY THROUGH MATH EDUCATION. JUMP MATH'S MISSION IS TO EMPOWER EDUCATORS AND CHILDREN WITH RESEARCH-INFORMED CURRICULUM-ALIGNED PRINT AND TECHNOLOGY RESOURCES AND APPROACHES THAT INSTIL CONFIDENCE AND SPARK A LIFE-LONG LOVE OF MATHEMATICS. WE DO THIS BY: DEVELOPING AND ENHANCING JUMP MATH'S KINDERGARTEN TO GRADE 8 RESOURCES AND APPROACHES TO PROMOTE THE USE OF THE PROGRAM AMONG TEACHERS EDUCATORS AND SCHOOL ADMINISTRATORS; PROVIDING PROFESSIONAL LEARNING AND SUPPORT FOR EDUCATORS PARENTS TUTORS AND VOLUNTEERS TO ENHANCE MATH EDUCATION FOR CHILDREN; INFORMING THE PUBLIC AND THE EDUCATION SYSTEM ON RESEARCH INTO MATH EDUCATION THROUGH ARTICLES INTERVIEWS SPEAKING ENGAGEMENTS AND CONFERENCES; WORKING COLLABORATIVELY WITH POST-SEONDARY INSTITUTIONS TO UNDERTAKE RESEARCH INTO MATH EDUCATION; AND PROMOTING AN 'ETHOS OF ABILITY' -THE NOTION THAT ALL STUDENTS HAVE THE POTENTIAL TO LEARN AND ACHIEVE SUCCESS IN MATHEMATICS AND OTHER SUBJECTS. JUMP MATH ALSO ENGAGES AND COLLABORATES WITH THOSE ADVANCING MATH EDUCATION AROUND THE WORLD TO FOSTER HUMAN POTENTIAL AND WELL-BEING.
