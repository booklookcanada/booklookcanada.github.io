What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

WE HOLD WORSHIP SERVICES WEEKLY. WE PROVIDE MINISTRIES FOR MEN WOMEN AND CHILDREN WEEKLY OR MONTHLY. WE GIVE PASTORAL CARE. WE OPERATE AND MAINTAIN A FOOD BANK TO PROVIDE EMERGENCY ASSISTANCE TO NEEDY INDIVIDUALS AND FAMILIES. TO ASSIST PERSONS COPING WITH THE EFFECTS TO SUBSTANCE ABUSE BY OFFERING EDUCATIONAL COUNSELLING AND BY ESTABLISHING MUTUAL SUPPORT GROUPS. TO ALSO ASSIST THE FAMILIES COPING WITH SUBSTANCE ABUSE AND IT'S AFTERMATH. PARENT SUPPORT GROUP FOR TEEN/YOUNG MOMS AND FAMILY MEMBERS HOMEMAKING SCHOOL PARENT-CHILD MOTHER GOOSE PROGRAM FOR FAMILIES. ANGER MANAGEMENT TO HELP PEOPLE BETTER UNDERSTAND WHERE WHAT HOW THEIR ANGER IS TRIGGERED AND HOW TO DEAL WITH THE ANGER. DIVORCE RECOVERY TO HELP PERSONS GOING DIVORCE. TO PROMOTE REDUCTION REUSE RECYCLING AND RECOVER OF WASTE GENERATED BY THE COMMUNITY. COMMUNITY KITCHEN TO HELP PEOPLE LEARN HOW TO COOK HEALTHY LOW- COST MEALS FOOD BUDGETING AND SHOPPING SKILLS.
