What it says it does

1) OPERATION OF A CRISIS AND PREGNANCY COUNSELLING CENTER NAMED REFUGE WHICH IS INVOLVED WITH SERVING WOEM IN THE MOMENT OF NEED; 2) PROVIDES CONFIRMATION (OF PREGNANCY) COUNSELLING AND PRACTICAL CARE; 3) ENGAGED IN KEEPING PEOPLE ABREAST OF CURRENT DEVELOPMENTS IN LIFE ISSUES AND MOTIVATING THEM TO USE THEIR VOICE.
