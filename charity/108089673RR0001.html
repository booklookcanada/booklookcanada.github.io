What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Aug 31, 2024)

Canada's National Ballet School recruits the most talented dance students through a national recruitment tour followed by a second-stage 4-week assessment program; provides financial assistance to students; trains dance students for a professional ballet career; provides dance students with an academic education; provides students outside the GTA with residential care; provides career transition program to dance students; trains students and former dancers to become ballet teachers; provides professional development classes for dance teachers; provides dance classes for adults and part-time classes for children; offers seminars for dance teachers; provides workshops for dance teachers and musicians and open classes for children in communities across Canada; provides a broad range of community dance programes for people of all ages and abilities.
