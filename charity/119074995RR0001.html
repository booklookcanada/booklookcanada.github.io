The highest pay band it reported was “$120,000–$159,999”. 1 employee was reported in that band. Pay is reported in bands — ranges, not exact salaries. The top band is the highest range with anyone in it: it is not one person's salary, and it is not what everyone there is paid.

What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

THE YMCA OWEN SOUND GREY BRUCE IS A CHARITY DEDICATED TO STRENGTHEN THE FOUNDATIONS OF COMMUNITIES. WE DO THIS BY NURTURING THE POTENTIAL OF CHILDREN TEENS AND YOUNG ADULTS; PROMOTING HEALTHY LIVING; AND FOSTERING SOCIAL RESPONSIBILITY. OUR FOCUS ON INCLUSIVENESS AND ACCESSIBILITY MEANS WE SERVE PEOPLE OF ALL AGES BACKGROUNDS AND ABILITIES THROUGH ALL STAGES OF LIFE. THROUGH YMCA FINANCIAL ASSISTANCE PROGRAMS THE YMCA IS ACCESSIBLE TO ALL. OUR CORE OFFERINGS INCLUDE: CHILD CARE CAMPING AQUATICS HEALTH & FITNESS RECREATION EDUCATION EMPLOYMENT SERVICES COMMUNITY INITIATIVES SETTLEMENT AND LANGUAGE SERVICES VOLUNTEER DEVELOPMENT AND OPPORTUNITIES TO GIVE
