What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

RUNNING & READING CLUBS - AFTER-SCHOOL PROGRAM THAT ENCOURAGES PHYSICAL MENTAL SOCIAL EMOTIONAL AND ACADEMIC OUTCOMES IN LOW-INCOME/EQUITY-DESERVING COMMUNITIES ACROSS CANADA. BACKPACK PROGRAM - PARTNERS WITH LOCAL SCHOOLS AND SCHOOL BOARDS TO DISTRIBUTE BACKPACKS FILLED WITH UP TO 6 MONTH'S WORTH OF SUPPLIES TO LOW-INCOME CHILDREN NATIONAWIDE. JUNIOR COACH LEADERSHIP DEVELOPMENT PROGRAM - OPPORTUNITIES FOR YOUTH IN MIDDLE SCHOOL TO RECEIVE MENTORING MENTOR YOUNGER CHILDREN AND LEARN LEADERSHIP SKILLS THAT WILL EQUIP THEM TO STAY IN SCHOOL GRADUATE AND SUCCEED. SUMMER ADVENTURE PROGRAM - ONLINE SUMMER PROGRAM TO KEEP CHILDREN LEARNING AND ENGAGED THROUGHOUT THE SUMMER MONTHS. BRAINWORX! VIRTUAL LEARNING PROGRAM - VIRTUAL PROGRAM ENCOURAGING EQUITY-DESERVING CHILDREN IN GRADE 1 TO 6 TO DEVELOP A LIFE-LONG LOVE OF LEARNING; FOCUS IS ON LITERACY MOVEMENT AND SOCIAL-EMOTIONAL LEARNING WITH A GROWTH MINDSET UTILIZING MENTORSHIP AND PARENTAL INVOLVEMENT. THRIVE! - IN-SCHOOL RESOURCE FOR TEACHERS DESIGNED TO HELP STUDENTS DEVELOP SOCIAL-EMOTIONAL COMPETENCIES AND BRIDGE MENTAL WELL-BEING GAPS. VOLUNTEERS - START2FINISH HAS 700 VOLUNTEERS ACROSS CANADA WHO HELP MAKE THESE PROGRAMS POSSIBLE.
