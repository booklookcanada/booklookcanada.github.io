What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Jun 30, 2024)

ST.JOHN'S -KILMARNOCK SCHOOL (SJK) IS A CO-EDUCATIONAL DAY SCHOOL FOR GRADES JK-GRADE 12 AND BOARDING SCHOOL FOR GRADE 9-12 WITH A CHRISTIAN BACKROUND WHRE SPIRITUALLY IS PRACTICED THROUGH THE TRADITIONS OF THE ANGLICAN CHURCH AND OFFERS A FULL PROGRAM FROM JUNIOR KINDERGATEN TO GRADE 12 . THE SCHOOL S A LOCATED IN THE HEART OF CANADA'S TEHCNOLOGICAL TRIANGLE AND PROVIDES A FULL CO-CURRICULAR AND VARISTY SPORTS PROGRAM. SJK PREPARE RESPONSIBILITES THINKING STUDENTS FOR THE RIGORS OF UNIVERSITY LEVEL STUDY.
