What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

Core Activities 1. Education Training & Learning - To create inclusive learning opportunities that build confidence skills and community connection for people from all walks of life through creative and therapeutic learning life skills and peer support outdoor learning in gardening farming and foraging as well as volunteer and work readiness programs. 2. Food Security - To ensure access to nutritious food while using food as a tool for connection dignity and community care through weekly food banks meal delivery community meals. 3. Transitional & Emergency Shelter - To provide safe supportive and dignified shelter for people in crisis or transition empowering them to rebuild their lives. We offer transitional housing for women and children housing for men in addiction recovery emergency shelters for vulnerable adults and participate in emergency winter partnerships. 4. Outreach & Community Engagement - To meet people where they are — on the streets in shelters or in precarious living situations — and connect them to vital supports through peer volunteer outreach teams crisis response and resource navigation and community relationship building.
