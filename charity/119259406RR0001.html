What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Jun 30, 2024)

THE ORGANIZATION PROVIDES YOUNG CHILDREN WITH SUPERIOR MUSICAL AND EDUCATIONAL OPPORTUNITIES BY PERFORMING THE FINEST AVAILABLE REPERTOIRE ESPECIALLY CANADIAN WORKS AND COMMISSIONS IN CANADA AND ACROSS FIVE CONTINENTS. THE CHORUS IS RECOGNIZED FOR ITS EXCELLENCE THROUGHOUT NORTH AMERICA AND EUROPE. IN ORDER TO SUBSIDIZE STUDENT COSTS THE ORGANIZATION CARRIES OUT VARIOUS FUND-RAISING ACTIVITIES THOUGHOUT THE YEAR INCLUDING FLOWER SALES AND AN ANNUAL GALA DINNER AND CONCERT. CONCERTS ARE HELD THOUGHOUT THE YEAR AND THE THE SENIOR CHORUS EMBARKS ON A MAJOR TOUR APPROXIMATELY EVERY OTHER YEAR.
