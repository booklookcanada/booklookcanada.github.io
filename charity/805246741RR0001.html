What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

THREE-FOLD MINISTRIES PREVIOUSLY OPERATED A RETREAT CENTER IN NICARAGUA PROVIDING SUPPORT FOR PASTORS MISSIONARIES VOLUNTEERS AND CHRISTIAN GROUPS THROUGH CONSTUCTION PROJECTS MEDICAL SERVICES AND MINISTRY TRAINING. HOWEVER ON SEPTEMBER 1 2024 THE NICARAGUAN GOVERNMENT SEIZED ALL OF THREE-FOLD MINISTRIES' ASSETS IN THE COUNTRY INCLUDING PROPERTY AND EQUIPMENT. AS A RESULT THE SKYLARK CENTER IS NO LONGER UNDER THREE-FOLD'S OWNERSHIP. WHILE ALL OPERATIONS IN NICARAGUA HAVE BEEN TERMINATED WE REMAIN AN ACTIVE CHARITY AND CONTINUE TO PURSUE OUR MISSION AND MINISTRY AS A WE ARE EXPLORING NEW OPPORTUNITIES IN ANOTHER CENTRAL AMERICAN COUNTRY BELIZE.
