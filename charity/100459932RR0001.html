What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Jan 31, 2024)

THE GOODFELLOWS NEWSPAPER DRIVE IS CARRIED OUT IN LATE NOVEMBER EACH YEAR TO RAISE FUNDS FOR FOOD BASKETS WHICH ARE DELIVERED TO THE NEEDY FAMILIES AND OTHER NEEDY PERSONS PRIMARILY AT CHRISTMAS AND EASTER BUT ALSO THROUGHOUT THE YEAR AS THE NEED ARISES. AN ANNUAL FUNDRAISING BANQUET IS ALSO HELD TO RAISE FUNDS. CASH DONATIONS ARE GRATEFULLY ACCEPTED FROM THE PUBLIC. THE BELLE RIVER GOODFELLOWS ALSO ACCEPT SUPPORT FROM OTHER CHARITIES AND PUBLIC INSTITUTIONS FROM THE GREATER BELLE RIVER AREA.

What this page can't tell you

  • Whether its work works. A return shows money, not results.
  • Whether anyone audited these figures. The CRA publishes returns as filed, without checking them. Some charities also post audited statements — BookLook compared the largest 200 charities' published statements with their returns (see “How to read this site”).
  • Anything after Jan 31, 2024, its newest return in the published data.
  • Who its directors are. The published data does not include them.
