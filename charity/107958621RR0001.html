What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

Save the Children Canada implemented programs focused on empowering girls and boys to fulfill their rights ensuring every last child has an equal opportunity to survive learn and be protected. This work included programs in health education protection economic empowerment and gender equality. In 2024 Save the Children Canada worked across multiple countries and contexts including but not limited to Gaza Ukraine Syria Colombia Peru Bolivia Niger Mozambique and Mali. Save the Children Canada worked in partnership with Indigenous Nations and organizations to support programming in relation to Indigenous Child Rights and Reconciliation. This work enabled programs in cultural revitalization climate change education mental health and emergency preparedness with over 100 Indigenous Nations communities and organizations.
