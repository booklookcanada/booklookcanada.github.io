What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

PROVIDE FOOD (NON-PERISHABLE) AND GROCERY GIFT CARDS TO OUR NEIGHBOURS IN NEED LIVING IN WEST WHITBY BOUNDARY. TO QUALIFY THEY MUST NOT HAVE HAD HELP IN PAST TWO MONTHS. WE ALSO HELP WITH PURCHASES OF FURNITURE & CLOTHING WHEN REQUESTED AND CHRISTMAS GIFT CARD FOR FAMILIES TO BUY XMAS GIFTS FOR CHILDREN DINNER INCLUDED HELP FOR SENIORS FOR EXTRA FOOD AT CHRISTMAS. WE ARE UNDER 30 VOLUNTEERS WHO BRING THE FOOD & VOLUNTEERS TO OUR CLIENTS DOOR TO DOOR. WE MEET ONCE PER MONTH TO PLAN & PREPARE.
