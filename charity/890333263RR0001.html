The highest pay band it reported was “$120,000–$159,999”. 2 employees were reported in that band. Pay is reported in bands — ranges, not exact salaries. The top band is the highest range with anyone in it: it is not one person's salary, and it is not what everyone there is paid.

HOSPICE MUSKOKA OPERATES A RESIDENTIAL HOSPICE IN PORT CARLING ON. THEY ALSO PROVIDE VISITING HOSPICE VOLUNTEER PROGRAMS AND GRIEF SUPPORT TO CLIENTS OF ALL AGES DEALING WITH END-OF-LIFE ISSUES. PROVIDES INFORMATION AND REFERRAL ON HOSPICE PALLIATIVE CARE END-OF-LIFE ISSUES SUCH AS ADVANCED CARE PLANNING. PROVIDES EDUCATION/WORKSHOPS ON HOSPICE PALLIATIVE CARE GRIEF AND BEREAVEMENT AND HAS A LENDING LIBRARY OF VARIOUS RESOURCES. CAREGIVER RESPITE AND SUPPORT IS AVAILABLE.
