What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

PROVIDES FUNDS TO THE ELEMENTARY & SECONDARY SCHOOLS TO ENSURE STUDENTS HAVE THE DAILY ESSENTIALS TO ATTEND SCHOOL. SOME STUDENTS THAT ARE LESS FORTUNATE ATTEND WITH NOT THE PROPER CLOTHING OR COME WITH AN EMPTY STOMACH. WE WANT TO ENSURE THESE STUDENTS RECEIVE URGENT ITEMS TO BE ABLE TO COME TO SCHOOL. WE ALSO PROVIDE FUNDING TO THE STUDENT NUTRITION PROGRAM THAT PROVIDES HEALTHY SNACKS TO OVER 9000 STUDENT IN SARNIA-LAMBTON. WE HAVE OVER 70 VOLUNTEERS REGISTERED THAT HELP WITH OUR FUNDRAISING EVENTS WHICH INCLUDES BINGOS GALAS AND FITNESS DAYS. AS WELL THE ASSIST WITH THE PAY IT FORWARD DAYS NOELLE'S GIFT HAS DURING THE SUMMER AND WINTER. NO NEW PROGRAMS
