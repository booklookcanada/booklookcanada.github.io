OPERATES AN ADULT DAY PROGRAM FOR PERSONS WITH ALZHEIMER'S DISEASE OR RELATED DEMENTIAS (ADRD). PROVIDES RELIEF TO CAREGIVERS OF PERSONS WITH ADRD THROUGH IN HOME RESPITE CARE.
