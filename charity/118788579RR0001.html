What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Jun 30, 2024)

Appleby College is an independent coeducation university-prepartory school for day students and boarders in grade 7-12. Founded in 1911 the College occupies a spectacular 59 -acre site on the shores of Lake Ontario. Appleby's Mission is to educate and enable our students to become leaders of character major contributors to and valued representatives of their local national and international communities. Appleby's vision is to develop intellectual curiosity instill a love of learning and promote personal excellence in all areas of endeavour. To provide access to outstanding experiences facilities faculty and staff. To be an internationally recognized model of educational excellence and innovation.
