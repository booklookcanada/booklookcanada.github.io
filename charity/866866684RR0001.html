What it costs to raise a dollar

Across its 2022, 2023, 2024 returns pooled together, it spent $5.99 on fundraising for every $100 of cash donations it received, as filed. Pooling three returns smooths out one unusual campaign. Gifts of goods are taken out of the donation side only where the CRA publishes that breakdown (the most recent year), so read this as approximate.

Who runs it — the people it pays

It reported 9 full-time positions and 4 part-time employees (year ended Dec 31, 2024). Total pay for all its positions: $977,941, as filed. The highest pay band it reported was “$80,000–$119,999”. 2 employees were reported in that band. Pay is reported in bands — ranges, not exact salaries. The top band is the highest range with anyone in it: it is not one person's salary, and it is not what everyone there is paid.

What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

The objectives of the ministry include communicating biblical truth about Israel and the Messiah while bringing physical and spiritual comfort to the Jewish people throughout Canada by providing religious educational activities or instructions publishing religious materials including books magazines pamphlets audio and video cassettes and radio broadcasting and by providing counselling activities.
