What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

GRANTING FUNDS TO AND MONITORING PROGRAMS OF CHARITIES WHOSE MAIN FOCUS IS ON ASSISTING THOSE STRUGGLING WITH POVERTY AND SUBSTANCE ABUSE BY PROVIDING FOOD CLOTHING HOUSING AND MEDICAL ASSISTANCE. GRANTING FUNDS TO AND MONITORING PROGRESS OF CHARITIES WHO WORK WITH PHYSICALLY AND MENTALLY HANDICAPPED PERSONS AS WELL AS PROGRAMS THAT SUPPORT YOUTH ACTIVITIES. TO FURTHER THE EDUCATION OF ECONOMICALLY DISADVANTAGED CHILDREN AND YOUTH BY CONSTRUCTING EQUIPPING STAFFING MAINTAINING AND/OR OPERATING SCHOOLS OR OTHER EDUCATIONAL FACILITIES IN IMPOVERISHED AREAS OF THE WORLD AND PROVIDING SCHOLARSHIPS FOR POST-SECONDARY EDUCATION.
