What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

THE MANDATE OF MACKENZIE HEALTH FOUNDATION ('THE FOUNDATION') IS TO CONDUCT FUNDRAISING ACTIVITIES ON BEHALF OF MACKENZIE HEALTH (THE 'HOSPITAL') TO SECURE THE RESOURCES NECESSARY TO REALIZE ITS VISION OF CREATING A WORLD-CLASS HEALTH EXPERENCE. THE FOUNDATION'S EXPANDED ROLE INCLUDED COMPLETING THE MAJOR COMPREHENSIVE CAPITAL CAMPAIGN WHICH RAISED THE COMMUNITY PORTION OF FUNDING TO BUILD AND EQUIP THE CORTELLUCCI VAUGHAN HOSPITAL AS WELL AS CONTINUING TO ATTRACT DONATIONS TO SUPPORT THE ADVANCEMENT OF PATIENT CARE AT BOTH THE CORTELLUCCI VAUGHAN AND MACKENZIE RICHMOND HILL HOSPITALS.
