What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Oct 31, 2024)

THE FLAMBOROUGH FOOD BANK IS A NON-FOR-PROFIT COMMUNITY BASED ORGANIZATION THAT SUPPLEMENTS INDIVIDUAL AND FAMILY HOUSEHOLD FOOD AND PERSONAL CARE NEED THROUGH COMMUNITY DONATIONS AND RESOURCES. IT IS LOCATED IN CARLISLE UNITED CHURCH. THE FLAMBOROUGH FOOD BANK IS OPEN TO ANYONE WHO IS IN NEED OF FOOD AND/OR PERSONAL CARE SUPPLEMENTS DISTRIBUTED AND RESPECTFUL MANNER. THE FLAMBOROUGH FOOD BANK ALSO OFFER OTHER ASSISTANCE SUCH AS EASTER THANKSGIVING CHRISTMAS FOOD HAMPER SCHOOL BACKPACKS IN SEPTEMBER SECRET SANTA IN DECEMBER AND A BABY PROGRAM.
