What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Apr 30, 2024)

THE PURPOSE OF THIS CHARITY IS TO PROVIDE HAMPERS OF FOOD CLOTHING AND TOYS TO NEEDY FAMILIES WITH YOUNG CHILDREN AT CHRISTMAS EACH YEAR. FUND RAISING ACTIVIES ARE CONDUCTED BY CHRISTMAS CHEER VOLUNTEERS AND THE THUNDER BAY FIREFIGHTER'S ASSOCIATION 'TOYS FOR TOTS' DURING NOVEMBER AND DECEMBER EACH YEAR. A CANNED GOOD BLITZ IS ALSO CONDUCTED IN DECEMBER THROUGHT THE SCHOOL SYSTEM AND GROCERY STORES.
