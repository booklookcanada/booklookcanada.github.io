What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

This organization manages a place of worship hosting daily services and weekly Friday sermons and offers special worship oppor‐ tunities during important religious periods like Ramadan. It also provides educational lectures on Islam for both the OakvilleMuslim community and the broader Oakville community.Additionally the organization operates a multi-use community center that provides various services rooted in Islamic tradition such as matrimonial services community outreach and Islamic resources. It runs a food bank and community kitchen to assistthose in need. The organization also offers family counseling services youth programs Quran lessons and weekend Islamic andArabic schools for children youth and adults.
