What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

RIVERBRINK ART MUSEUM INC. EDUCATES AND INCREASES THE PUBLIC'S UNDERSTANDING AND APPRECIATION OF THE ARTS BY DEVELOPING MAINTAINING AND INTRODUCING THROUGH THE NIAGARA REGION CANADA AND INTERNATIONALLY A COLLECTION OF WORKS OF ART BOTH HISTORIC AND CONTEMPORARY WITH SPECIAL BUT NOT EXCLUSIVE REFERENCE TO CANADA. THE ORGANIZATION OPERATES A PUBLIC ART MUSEUM YEAR ROUND OFFERING TOURS EDUCATIONAL COURSES IN ART HISTORY AND ART APPRECIATION AND ART WORKSHOPS TO THE PUBLIC AND OPERATES A SMALL GIFT SHOP ON THE PREMISES.
