What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

The mandate of Carefirst Seniors & Community Services Association is to provide a full range of community and social services to seniors and where appropriate others in needs of services to enable them living a quality and enriched life in the community. The services and programs include: (1) Integrated adult day program (2) Assisted Living Services (3) Chinese bereavement service (4) Community development program (5) Community support services/programs (meal-on-wheels service / transportation / friendly visiting / hospice visiting / security checks / congregate dining program / supportive counseling / information and referral) (6) Chronic disease management program (7) Elder abuse prevention and education (8) Exercise and Falls Prevention Program (9) Geriatric Assessment and Intervention Network (10) Home care / personal support / home help services (11) Home At Last / Home First programs (12) Integrated Dialysis Care Program (13) Integrated Virtual Care Program (14) Overnight stay program (15) Remote Care Monitoring program (16) Seniors Active Living Centres (17) Supportive housing services (18) Social and recreation programs (19) Transitional Care Centre (20) Vocational Training Centre (21) Youth leadership volunteer development and coordination programs (22) Wellness education and health promotion programs and (23) High Priority Communities Program.
