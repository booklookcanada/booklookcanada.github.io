What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

Our purpose is to assist new mothers and their new born babies. Often these women have no where else to go or any other support during their pregnancy. We are often the ones who go to the hospital when the mother is about to give birth. The women and babies reside at the home for a one year period and while there receive support training and care. They assist in the daily chores of the house. We provide food and lodging and supervision 24 hours a day seven days a week 365 days a year. We may also assist in paying for things like dental bills medical prescriptions and other bills and legal bills for which the women and babies may not have any coverage.
