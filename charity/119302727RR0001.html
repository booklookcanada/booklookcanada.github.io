What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

THE CHARITY OPERATES A 36 BED EMERGENCY SHELTER AND COMMUNITY SUPPORT OUTREACH PROGRAM FOR WOMEN AND THEIR CHILDREN THAT ARE FACING VIOLENCE POVERTY HOMELESSNESS AND OPPRESSION. AT THE SHELTER WE PROVIDE SHELTER SAFETY COUNSELLING REFERRAL FOR SOCIAL HOUSING MEDICAL AND LEGAL ASSISTANCE. AT THE COMMUNITY SUPPORT ADN OUTREACH PROGRAMS WE PROVIDE COUNSELLING HOUSING REFERRALS AND DROP IN COMMUNITY FOOD PROGRAMS.
