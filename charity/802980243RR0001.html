What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

TO ADVANCE AND TEACH THE RELIGIOUS TENETS DOCTRINES AND OBSERVANCES ASSOCIATED WITH THE JEWISH FAITH. THE ORGANIZATION INTENDS TO OPERATE AS AN OUTREACH ORGANIZATION FOR MEMBERS OF THE JEWISH COMMUNITY IN TORONTO.. IT WILL CONDUCT CLASSES IN JEWISH TOPICS SUCH AS (BUT NOT LIMITED) BIBLE AND JEWISH THOUGHT ACCORDING TO THE PHILOSOPHY OF CHABAD LUBAVITCH. IT WILL ALSO OPERATE A LIBRARY OF JEWISH BOOKS AND CONDUCT ACTIVITIES RELATED TO THE JEWISH HOLIDAYS (SUCH AS CLASSES AND THE RITUALS CONNECTED TO THAT PARTICULAR HOLIDAY).
