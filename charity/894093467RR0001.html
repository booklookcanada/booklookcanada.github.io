What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

1)VICTIM CRISIS RESPONSE: IMMEDIATE ON-SITE CRISIS/TRAUMA COUNSELLING AND PRACTICAL ASSISTANCE FOR VICTIMS OF CRIME AND/OR TRAGIC CIRCUMSTANCES. PROGRAM OFFERED 24 HOURS A DAY-365 DAYS A YEAR. 2) HIGH RISK SUPPORT SERVICES: SAFETY SERVICES SUCH AS SAFETY AUDITS COMPREHENSIVE SAFETY PLANS AND A 9-1-1 LINKED CELLPHONE FOR PEOPLE AT HIGH RISK OF BODILY HARM OR DEATH FROM AN EX-PARTNER. ONGOING CASE MANAGEMENT SERVICES. 3) ENHANCED SERVICES: INTENSIVE CASE MANAGEMENT SERVICES FOR VULNERABLE VICTIMS OF MAJOR CRIMES 4) VICTIM QUICK RESPONSE PROGRAM: ASSISTS VICTIMS OF MAJOR CRIMES WITH ACCESS TO RESOURCES AND SUPPORTS SUCH AS PAID FUNERAL EXPENSES COUNSELLING CRIME SCENE CLEAN-UP AND SO FORTH. 5)T.E.A.R. (TEENS ENDING ABUSIVE RELATIONSHIPS): EDUCATIONAL WORKSHOPS FOR TEENAGERS ON RECOGNIZING AND PREVENTING ABUSE. 6) TRAUMA DOG PROGRAM: TRAUMA DOG ASSISTS VICTIMS OF VIOLENT CRIME IN COURT AND WHILE GIVING STATEMENTS TO POLICE. 7) HUMAN TRAFFICKING PREVENTION & INTERVENTION: PROVIDES CASE MANAGEMENT ADVOCACY AND CRISIS RESPONSE FOR HUMAN TRAFFICKING SURVIVORS WORKSHOPS TO RAISE AWARENESS ABOUT THE RISKS OF HUMAN TRAFFICKING AND HOW TO PREVENT IT.
