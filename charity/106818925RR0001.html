What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

As a charitable not-for-profit cultural and educational organization we provide Burlington and surrounding areas an equitable and inclusive place to create collaborate and innovate. We deliver thought-provoking exhibitions educational encounters and public programs that spark opportunities for people to learn see think and make. Our onsite and digital learning opportunities push the boundaries of traditional arts education and support artists youth and the broader community in their personal professional and artistic development. As a place of creative activity our studios are open to artists and makers at all levels of skill and achievement. Boasting the world's most extensive collection of contemporary Canadian ceramics we actively engage in the study preservation and circulation of clay practice and continually explore the intersection of craft and contemporary art.
During 2024 we continued with an in-person approach and provided more courses for adults and children throughout the year. We continued to sharpen our skills and rearticulated our goals to invest in programming with social impact that privileged the health and wellbeing of our community built intercultural competence foregrounded continuing education and strengthened community relationships. We continued to offer unique and inclusive arts programming for all ages and committed to fostering creative growth by merging technical and critical understandings of the arts and a deeper understanding of how art can reflect the most critical issues of our time.
