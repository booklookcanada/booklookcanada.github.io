What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

OUR MISSION STATEMENT IS THAT OF 'TURNING HEARTS AROUND THE WORLD TOWARDS THE LAND PEOPLE AND GOLD OF ISRAEL'. THE MINISTRY TRAVELS TO CONDUCT TEACHING SEMINARS IN CHURCHES IN CANADA AND ENCOURAGES CHRISTIANS TO PRAY AND INTERCEDE FOR ISRAEL. WE ALSO PROVIDE AND SUPPORT PRACTICAL AND HUMANITARIAN AID PROJECTS FACILITATED BY CHRISTIAN PARTNER ORGANIZATIONS IN ISRAEL. THIS ACTIVITY INCREASED SIGNIFICANTLY AFTER CONFLICT BROKE OUT IN OCTOBER 2023 AND AS A RESULT MORE PROJECTS WERE FUNDED THAT WERE OF AN EMERGENCY CRISIS RESPONSE IN NATURE . AS A MINISTRY WE PRODUCE A TV PROGRAM( CALLED ' FIRST CENTURY FOUNDATIONS ') TAPED ON LOCATION IN ISRAEL AND AIRED ACROSS CANADA ON VISION TV AND DAYSTAR CANADA WHICH EXPLAINS OUR JUDEO-CHRISTIAN ROOTS IN THE HOLY LAND AS WELL AS HIGHLIGHTS SOME OF OUR PROJECTS IN ISTRAEL. WE ALSO AIR OUR PROGRAM ACROSS THE USA.
