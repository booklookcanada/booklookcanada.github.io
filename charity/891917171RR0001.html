What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Apr 30, 2024)

Programs for worship and prayer. Providing religious education and instruction for all ages. Pastoral and compassionate care. Holding weddings baptism funerals and Holiday programs. We are a caring community of believers committed to worship God and sharing the gospel loving and serving families and neighbours here at home and globally. We also share with our Markdale church in grain growing operations for the Canadian Foodgrains Bank discover Jesus seekers group young adult mentoring wonder club for kids fall carnival for community. Life group bible study and community outreach through donations to the Cambridge Food Bank and providing community meals for the needy. Finding Freedom ministry providing an environment of healing of relationships and restoration in the lives of those who are motivated to work on their hurts habits and hang-ups. After school Drop in program for all Youth Tutoring services to children in grades 1-11 by Board certified teacher ESL for New Immigrants Share in providing meals two Tuesdays a month for people in need in conjunction with Kinbridge Community Centre. Research found people wanted more than food but a sense of community building and interaction as well as a sense of belonging. In addition to meals music will be provided with during the supper time and be part of supporting families in our area. Develop the children's program and provide activities on nights when supper is not available and families may not take advantage of meals in other locations in Cambridge.
On line and in person bible study Thursday evenings
