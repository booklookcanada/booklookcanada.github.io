What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Apr 30, 2024)

ORGANIZATION – THE CANADIAN NETWORK FOR ARTS & LEARNING FOUNDATION WAS ORIGINALLY INCORPORATED JULY 24 1996 UNDER THE NAME 'ONTARIO ARTS EDUCATION INSTITUTE'. THE NAME WAS CHANGED TO ITS CURRENT FORM ON JUNE 8 2012. MISSION TO UNITE LOCAL REGIONAL AND NATIONAL ARTS AND LEARNING COMMUNITIES IN A NETWORK FOR EXCHANGE RESEARCH AND COLLABORATION CULTIVATING A MORE CREATIVE INNOVATIVE AND PROSPEROUS CANADA. ACTIVITIES: - CONVENE PRACTITIONERS AND ENABLERS FOR KNOWLEDGE EXCHANGE - COMMISSION AND DISSEMINATE RESEARCH ON THE BENEFITS OPPORTUNITIES AND OUTCOMES OF ARTS EDUCATION - CELEBRATE AND PROFILE LEADERS AND SHARE EXEMPLARY PRACTICES AND INSIGHTS - REPRESENT THE INTERESTS OF THE NETWORK TO KEY STAKEHOLDERS ACROSS CANADA AND AROUND THE WORLD
