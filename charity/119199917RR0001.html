What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Oct 31, 2024)

WE OFFER MONTHLY GARDEN TALKS OPEN TO THE PUBLIC BY ZOOM AND IN PERSON. WE PROMOTE LEARNING AND SHARING HORTICULTURAL AND VEGETABLE INFORMATION TO NEW AND EXPERIENCED GARDENERS. WE CHAMPION ENVIRONMENT AND REMOVAL OF INVASIVE SPECIES. WE MAINTAIN PUBLIC GARDENS AND URNS. WE OFFER FREE WORKSHOPS TO THE PUBLIC LIBRARIES SCHOOLS AND MEMBERS. WE COLLECT PACKAGE AND GIVE AWAY FLOWER AND VEGETABLE SEEDS AND STOCK SEED LIBRARIES IN PUBLIC LIBRARIES IN PERTH COUNTY. WE MAINTAIN A WEBSITE WHCH IS AN ACCESSIBLE AND AUDIO GARDEN GUIDE FOR CITY GARDENS NOT JUST THE ONES WE MAINTAIN. THIS IS USED BY LOCALS AND TOURISTS. WE HOLD PLANT SLAES AND GARDEN TOURS. WE RESCUE PLANT IN AREAS SLATED FOR DESTRUCTION.
NEW WEBSITE. MENTORING HOMEOWNERS WHO HAVE INHERITED GARDENS. WEEKLY HOUR LONG GARDEN STROLLS IN PRIVATE GARDENS HOSTED BY HOME OWNERS. WE COLLABORATE WITH CITY ENERGY AND ENVIRONMENT UPPER THAMES CONSERVATION AND TR COUNTY MASTER GARDENERS TO REMOVE INVASIVE SPECIES FROM PUBLIC PARKLAND. WE SUPPLY NATIVE PLANTS TO RESIDENTS OF PERTH COUNTY AND TO RE-FOREST PARKLAND.
