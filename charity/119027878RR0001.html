What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

WE HAVE OFFERED HOSPITALITY INCLUDING ROOM AND BOARD TO PEOPLE REQUIRING ASSISTANCE. WE HAVE ALSO PROVIDED COUNSELLING AND SUPPORT FOR THEM IN THEIR SEARCH FOR GOD. WE HAVE TRAINED SEVERAL YOUNG PEOPLE IN THE SPIRIT AND AIMS OF OUR APOSTOLATE. IN ADDITION WE HAVE OPERATED A SECOND HAND CLOTHING STORE AND A LOAN CUPBOARD FOR SICK ROOM SUPPLIES IN CO-OPERATION WITH NURSING ASSISTANCE WHERE NEEDED.
