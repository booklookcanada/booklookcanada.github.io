Who runs it — the people it pays

It reported 42 full-time positions and 65 part-time employees (year ended Mar 31, 2024). Total pay for all its positions: $2,938,081, as filed. The highest pay band it reported was “$80,000–$119,999”. 1 employee was reported in that band. Pay is reported in bands — ranges, not exact salaries. The top band is the highest range with anyone in it: it is not one person's salary, and it is not what everyone there is paid.

What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

WYNDHAM HOUSE PROVIDES A CONTINUUM OF CARE FOR HOMELESS AND AT-RISK YOUTH AGED 16 TO 24 THAT INCLUDES: - A RESIDENTIAL PROGRAM FOCUSING ON EDUCATION COMMUNITY INVOLVEMENT AND LIFE SKILLS; - AN EMERGENCY SHELTER FOR ANY YOUTH EXPERIENCING A HOUSING CRISIS; - A RESOURCE CENTRE PROVIDING OUTREACH SUPPORT AND SERVICES IN THE AREAS OF HEALTH EDUCATION EMPLOYMENT AND COMMUNITY ENGAGEMENT AS WELL AS BASIC NEEDS.
