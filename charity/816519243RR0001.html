What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

The Charity offers 3 annual Orphans' camps that provide grief support and various activities for 40-50 children between the ages 12-17 that have lost their parent while serving in the IDF. The programs include daily trips over-night camps and various social activities.
