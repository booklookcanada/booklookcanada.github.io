What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

FIND HOMES FOR STRAY & ABANDONED CATS & KITTENS; HELP LOW INCOME FAMILIES GET THEIR CATS & KITTENS NEUTERED & VACCINATED; HELP THE COMMUNITY WITH TRAP NEUTER RETURN TO FIELD (TNR) FOR FERAL CATS; HELP FAMILIES IN NEED WITH FOOD & LITTER FOR THEIR CATS. ALL CATS/KITTENS THAT COME THROUGH THE RESCUE ARE SEEN BY A VET NEUTERED VACCINATED AND TREATED FOR INJURIES ILLNESS AND PARASITES. IN 2023-WE ADOPTED OUT 198 CATS/KITTENS HELPED LOW INCOME FAMILIES GET 105 CATS NEUTERED/VACCINATED/SEEN BY A VET; NEUTERED 321 CATS IN TOTAL AND VETTED ANOTHER 80 CATS. CURRENTLY WE HAVE 45 FOSTER HOMES VOLUNTEERING THEIR HOMES AND THEIR TIME TO HELP SAVE THE LIVES OF DOMESTIC CATS & KITTENS. WE ARE VOLUNTEER RUN & OPERATED; NO PAID STAFF.
