What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

THE FOUNDATION STRIVES TO IMPROVE THE HEALTH AND QUALITY OF LIFE OF CANADIANS BY SUPPORTING ADVANCES IN HEALTH CARE RESEARCH AND TECHNOLOGIES CONTINUING EDUCATION AND TECHNICAL TRAINING. THE FOUNDATION SUPPORTS THE ONGOING DEVELOPMENT OF INNOVATION CHARITABLE PROGRAMS SUCH AS HEALTH CLINICS CAREGIVER PROGRAMS PALLIATIVE CARE PROGRAMS MEETING THE PHYSICAL EMOTIONAL AND SPITIRUAL NEEDS OF PATIENTS AND THEIR FAMILIES GUIDEBOOKS DISTRIBUTED FREE OF CHARGE TO INDIVIDUALS FAMILIES AND ORGANIZATIONS ACROSS CANADA. THE FOUNDATION SEEKS CHARITABLE SUPPORT DESIGNED TO IMPROVE ABORIGINAL HEALTH CARE BY GIVING FIRST NATION COMMUNITIES ACCESS TO INNOVATIVE SECURE WEB BASED PROGRAMS THAT ASSIST THEM IN DEVEOPING EXCELLENT CARE USING THE LATEST TECHNIQUES.
