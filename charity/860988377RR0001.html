JABEZ BLANKET MINISTRY PROVIDES 'BACKPACKS OF HOPE' (BLANKETS TEDDY BEARS AND SCHOOL SUPPLIES) FOR ORPHANS LIVING IN ORPHANAGES IN POVERTY-STRICKEN COUNTRIES AROUND THE WORLD VIA WORKING WITH SEVERAL NORTH AMERICAN MISSION ORGANIZATIONS - (SEE THE LIST ON FORM T1236).
