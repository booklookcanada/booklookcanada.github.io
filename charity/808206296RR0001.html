What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

THE ORGANIZATION OPERATES AS AN ASSEMBLY OF CHRISTIAN REFORMED CHURCHES IN THE TORONTO AND SURROUNDING AREAS. THE ORGANIZATION ESTABLISHED AND MAINTAINS MINISTRIES WHICH CANNOT BE MAINTAINED BY A SINGLE CONGREGATION BY COLLECTING MINISTRY SHARES AND OFFERINGS FROM ITS MEMBERS FOR THE FOLLOWING OPERATIONAL PURPOSES; CAMPUS CHAPLAINCY HOME MISSIONS CANDIDACY YOUTH MINISTRY MISSION CATALYST SAGE CHURCH RACE RELATIONS AND OPERATING EXPENSES. THE ORGANIZATION ALSO PROVIDED GIFTS TO THE FOLLOWING QUALIFIED DONEES; AFRICAN INLAND MISSION SPIRITUAL GROWTH MINISTRIES CROSSTOWN CHURCH AND THE CHRISTIAN REFORMED CHURCH IN NORTH AMERICA.
