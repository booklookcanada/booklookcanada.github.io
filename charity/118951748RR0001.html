What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Apr 30, 2024)

Promotion of the appreciation and conservation of flora fauna and wildlands by: 1) Providing member services; including monthly meetings with speakers (open to the public) published newsletter (9x a year) nature appreciation walks and field trips public internet bird sighting postings and internet website with live webcams; 2) providing a bird study group with monthly meetings; 3) Conservation committee monitors and acts on local issues; 4) Hamilton Community Peregrine Project - a volunteer program to ensure survival of peregrine falcon fledglings; 5) Operation of 9 nature sanctuaries as well as 2 leased sanctuaries; as well as the development of a leased sanctuary; 6) Fundraising campaigns for future nature sanctuary acquisitions; 7) The club support conservation organizations in nature sanctuary acquisition; 8) Pollinator Protection Project - a project with Environment Hamilton which plants pollinator friendly gardens. This includes educating the public about pollinators such as bees butterflies and other insects; 9) Trees Please Air Quality/Tree Planting Project - a partnership with Environment Hamilton which monitors air quality in some Hamilton neighborhoods and plant trees to improve air quality; 10) Grimsby Wetlands Projects - a project to improve the Niagara Peninsula Conservation Authority's Grimsby Wetlands property by improving the trails signage bird breeding facilities and native vegetation content of the wetlands; 11) Biodiversity in the Schoolyard - a project to engage students in improving Hamilton's biodiversity through schoolyard plantings and an education program for students; 12) Reimagining Nature Education during Covid-19 a project developing virtual nature programming for teachers and students assisted by outdoor educators with experience in the field.
