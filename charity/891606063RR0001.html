What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

TO DONATE FUNDS TO OTHER CHARITIES IN THE HEALTH AND WELFARE AND PERTAINING TO EDUCATION. THE FOUNDATION ALSO PROVIDES PROPERTY TO STUDENT ORGANIZATIONS AT VARIOUS ONTARIO UNVERSITIES FOR USE BY STUDENTS AS A MEETING PLACE AND RESOURCE CENTER FOR NO CONSIDERATION. THE FOUNDATION IS CONTINUALLY BEING ASKED TO DONATE TO VARIOUS NEEDY CHARITIES. IT RESPONDS TO THOSE CHARITIES WHOSE AIMS CLOSELY MATCHES ITS OWN.
