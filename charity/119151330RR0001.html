The highest pay band it reported was “$200,000–$249,999”. 1 employee was reported in that band. Pay is reported in bands — ranges, not exact salaries. What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Aug 31, 2024)

THE PRIMARY PURPOSE OF THIS CHARITY IS THE ADVANCEMENT OF RELIGION SOCIAL OUTREACH AND PASTORAL/SPIRITUAL PROGRAMS THAT ADDRESS POVERTY ISOLATION AND LACK OF COMMUNITY AND SPIRITUAL VOID IN OUR CITIES. MAJOR AREAS OF FOCUS INCLUDE SOCIAL OUTREACH; JUSTICE PEACE AND CARE OF EARTH; EDUCATION AND FORMATION HEALTH PARISH AND SPIRITUAL CARE MINISTRY.
