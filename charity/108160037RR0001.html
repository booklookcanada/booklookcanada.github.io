What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

OWCNEO SUPPORTS QUALIFIED DONEES IN THE DISTRICTS OF COCHRANE MANITOULIN NIPISSING PARRY SOUND SUDBURY AND TIMISKAMING THAT PROVIDE THE DELIVERY OF SOCIAL SERVICES TO THE COMMUNITY'S MOST VULNERABLE POPULATIONS IN THE FOCUS AREAS OF POVERTY CHILDREN AND YOUTH AND STRONG COMMUNITIES. BY WORKING IN PARTNERSHIP WITH OUR COMMUNITIES WE IDENTIFY NEEDS AND INVEST IN OR PROVIDE TARGETED PROGRAMS THAT HELP CHANGE LIVES. CONTINUED FINANCIAL SUPPORT OF MEMBER CHARITABLE ORGANIZATIONS THROUGHOUT THE SUDBURY AREA THROUGH PUBLIC CONTRIBUTIONS FROM ANNUAL FUNDRAISING AND OTHER VARIOUS PROGRAMS.
1. KEEPING SENIORS WARM (OUTFITTING LOW-INCOME SENIORS WITH THE OPPORTUNITY TO SHOP FOR NEW WARM WINTER CLOTHING) 2. YOUNG LEADERS ON BOARD - PROVIDING TRAINING TO YOUNG ADULTS UNDER THE AGE OF 39 ON THE DUTIES AND ACTIVITIES OF A BOARD INCLUDES INTERNSHIP WITH LOCAL BOARD.
