What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

WE PROVIDE SUPPORT AND ASSISTANCE TO WOMEN AND THEIR CHILDREN IF INTIMATE PARTNER ABUSE BY PROVIDING SHELTER FOOD AND COUNSELLING. WE ALSO REFER CLIENTS TO OTHER COMMUNITY AGENCIES. WE OFFER COUNSELLING TO CHILDREN AND YOUTH AS WITNESS OF INTIMATE PARTNER ABUSE. WE OFFER ASSISTANCE TO WOMEN AND THEIR CHILDREN TO DEVELOP SAFE PLACES WHEN RE-ESTABLISHING IN THE COMMUNITY.
