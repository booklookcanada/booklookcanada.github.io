Who runs it — the people it pays

It reported 29 full-time positions and 4 part-time employees (year ended Dec 31, 2024). Total pay for all its positions: $1,488,204, as filed. The highest pay band it reported was “$120,000–$159,999”. 2 employees were reported in that band. Pay is reported in bands — ranges, not exact salaries. The top band is the highest range with anyone in it: it is not one person's salary, and it is not what everyone there is paid.

What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

Academic tutoring to improve academic success for at-risk youth. Supervised instruction in sports to build and develop life and social skills. Academic workshops for high school students. Relieve poverty by operating a subsidized day camp to middle school students from low and modest income families in Hamilton. Advance education and benefit the community by building a library and joint conference centre in Paynesville Liberia. Southwestern Ontario Black Entrepreneurship Network brings together a consortium of partners to offer a dynamic resource for Black business owners entrepreneurs and nonprofit leaders in the greater Hamilton and Windsor areas.
