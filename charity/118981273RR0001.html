WE HOLD WEEKLY WORSHIP SERVICES. WE PROVIDE PROGRAMS FOR SENIORS ADULTS MOTHERS OF PRESCHOOLERS (MOPS) YOUTH AND CHILDREN AS WELL AS FOR TODDLERS AND INFANTS. WE HOLD SMALL GROUP BIBLE STUDIES.
