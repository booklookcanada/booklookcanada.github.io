Who runs it — the people it pays

It reported 20 full-time positions and 298 part-time employees (year ended Mar 31, 2024). Total pay for all its positions: $5,780,228, as filed. The highest pay band it reported was “$160,000–$199,999”. 1 employee was reported in that band. Pay is reported in bands — ranges, not exact salaries. The top band is the highest range with anyone in it: it is not one person's salary, and it is not what everyone there is paid.

What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

We provided after-school programs at 7 locations and provided healthy snacks. Operated after school and evening programs at 7 outreach locations. Our programs all offer social and recreational activities for children and youth. We offered 1 winter camp. We offered March Break programming and 6 summer camps. We operated 3 licensed after school programs for ages 4 and 5 year old's. We offered several educational and physical activity programs at all 7 sites. We also offered licensed daycare.
Continued geographic expansion west of Kingston. Expanded STEM programming to education offering. Added licensed daycare.
