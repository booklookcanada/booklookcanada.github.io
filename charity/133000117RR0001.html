What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

Karis Global Partnerships serves International communities around the world through the implementation of holistic practical and long-term development programs. Our focus is to meet the needs to at risk people. We are dedicated to bringing lasting change through education and training extensive children's services nutrition support income generating programs and leadership development. We work with global partners in Ethiopia Guatemala Haiti Kenya Nicaragua Uganda. Our Vision: People who experience disabilities belong to communities in which their God-given gifts are valued and celebrated. Our Mission: As an expression of Christian faith we work together with people who experience disabilities to accomplish their goals and nurture communities where everybody belongs. Our Values: Valuing people - we believe that all people are created in the image of God and are loved and valued equally. Fostering belonging - we strengthen communities by fostering belonging with people with disabilities. Serving others - we mobilize our faith to effectively and compassionately support those around us. Celebrating gifts - we celebrate the many skills abilities and contributions that everybody including people with disabilities offers the world around them.
No new programs were undertaken during the year.
