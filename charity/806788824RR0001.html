What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended May 31, 2024)

GRIMSBY LIFE CENTRE MINISTRIES PROVIDES INDIVIDUALS WITH ONGOING ASSISTANCE THROUGH CHRISTIAN MINISTRY SUPPORT AND GUIDANCE. THE CENTRE IN PARTNERSHIP WITH OTHER CHARITABLE ORGANIZATIONS ASSISTS IN PROVIDING FOOD AND BASIC HOUSEHOLD SUPPLIES AND TRANSITIONAL HOUSING (ONE APARTMENT) FOR LOW INCOME PERSONS. THE ASSIST PERSONS IN NEED BY PROVIDING COUNSELING LIFE SKILLS TRAINING AND OTHER PROGRAMS EITHER INDIVIDUALLY OR IN SMALL GROUPS.
