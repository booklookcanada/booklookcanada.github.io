What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

JVS Toronto plays a vital role in Toronto and York Region ensuring that jobseekers reach their potential at work and employers build the workforce they need to be successful. Programs and services include a mix of general and specialized services to job seekers from all backgrounds and situations and employers from all sectors. JVS Toronto has extensive experience providing career exploration job search support employment recruitment mentoring job coaching and retention as well as vocational assessments delivered across nine locations. A team comprised of multi-disciplinary professionals is experienced in providing services to youth and adults. Specialized services support individuals with disabilities newcomers to Canada and at-risk youth.
