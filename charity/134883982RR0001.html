What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Aug 31, 2024)

Ongoing Programs Halton Hills Christian School continues its charitable service to the community by providing a Christian education through its curriculum and activities. Our vision is to be a leader in kindergarten to grade 8 education in the greater Halton Hills area. We challenge teach nurture and equip our students to develop all of their gifts and talents through a program that is academic physical social and spiritual. Our school program offering includes the Arrowsmith program a brain based cognitive program to help students overcome learning challenges Fast Forward which stresses auditory development and strong and varied student leadership initiatives as well as a before and after-school care program. Our grade 8 students participate in a six week business intern position during the year. Students engage in learning both inside and outside of the classroom utilizing our property that includes a newly developed wetland. We also do an extensive Christmas musical production open to the surrounding community. Student learning extends beyond the classroom walls to engage the larger community through project-based and service education.
