What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

THE MISSION OF ONTARIO SPCA IS TO FACILITATE AND PROVIDE FOR PROVINCE-WIDE LEADERSHIP ON MATTER RELATING TO THE PREVENTION OF CRUELTY TO ANIMAL AND THE PROMOTION OF ANIMAL WELFARE. ONTARIO CPCA STRIVES TO BE A STRONG UNIFIED AND COLLABORATIVE ORGANIZATION DEDICATE TO THE CULTIVATION OF A COMPASSIONATE SOCIETY FOR ALL ANIMALS . THE SOCIETY'S PROGRAMS AND SERVICES INCLUDE: • SHELTERING AND ADOPTIONS • HUMANE EDUCATION • REDUCING PET OVERPOPULATION • EMERGENCY RESCUE AND TREATMENT • REUNITING LOST PETS WITH THEIR OWNERS • SPAY/NEUTER SERVICES • RESCUE & RELIEF SERVICES
