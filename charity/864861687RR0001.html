What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

REXALL CARE NETWORK RAISES FUNDS THROUGH AN ANNUAL GOLF TOURNAMENT AND INITIATIVES AT THE RETAIL STORES OF REXALL PHARMACY GROUP LTD. INCLUDING COIN BOX DONATIONS RAFFLE TICKETS CHARITY BEAR AND REQUESTS FOR A DONATION AT CHECKOUT (INCLUDING BUBBLE CAMPAIGNS AND ROUNDING A CUSTOMER'S PURCHASE TO THE NEAREST DOLLAR). THE REXALL CARE NETWORK DONATES THE FUNDS RAISED TO CHARITABLE ORGANIZATIONS THAT PROVIDE CAREGIVERS WITH DIRECT SUPPORT AND ASSISTANCE TO ENABLE THEM TO CONTINUE TAKING CARE OF THE ONES THEY LOVE; AND BY FUNDING PROGRAMS IN SUPPORT OF CHILDREN AND OLDER ADULTS THEY CARE FOR PROVIDING THE CAREGIVER WITH MUCH NEEDED RESPITE.
