What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Oct 31, 2024)

PURPOSE IS TO OPERATE EXCLUSIVELY AS A CHARITABLE ORGANIZATION TO RECEIVE AND MAINTAIN FUNDS AND APPLY FROM TIME TO TIME THE INCOME EARNED SOLELY FOR CHARITABLE PURPOSES AS DEEMED APPROPRIATE BY THE ARTICLES OF INCOROPRATION INCLUDING; RELIEVE POVERTY & HUMANS SUFFERING & DISTRESS BY PROVIDING BASIC AMENITIES AND OTHER MEANS TO RELIEVE POVERTY TO POOR INDIVIDUALS WHOSE LIVES LIVELIHOOD SECURITY OR WELL-BEING ARE AT RISK; TO PROVIDE THE NECESSITIES OF LIFE TO VICTIMS OF DISASTERS; TO ASSIST FIRE FIGHTERS WHEREVER SITUATED BUT PARTICULARLY IN SOCIALLY AND ECONOMICALLY DISADVANTAGED COMMUNITIES WITH EDUCATION TRAINING EQUIPMENT AND SUPPLIES. TO ASSIST WITH THE PROVISION OF HEALTH CARE AND EMERGENCY SERVICES AND SUPPORT INCLUDING DONATIONS OF EQUIPMENT AND SUPPLIES IN SUPPORT OF THESE SERVICES IN SOCIALLY AND ECONOMICALLY DISADVANTAGED COMMUNITIES AROUND THE WORLD; TO APPLY AMOUNTS FROM THE FUND (OF FUNDS) WHETHER SOURCED FROM PRINCIPAL PR INCOME FROM TIME TO TIME TO QUALIFIED DONEES ASS DEFINED IN SUBSECTION 149.1(1) OF THE INCOME TAX ACT (CANADA)
