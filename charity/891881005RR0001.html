What it says it does

THE CHARITY CARRIES ON FOUR MAJOR PROGRAMS INCLUDING 1) RAINY DAY FUND PROVIDING FINANCIAL SERVICES FOR NON-MEDICAL EXPENSES ASSOCIATED WITH LIFE THREATENING ILLNESSES I.E. PAST-DUE RENT UTILITIES. 2) RAINY DAY GIFTS BAGS PROVIDED TO MEN WOMEN AND CHILDREN FACING A LIFE THREATENING ILLNESS. 3) CAMP SCHOLARSHIPS FOR CHILDREN AND SIBLINGS FOLLOWING TREATMENT OF A LIFE THREATENING ILLNESS 4) BACKPACKS FOR CHILDREN RECOVERING FROM A LIFE THREATENING ILLNESS.
