What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

Weekly worship services and Sunday School at the church and once a month a worship service at a retirement centre. Ongoing ministries to all including Christian education (through Sunday School; baptism early communion preparation for marriage confirmation bible studies and lay reader training) and the provision of pastoral care including visitation to sick and shut-ins ministry of those approaching death and comforts to the bereaved. Support of a variety of outreach programs including a neighbourhood food bank a regional church camp and church sponsored and international aid programs.
