What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

We offer the first year of the 2 year Equine Care & Management Diploma with the University of Guelph Ridgetown Campus. The programs educate students in proper horse handling care etc. Fanshawe College offers full time programs in PSW Healthcare Office Assistant ECE Diploma and part-time programs at our location for those who cannot travel to their main campus. They also offer short courses for skills upgrades. We provide workshops clinics and demonstrations for the public on Equine topics. We have a partnership with Contact North and host their office in our building which supports students with online learning and computer programs. Fanshawe College along with the Avon Maitland and Huron-Perth Catholic District School boards hold their School College Work initiative program her to offer dual credits to high school students in both the art and culinary fields. We work in conjunction with the local High School St. Anne's on their culinary programs in our Commercial kitchen. We offer rooms to non-profit and charitable groups to train volunteers hold meetings and small events. We offer nights for local horse riders to come in and ride their horses indoors at a low cost. Offer low cost schooling shows for the local community members to keep their horses showing through the winter and provide an entry level show for beginners and children to start up at.
