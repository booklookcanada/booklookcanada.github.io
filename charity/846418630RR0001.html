What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

TO EMPOWER INDIVIDUALS WITH DISABILITIES TO ACTIVELY PARTICIPATE IN THE SPORT OF SKIING BY PROVIDING ACCESS TO SPECIALIZED EQUIPMENT ACCESSIBLE SAFE AND SUPPORTIVE ISNTRUCTIONS TAILORED TO EACH PARTICIPANT'S ABILITIES. THE PROGRAM AIMS TO FOSTER SELF-ESTEEM CONFIDENCE PHYSICAL DEVELOPMENT AND SOCIALIZATION WHILE PROMOTING INDEPENDENCE COMMUNITY INTEGRATION AND LIFELONG WELLNESS THROUGH ADAPTIVE SKIING EXPERIENCES.
EXPAND THE RANGE OF ADAPTIVE EQUIPMENT SUCH AS MONO-SKIS BI-SKIS OUTRIGGERS TO ACCOMODATE DIVERSE PHYSICAL NEEDS INCLUDING THOSE WITH LIMITED MOBILITY OR BALANCE ISSUES. REGULARLY UPDATE AND MAINTAIN EQUIPMENT TO ENSURE SAFETY AND COMFORT FOR ALL PARTICIPANTS. ORGANIZE ADAPTIVE SKIING DEMONSTRATIONS COMPETITION DAYS COMMUNITY AWARENESS EVENTS AND FUNDRAISING ACTIVITIES TO RAISE VISIBILITY AND CELEBRATE ACHIEVEMENTS. PROVIDE SPECIALIZED TRAINING FOR VOLUNTEERS ON ADAPTIVE SKIING TECHNIQUES AND DISABILITY AWARENESS TO ENSURE HIGH QUALITY INDIVIDUALIZED INSTRUCTION.

What this page can't tell you

  • Whether its work works. A return shows money, not results.
  • Whether anyone audited these figures. The CRA publishes returns as filed, without checking them. Some charities also post audited statements — BookLook compared the largest 200 charities' published statements with their returns (see “How to read this site”).
  • Anything after Dec 31, 2024, its newest return in the published data.
  • Who its directors are. The published data does not include them.
