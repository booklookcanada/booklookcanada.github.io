What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

RIVERWATCH – WE TRAIN AND ENGAGE VOLUNTEERS IN COMMUNITIES ALONG THE OTTAWA RIVER AND ITS TRIBUTARIES WHO ARE DEDICATED TO PROTECTING THEIR LOCAL RIVER. POLLUTION HOTLINE – WE PROVIDE TOOLS FOR THE PUBLIC TO REPORT DISCHARGE FISH KILLS ILLEGAL SHORELINE ALTERATIONS AND FOLLOW-UP WITH REGULATORS. EDUCATION & OUTREACH: WE PROVIDE OTTAWA RIVER EXPERTISE AND EDUCATIONAL ACTIVITIES AT EVENTS AND GIVE PRESENTATIONS THROUGHOUT THE WATERSHED; SWIM GUIDE – THE SWIM GUIDE IS A WEBSITE AND A FREE APP THAT MAKES IT EASY TO FIND AND LEARN ABOUT LOCAL BEACHES. RESTORATION OF NATIVE SPECIES – WE WORK WITH PARTNERS THROUGHOUT THE WATERSHED ON PROJECTS TO HELP RECOVER THE ENDANGERED AMERICAN EEL THE LAKE STURGEON AND THE HICKORYNUT MUSSEL. OTTAWA RIVER WATERSHED NETWORK – OTTAWA RIVERKEEPER IS CONVENING MEMBERS OF OUT WATERSHED COMMUNITY (GOVERNMENTS INDUSTRY CIVIL SOCIETY)
WATERSHED HEALTH ASSESSMENT AND MONITORING PROGRAM: THIS PROGRAM SEEKS TO UNDERSTAND AND EVALUATE THE HEALTH OF THE OTTAWA RIVER WATERSHED. YOUTH WATER LEADERS – PROGRAM PROVIDES PARTICIPANTS WITH THE SKILLS AND TOOLS NECESSARY TO IDENTIFY AND PURPOSE SOLUTIONS TO ENVIRONMENTAL ISSUES WITHIN THE OTTAWA RIVER WATERSHED RESEARCH AND PUBLIC ENGAGEMENT – PROMOTE THE SAFE STORAGE OF NON-FUEL NUCLEAR WASTE WITHIN THE OTTAWA RIVER WATERSHED
