What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

We provide rehabilitation services which support participation in all areas of life for children youth and young adults with physical communication or developmental needs. We do this through the following programs: Adaptive Technology Services Augmentative Communications Acquired Brain Injury Autism Blind Low Vision Cleft Lip and Palate Early Childhood Services Equipment Services Infant Hearing Medical Clinics Opportunities to Participate Preschool Speech and Language Research School Age Adolescent Services Seating and Volunteer Program.
