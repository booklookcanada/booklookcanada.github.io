THE ORGANIZATION CONDUCTS CAMP BASED PROGRAMMING INCLUDING SUMMER IN-PERSON AND VIRTUAL CAMPS OFF-SEASON WEEKEND RETREATS AND REUNIONS AND YEAR ROUND COMMUNITY BASED PROGRAMS FOR THE ENTIRE FAMILY. THE ORGANIZATION OPERATES CAMPS IN ONTARIO MANITOBA AND ALBERTA WITH YEAR ROUND AND VIRTUAL PROGRAMS THAT ARE RUN BY VOLUNTEERS COMMUNITY FUNDED AND AVAILABLE AT NO COST TO FAMILIES AFFECTED BY CHILDHOOD CANCER.
