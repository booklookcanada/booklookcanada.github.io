What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

(1). To advance religion through study of scriptures for children and youth using well structured curriculum age appropriate textbooks skits arts crafts discussions debates and play every Sunday. (2). To advance religion for adults through the study of Hindu scriptures and Bhagavad Gita classes for Adults every Sunday. (3). Weekly meditation and discussion classes every Monday (4). Weekly classes on Fridays on one of Hindu Scriptures (5). Weekly classes on Saturdays to facilitate discussion on one of Hindu scriptures and commentary by Swami Chinmayananda (6). Knowledge series and Self-development workshops on topics relevant to daily living by invited speakers couple of times a year. (7). Celebration of Hindu festivals including their rituals & worship. (8). Worship & prayer on Mondays and Tuesdays and during all our sessions. (9). Monthly sessions for women focusing on discussion on Hindu scriptures
