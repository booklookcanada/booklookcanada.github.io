What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

Cairn operates several low-cost summer camp programs for children youth and families for the purpose of advancing the Christian faith. The camping programs offered by Cairn are as follows: • The Glen Mhor Program offers a traditional week-long residential camping program at its site on Echo Lake near Baysville ON (July-August); • The Iona Wilderness Program offers a week-long wilderness camping program (July-August); • Three Leadership Programs between one week and one month in duration equipping for youth aged 13 to 16.
