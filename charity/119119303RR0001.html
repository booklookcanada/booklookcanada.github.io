What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

RICHVIEW BAPTIST CHURCH HOLDS WEEKLY WORSHIP SERVICES ON SUNDAY MORNINGS WHICH ARE ATTENDED BY 160+ PEOPLE. THESE SERVICES PROVIDE OPPORTUNITIES TO GATHER TO WORSHIP & LEARN TOGETHER THRU BIBLE-BASED PREACHING PRAYER AND SINGING. IN ADDITION THERE ARE WEEKLY OPPORTUNITIES FOR SMALLER GROUPS WITHIN OUR CHURCH COMMUNITY AND THE COMMUNITY AROUND US TO STUDY THE BIBLE ENGAGE WITH ONE ANOTHER AND HEAR ABOUT HOW GOD IS AT WORK IN THE WORLD. THESE PROGRAMS INCLUDE ALPHA. STARTING POINT AND SMALL GROUP BIBLE STUDIES. WE HAVE 4 SMALL GROUP BIBLE STUDIES WHICH RANGE FROM 3 TO 20 PEOPLE PER GROUP WHERE THEY STUDY A BOOK IN THE BIBLE OR WHAT THE BIBLE HAS TO SAY ABOUT A GIVEN TOPIC. THESE ARE PRIMARILY WEEKLY STUDIES WHICH ALSO INCLUDE TIMES OF PRAYER. FOR THE CHILDREN WE AVERAGED 15+ IN ATTENDANCE AT OUR WEEKLY KIDS CLUB. FOR THE YOUTH (AGE 11 TO 18) WE AVERAGED 20+ IN ATTENDANCE AT OUR WEEKLY JUNIOR HIGH AND SENIOR HIGH PROGRAMS. WE ALSO HAVE A YOUNG ADULT GROUP FORMED FORMED FOR BIBLE STUDIES.
FOR SENIORS IN OUR COMMUNITY WE HOLD REGULAR JOY FELLOWSHIP LUNCHEONS WHERE AN AVERAGE OF 85 SENIORS WERE IN ATTENDANCE. HYMN SINGS WERE ALSO HELD ON A REGULAR BASIS WITH AN AVERAGE OF 30+ IN ATTENDANCE. WE OPEN OUR DOORS TO OUR COMMUNITY WITH REGULARITY FOR EVENTS. IN ADDITION WE PARTNER WITH ORGANIZATIONS TO SHARE THE GOOD NEWS OF JESUS TO OUR WORLD. IN ADDITION WE PROVIDE REGULAR PRAYER SUPPORT FOR OUR COMMUNITY AND WORLD AT LARGE.
