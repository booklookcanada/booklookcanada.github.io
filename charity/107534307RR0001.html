What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

OUR MISSIONS HAS FOUR MAIN PRUPOSES: 1. TO PREACH AND ADVANCE THE TEACHINGS OF CHRISTIANITY AND THE RELIGIOUS TENETS DOCTRINES OBSERVANCES AND CULTURE ASSOCIATED WITH THE FAITH WHERE CHRISTIANS ARE BEING PERSECUTED. 2. TO SUPPORT AND MAINTAIN CHRISTIAN MISSIONS WHERE CHRISTIANS ARE PERSECUTED. 3. TO PROVIDE FOR RELIEF TO IMPOVERISHED CHRISTIANS AND THEIR FAMILIES WHO ARE PERSECUTED. 4. TO EDUCATE THE CANADIAN PUBLIC REGARDING THE EXPERIENCE AND RELIGIOUS TEACHINGS OF PERSECUTED CHRISTIANS. EACH YEAR A LARGE PART OF THE CANADIAN ACTIVITIES CONSISTS OF INFORMING THE PUBLIC ABOUT TRUE CONDITIONS OF THE CHRISTIAN CHURCH IN OTHER COUNTRIES. THIS IS ACCOMPLISHED BY MEANS OF MONTHLY NEWSLETTERS MAILED FREE OF CHARGE AND THROUGH MEETINGS CONDUCTED BY OUR REPRESENTATIVES. IN COOPERATION WITH SISTER MISSIONS AROUND THE WORLD WE CONTRIBUTE FUNDS TO AN INTERNATIONAL COMMITTEE WHO UNDERTAKE PROJECTS IN KEEPING WITH OUR ABOVE PURPOSES.
