What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

SUNDAY WORSHIP PROGRAMMES. PASTORAL CARE FOR THOSE WHO NEED IT. SUNDAY SCHOOL FOR THE CHILDREN ON SUNDAYS VACATION BIBLE SCHOOL IN AUGUST FOR 1 WEEK. SENIORS MEET ONCE A WEEK FOR FELLOWSHIP AND GAMES. THE LADIES AID FUNCTIONED SUPPLYING AN AFTERNOON TEA AND ALSO CHRISTIAN BAZAAR FOR FUND RAISING. WE CONTINUED WITH AN EASTER EGG HUNT FOR CHILDREN IN THE COMMUNITY. FOOD WAS SUPPLIED TO DOON COLLEGE INTERNATIONAL STUDENTS. WE AGAIN HAD A TREE LIGHTING SERVICE AND SERVED CHILI TO THE COMMUNITY AND CHURCH MEMBERS. WE VOLUNTEER AT BINGO TO RECEIVE SOME FUNDS.
WE PUT TOGETHER BAGS WITH GIFT CARDS PENCILS PENS WRITING PADS AND SOME PERSONNAL ITEMS FOR THE STUDENTS AT DOON COLLEGE INTERNATIONAL STUDENTS. OUR SUNDAY SCHOOL CHILDREN DECORATED BAGS WHICH WERE DISTRIBUTED TO THE LOCAL COMMUNTY FOR FOOD FOR THE FOOD BANK. A PRAYROUND WAS SET UP IN THE CHURCH SANCTUARY FOR THE CHILDREN OF THE CONGREGATION.
