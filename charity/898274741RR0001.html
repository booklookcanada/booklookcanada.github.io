What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Jun 30, 2024)

THE TORONTO BAPTIST SEMINARY AND BIBLE COLLEGE PROVIDES EDUCATION AND TRAINING TO STUDENTS WHO WISH TO BECOME PASTORS MISSIONARIES EVANGELISTS AND BIBLE SCHOOL TEACHERS. THIS IS LARGELY DONE BY PHYSICALLY ATTENDING CLASSES BUT ALSO TO A LESSER DEGREE BY CORRESPONDENCE AND ATTENDING ONLINE COURSES. THE TORONTO BAPTIST SEMINARY AND BIBLE COLLEGE PROVIDES RESIDENCE FACILITIES AND A MEAL PLAN SERVICE FOR STUDENTS DURING THE ACADEMIC YEAR. THEY ALSO HOST CONFERENCES ON CHRISTIANITY.
