What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

REENA FOUNDATION'S MANDATE IS TO HELP SECURE FUNDS IN SUPPORT REENA INC.- A NOT FOR PROFIT SOCIAL SERVICE AGENCY THAT IS DEDICATED TO ENRICHING THE LIVES OF INDIVIDUALS WITH DEVELOPMENTAL DISABILITIES AND BATAY REENA - A CHARITABLE CORPORATION THAT PROVIDES FUNDS AND LOW-COST HOUSING TO REENA FOR THE BENEFIT OF REENA'S CLIENTS. REENA INC. PROVIDES TO INDIVIDUALS WITH DEVELOPMENT DISABILITIES THROUGH RESIDENTIAL SERVICES DAY PROGRAMS AND RESPITE SERVICES. REENA FOUNDATION ALSO WORKS TO EDUCATION THE GENERAL PUBLIC TO BETTER UNDERSTAND THE NEEDS OF THOSE WITH DEVELOPMENTAL DISABILITIES IN ORDER TO ENSURE INDIVIDUALS WITH DEVELOPMENTAL DISABILITIES ARE EMPOWERED TO REALIZE THEIR FULL POTENTIAL AND ARE INTEGRATED INTO SOCIETY. REENA FOUNDATION OPERATES EXCLUSIVELY AS A PUBLIC FOUNDATION FOR THE BENEFIT OF REENA INC. AND BATAY REENA - RECEIVING AND MAINTAINING FUNDS ALL OR PART OF WHICH ARE TRANSFERED TO REENA INC. OR BATAY REENA WHICH ARE BOTH REGISTERED UNDER THE INCOME TAX ACT.
