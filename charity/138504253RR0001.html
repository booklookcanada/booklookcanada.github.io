Weekly Worship Services: We provide weekly worship gatherings including prayer teaching and spiritual support to advance the Christian faith. Weekly Bible School (online and in person): We deliver Christian education through Bible studies for adults and children to build faith and knowledge of Scripture. Youth & Young Adult Ministry: Weekly and monthly services classes and events designed to develop Christian values leadership and community among youth and young adults. Discipleship and Converts Classes: We offer regular discipleship classes to support spiritual growth and foundational Christian teaching for new believers.
