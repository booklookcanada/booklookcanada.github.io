What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Aug 31, 2024)

Our focus is the rural village of Dekpor Ghana West Africa. We operate entirely with unpaid directors and volunteers there are no employees. Overhead is nominal. Food programs: A child is given the opportunity to attend school and have the necessary costs covered. Support provides a breakfast and lunch school uniform mosquito net all school supplies exam fees and medical attention. All child sponsorship money goes towards worthy initiatives that will directly benefit children. Teacher sponsorships: Sponsoring educators at Dekpor Basic School. Building fund: Provides for the construction of new buildings repairs and renovations to existing ones. The organization also supports Dekpor Basic School's students by improving water sanitation and health resources; providing educational supplies and materials; and supporting other projects that benefit Dekpor residents.
