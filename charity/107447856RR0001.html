What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

TO PROVIDE OUTPATIENT SERVICES TO CHILDREN AND YOUNG ADULTS WITH PHYSICAL AND COMMUNICATION DISABILITIES WITH AN AIM TO ACHIEVE THEIR HIGHEST LEVEL OF INDEPENDENCE IN THE COMMUNITY. SERVICES INCLUDE PHYSIOTHERAPY OCCUPATIONAL THERAPY SPEECH-LANGUAGE PATHOLOGY AUDIOLOGY FAMILY SUPPORT MEDICAL AND PSYCHOLOGICAL SERVICES RECREATION AND VARIOUS SPECIALTY CLINICS. IN ADDITION WE DELIVER SERVICES UNDER THE ONTARIO AUTISM PROGRAM (OAP) AS WELL AS THE AUTISM SPECTRUM DISORDER HUB. SERVICES ARE PROVIDED AT THE MAIN FACILITY IN OSHAWA AND OUR SATELLITE/COMMUNITY FACILITIES IN AJAX WHITBY PORT PERRY BOWMANVILLE AND VARIOUS OTHER SITES THROUGHOUT THE REGIONAL MUNICIPALITY OF DURHAM.
