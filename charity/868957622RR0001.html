What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

THROUGH THE COMPASSION AND GENEROSITY OF DONORS THE HALTON CATHOLIC CHILDREN'S EDUCATION FOUNDATION (HCCEF) IS COMMITTED TO ENHANCING THE EDUCATION EXPERIENCE AND ENRICHING THE LIVES OF STUDENTS WITHIN OUT CATHOLIC SCHOOL COMMUNITIES BY HELPING THEM WITH FINANCIAL BARRIERS TO EDUCATION. ON THE RECOMMENDATION OF OUR CATHOLIC SCHOOL COMMUNITIES HCCEF PROVIDES FINANCIAL ASSISTANCE TO STUDENTS TO ENSURE THAT ALL ARE ABLE TO ATTEND SCHOOL WITH THE BASIC NECESSITIES OF LIFE (I.E. FOOD & CLOTHING) AS WELL AS ALL ARE ABLE TO PARTICIPATE IN ALL ASPECTS OF SCHOOL LIFE WHICH ARE NORMALLY OPEN TO ALL STUDENTS (E.G. SCHOOL TRIPS/EXPERIENCES) AND THEREFORE HELP THEM TO SUCCEED AND ACHIEVE THEIR FULL POTENTIAL
