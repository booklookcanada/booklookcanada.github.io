THE SPECIAL MISSION OF TORONTO METROPOLITAN UNIVERSITY IS THE ADVANCEMENT OF APPLIED KNOWLEDGE AND RESEARCH TO ADDRESS SOCIETAL NEEDS AND THE PROVISION OF PROGRAMS OF STUDY THAT PROVIDES A BALANCE BETWEEN THEORY AND APPLICATION THAT PREPARES STUDENTS FOR CAREERS IN PROFESSIONAL AND QUASI-PROFESSIONAL FIELDS. AS A LEADING CENTRE FOR APPLIED EDUCATION IT IS RECOGNIZED FOR THE EXCELLENCE OF ITS TEACHING THE RELEVANCE OF ITS CURRICULUM AND SUCCESS OF ITS STUDENTS IN ACHIEBING THEIR ACADEMIC AND CAREER OBJECTIVES THE QUALITY OF ITS SCHOLARSHIPS RESERACH AND CREATIVE ACTIVITY AND ITS COMMITMENT TO ACCESSIBILITY LIFE LONG LEARNING AND INVOLVEMENT IN THE BROADER COMMUNITY. THE SCHOOL WILL FOCUS ON DEVELOPING INNOVATIVE WELL-ROUNDED PHYSICIANS WHO ARE RESPONSIVE TO SOCIETAL AND COMMUNITY NEEDS. ADMISSIONS WILL BEGIN ON OCTOBER 9 FOR THE 2025 ACADEMIC YEAR STARTING IN SEPTEMBER 2025.
