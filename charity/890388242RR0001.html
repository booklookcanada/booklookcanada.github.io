Who runs it — the people it pays

It reported 53 full-time positions and 44 part-time employees (year ended Dec 31, 2024). Total pay for all its positions: $2,431,364, as filed. The highest pay band it reported was “$120,000–$159,999”. 1 employee was reported in that band. Pay is reported in bands — ranges, not exact salaries. The top band is the highest range with anyone in it: it is not one person's salary, and it is not what everyone there is paid.

WORKING WITH STREET YOUTH AGED TWELVE AND OVER IN THE KITCHENER-WATERLOO AREA IN ORDER TO MAINTAIN THEIR HEALTH AND SAFETY. PROVIDING RESOURCES AND SERVICES NEEDED TO GET YOUTH OFF THE STREET AND TO DEVELOP STABILITY IN THEIR LIVING SITUATIONS. PROVIDING SUBSTANCE ABUSE EDUCATION AND INTERVENTION. THE NEXT STEPS HOUSING PROGRAM PAIRS INDEPENDANCE WITH A MIX OF HELPFUL IN-HOUSE AND COMMUNITY RESOURCES.
