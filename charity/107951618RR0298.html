What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

THE SALVATION ARMY'S TORONTO HOUSING AND HOMELESS SUPPORTS (THHS) OPERATES SIX SHELTERS IN TORONTO FOR PEOPLE EXPERIENCING HOMELESSNESS. THESE SIX SHELTERS MEET BASIC NEEDS FOR FOOD SHELTER AND CLOTHING AS WELL AS PROVIDE SPIRITUAL AND RELIGIOUS CARE ADDICTIONS COUNSELLING CASE MANAGEMENT HOUSING HELP OUTREACH SERVICES AND COMMUNITY FOLLOW-UP SUPPORTS. OF THHS' SIX SITES MAXWELL MEIGHEN CENTRE THE GATEWAY AND NEW HOPE LESLIEVILLE EACH PROVIDE THESE SERVICES TO ADULT MEN. EVANGELINE RESIDENCE AND FLORENCE BOOTH HOUSE PROVIDE SERVICES TO ADULT WOMEN. ISLINGTON SENIORS' SHELTER THHS' NEWEST SHELTER SERVES A CO-ED SENIOR (55+) POPULATION. TORONTO HOUSING AND HOMELESS SUPPORTS ALSO MANAGES A SOCIAL ENTERPRISE AND OTHER SERVICES THAT HELP SHELTER RESIDENTS TRANSITION INTO THEIR OWN HOUSING. SOCIAL ENTERPRISE GATEWAY LINENS AND DISPOSAL SERVICES (GLDS) IS AN INDUSTRIAL LAUNDRY FACILITY THAT PROVIDES TRAINING AND EMPLOYMENT TO PEOPLE LIVING IN THE SHELTER SYSTEM WITH THE GOAL OF REINTEGRATION INTO THE WORKFORCE. GLDS PROVIDES THEIR LAUNDRY SERVICES TO LOCAL BUSINESSES AND SHELTERS. IN ADDITION DURING THE COVID-19 PANDEMIC THHS HAS BEEN OPERATING THREE SHELTER PROGRAMS IN TWO HOTEL LOCATIONS IN TORONTO
