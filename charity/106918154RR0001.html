What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

We hold worship services weekly and a mid-week services during Lent. Meditation on line and in-person. We give pastoral care. We offer baptism wedding and funeral/memorial services as needed. We provide ministries for children youth and adults. We run a community garden. We provide space to other groups for their activities. We donate funds to denomination headquarters for support of their organization and benevolence activities. The Foodbank of Waterloo Region is supported through the collection of food.
Provided dinners where members of the congregation could find fellowship together with other members of the community.
