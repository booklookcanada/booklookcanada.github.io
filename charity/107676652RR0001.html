Who runs it — the people it pays

It reported 104 full-time positions and 100 part-time employees (year ended Mar 31, 2024). Total pay for all its positions: $7,417,915, as filed. The highest pay band it reported was “$80,000–$119,999”. 7 employees were reported in that band. Pay is reported in bands — ranges, not exact salaries. The top band is the highest range with anyone in it: it is not one person's salary, and it is not what everyone there is paid.

MARYVALE IS A FULLY ACCREDITED MULTI-DISCIPLINARY CHILDREN'S MENTAL HEALTH CENTRE. WE SERVE FAMILIES WITH CHILDREN AND ADOLESCENTS WHO ARE EXPERIENCING SIGNIFICANT SOCIAL EMOTIONAL AND BEHAVIOURAL DIFFICULTIES. WE OFFER DAY TREATMENT CRISIS DAY PROGRAMS ACUTE CARE AND PSYCHIATRIC HOSPITAL BEDS. (NEW PROGRAM) PARENT CALL IN LINE. CRISIS AFTERCARE - THE PROGRAM FOCUSES ON ACHIEVING CONTINUITY OF CARE AND RELATIONSHIPS TO PREVENT FURTHER RECURRING CRISIS SITUATIONS FOR THE YOUTH AND FAMILIES. WE OFFER WALK IN CLINICS FOR COUNSELLING IN 3 RURAL SURROUNDING AREAS WHERE CLIENTS DO NOT HAVE TRANSPOTATION.
