What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Apr 30, 2024)

We provide emergency shelter services for homeless men which includes lodging meals clothing and laundry facilities. We hold daily chapel services. We operate residential and non residential drug and alcohol addictions treatment programs. we provide hospice services for the homeless men and women suffering from terminal illness. We provide spiritual and grief counselling. We provide free health care and dental services to the homeless and those at risk of being homeless. We provide training for workers interested in the food services and building maintenance industries. We provide educational assistance and counselling. We provide assistance in obtaining and maintaining independent housing. We provide supportive housing to graduates of drug and alcohol rehabilitation programs including ongoing counselling.
We provide a mobile food service for those in need and at a risk of becoming homeless in the Ottawa Area.
