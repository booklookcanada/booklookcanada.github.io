What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

TRADITIONAL ONE TO ONE MENTORING PROGRAMS THAT IS COMMUNITY-BASED. SCREENED ADULT VOLUNTEER MENTORS ARE MATCHED WITH A CHILD (6-16 YEARS OF AGE) AND VISIT FOR 3-4 HOURS A WEEK. COUPLE MENTORING PROGRAMS IS A COMMUNITY-BASED PROGRAMS THAT HAS A STABLE COUPLE MATCHED WITH A CHILD AND VISITS FOR 3-4 HOURS A WEEK. IN-SCHOOL MENTORING PROGRAMS SEES MENTORS VISIT WITH A CHILD AT THEIR SCHOOL FOR ONE HOUR A WEEK. GROUP PROGRAMMING PROVIDE MENTORING SUPPORTING TO YOUTH BETWEEN THE AGES OF 6-16 IN SMALL GROUPS OF NO MORE THAN 10 YOUTH. GROUP PROGRAMMING IS PROVIDED IN THE SCHOOLS AND IN OUR COMMUNITY. SUMMER CAMP PROGRAMS - WEEK LONG DAY CAMP PROGRAMS THAT ARE DESIGNED TO ADDRESS THE DEVELOPMENT OF SOCIAL SKILLS MENTAL WELLNESS AND SELF-ADVOCACY. KID N KOPS PROGRAM IS WEEK LONG SUMMER PROGRAM IN PARTNERSHIP WITH LOCAL POLICE SERVICES.
