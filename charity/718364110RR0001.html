What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

A DENOMINATION WITH A RICH INNOVATIVE TRADITION UPON WHICH TO BUILD FOR THE BETTERMENT OF HUMANITY. THE CHURCH OF CANADA SHALL BE BOTH A COMMUNITY OUTREACH AND A RELIGIOUS BODY AND SHALL FURTHER THE PROSECUTION OF PETITIONS FOR MISSIONS AND FURTHERANCE OF ITS OBJECTS. IT SHALL FOSTER COMMUNITY SERVICE RELIGIOUS SPIRITUAL AND PASTORAL CARE SERVICES IN COMMUNITIES BOTH AT HOME AND ABROAD REGARDLESS OF RELIGIOUS AFFILIATION PRACTICE AND/OR BELIEF. AS SUCH IT SHALL AIM TO BRING PEOPLE IDEAS AND RESOURCES TOGETHER TO UNDERSTAND AND ADVANCE ISSUES EXPLORE CREATIVE SOLUTIONS AND BE A CATALYST AND INSTRUMENT FOR LONG TERM CHANGE. WE ARE CREATING OPPORTUNITIES FOR CANADA BY LINKING IT WITH GLOBALC OMMUNITY FOR ADVANCEMENT.
THE CHURCH OF CANADA SHALL FURTHER SUCH REGULATIONS AND REQUIREMENTS AS MAY BE ENACTED FROM TIME TO TIME BY THE GOVERNMENT OF CANADA PROVINCIAL OR TERRITORIAL GOVERNMENTS. SUCH OTHER APPROPRIATE PRAYER OFFERING AND PROGRAMMES APPROVED BY THE SAID AND IF THE SAID LAWFULLY BENEFITS ITS ADVANCEMENT. FURTHER ALL SUCH OTHER THINGS AS ARE INCIDENTAL OR ANCILLARY TO THE ATTAINMENT OF THE MENTIONED PURPOSES THROUGH STRATEGIC CHANGE AND ALIGNMENT.

What this page can't tell you

  • Whether its work works. A return shows money, not results.
  • Whether anyone audited these figures. The CRA publishes returns as filed, without checking them. Some charities also post audited statements — BookLook compared the largest 200 charities' published statements with their returns (see “How to read this site”).
  • Anything after Mar 31, 2024, its newest return in the published data.
  • Who its directors are. The published data does not include them.
  • Questions worth asking it directly: Do you publish audited financial statements? Who sits on your board this year? What did last year's programs achieve, and how do you know?
