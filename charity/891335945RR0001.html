What it says it does

DCI-CANADA WORKS IN PARTNERSHIP WITH YOUNG PEOPLE TO PROVIDE INFORMATION AND EDUCATION WITH REGARD TO THE UN CONVENTION OF THE RIGHTS OF THE CHILD INCLUDING AN ANNUAL LECTURE PRESENTATION AT CONFERENCES PARTICIPATION IN CHANGES TO LEGISLATION INVOLVING CHILDREN AND YOUTH AND ACTING AS AN INTERVENER ON COURT CASES INCLUDING SEVERAL AT THE SUPREME COURT OF CANADA.
