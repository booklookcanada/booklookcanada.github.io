What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Jun 30, 2024)

Compassion Canada is a leading Christian child development organization whose mission is to release children from poverty in Jesus' name. Our proven child development program provides infants children and youth around the world with the resources and relationships to overcome poverty and develop in every aspect of life: physical cognitive spiritual and social-emotional. Compassion has three core programs that work together to care for children at every stage of development from the womb to the workforce: our Survival program focuses on child and maternal health Child and Youth Development supports holistic child development and Complementary Interventions help build communities where children can thrive. As part of the Compassion global partnership Compassion Canada serves children in 29 countries around the world seeking to release them from poverty in Jesus' name.
