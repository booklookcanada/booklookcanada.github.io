What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

TO ASSIST MUNICIPAL GOVERNMENTS IN CANADA TO LEVER INVESTMENTS IN MUNICIPAL ENVIRONMENTAL PROJECTS AND TO PROVIDE GRANTS FOR FEASIBILITY STUDIES ASSESSMENTS SUSTAINABLE COMMUNITY PLANS AND FIELD TESTS AND GRANTS LOANS AND/OR LOAN GUARANTEES TO ELIGIBLE RECEIPIENTS FOR PROJECT IMPLEMENTATION. THE GREEN MUNICIPAL FUND IS A REVOLVING FUND ADMINISTERED BY THE FEDERATION OF CANADA MUNICIPALITIES. THE GMF WAS ESTABLISHED TO ENHANCE CANADIANS' QUALITY OF LIFE BY IMPROVING AIR WATE AND SOIL QUALITY AND PROTECTING THE CLIMATE. THE GMF SUPPORTS GRANTS LOANS AN LOAN GUARANTEES TO ENCOURAGE INVESTMENT IN ENVIRONMENTAL MUNICIPAL PROJECTS. ELIGIBLE PROJECTS MAY FALL INTO ONE OR MORE OF THE FOLLOWING CATEGORIES: BROWNFIELDS ENERGY TRANSPORTATION WASTE AND WATER IN THE FORM OF SUSTAINABLE NEIGHBORHOOD ACTION PLANS COMMUNITY BROWNFIELD ACTION PLANS GREENHOUSE GAS REDUCTION PLANS FEASIBILITY STUDIES PILOT PROJECTS AND CAPITAL PROJECTS. GMF FUNDING IS AVAILABLE TO ALL MUNICIPAL GOVERNMENTS AND THEIR PARTNERS IN ELIGIBLE PROJECTS.
