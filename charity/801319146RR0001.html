What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

BECOMING NEIGHBOURS IS A HOST PROGRAM DESIGNED TO SUPPORT NEW IMMIGRANTS AND REFUGEES DURING THEIR ADJUSTMENT IN TORONTO TO CANADIAN SOCIETY. PROMOTING CULTURAL INTEGRATION AND ENRICHMENTS BY ASSISTING IMMIGRANTS AND REFUGEES TO BECOME ACTIVE AND FULLY PARTICIPATING MEMBERS OF THE COMMUNITY. PROGRAMS PROVIDED TO IMMIGRANTS AND REFUGEES IN COLLABORATION WITH OTHER ORGANIZATIONS. A MENTOR-SHIP PROGRAM FOR VOLUNTEERS IN THEIR SUPPORT OF IMMIGRANTS AND REFUGEES. AN INCREASE AWARENESS OF THE BARRIERS FOR IMMIGRANTS AND REFUGEES IT PROVIDES FOR THEM TO BECOME FULLY CONTRIBUTING MEMBERS OF THE COMMUNITY.
