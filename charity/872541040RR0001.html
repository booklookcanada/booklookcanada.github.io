What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

Humanitarian relief work in Canada and around the world. Assist victims of natural disasters including flood famines earthquakes illness or any other misfortune of life. Help relieve poverty and deprivation that may exist any where in the world. Help hungry through food bank and feed a family program. Assist senior citizens. Working on various international project including Senegal hospital and Ivory Coast Hospital. Actively involve in sponsoring displaced refugees and their settlement in Canada from various countries. Alleviate the homelessness issue through Feed the Homeless program. Humanity First undertakes focused and results-oriented development programs that promote and seek to build stronger communities in which individuals are engaged and in-tuned in helping those in need through responsible caring and skilled volunteers. The organization’s mission has following four themes to serve ordinary Canadians and humanity in-general: 1. Poverty Reduction: Enhance economic potential of individuals and families by bridging the gap between poverty and prosperity. 2. Disaster Relief: Relieve human suffering caused by natural disasters or human conflicts by providing urgent short and long term assistance to alleviate suffering. 3. Education and Training: Eradicate poverty and homelessness by promoting and providing education and skill building training to underprivileged Canadians. In addition assist individuals through the establishment of business ventures and small industries to get back on their feet. 4. Canada Cares: Become flag-bearers of Canada and promote essential Canadian values of unity brotherhood and generosity without regards to race religion or ethnicity across the globe and showcase Canada’s commitment to humanity.
Humanity First is building a primary school in Cameroon and also started small project in Burundi (food security Orphan care) Humanity First Canada with the partnership of Humanity First UK is also building hospital in Ivory Cost. The hospital project will be completed in 2026. Humanity First is also building housing project in Burkina Faso for internally displaced persons.
