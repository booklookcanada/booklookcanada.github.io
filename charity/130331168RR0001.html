What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

ARTICLE 2 OF OUR CONSTITUTION AND BY-LAWS READS: THE PURPOSE OF THIS CONGREGATION SHALL BE TO SERVE ITS MEMBERS AND TO SPEAD THE KINGDOM OF GOD BY PREACHING THE WORD OF GOD ADMINISTRATION OF THE SACRAMENTS AND BY RELIGIOUS INSTRUCTION OF YOUTH AND ADULTS ACCORDING TO THE CONFESSIONAL STANDARD OF THE LUTHERAN CHURCH AND TO FOSTER CHRISTIAN FELLOWSHIP AND CHARITY. WEEKLY WORSHIP SERVICES SUNDAY SCHOOL BIBLE CLASSES AND ANNUAL VACATION BIBLE SCHOOL. WE ALSO DONATE FUNDS TO OUR ONTARIO DISTRICT FOR ITS WORK PROGRAM. WE HAVE SUPPORTED A MISSIONARY IN NICARAGUA FOR ABOUT 20 YEARS AND FOR THE LAST FIFTEEN YEARS HAVE PARTICIPATED WITH OTHER SISTER CHURCHES WHEREBY MEMBERS VISIT LEON NICARAGUA AND ASSIST IN CONDUCTING A VACATION BIBLE SCHOOL FOR THE CHILDREN AND BIBLE STUDIES FOR THE ADULTS. WE ALSO ASSIST IN CONSTRUCTION PROJECTS.
