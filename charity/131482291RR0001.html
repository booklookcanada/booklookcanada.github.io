What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Jun 30, 2024)

THE SHARING PLACE PROVIDES FOOD AND HYGEINE PRODUCTS TO INSECURE INDIVIDUALS IN THE ORILLIA AREA. THE SCHOOL FOOD PROGRAMS PROVIDES FOOD AT A DISCOUNTED PRICE TO 23+ SCHHOLS IN SIMCOE COUNTY. MEALS4CHANGE PROGRAM PROVIDES READY TO HEAT MEALS FOR INDIVIDUALS WHO ARE FOOD INSECURE WHILE TAGRETING THOSE IN ALTERNATIVE LIVING SITUATIONS. FOOD EDUCATION AND ADVOCACY PROGRAMS AIM TO EDUCATE MEMBERS REGARDING FOOD INSECURITY AND DIGNIFIED SERVICE.
MEALS4CHANGE PROGRAM PROVIDES FROZEN PREPARED MEALS IN HOUSE FOR MEMBERS. SUNSHINE SOUP PROGRAM IS A WEEKLY PROGRAM FOR SENIORS T ACCESS BY HELPING CREAT SOUP WITH OUR CHEF. EACH PARTICIPATING INDIVIDUAL CAN TAKE SOUP HOME AND THE REMAINDER GOES OUT THROUGH THE FOOD BANK PROGRAM.
