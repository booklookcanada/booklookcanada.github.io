What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Sep 30, 2024)

FLL produces weekly video and audio programs of Roman Catholic faith content to be broadcast/distribute through various television radio and online (e.g YouTube) channels as well as our website (www.FLL.cc) and social media sites. Our volunteers help to produce religious songs to enhance our programs and to further our purposes. We operate a Resource and Media Centre selling religious books gifts multimedia resources etc. Occasionally we organize seminars and workshops for dual purposes: to spread and strengthen the Catholic faith/knowledge among the participants and through which to inspire them to help advance our mission through their active participations and involvements.
