The highest pay band it reported was “$300,000–$349,999”. 1 employee was reported in that band. Pay is reported in bands — ranges, not exact salaries. The top band is the highest range with anyone in it: it is not one person's salary, and it is not what everyone there is paid.

What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

THE GROUP HEALTH ASSOCIATION (GHA) PLAYS A CRITICAL ROLE IN SUPPORTING THE DELIVERY OF HEALTHCARE SERVICES TO THE RESIDENTS OF SAULT STE. MARIE AND THE ALGOMA REGION. AS A QUALIFIED DONEE GHA RECIEVES MEDICAL EQUIPMENT FUNDED BY GHC TRUST FUND TO ENHANCE PATIENT CARE AND IMPROVE HEALTHCARE OUTCOMES WITHIN THE COMMUNITY. GHA'S ONGOING ACTIVITIES FOCUS ON ENSURING THAT HEALTHCARE PROVIDERS HAVE ACCESS TO THE TOOLS AND EQUIPMENT NECESSARY TO DELIVER TIMELY AND EFFECTIVE CARE. THROUGH THIS COLLABORATION WITH THE GHC TRUST FUND AND OTHER HEALTHCARE PARTNERS GHA WORKS TO STRENGTHEN THE REGION'S HEALTHCARE INFRASTRUCTURE SUPPORTING INITIATIVES THAT PROMOTE THE HEALTH AND WELL-BEING OF THE POPULATION IT SERVES.
