What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

The Queensway Carleton Hospital Foundation's objective is to raise maintain and manage donated funds to be distributed to the Queensway Carleton Hospital to support the Hospital's patient care programs equipment purchases and redevelopment projects. The Queensway Carleton Hospital is a charitable non-for-profit organization providing health care within various clinical programs in inpatient and outpatient settings. The Queensway Carleton Hospital foundation abides by Imagine Canada Ethical Fundraising and Financial Accountability code and adheres to the Association of Fundraising Professionals' Code of Ethics and the Association of Healthcare Philanthropy standards.
