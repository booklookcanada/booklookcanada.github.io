What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

We operate an Indigenous women's emergency shelter and provide supports and services to assist women in living a life free from violence. We operate an Indigenous women's provincial help line. We offer human trafficking supports to community members and education/awareness to the community. We provide safe and affordable housing in Thunder Bay. We operate an Indigenous women's Transitional Home.
