What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

The Particular Council animates and coordinates the activities of conferences in their ministry. It serves the conferences by furthering charitable activities; by keeping them informed about changing social conditons new issues and programs changes or additions in the Society's internal policies and procedures and major events; by providing conferences financial assistance if needed and other pertinent forms of support. The Scarborough West Particular Council currently serves 8 conferences. It is not directly engaged in charitable activities which are carried out by the conferences.
