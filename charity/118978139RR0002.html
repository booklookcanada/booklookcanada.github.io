What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

THE KAWARTHA-HALIBURTON CHILDREN'S FOUNDATION WAS ESTABLISHED TO PRIMARILY SUPPORT PROJECTS OF BENEFIT TO THE CHILDREN IN THE KAWARTHA-HALIBURTON CHILDREN'S AID SOCIETY. THE COSTS OF THESE PROJECTS FALL OUTSIDE OF THE FUNDING PROVIDED BY THE GOVERNMENT FOR CHILD WELFARE SERVICES. THESE PROJECTS INCLUDE THE FOLLOWING: - SUMMER CAMP FUND - MARGARET DAVIES BURSARY FUND - SPORTS AND RECREATION - MUSIC AND DANCE - SANTA'S SLEIGH - YOUTH TRANSITIONING OUT OF FOSTER CARE GIFT CARD PROGRAM - YOUTH IN CARE GRADUATION CEREMONY - SWEET DREAMS PAJAMAS AND BEDDING - CHILD SAFETY FUND - KINSHIP SUPPORT FUND
