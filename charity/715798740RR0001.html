What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

As a newly formed religious Christian church organization we have been dedicated to conduct weekly worship for congregants and friends to worship the Trinity God. We also conduct weekly children bible school for the kids of our congregants and friends. We also conduct weekly bible study groups for different age groups and also biweekly prayer meeting.
We are planning to expand the gospel to non-believers in the community of Mississauga to help people coming back to God and accepting Jesus Christ as the Saviour and having eternal life through community activities.
