What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

A) Terrestrial Parks and Protected Areas Program - To establish new and expanded terrestrial protected areas while protecting nature in our existing parks; - To protect wilderness ecosystems across Canada; and - To educate Canadians about the importance of terrestrial conservation. B) Ocean Marine Protected Areas Program - To establish new and expanded marine protected areas while protecting nature in existing marine protected areas; - To protect natural marine ecosystems in Canada's oceans; and - To educate Canadians about the importance of marine conservation. C) Forests Climate and Species at Risk Program - To protect Canada's forests through scientific conservation planning; - To preserve the full diversity of habitats for Canadian wildlife species; and - To encourage and educate Canadians to support nature-based solutions to climate change.
