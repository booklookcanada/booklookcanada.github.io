Who runs it — the people it pays

It reported 5 full-time positions and 3 part-time employees (year ended Aug 31, 2024). Total pay for all its positions: $344,623, as filed. The highest pay band it reported was “$80,000–$119,999”. 2 employees were reported in that band. Pay is reported in bands — ranges, not exact salaries. The top band is the highest range with anyone in it: it is not one person's salary, and it is not what everyone there is paid.

What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Aug 31, 2024)

The Halton Learning Foundation was established to support quality education for students of the Halton District School Board. HLF provides emergency funds for students in need post-secondary scholarships and grants to assist schools that have classroom needs beyond core education funding.
