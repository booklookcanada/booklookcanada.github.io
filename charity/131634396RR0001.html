What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Feb 28, 2024)

SLEEPING CHILDREN AROUND THE WORLD PROVIDES BEDKITS TO CHILDREN IN NEED IN DEVELOPING COUNTRIES. BEDKITS ARE LARGELY COMPRISED OF A MAT OR MATTRESS/BEDDING A MOSQUITO NET TO COMBAT THE THREAT OF MALARIA & OTHER VECTOR-BORNE DISEASES SCHOOLBAG AND SCHOOL SUPPLIES AS WELL AS CLOTHING AND FOOTWEAR. ALL ITEMS ARE SOURCED LOCALLY IN A DEVELOPING COUNTRY WHERE POSSIBLE WITH POSITIVE ECONOMIC IMPACT TO THE LOCAL COMMUNITY. HUNDREDS OF TRAINED VOLUNTEERS TRAVEL TO DEVELOPING COUNTRIES PAYING THEIR OWN WAY TO ENSURE THE DELIVERY OF BEDKITS TO CHILDREN IN NEED. EIGHT BEDKIT DISTRIBUTIONS WERE COMPLETED DURING THE FISCAL YEAR TO EIGHT COUNTRIES.
NO NEW PROGRAMS.
