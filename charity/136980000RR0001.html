What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

As a protestant church we conduct weekly services and meetings both virtually and in-person. We provide funds to the denomination (CRCNA) and to other partners. Our Diaconate supports those in need through short-term financial and practical assistance. In addition members have worked alongside other community partners to support the Roehampton Shelter which serves people in the neighbourhood who are homeless.
The church raised money to sponsor a refugee family.
