What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

THE PARISH IS DEDICATED TO FOSTERING SPIRITUAL GROWTH THROUGH THE ADVANCEMENT OF ROMAN CATHOLIC RELIGIOUS WORSHIP INSTRUCTION AND THE MINISTRY TO MEMBERS IN THE COMMUNITY AT LARGE. TO THIS RESPECT THE PARISH: - HOLDS WORSHIPS SERVICE DAILY AND ON WEEKENDS -PREPARES INDIVIDUALS FOR THE RECEPTION OF SACRAMENTS IN THE CYCLE - ORGANIZES SPECIAL DAYS OF PARISH RETREATS - COUNSELS ADULTS CONTEMPLATING MARRIAGE; - ASSISTS THE DYING AND THOSE IN GRIEF.
