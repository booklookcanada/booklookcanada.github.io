Help to alleviate poverty in community by distributing free food to clients; supply snacks for school to families with children; provide packs of food for needy school children for weekends; home deliveries to physical/mobility challenged clients; provide food to churches and other local organizations who prepare community meals for needy; share extra food with other local food banks; provide personal hygiene items for needy secondary school students
