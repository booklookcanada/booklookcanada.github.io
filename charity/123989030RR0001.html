What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

WE OFFER: - A WIDE RANGE OF CLINICAL SERVICES WHICH ARE DETERMINED INDIVIDUALLY FOR EACH PERSON WITH ASD: PRESCHOOL AND SCHOOL-AGED CHILDREN AND THEIR FAMILIES INDIVIDUALS OVER 18 YEARS OF AGE; - COMPREHENSIVE EDUCATION AND TRAINING SERVICES WHICH ENABLE PARENTS AND PROFESSIONALS TO BECOME EFFECTIVE INTERVENOR AND ADVOCATES FOR PEOPLE WITH ASD; - RESOURCES FOR PARENTS PROFESSIONALS AND THOSE AFFECTED BY ASD; - SPECIALIZED PROGRAMS FOR CHILDREN WITH ASPERGER SYNDROME; - RESPITE/RELIEF PROGRAMS PROVIDING PARENTS WITH A BREAK FROM THEIR CARE-GIVING RESPONSIBILITIES; - SUPPORT GROUPS WHICH PROVIDE PARENT TO PARENT SUPPORT; SIBLINGS GROUPS PROVIDE AN OPPORTUNITY FOR BROTHERS AND SISTERS TO RECEIVE INFORMATION EDUCATION AND SUPPORT - AUTISM WORKFORCE DEVELOPMENT HUB - BY ASSISTING JOB SEEKERS AND EMPLOYERS WE ARE BRIDGING THE GAP BETWEEN EMPLOYMENT AND AUTISM SERVICES TO BETTER PREPARE EVERYONE FOR A SUCCESSFUL AND ACCESSIBLE EMPLOYMENT EXPERIENCE.
