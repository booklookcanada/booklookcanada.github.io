What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

Developing updating and distributing Educational Resources and Programs for multiple-birth individuals and their families and professional groups to care and instruct multiple-birth families. Resources and programs are given free or sold for a nominal charge to cover costs. Multiple Births Canada provides national workshops annually and through electronic avenues (videos and online discussions). Promotes the unique needs of multiple-birth individuals and Multiple Births Canada by planning a Multiple Births Awareness Day to educate the public about the needs and challenges of multiple birth individuals and their families.
