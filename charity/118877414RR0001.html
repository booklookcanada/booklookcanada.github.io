What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

We hold worship services weekly We provide ministries for women men youth and children We provide pastoral care We donate funds to denomination headquarters for domestic and overseas mission activities We donate to local charities as part of our mission and outreach program Kyiv Home Project - starting in 2022 Cottam Pastoral Charge partnered with a local developer and building company to build a home whose original intent was to provide transitional housing for Ukrainians fleeing the war in their homeland. The home has an upper and lower residence and can comfortably house two refugee families. With the help and generosity of numerous contractors suppliers and community donors we are able to provide affordable housing and support enabling the families to get established in our community until they are self-sufficient and able to move on. Hopefully soon the demand for this service will no longer be required. When this happens the house will continue to be used to provide transitional housing for newcomers and others experiencing precarious housing. Donations for this project continue to be used to upgrade furnishings and support the families.
