What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Jun 30, 2024)

THE CHRISTIAN REFORMED CHURCH IN NORTH AMERICA (CRCNA) IS THE DENOMINATIONAL HEAD OFFICE OF THE CHRISTIAN REFORMED CHURCH IN CANADA AND THE UMBRELLA ORGANIZATION FOR THE VARIOUS MINISTRIES OF THE CHRISTIAN REFORMED CHURCH. THERE ARE APPROXIMATELY 250 CHRISTIAN REFORMED CHURCH CONGREGATIONS ACROSS CANADA. ACTIVITIES INCLUDE SUPPORT OF EXISTING CHURCHES AND INDIGENOUS MINISTRY CENTRES IN WINNIPEG REGINA AND EDMONTON AND INTERNATIONAL EVANGELISM AND DISCIPLESHIP MINISTRIES.
