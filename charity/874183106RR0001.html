What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Jun 1, 2024)

TO USE NET ANNUAL REVENUES TO ESTABLISH ONE OR MORE SCHOLARSHIPS TO BE KNOWN AS THE JAMES & HILDA WARWICK SCHOLARSHIP(S). EACH SCHOLARSHIP SHALL BE A MAXIMUM DURATION OF 5 YEARS. THE SCHOLARSHIP IS AVAILABLE TO ONLY THOSE APPLICANTS WHO HAVE BEEN EDUCATED AT WESTMINSTER SCHOOL LONDON ENGLAND AND WHO INTEND TO CONTINUE THEIR STUDIES AT EITHER THE UNIVERSITY OF TORONTO OR THE UNIVERSITY OF ALBERTA.
