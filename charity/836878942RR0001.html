What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Jun 30, 2024)

Weekly worship services and programs for adults children and youth in all age groups are provided including Pastoral care to our community. We operate other programs such as Summer family activities and a bi-monthly Food Market and support Canadian Missionary offices for overseas and local missionary activities. We hold various outreach activities and events to invite the community into the church. As part of outreach we also oversee the 541 Eatery & Exchange Cafe in East Hamilton Ontario.
