What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Jun 30, 2024)

1 - CLIMATE CHANGE ADAPTATION AND MITIGATION: BUILDING COMMUNITY RESILIENCE TO CLIMATE CHANGE IN SENEGAL WEST AFRICA BY SUPPORTING CLIMATE-SMART AGRICULTURE AND INCREASING KNOWLEDGE ON FOREST PROTECTION. 2 - RECONCILIATION INITIATIVES WITH INDIGENOUS PARTNERS: SUPPORTING INDIGENOUS AND NON-INDIGENOUS YOUTH TO ADDRESS RECONCILIATION AND DECOLONIZATION ISSUES THROUGH SERVICE-LEARNING PROJECTS THAT ENHANCE LOCAL COMMUNITIES ACROSS CANADA. 3 - HABITAT CONSERVATION IN AFRICA: PROTECTING GREAT APES AND THEIR HABITAT THROUGH CONSERVATION PROGRAMS WHICH ALSO HELP COMMUNITIES TO IMPROVE THEIR STANDARDS OF LIVING AND SUPPORT CHIMPANZEE SANCTUARIES THAT PROVIDE SAFE HAVEN TO ORPHANED CHIMPANZEES. 4 - ROOTS & SHOOTS PROGRAM: ENVIRONMENTAL AND HUMANITARIAN EDUCATION PROGRAM INSPIRING YOUNG CANADIANS TO TAKE ACTION TO MAKE THEIR COMMUNITIES BETTER PLACES THROUGH RESOURCES WORKSHOPS PARTNERSHIPS WITH OTHER ORGANIZATIONS AND AN ONLINE COMMUNITY FOR SHARING OF INFORMATION. 5 - PUBLIC EDUCATION/COMMUNICATION: RAISING AWARENESS OF ENVIRONMENTAL AND HUMANITARIAN ISSUES IN CANADA THROUGH COMMUNICATIONS PROGRAMS AND THROUGH A SERIES OF WORKSHOPS AND LECTURES BY JGI STAFF AND BY DR. JANE GOODALL.
1. Conservation and Sustainable Management of Coastal and Marine Ecosystems in Kenya and Tanzania. 2. Femmes en Action in the Democratic Republic of Congo.
