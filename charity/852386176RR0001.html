What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

THE FOUNDATION MAKES GRANTS TO QUALIFIED CHARITABLE ORGANIZATIONS INVOLVED IN THE PROVISION OF EDUCATION IN THE AREAS OF NUTRITION EXERCISE HEALTH AND WELLNESS LIFE SKILLS TRAINING AND COUNSELLING AND MENTORING TROUBLED YOUTH TO ASSIST IN REHABILITATION AND DEVELOPMENT. THE FOUNDATION ALSO MAKES GRANTS TO QUALIFIED CHARITABLE ORGANIZATIONS INVOLVED IN PROGRAMS THAT OFFER SUPPORT AND ASSISTANCE TO PATIENTS AND THEIR FAMILIES FACED WITH A LIFE ALTERING DIAGNOSIS WITH A FOCUS ON IMPROVING QUALITY OF LIFE. THE PRIMARY GOAL OF THE FOUNDATION IS TO PROVIDE ASSISTANCE IN IMPROVING QUALITY OF LIFE AND AN ENHANCED LIFE EXPERIENCE FOR YOUNG PEOPLE AS WELL AS THOSE FACED WITH A LIFE ALTERING DIAGNOSIS. IN 2014 THE FOUNDATION ADDED GRANTS TO QUALIFIED CHARITABLE ORGANIZATIONS THAT PROVIDE COUNSELING SUPPORT AND SPECIFIC PROGRAMS AIMED AT BETTER EQUIPPING INDIVIDUALS TO CONTRIBUTE POSITIVELY TO THEIR FAMILY COMMUNITY AND/OR SOCIETY. THE FOCUS IS ON IMPROVED SKILL SETS TO BETTER EQUIP INDIVIDUALS TO PARTICIPATE IN THEIR COMMUNITIES AND A MEANINGFUL WAY.

What this page can't tell you

  • Anything after Dec 31, 2024, its newest return in the published data.
  • Who its directors are. The published data does not include them.
