What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

Our vision is a sustainable future for Canada where young people and communities thrive. Our mission is to empower young people and Veterans to build resilient future-ready communities. YOUTH PROGRAMS Our programs focus on purposeful employment for youth facing barriers which includes skill-building workshops to gain pre-employment skills like problem-solving professional communication digital literacy networking or interviewing; career exploration programs to learn about sector specific employment opportunities while developing the networks and skills needed for participants to evolve in their career journeys; introductory career exploration sessions focusing on specific sectors like technology featuring industry experts and a question-and-answer session; and hands-on programs to receive training meet potential future employers and increase the chance of employment. VETERAN PROGRAMS AND INITIATIVES Our programs and initiatives empower Veterans and transitioning military to explore a civilian mission after service including self-employment and entrepreneurship. Programs help them with support systems resources and education. Program participants access a number of programs including info sessions workshops mentoring cohort-based boot camps BuyVeteran Community workshops Discover: Green Careers and sustainable business planning resources that will open new opportunities to a second career.
