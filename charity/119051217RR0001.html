What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended May 31, 2024)

MAKES QUALIFYING DISBURSEMENTS PRIMARILY IN RESPECT OF WOMEN AND GIRLS: SOCIAL SERVICES INTERNATIONAL AID AND DEVELOPMENT; EDUCATION AND RESEARCH CULTURE AND ARTS RELIGION HEALTH AND COMMUNITY BENEFITS EQUALITY AND HUMAN RIGHTS. CONDUCTS A PROJECT TO INCREASE THE PRESENCE AND ACCESSIBILITY ON THE INTERNET OF THE HISTORY OF WOMEN AND GIRLS IN CANADA AND WORKS TO INCREASE THE INCLUSION OF WOMEN AND GIRLS IN CANADA'S HISTORY- AND RIGHTS-FOCUSED INSTITUTIONS. PARTICIPATES IN INITIATIVES RELATING TO SYSTEMIC DISCRIMINATION AGAINST AND THE EQUALITY OF WOMEN AND GIRLS.
