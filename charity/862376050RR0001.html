What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

TO SUPPORT THE CHARITABLE WORKS OF THE CANADIAN SERRA CLUBS' MEMBERS BY PROVIDING EDUCATIONAL MATERIALS ETC. FOR THE GENERAL PUBLIC AND FINANCIAL SUPPORT FOR INDIVIDUALS PERSUING A CATHOLIC RELIGIOUS VOCATION AND THE CATHOLIC INSTITUTIONS THAT EDUCATE THEM. TO FOSTER AND PROMOTE VOCATIONS TO THE MINISTERIAL PRIESTHOOD AND RELIGIOUS LIFE IN THE CATHOLIC CHURCH. WE ARE CONTINUING ON WITH OUR FUNDRAISING GALA DINNER AND LUNCHEON. THIS PROGRAM IS TO HONOR THE CURRENT SEMINARIANS WHO ARE STUDYING IN THE ONTARIO SEMINARIES AND WILL BE ORDAINED AS ROMAN CATHOLIC PRIESTS IN THE CURRENT YEAR FOR THE VARIOUS DIOCESES IN CANADA.
