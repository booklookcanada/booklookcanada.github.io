What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Aug 31, 2024)

NEXT CANADA'S PROGRAMS ARE COMPRISED OF TWO MAJOR COMPONENTS FOR UNDERGRADUATE UNIVERSITY STUDENTS: THE ENTREPRENEURSHIP INSTITUTE AND THE HANDS-ON VENTURE DEVELOPMENT COMPONENT. THE ENTREPRENEURSHIP INSTITUTE CONSISTS OF ROUGHLY 150 HOURS OF IN-CLASS INSTRUCTION TAUGHT BY INTERNATIONALLY ACCLAIMED FACULTY AND CANADIAN BUSINESS LEADERS DIRECTED BY ONE OF THE ROTMON SCHOOL'S TOP RATED PROFESSORS. UPON ACCEPTANCE INTO THE PROGRAM IN JANUARY ENTREPRENEUR- STUDENTS IDENTIFY COFOUNDERS WITH WHOM TO BUILD THEIR BUSINESSES AND IDENTIFY THE IDEA(S) ON WHICH THEIR BUSINESSES WILL BE BUILT. FROM DECEMBER TO APRIL WHILE COMPLETING THEIR UNDERGRADUATE STUDIES STUDENTS BUILD THEIR BUSINESSES REMOTELY WITH INPUT FROM THE NEXT CANADA AND THEIR DEDICATED MENTORS. BEGINNING IN MAY THE RELOCATE TO TORONTO TO BEGIN THE ENTREPRENEURSHIP INSTITUTE COMPONENT OF THE PROGRAM WHILE CONTINUING TO BUILD THEIR BUSINESS. THE PROGRAM CULMINATES IN AUGUST AT VENTURE DAY WHERE ENTREPRENEUR-STUDENTS PRESENT TO INVESTORS AND BUSINESS LEADERS ACROSS CANADA. NEXT CANADA ALSO CONDUCTS A NEXT FOUNDERS ENTREPRENEURS PROGRAM AND A NEXT ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE ENTREPRENEURS PROGRAM (NEXT AI).
