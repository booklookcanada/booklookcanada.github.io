Who runs it — the people it pays

It reported 55 full-time positions and 28 part-time employees (year ended Dec 31, 2024). Total pay for all its positions: $3,526,487, as filed. The highest pay band it reported was “$120,000–$159,999”. 2 employees were reported in that band. Pay is reported in bands — ranges, not exact salaries. The top band is the highest range with anyone in it: it is not one person's salary, and it is not what everyone there is paid.

What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

Provide animal control and shelter operations for the cities of Kitchener Waterloo and Stratford. Provide educational seminars at the shelter in schools and other community centers. Educational literature distributed throughout the region and cities.
