What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

Health Programs: Deliver medical care and health education to underserved communities. Education Programs: Enhance literacy and education through school construction scholarships teachers training and educational materials. Emergency Relief: Provide food shelter medical aid and essential supplies to communities affected by natural disasters and crises globally. Water and Sanitation: Improve access to clean water and sanitation by building wells installing purification systems and promoting hygiene education. Child Sponsorship Program: Support children's holistic development with education healthcare and nutrition. Food Distribution: Combat hunger and food insecurity through food distribution in Canada and worldwide. Shelter and Housing: Offer temporary housing essential supplies and rebuilding efforts for permanent housing solutions for displaced families.
