What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

We organize & convene accessible weekly public services of worship & prayer provide religious instruction and interpretation for all ages and minister to men women youth and children on an ongoing basis with pastoral and compassionate care for the sick infirm and grieving. We support social and community outreach programs with accessible gathering space and fund United Church of Canada missions and other local and regional mission causes through the dedicated year-round efforts of many volunteers.
