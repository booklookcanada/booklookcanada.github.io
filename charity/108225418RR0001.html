What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

PROMOTION AND PROTECTION OF HEALTH INCLUDING FIRST AID AND INFORMATION SERVICES; DAYCARE AND AFTER SCHOOL CARE. SUMMER CAMPS DID NOT RUN DUE TO COVID19. WE ARE A CHARITY DEDICATED TO STRENGTHENING THE FOUNDATION OF COMMUNITIES. WE DO THIS BY NURTURING THE POTENTIAL OF CHILDREN TEENS AND YOUNG ADULTS; PROMOTING HEALTHY LIVING; AND FOSTERING SOCIAL RESPONSIBILITY. OUR FOCUS ON INCLUSIVENESS AND ACCESSIBILITY MEANS WE SERVE PEOPLE OF ALL AGES BACKGROUNDS AND ABILITIES THROUGH ALL STAGES OF LIFE. THROUGH YMCA FINANCIAL ASSISTANCE PROGRAMS THE YMCA IS ACCESSIBLE TO ALL. OUR CORE OFFERINGS INCLUDE CHILDCARE CAMPING AQUATICS HEALTH & FITNESS VOLUNTEER DEVELOPMENT GLOBAL INITIATIVES COMMUNITY INITIATIVES AND OPPORTUNITIES TO GIVE.
