What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Oct 31, 2024)

The Foundation receives submissions from Arbor Memorial Inc's (AMI) funeral homes and cemeteries across Canada for prospective worthy causes. An internal committee comprised of AMI employees meets twice a year to review the submissions and determine the charitable organizations they want to support.
