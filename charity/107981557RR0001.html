What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

WEEKLY IN PERSON & ONLINE WORSHIP SERVICE IN PERSON SUNDAY SCHOOL WEEKLY COFFEE & FELLOWSHIP HOUR UNITED CHURCH MISSION & SERVICE ORGANIZATION RENT SPACE TO THE BACKDOOR MISSION FOR THE RELIEF OF POVERTY DURHAM WOODWORKERS CLUB & MURRAY MCKINNON FOUNDATION ATTENDANCE CENTRE.
In February 2024 the SSUC Trustees turned over the church property to the East Central Ontario Regional Council to be managed by Kindred Works. In April 2024 the Simcoe St. congregation held its final worship service in Simcoe St. Church and then merged/amalgamated with Kingsview United Church (BN118982495RR0001). Going forward the amalgamated congregation will operate as Kingsview United Church using the Kingsview BN (118982495). The Simcoe St. United Church BN (107981557) should be retired.
