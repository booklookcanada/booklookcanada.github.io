What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

OCPSA assists athletes with cerebral palsy and related physical disabilities develop through sport from grass roots to high performance. OCPSA provides recreational and competitive opportunities regionally provincially and nationally. OCPSA facilitates child and youth participation. OCPSA trains and assists in the education and professional development of coaches and sport officials involved in association programs. OCPSA provides a fair and accessible environment for participation in sport by individuals with cerebral palsy and related disabilities.
