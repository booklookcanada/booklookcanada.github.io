What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

Imagine Canada is a national charitable organization whose cause is Canada's charities and nonprofits. Its four Strategic Directions are to: energize the sector's voices; ensure the sector's relevance; influence the sector's environment; and elevate the sector's excellence. It conducts research accredits organizations evaluates and comments on public policy matters and provides services and information to charities and others. Imagine Canada est un organisme de bienfaisance national dont la mission est de faire la promotion des organismes de bienfaisance et sans but lucratif au Canada. Il s’est doté de quatre orientations stratégiques : dynamiser la voix maintenir la pertinence influencer l’environnement et faire progresser l’excellence du secteur. Il produit des rapports de recherche confère l’agrément aux organismes qualifiés évalue et commente des enjeux de politiques publiques et offre des services et informations aux organismes caritatifs et au grand public.
