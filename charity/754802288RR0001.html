Who runs it — the people it pays

It reported 15 full-time positions (year ended Dec 31, 2024). Total pay for all its positions: $996,721, as filed. The highest pay band it reported was “$200,000–$249,999”. 1 employee was reported in that band. Pay is reported in bands — ranges, not exact salaries. The top band is the highest range with anyone in it: it is not one person's salary, and it is not what everyone there is paid.

What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

THE OBJECTIVES OF THE CANADIAN CIVIL LIBERTIES ASSOCIATION ARE TO IMPROVE THE MORAL OR ETHICAL DEVELOPMENT OF THE COMMUNITY BY PROMOTING RESPECT FOR FUNDAMENTAL HUMAN RIGHTS AND CIVIL LIBERTIES IN ACCORDANCE WITH THE LAWS OF CANADA AND INTERNATIONALLY TO UPHOLD HUMAN RIGHTS AND CIVIL LIBERTIES AND TO ASSIST IN THE ADMINISTRATION AND ENFORCEMENT OF RELATED LAWS IN CANADA AND INTERNATIONALLY BY DEFENDING EXTENDING AND FOSTERING THE RECOGNITION OF THESE RIGHTS AND LIBERTIES
