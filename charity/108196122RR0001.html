What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

We hold weekly public services for prayer. We offer religious education to adults and children. We donate funds to denominational head office for missionary activities. We donate funds to other local charities. We provide community oriented space for public use host a variety of non-denominational events throughout the year and provide support to other local community groups by way of volunteers supplies and funds. We have a certified commercial kitchen for use by the church and other community groups. We have a community electronic message sign used by the church as well as other charitable groups in the community.
