What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

William Osler Health System Foundation (Osler Foundation) provides funding to William Osler Health System (Osler) a leading hospital system serving more than 1.3 million people in Brampton North Etobicoke Caledon and surrounding communities. Osler Foundation works with the community to raise funds to support Osler’s capital equipment education research and expansion needs.
