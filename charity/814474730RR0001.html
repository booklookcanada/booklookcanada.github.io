What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Jun 30, 2024)

The Help Our Students Program provides financial support to hard-working students living in difficult financial circumstances to help them graduate high school. The Program gives a monthly stipend directly into the student's bank account. The students are selected by school officials. The Board of Directors is a volunteer Board and the Directors pay all administrative expenses.
