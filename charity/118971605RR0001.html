What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Aug 31, 2024)

TO PROMOTE MUSICAL STUDY AND COMPETITION IN OUR SMALL COMMUNITY AND SURROUNDING AREA.TO ENCOURAGE CHILDREN TO CONTINUE THEIR MUSICAL STUDIES TO COMPETE AND TO POSITIVELY REINFORCE AND REWARD THEIR EFFORT TIME AND DEDICATION TO MUSIC STUDY AND PERFORMANCE. THE IROQUOIS FALLS MUSIC FESTIVAL COMMITTEE ORGANIZES A FIVE DAY PROGRAM OF COMPETITION WITH RCM ADJUDICATORS PRESIDING. AND AWARDS CEREMONY ON THE SUNDAY FOLLOWING THE FESTIVAL OFFERS THE STUDENTS THE OPPORTUNITY TO PERFORM AWARD WINNING SELECTIONS AND ALSO TO RECEIVE SCHOLARSHIPS/AWARDS IN THE PRESENCE OF THEIR PEERS AND COMMUNITY. AREAS OF COMPETITION INCLUDE PIANO VIOLIN VOICE INSTRUMENTAL CHOIR BAND RHYTHM BAND AS WELL AS DUET/TRIO CLASSES IN THE ABOVE MENTIONED. THE FESTIVAL ENCOURAGES PARTICIPATION OF MUSIC STUDENTS AND THEIR TEACHERS AND ALSO BY THE PARTICIPATION AND SUPPORT OF ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY SCHOOLS. WE RELY ON DONATIONS FROM OUR COMMUNITY TO SUPPORT THIS IMPORTANT COMPETITION. WE CONSIDER OUR FESTIVAL A GREAT SUCCESS SINCE MORE STUDENTS ARE PARTICIPATING AND PURSUING MUSICAL STUDIES AS A PAST TIME AND ALSO A CAREER. THERE WAS NO MUSIC FESTIVAL AGAIN IN 2024 THE COMMITTEE WAS NOT ABLE TO RECRUIT VOLUNTEER TO RESTART THE FESTIVAL FALLOWING THE COVID YEAR.
