What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Jun 30, 2024)

Founded in 1906 Orpheus Musical Theatre Society is Canada's longest-running organization of its kind. Based in Ottawa Orpheus is a leading community theatre company that engages more than 400 volunteers annually - performers technicians set builders and production staff - providing structured opportunities for skills development community building and lifelong learning in the performing arts. Orpheus produces three large-scale musical theatre productions delivers year-round programming that includes training classes professional seminars community performance ensembles and participation in regional festivals.
Orpheus is committed to equitable access to high-quality arts education. Following the COVID-19 shutdown the organization launched its Orpheus Schoolhouse program for youth aged 6 to 18. Delivered by established Ottawa-based performing arts professionals the program operates on a pay-what-you-can/pay-what-you-want model to reduce financial barriers to participation. On average approximately 30% of the 175 participants have paid nothing to attend.
