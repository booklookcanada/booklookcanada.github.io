What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

We provide information and community resources to those affected by multiple sclerosis (MS). We provide assistance for clients to maintain their independence within their community. We facilitate education and support group meetings. We fund research projects at universities and hospitals to find ways to prevent MS new treatments and discover a cure. We educate all levels of government on the specific issues and needs of Canadians living with MS.
