What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

Recruit train and send missionaries teachers medical personnel technicians and other personnel who are qualified to support and/or establish and develop national churches. Provide pastoral care to immigrant and non-immigrants in Canada. Receive in Canada and send to other Liebenzell Mission International ministry countries student volunteers for short term mission assignments for up to one year. Support Liebenzell Mission International programs in Canada.
No new programs in 2023.
