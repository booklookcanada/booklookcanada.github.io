What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

OUR MISSION IS TO PROVIDE SERVICES TO WOMEN YOUTH AND CHILDREN WHO EXPERIENCE VIOLENCE AND TO ULTIMATELY STOP THE VIOLENCE. WE PROVIDE THE FOLLOWING SERVICES TO ALLOW THE ORGANIZATION TO MEET THESE GOALS: EMERGENCY SHELTER: SHORT TERM SUPPORT AND ACCOMMODATION FOR ABUSED WOMEN AND THEIR CHILDREN SECOND STAGE TRANSITIONAL HOUSING: TRANSITIONAL SUPPORT FOR WOMEN YOUTH & CHILDREN ESTABLISHING THEMSELVES INDEPENDENTLY IN THE COMMUNITY FAMILY COURT: PROVIDES DIRECT SUPPORT TO VICTIMS OF DOMESTIC VIOLENCE WHO ARE INVOLVED IN FAMILY COURT PROCESS
