What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Aug 31, 2024)

The London Children's Museum (LRCM) provides safe inclusive accessible and educational experiential learning through play for children and their adults through immersive exhibits collections and programs. The LRCM supports schools and students by providing in class and on site educational programs aligned with the Ontario Curriculum. The LRCM offers engaging inclusive summer and PA day camps as well as birthday parties facility rentals outreaches and events.
