What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

The Quinte Community and Housing First Partnership addresses all of the major outcomes of the Belleville Community Plan and Reaching Home Priorities and as such is viewed as an important player in an integrated co-ordinated community approach to homelessness. All-Together is the lead agency for the Partnership and local Housing First champion. Our efforts to reduce homelessness through our own housing projects have been recognized in Parliament by our former local MP. Our Partnership model was awarded a Seed Grant in 2018 by the Ontario Trillium Foundation recognizing our efforts to build better lives for vulnerable individuals (including those who were homeless or at risk of homelessness) by connecting them to affordable housing and community services. December 2019 our Partnership was awarded funding by the Catherine Donnelly Foundation for its unique partnership with private market landlords to create more affordable housing and we are grateful for the support we have received from United Way-HPE and Reaching Home in the past. Successfully we raised more than $350 000 to renovate a donated property at 51 Victoria Avenue. The completed project includes a mix of affordable and transitional units. The Victoria Avenue housing project is now a part of the Quinte Community and Housing First Partnership.
24 Starling Street in Belleville is another successful project. It is an 18-unit affordable housing building created through the Affordable Housing Initiative in agreement with Hastings County in 2009. The owner of Springale Mr Phil Spry is the President of MAPS Development Corporation responsible for the development of 52 units of affordable housing at 450 Sidney Street Belleville. Starling Street will be brought under the umbrella of the Partnership in 2023. All-Together Affordable Housing Corporation and Springale Development have formed a Joint Venture Agreement for the purpose of creating more affordable housing projects in Belleville Quinte West and Hastings County. Our current Joint Venture housing projects include: 111 Great St. James (GSJ) at the end of Great Saint James Street A multi-residential dwelling GSJ is a mix of affordable and market rent units including 3 2. and 1 bedroom units. The 3 bedroom units are leased by the Enrichment Centre as a new home for its Murney Street transitional home. The building features a Community Hub office space and a huddle room for meetings and video conferencing. The completed project in May 2022 is another part of the Quinte Community and Housing First Partnership (QCHP). The next project Fred's Place (Deloro) is located at 1 Private Road Deloro Fred’s Place will be a new multi-residential apartment building that will open in December 2025 in the hamlet of Deloro. Following a similar model to Sidney Place and Great St James Fred’s Place is located in an established residential neighbourhood near town. The property is co-owned by All-Together Housing and Springale Development and building will begin in early 2025 with completion scheduled for December 2025. Fred’s Place will offer 15 one bedroom units for low-income seniors as well as a Community Hub laundry room office universal washroom and a storage shed
