What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

1. The organization hosts an annual Human Rights Film Festival for the public. 2. The organization operates an award-winning arts and social justice training program (iAM) offered to 300+ equity-seeking youth in the GTA each year. 3. Year-Round Programming: JAYU offers showcase events throughout the year including documentary screenings spoken word poetry slams speakers events exhibitions workshops and more. 4. iAM Action is a complementary stream of programming to iAM that provides professional development and leadership training in areas such as CV/portfolio development grant writing entrepreneurship financial literacy community outreach mentorship training and anti-oppression training from an equity lens. 5. iAM Employment is a supplement that has grown out of our annual iAM programs finding ways to connect participants developing artistic skills to paid opportunities.
