What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

Community Living Toronto provides a variety of innovative supports to children youth and adults with an intellectual disability and their families. Our person-centered services and supports aim to foster natural community networks and engagements that promote independence and personal growth. Our services include employment support and training early childhood support respite supported independent and group living community and family support and recreation. Additionally we also have specialized homes for children for those who have been diagnosed with Autism Spectrum Disorder and present with unique and complex needs. Community Living Toronto’s services are categorized into three main streams: Specialized Services Community Participation Supports and Respite as well as Supported Living.
