What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

Individual and family counselling for Torontonians with epilepsy and their families. Epilepsy education workshops newsletters website and library resources. Employment counselling and job development for adults with epilepsy. Support groups for adults youth and children with epilepsy and their families. Recreation programs for adults and youth with epilepsy. Life skills and housing support program for adults with epilepsy. Targeted epilepsy education and outreach in communities of diversity. Epilepsy education in schools. Epilepsy awareness to the public at large. CBT groups and psychotherapy for individuals with functional seizures.
