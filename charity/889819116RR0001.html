What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

THRIVE - IS AN INNOVATIVE ADULT DAY PROGRAM FOR INDIVIDUALS 18+ WITH DEVELOPMENTAL DISABILITIES. EMPLOYMENT SERVICES - SUPPORTS INDIVIDUALS IN BUILDING SKILLS TO ENSURE JOB READINESS TO PARTICIPATE IN THE LABOUR MARKET. SPORTS & RECREATION - PROMOTES DEVELOPMENT OF PHYSICAL LITERACY AND PROVIDES OPPORTUNITIES FOR INDIVIDUALS OF ALL ABILITIES TO BE ACTIVE. ACADEMY FOR STUDENT ATHLETE DEVELOPMENT - DELIVERS HIGH PERFORMANCE STUDENT PROGRAMS FOR ABLE-BODIED PARALYMPIC OLYMPIC AND SPECIAL OLYMPIC PATHWAYS. MIXED ABILITY SPORTS - INVOLVES EDUCATING COACHES TO ENGAGE EVERYONE INCLUDING INDIVIDUALS WITH DISABILITIES INTO MAINSTREAM SPORTS. YOU.ME.WE IS A VIRTUAL PROGRAM THAT SUPPORTS PARTICIPATION IN PHYSICAL ACTIVITY FOR CHILDREN AND YOUTH IN CHILDREN'S HOSPITALS TREATMENT CENTRES AND SCHOOLS. THERAPEUTIC RECREATION - USES COMMUNITY AND ACTIVITY BASED BASED PROGRAMMING TO IMPROVE PHYSICAL AND MENTAL HEALTH AS WELL AS FUNCTIONAL CAPACITY FOR INDIVIDUALS WITH DISABILITIES. POST REHABILITATION AND HEALTH MAINTENANCE - SUPPORTS INDIVIDUALS TRANSITIONING FROM REHABILITATION TREATMENT (STROKE MS ABI HEART ATTACK ANGINA COPD ETC.) INTO COMMUNITY EXERCISE PROGRAMS. ACCESSIBILITY CONSULTING (LEAD) - HELPS ORGANIZATIONS EMBED ACCESSIBILITY AND INCLUSION STRATEGIES IN MEETING THE NEEDS OF PEOPLE WITH DISABILITIES.
