What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

Collect and distribute funds to our local provincial and national Saint Vincent de Paul organizations to support their charitable programs. Respond to specific request for financial relief on an ask basis. Provide administrative and financial support to the Saint Vincent de Paul local conferences under the umbrella of Halton Particular Council. Monthly training is provided to assist conference presidents with cyclical activities such as recruitment fundraising budgeting neighbors' programs and member recognition.
