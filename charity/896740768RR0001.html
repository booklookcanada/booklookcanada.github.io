What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

THE PREGNANCY HELP CENTRE OF DURHAM PROVIDES SUPPORT AND EDUCATION TO YOUNG FAMILIES IN DISTRESS DURE TO PREGNANCY. WE PROVIDE PRENATAL INFORMATION AND PROVIDE ONGOING SUPPORT FOR THOSE WHO FIND THEMSELVES PREGNANT AND CHOOSE TO CARRY TO TERM. WE PROVIDE ASSISTANCE IN FINANCIALLY STRAINED SITUATIONS WITH MENTERIAL ASSISTANCE WITH INFANT FOOD CLOTHING BABY EQUIPMENT. WE ALSO PROVIDE INFORMATION TO YOUTH REGARDING THE IMPLICATIONS OF EARLY SEXUAL INVOLVEMENT STIS PREGNANCY OPTIONS AND RELATIONSHIP DEVELOPMENT. THERE ARE TWO SITES OPEN TO SERVE THE FAMILIES OF DURHAM REGION.
WE ARE IN THE PROCESS OF DEVELOPING THE WONDERFULLY MADE PROGRAM - TARGETED AT GRADES 4-8 TO CELEBRATE THE WONDER OF HUMAN DEVELOPMENT.
