What it says it does

1. TO PROTECT AND PROMOTE ANIMAL WELFARE BY RESCUING ABANDONED ABUSED SICK ELDERLY OR INJURED CATS. 2. TO PROVIDE FOR THE REHABILITATION FOSTERING AND ADOPTION OF SUCH CATS. 3. TO EDUCATE THE PUBLIC ABOUT THE PROPER CARE FOR CATS AND TO PROVIDE INFORMATION AIMED AT REDUCING THE NUMBER OF HOMELESS CATS.
