What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

Maternal Health Program This program supports women and girls in accessing essential maternal sexual and reproductive health services. Food Protection Program This program provides refugee families with monthly food supplies to ensure their nutrition and support their children's education. Each family receives between 50 to 75 kilos of food every month for a year. Additionally households receive over 100 kilos of basic nutritious food monthly. Educational Support for Girls This program provides financial assistance to help girls attend school and continue their education.
