What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

SHELTER-BASED PRIMARY CARE MEDICAL CLINICS ARE STAFFED PART-TIME BY A FAMILY DOCTOR A NURSE PRACTITIONER AND A COMMUNITY PARAMEDIC. WE SERVE PEOPLE WHO DO NOT HAVE A DOCTOR OR WHO CANNOT SEE THEIR DOCTOR. WE OFFER PREVENTATIVE SERVICES SUCH AS VACCINATIONS PAP TESTS BLOOD PRESSURE TREATMENT ETC. WE CONNECT PEOPLE WITH LOCAL FAMILY PRACTICES OR COMMUNITY HEALTH CENTERS SUCH AS QUEST AND CARE FOR THEM UNTIL THEY ARE ACCEPTED INTO CARE.
