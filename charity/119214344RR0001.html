What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Jun 30, 2024)

THEATRE PASSE MURAILLE (TPM) RUNS AN ANNUAL SEASON OF 6 TO 15 CONTEMPORARY THEATRE PRODUCTIONS. IN ADDITION TPM: - DEVELOPS AND SUPPORTS ARTISTS AND THE CREATION OF NEW PLAYS WHILE PROVIDING SPACE TO PRESENT THIS WORK TO THE PUBLIC - PRODUCES ALL NEW CANADIAN LIVE THEATRE AS WELL AS DIGITAL MEDIA PROGRAMMING - WORKS WITH ACTORS WRITERS DIRECTORS DESIGNERS STAGE MANAGERS AND OTHER ARTISTS - RUNS A MULTI-USE FACILITY DEDICATED TO THE ARTS - PROVIDES COMMUNITY BOX OFFICE SERVICES. - PROVIDES ACCESSIBILITY INITIATIVES TO AUDIENCES WITH ACCESSIBILITY NEEDS (ASL AD RELAXED PERFORMANCES IN-EAR TRANSLATION TOUCH TOURS CAPTIONING ETC.) - DEVELOPS AND PRODUCES ACCESSIBLE THEATRE AND SHARES KNOWLEDGE ON ACCESSIBILITY WITH ARTISTS AND THE ARTISTIC COMMUNITY - DEVELOPS AND PRODUCES COMMUNITY ENGAGEMENT PROJECTS WITH NEIGHBOURING PARTNERS
