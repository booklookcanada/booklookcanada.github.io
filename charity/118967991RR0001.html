1) HEALTH OUTREACH (HOW) - FOCUS OF PROGRAM IS TO ORGANIZE AND FACILITATE HEALTH PROMOTION ILLNESS PREVENTION AND FAMILY VIOLENCE WORKSHOPS AND SEMINARS 2) COURTWORKER - ASSIST INDIGENOUS PEOPLE EXPERIENCING CRIMINAL & FAMILY COURT MATTERS. . RESTORATIVE JUSTICE (RJ) - FOCUSES ON TAKING RESPONSIBILITY FOR ACTIONS HEALING AND IN ADDRESSING ROOT CAUSES OF CRIME RATHER THAN USING CONVENTIONAL COURT ORDERED PUNISHMENT APPROACHES.
