What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

THE SALVATION ARMY IS AN INTERNATIONAL ORGANIZATION DEDICATED TO SERVING GOD ANDHUMANITY. THE SALVATION ARMY ST. CATHARINES BOOTH CENTRE HAS TWO SEPERATE PROGRAMS TO SERVE MEN 18 + IN THE COMMUNITY. WE OFFER A 26 BED DORMITORY STYLE SHELTER TO MEN EXPERIENCING HOMELESSNESS. SERVICES INCLUDE MEALS CLOTHING ASSISTANCE LAUNDRY FACILITIES PASTORAL CARE AND ACCESS TO CASEWORKERS TO HELP WORK TOWARDS HOUSING GOALS AND REFERRAL SERVICES TO MEET THE NEEDS OF THE INDIVIDUAL. THE SALVATION ARMY COMMUNITY RESIDENTIAL FACILITY OFFERS 15 BED ACCOMMODATION FOR MEN 18 + WHO ARE RELEASED FROM FEDERAL INSTITUTIONS ON TEMPORAY ABSENCE DAY PAROLE OR STATUTORY RELEASE. THIS ALLOWS FOR REINTEGRATION INTO THE COMMUNITY. CASEWORKERS PROVIDE SUPPORT THROUGH THE REINTEGRATION PROCESS WITH CASE PLANS JOB SEARCH AND REFERRAL SERVICES FOR OVERALL WELLBEING.
