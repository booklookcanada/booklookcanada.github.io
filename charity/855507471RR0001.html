What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Aug 31, 2024)

TO PROVIDE AN EDUCATIONAL CENTRE AND HOUSE ORGANIZATIONS THAT ARE REGISTERED CHARITIES PURSUANT TO THE CANADIAN INCOME TAX ACT. TO PROVIDE A COMPREHENSIVE REMEDIAL PROGRAM FOR STUDENTS THAT FALL BEHIND IN THEIR STUDIES. ITS CURRICULUM WILL BE TAILORED TO THE ABILITY TO THE RESPECTIVE CHALLENGED STUDENT OR GROUP OF STUDENTS . THE GOAL IS TO EVENTUALLY MAINSTREAM THEM IN ALL AREAS OF STUDIES. TO PROVIDE AND SUBSIDIZE DAYCARE SERVICES. TO PROVISDE AND SUBSIDIZE NUTRITION PROGRAM.
