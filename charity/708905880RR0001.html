What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

THE SENTRE CURRENTLY RUNS 5 PROGRAMS NAMELY: 1. COMMUNITY KITCHEN - THE MISSION OF THIS PROJECT IS TO HELP RELIEVE FOOD INSECURITY WITHIN OUR COMMUNITY BY PROVIDING NUTRITIOUS MEALS TO MARGINALIZED INDIVIDUALS INCLUDING THOSE THAT ARE LOW INCOME OR LIVING IN POVERTY INDIVIDUALS IN GROUP HOMES OR SUPPORTIVE LIVING DUE TO MENTAL ILLNESS AND THE SENIOR AND ELDERLY POPULATIONS. 2. YOUTH IMPACT - THE GOAL IS TO IMPACT THE LIVES OF OUR YOUTH AGES 7-17 LIVING IN CRYSTAL BEACH AND THE SURROUNDING AREA BY PROVIDING A SAFE PLACE FOR THEM TO GROW PHYSICALLY SPIRITUALLY MENTALLY AND EMOTIONALLY. 3. SCHOOL OF MUSIC AND PERFORMING ARTS - THE SCHOOL OF MUSIC AND PERFORMING ARTS (SMPA) IS A SPECIAL PROGRAM THAT ALLOWS INDIVIDUALS OF ALL AGES TO EXPLORE DREAMS AND DESIRES THAT MAY HAVE BEEN UNDISCOVERED BEFORE. 4. MOMS SUPPORTING MOMS - MOMS SUPPORTING MOMS IS A FAITH-BASED SUPPORT GROUP FOR MOMS WITH YOUNG CHILDREN THAT DESIRES TO SEE MOMS SUPPORT AND HEALTHY IN ALL AREAS OF LIFE FOR THEMSELVES AND THEIR CHILDREN. 5. DROP-IN LUNCHEON - THE PROGRAM ENABLES PEOPLE TO: ENJOY A HEALTHY MEAL & FRIENDLY CONVERSATION
