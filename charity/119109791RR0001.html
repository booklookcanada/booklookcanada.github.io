What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

The Roman Catholic Episcopal Corporation for the Diocese of St. Catharines in Canada exists for the purpose of the advancement of the Roman Catholic faith with responsibility for 44 parishes. The Diocese provides daily worship services for prayer and spiritual growth that model Christ's teachings; teaching core values principles and beliefs of the Roman Catholic Church through adult and youth religious education programs preparing for and celebrating the sacraments of the Roman Catholic religion. Relieves poverty through community outreach and donations to other registered charities locally nationally and internationally. Teaches social consciousness and awareness by encouraging volunteerism and teaching parishioners how to meet societal needs through volunteer and charitable activities. Comforts the bereaved visiting the sick the elderly and the incarcerated through pastoral care teams. Promotes vocations to the priesthood and religious life.
