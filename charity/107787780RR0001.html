What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

Actively manage floodplains in accordance with the Conservation Authorities Act & Provincial Policy Statement meeting principles of prevention protection and emergency response. As well as Natural Heritage Plans as part of Municipal Planning Process (Official Plans Zoning By-Laws Land Development applications). Land use planning for development outside hazardous & ecologically significant areas. Land and Water programs provide acquisition management and rehabilitation of significant lands within our watershed ensuring long term watershed health habitat and recreation. Provide technical financial and project management to support landowners and local community groups. Environmental Education & Outreach for students and residents of our watershed.
