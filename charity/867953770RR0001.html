What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

As per our constitution to show forth New Testament purpose and patterns of local church to enable the assembling of ourselves together to have regular Bible study to have fellowship and to be faithful to His church. Activities include: Weekly church services bible studies home church groups Sunday school clubs for boys and girls youth group special activities for these groups counseling and visitation and Men's and Women's ministry. Other activities include benevolent support towards needs in the local community. The church supports charitable organizations in Canada Missionaries in Mexico Haiti and Liberia (involving mission trip teams).
