What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

OPERATE A RESEARCH AND ANALYTICS INSTITUTE FOR THE PURPOSE OF TRANSLATING DATA INTO EVIDENCE TO INFORM THE DEVELOPMENT OF PUBLIC POLICY INCLUDING WITHOUT LIMITATION IN THE AREAS OF HEALTH CARE AND THE SOCIAL DETERMINANTS OF HEALTH ALL WITH A VIEW TO IMPROVING THE LIVES OF PEOPLE. - UNDERTAKE RESEARCH AND STATISTICAL ANALYSIS AND COMMUNICATE THE RESULTS OF RESEARCH AND STATISTICAL ANALYSIS. - ORGANIZE AND HOST CONFERENCES SEMINARS AND FORUMS. - PROVIDE ADVANCED TRAINING FOR RESEARCHERS AND OTHER DATA-RELATED SUBJECT-MATTER EXPERTS. - ENCOURAGE COLLABORATION AMONGST RESEARCHERS. - ENCOURAGE COMMUNICATION BETWEEN RESEARCHERS AND GOVERNMENT DECISION-MAKERS.
