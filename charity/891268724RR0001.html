CANADA'S NATIONAL BALLET SCHOOL FOUNDATION RECIEVES MAINTAINS AND INVESTS FUNDS AND APPLIES ALL OR A PART OF THOSE FUNDS AND INCOME EARNED THEREON FOR THE BENEFIT OF CANADA'S NATIONAL BALLET SCHOOL. THE SCHOOL RUNS PROGRAMS TO TRAIN DANCE STUDENTS TEACHERS AND ACCOMPANISTS INCLUDING PROVIDING AN ACADEMIC EDUCATION AND RESIDENTIAL CARE.
