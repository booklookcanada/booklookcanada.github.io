What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

Gallery TPW programs support Canadian and international artists and educate the public about the vital role that still photographs and moving images play in contemporary culture. We present up to five exhibitions per year at Gallery TPW that explore the exchange between photography culture new technologies and time-based art. We maintain a content-rich website presenting current information about contemporary photo and media-based art as well as an archive of essays and images dating from 1976 to the present. We commission and publish essays to provide critical context for each exhibition. We hold public events such as artist talks lectures and panel discussions. We provide training and mentoring for youth who are pursuing careers in the cultural sector through co-op placements and paid internships and specialized programs.
