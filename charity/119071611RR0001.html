What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

THE INITIATIVE AIMS TO PROMOTE THE TEACHINGS OF BUDDISM WITH THE NATIONAL CAPITAL REGION FOSTERING A DEEPER UNDERSTANDING OF BUDDHIST PHILOSOPHY AND PRACTICES. IT SEEKS TO PROVIDE RELIGIOUS EDUCATIONAL INSTRUMENT HELPING INDIVIDUALS TO EXPLORE AND CONNECT WITH THE BUDDHIST FAITH WHILE ALSO CONTRIBUTING TO THE BROADER WELL-BEING OF SOCIETY.
PROGRAM TITLE: COMMUNITY SUPPORT AND WELLBEING INITIATIVE OBJECTIVE: TO MEET THE CULTURAL SOCIAL AND CHARITABLE NEEDS OF THE COMMUNITY BY OFFERING ESSENTIAL SERVICES AND FOSTERING POSITIVE VALUES THAT PROMOTE UNITY PEACE AND SOCIAL COHESION. CORE SERVICES: CULTURAL AND SOCIAL SUPPORT: PROVIDE ASSISTANCE TO INDIVIDUALS AND FAMILIES IN FULFILLING THEIR CULTURAL AND SOCIAL NEEDS. ORGANIZE COMMUNITY EVENTS CULTURAL PROGRAMS AND ACTIVITIES THAT ENCOURAGE SOCIAL INCLUSION AND MUTUAL SUPPORT. CHARITABLE SERVICES: FACILITATE CHARITABLE INITIATIVES THAT AIM TO ALLEVIATE HARDSHIP AND PROVIDE RESOURCES TO UNDERSERVED INDIVIDUALS OFFER FINANCIAL MATERIAL AND EMOTIONAL SUPPORT TO THOSE IN NEED PARTICULARLY IN TIMES OF CRISIS OR LOSS. FUNERAL AND MEMORIAL SERVICES: PROVIDE DIGNIFIED AND RESPECTFUL FUNERAL MEMORIAL AND BURIAL SERVICES FOR INDIVIDUALS IN THE COMMUNITY. OFFER GRIEF COUNSELING AND SUPPORT FOR FAMILIES COPING WITH THE LOSS OF LOVED ONES. WEDDING AND BLESSING SERVICES: ASSIST WITH WEDDING CEREMONIES INCLUDING COORDINATION PREPARATION AND EXECUTION ENSURING THAT THE CEREMONY IS CULTURALLY AND SOCIALLY MEANINGFUL. OFFER BLESSING SERVICES FOR VARIOUS LIFE EVENTS INCLUDING BIRTHS HOUSEWARMINGS AND OTHER SIGNIFICANT MILESTONES. VALUES PROMOTED: PEACE: FOSTER A PEACEFUL ENVIRONMENT BY PROMOTING CONFLICT RESOLUTION AND OPEN DIALOGUE WITHIN THE COMMUNITY. MORALITY: UPHOLD ETHICAL STANDARDS BY ENCOURAGING INTEGRITY HONESTY AND RESPECT FOR ALL INDIVIDUALS. LOVING KINDNESS AND COMPASSION
