2ND CHANCE YOUTH CENTRE - PROVIDING BREAKFAST AND LUNCH FOOD PERSONAL HYGIENE SUPPLIES LAUNDRY FACILITIES SHOWERS ETC. ON A WEEKDAY BASIS TO HOMELESS AND STREET YOUTH IN THE DURHAM REGION. WELLNESS - PROGRAMMING FOCUSSED ON HEALTHY EATING AND ACTIVE LIVING FOR HOMELESS AND STREET YOUTH. THE REC. ROOM - A WEEKDAY EVENING DROP-IN PROGRAM FOR HOMELESS AND STREET YOUTH. FAMILY NIGHT - A DINNER PROGRAM ON FRIDAY NIGHTS FOR HOMELESS AND STREET INDIVIDUALS WILDERNESS ROCKS - AN OUTDOORS/ADVENTURE CMAPING PROGRAM FOR HOMELESS YOUTH. NEW LEAF CATERING - USING A HOUSING FIRST APPROACH THE REFUGE WORKS WITH ELIGIBLE YOUTH TO HOUSE THEM AND USING ICM MAINTAIN THEIR HOUSING. EDUCATIONAL PROGRAMMING - WORKING WITH LOCAL AREA SCHOOLS AND COMMUNITY GROUPS - PROVIDE A LIFE IMPACTING EDUCATIONAL COUNSELLING - FORMAL AND INFORMAL COUNSELLING SESSIONS ON A DROP-IN BASIS FOR HOMELESS YOUTH. NEW LEAF CATERING - A SOCIAL ENTERPRISE THAT WORKS WITH HOMELESS YOUTH PROVIDING CATERING SERVICES TO COMMUNITY CLIENTS.
