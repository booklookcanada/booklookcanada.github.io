What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

Runnymede United Church is an active Toronto congregation of the United Church of Canada. We conduct regular worship services provide Christian bible and book study programmes have a youth and Sunday School programme offer weekly support programmes to both church members and neighbours and provide a number of community centred and outreach activities. Church services are conducted in-person and via live streaming in both our Runnymede campus and our Windermere campus (see below). Windermere United Church (BN 1192 99535 RR0001) merged INTO Runnymede United Church on 1 June 2023. This information was filed with the CRA and confirmed on 17 April 2024 in a letter with subject 'Amalgamation' and Reference Number CH233330818530. Runnymede United Church is the surviving organization.
Runnymede United Church has built out a Food Community Outreach program which includes a monthly Communiity Meal weekly preparation of soup meals for the Bloor West Food Bank and snacks for a local elementary school.
