What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

A PLACE CALLED HOME OPERATES 1 19-BED HOSTEL FOR HOMELESS SINGLE ADULTS COUPLES & FAMILIES WIITH CHILDREN. SERVICES INCLUDE 24-HOUR STAFFING & SUPPORT; ADVOCACY & REFERRAL SERVICES; MEALS LAUNDRY & SHOWER FACILITIES. STAFF ASSISTS EACH CLIENT DEVELOP A PERSONAL PLAN OF ACTION TO ASSIST THEM FIND SAFE & AFFORDABLE PERMANENT HOUSING; HOPEFULLY ENDING A POSSIBLE CYCLE OF HOMELESSNESS. IN ADDTION APCH HAS A NUMBER OF PROGRAMS WHICH PREVENT HOMELESSNESS. TRANSITIONAL HOUSING TO 5 TO 6 HIGH-NEEDS CLIENTS AT AN ADJACENT BUILDING TO THE MAIN SHELTER.
