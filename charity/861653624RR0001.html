What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Aug 31, 2024)

The Catherine Donnelly Foundation allocates its funding to a combination of programs and projects in its priority areas of Environment Housing and Civic Engagement for Social Change. It aims to promote joint action building on one another’s strengths constituencies and common experiences in pursuit of its objectives. The Catherine Donnelly Foundation supports projects that promote active citizenship democracy and personal and social transformation. The Catherine Donnelly Foundation works to accelerate the transition to a post-carbon world. The Foundation models this commitment by granting funds for the protection and preservation of the environment and through its impact investing. The Catherine Donnelly Foundation applies funds for the provision of adequate and affordable housing to persons of low income. It collaborates with organizations working to alleviate and end homelessness by addressing a lack of affordable housing through innovative and collaborative strategies and solutions.
