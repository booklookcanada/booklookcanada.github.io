What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

St. Matthew's House is a multiservice innovative and forward-thinking non-profit agency located in Hamilton Ontario Canada. A frontline organization committed to lifting up the most vulnerable people among us - providing essential support to meet the needs of families and children and vulnerable seniors 55+ living in wards 1-4 in Hamilton by connecting them to food housing healthcare childcare and financial security. Among the programs we provide are seniors support services housing focused street outreach deeply affordable housing holiday relief for families and seniors and licensed child care and before and after school care.
