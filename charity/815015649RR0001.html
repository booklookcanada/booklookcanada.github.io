Who runs it — the people it pays

It reported 2 full-time positions and 1 part-time employees (year ended Dec 31, 2024). Total pay for all its positions: $164,750, as filed. The highest pay band it reported was “$40,000–$79,999”. 2 employees were reported in that band. Pay is reported in bands — ranges, not exact salaries. The top band is the highest range with anyone in it: it is not one person's salary, and it is not what everyone there is paid.

What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

DRESS FOR SUCCESS KINGSTON IS A NON-PROFIT ORGANIZATION DEDICATED TO PROVIDING SUPPORT GUIDANCE AND MENTORSHIP TO WOMEN IN NEED IN THE KINGSTON AND SURROUNDING COMMUNITY. OUR MISSION IS TO EMPOWER WOMEN BY PROVIDING PROFESSIONAL ATTIRE AND A PERSONAL GROWTH PLAN TO HELP HER THRIVE IN HER CAREER AND IN LIFE. WE WORK TO ACHIEVE OUR MISSION THROUGH OUR BOUTIQUE WHERE VOLUNTEERS AND STAFF SORT DONATED CLOTHING AND HELP TO DRESS WOMEN WHO ARE SEEKING TO GAIN EMPLOYMENT BY PROVIDING WORK APPROPIATE ATTIRE AND THROUGH THE SUCCESS CENTRE WHERE WE PROVIDE CLIENTS WITH ONGOING CAREER ADVICE TECHNOLOGY SKILLS JOB SEEKING SUPPORT A LENDING LIBRARY OF RESOURCE MATERIALS FINANCIAL LITERACY AND CAREER BUILDING INFORMATION.
