What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

The Bridge Hospice is a 3 bed tranquil intimate home away from home where people facing end of life and their families receive exceptional care guidance and support from professional care providers and compassionate volunteers. The hospice operates with paid staff and a team of volunteers. Our nurses and support workers work under the direction of local palliative physicians and nurse practitioners.
