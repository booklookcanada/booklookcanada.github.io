What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

The purpose of the Foundation is to receive and maintain funds in order to help the needy and the poor such as children and families whose income is below the national poverty level. Some funds will also be directed to hospitals education and arts and culture. The Foundation intends to fulfill its vision by providing funding to a broad range of registered local provincial and national charitable organizations that share in the same purpose.
