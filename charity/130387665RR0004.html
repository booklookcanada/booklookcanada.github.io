What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Aug 31, 2024)

Govern and administer in partnership with the Department of National Defence a youth movement which offers development of citizenship leadership and life skills for youth with an interest in aviation. There are approximately 8 500 youths 12 to 18 years of age in this jurisdiction. There are over 100 squadrons throughout most of Ontario. The young men/women Air Cadets participate in local training and activities during the school year.
