What it says it does

To advance religion by establishing and maintaining a religious school of instruction on the subject of Judaism for youth. To advance education by establishing and operating a secondary school for Jewish youth. To advance education by providing publicly available scholarships bursaries and other forms of financial assistance to students attending Yeshiva Mishkan Yosef. To undertake activities ancillary and incidental to the attainment of the above charitable programs.
