What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

We provide ongoing support to the local schools through achievement awards for students and financial support for activities for students with special needs. We also provide financial support as required by the school. We provided financial support for meals to seniors and shut-ins in partnership with the Beaver Valley Outreach. We support community initiatives that will benefit the health and welfare of our community members with special emphasis on youth. Working to support the renovation and expansion of the skateboard park in partnership with the municipality.
