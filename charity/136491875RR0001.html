What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

THE PURPOSE OF TORONTO FOUNDATION IS TO CREATE A MORE FAIR AND JUSTICE SOCIETY WHERE EVERYONE CAN THRIVE BY MOBILIZING THOSE WITH RESOURCES AND THE WILL TO PARTNER WITH OTHERS. THE FOUNDATION PUBLISHES RESEARCH INCLUDING THE TORONTO'S VITAL SIGNS REPORT TO TRACK THE CITY'S HEALTH AND WELLBEING AND ITS GROWING INEQUALITY. THROUGH THE VITAL TORONTO FUND THE FOUNDATION MAKES STRATEGIC GRANTS TO LOCAL ORGANIZATIONS AND ISSUES BASED ON ITS RESEARCH FINDINGS. AS WELL THROUGH DONOR EDUCATION EVENTS THE FOUNDATION BUILDS THE CAPACITY OF DONORS TO HAVE AN IMPACT AND TO CONNECT WITH ORGANIZATIONS AND LEADERS MAKING MEANINGFUL CHANGE.
