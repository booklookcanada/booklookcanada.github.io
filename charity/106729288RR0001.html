What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Jun 30, 2024)

TO ESTABLISH PROVIDE AND CONDUCT SCHOOLS FOR INSTRUCTION IN TORAH JEWISH RELIGIOUS PRACTICES TRADITION CULTURE LITERATURE HISTORY HEBREW LANGUAGE AND RELATED SUBJECTS. TO INSTILL IN CHILDREN A SENSE OF COMMITMENT AND INDENTIFICATION WITH TORAH LANGUAGE JEWISH PEOPLE IDEALS AND TRADITIONS AND WITH THE STATE OF ISRAEL. TO ESTABLISH PROVIDE AND CONDUCT SCHOOLS FOR SECULAR STUDIES IN ACCORDANCE WITH THE REQUIREMENTS AND REGULATIONS AS MAY BE FROM TIME TO TIME ESTABLISHED BY THE ONTARIO MINISTRY OF EDUCATION.
