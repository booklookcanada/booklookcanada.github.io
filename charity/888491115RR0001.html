What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

ANISHNABEG OUTREACH EMPLOYMENT AND TRAINING INC.'S (AO) VISION STATEMENT IS TO BUILD PROCESSES AND TOOLS THAT WILL POSITION CURRENT AND FUTURE FIRST NATIONS METIS AND INUIT (FNMI) GENERATIONS AS PROSPEROUS LEADERS AND STRATEGIC PARTNERS IN CANADA'S FUTURE. AO IS HELPING FNMI BY CREATING A SUSTAINABLE AND COMPREHENSIVE SUITE OF CENTRALIZED WRAP AROUND SERVICS FOR URBAN FNMI AND TO ESTABLISH AO AS AN INNOVATIVE HUB/HEART OF BEST PRACTICES FOR ECONOMIC DEVELOPMENT TRAINING AND EMPLOYMENT ON RESERVES.
