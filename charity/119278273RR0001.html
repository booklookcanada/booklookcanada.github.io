What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

WE HOLD AN ANNUAL FUNDRAISING CAMPAIGN TO SOLICIT DONATIONS FOR OUR AGENCY MEMBERS. WE DISTRIBUTE MONIES COLLECTED TO OUR AGENCY MEMBERS IN ACCORDANCE WITH THEIR AGENCY AGREEMENT ALLOCATIONS. WE PROVIDE NEW INITIATIVE FUNDING FOR NEW COMMUNITY AGENCIES. WE PROVIDE TRAINING SESSIONS FOR OUR AGENCY MEMBERS AND LIKE COMMUNITY AGENCIES IN THE AREAS OF FUNDRAISING GOVERNANCE ETC. WE OPERATE A GIFTS IN KIND CENTRE WHERE IN-KIND DONATIONS ARE MADE AVAILABLE TO AGENCY MEMBERS FREE OR AT GENTLY REDUCED PRICES. WE ENGAGE IN A NUMBER OF YOUTH PROGRAMS INCLUDING ANNUAL YOUTH SUMMIT AND THE YOUTH TO YOUTH BOARD A JUNIOR BOARD OF DIRECTORS.
