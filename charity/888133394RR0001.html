What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

Provide financial support and gifts for pastors in Ukraine and Poland. Provide gifts and basic necessities throughout the year for children who are orphaned or who live in poverty. Provide for the humanitarian needs of poor seniors the disabled those living in poor villages around Ukraine and Poland and those affected by war. Examples are food packages blankets heating help safe appliances clean water and minor home repairs. Supply Christian literature and support for volunteers doing personal home visits for people in need. Financial support for children's camps in Ukraine and Poland. Distribution of written and spoken material to encourage people in their faith. Provide humanitarian assistance primarily food in Poland for refugees fleeing the war in Ukraine. Assist with renovation materials and labour for church buildings to create and maintain safe warm spaces for the vulnerable.
