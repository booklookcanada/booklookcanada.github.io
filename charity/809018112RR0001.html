What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

The Douglas Wright Foundation is a not-for-profit charitable organization devoted to fighting Melanoma cancer in Canada by increasing awareness educating the public about early detection and preventative measures and fostering partnerships between like minded organizations. In 2024 the foundation again committed funds to joint projects with like minded foundations to deliver sun safe materials to the public which includes distribution of sunscreen and sun tents as well as raise awareness of sun safety. The foundation also continued to fund a grant that is awarded to individuals in the research/preventative space for Melanoma.
