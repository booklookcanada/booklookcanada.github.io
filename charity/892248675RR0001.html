What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

The SOSMWS operating as the South Okanagan Mental Wellness Centre provides support and resources for those struggling with mental illness and to those whose loved ones struggle with mental illness. Our support and resources fall within two programs; the Peer Support Program - for individuals with Mental Illness and the Family Support Program - for family members and friends of individuals with mental illness. Our services are provided by trained employees some of whom have lived experience. We offer educational workshops and weekly gathering meetings. We offer resources and provide referrals to other resources in the region of the South Okanagan Similkameen. Our resource library on various topics of mental health is available to the public.
No new programs as at Mar 31 2024.
