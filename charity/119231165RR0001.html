What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

This orgnization provides funds to Coptic (Egyptian Christians) in Egypt as they don't receive any assistance from the Egyptian Government. These funds are to spend on food shelters medical treatments and to help in education for the poor and fneedy families for the 28 diocese accros Egypt. needy families and youth. This organization also promotes the distribution of christian material such as bilbles religious CD's and publications in English French and Arabic to Coptic Churches and Christian groups in Canada.
