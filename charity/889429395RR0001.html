What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

We hold weekly Worship Services and Sunday School for all who would like to attend. We were able to hold Vacation Bible School which takes place for a week each summer. We have 2 volunteer organists who provide music each Sunday. We have 2 volunteers who decorate our church for various special services and seasons. Our Church Treasurer is also a volunteer who looks after all of our financial responsibilities. We have many volunteers who contribute to each of our fundraising activities throughout the year. We have 3 rotating Sunday School teachers.
We are having a coffee drop in on Tuesday mornings for anyone that would like to attend. We have also started a quilting group making prayer quilts for those who are very ill. The church is used for ukulele lessons every Wednesday afternoon and piano lessons every Wednesday evening.
