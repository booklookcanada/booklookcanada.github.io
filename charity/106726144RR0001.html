What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Jun 30, 2024)

Ashbury College is an independent school providing co-education from Grade 4 - 12. The school has a population of approximately 700 students which includes over 100 boarders living on site during the school year. Ashbury’s mission is to inspire students in a dynamic learning environment as intellectually curious compassionate and responsible global citizens. Ashbury College offers a variety of programs including the Ashbury Diploma a Bilingual Diploma and the International Baccalaureate Diploma.
