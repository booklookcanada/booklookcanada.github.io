Who runs it — the people it pays

It reported 40 full-time positions and 76 part-time employees (year ended Jul 31, 2024). Total pay for all its positions: $5,258,530, as filed. The highest pay band it reported was “$80,000–$119,999”. 6 employees were reported in that band. Pay is reported in bands — ranges, not exact salaries. The top band is the highest range with anyone in it: it is not one person's salary, and it is not what everyone there is paid.

What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Jul 31, 2024)

We have established and operate multi-denominational Christian schools in Belleville Picton and Trenton Ontario. Our purpose is to advance education by operating Christian elementary and secondary schools in the Quinte region. Using Christ centered curriculum QRCS (Quinte Region Christian Schools) provides each student with a level of academic and religious learning that helps them to develop into well rounded individuals. QRCS also offers a preschool program .
