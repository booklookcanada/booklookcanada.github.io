What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

The primary purpose of the church is to promote the teaching and spreading of the gospel of Jesus Christ with the objective of bringing love peace unity hope healing and restoration in people's lives so they can have a positive impact on their families and the community. we meet for worship every Sunday and for Holy Communion. Small groups meet for prayer and bible study as well as training and equipping. We minister to people in hospitals and homeless shelters in Toronto and in other places when Missions teams are sent out.
