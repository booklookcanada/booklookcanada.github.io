It reported 12 full-time positions and 3 part-time employees (year ended Nov 30, 2024). Total pay for all its positions: $1,657,585, as filed. The highest pay band it reported was “$250,000–$299,999”. 1 employee was reported in that band. Pay is reported in bands — ranges, not exact salaries. The top band is the highest range with anyone in it: it is not one person's salary, and it is not what everyone there is paid.

What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Nov 30, 2024)

THE FOUNDATION IS COMMITTED TO REDUCING POVERTY IN CANADA. IT SEEKS TO ACCOMPLISH THIS OBJECTIVE THROUGH: (A) A GRANTING PROGRAM (PROVIDING GIFTS TO QUALIFIED DONEES) (B) PROGRAMMING THAT PROMOTES LEADERSHIP IN POVERTY REDUCTION AND ASSISTS PEOPLE LIVING IN POVERTY TO PARTICIPATE FULLY IN THEIR COMMUNITIES (C) RESEARCH AND POLICY ANALYSIS TO SUPPORT PUBLIC POLICY TO REDUCE POVERTY AND (D) PROGRAMMING THAT STRENGTHENS THE CAPACITY OF THE COMMUNITY SECTOR.
