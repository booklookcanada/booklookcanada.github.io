LOCAL CHURCH ASSEMBLY THAT HOLDS WEEKLY SUNDAY CORPORATE WORSHIP AND CHRISTIAN INSTRUCTION SERVICES ON-SITE AND STREAMING. PROVIDES SPIRITUAL RELATIONAL AND EMOTIONAL SUPPORT/INSTRUCTION SPECIFICALLY TARGETED TO: PRESCHOOLERS K-GR6 JR HI SR HI YOUNG ADULTS PARENTS OF YOUNG CHILDREN SENIORS MEN'S GROUPS WOMEN'S GROUPS AND OTHER SPECIAL INTEREST GROUPS. HOLDS REGULAR WEEKLY MEETINGS FOR JUNIOR HIGH SENIOR HIGH AND YOUNG ADULTS. PROVIDES PASTORAL CARE TO APPROXIMATELY 1900 CONGREGANTS AND THEIR FAMILIES. SUPPORTS GLOBAL WORKER GROUPS AND SENDS TEAMS THAT WORK WITH THE NEEDY BOTH IN INTERNATIONAL AREAS AS WELL AS IN CANADA. HELPS SUPPORT LONG TERM GLOBAL WORKERS IN INTERNATIONAL LOCATIONS. CONTINUES REGIONAL OUTREACHES TO SENIOR'S HOMES HOMELESS ENCAMPMENTS MIGRANT WORKERS AT-RISK GROUPS AND CONTINUES ASSISTING LOCAL COMMUNITY PROJECTS. SPONSORS/HOSTS PAKISTANI CHURCH PLANT AND HELPS NEW IMMIGRANTS.
