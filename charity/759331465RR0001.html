What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

ORPHAN CARE PROGRAM: CURRENTLY A TOTAL OF 30 CHILDREN AGED BETWEEN 2-18 ARE SUPPORTED THOUGH THIS PROGRAM. THESE CHILDREN ARE ORPHANED AND OR ABANDONED AND CURRENTLY LIVE IN TWO REGISTERED NOT FOR PROFIT SHELTER HOMES IN SRI LANKA. OUR PROGRAM HELPS THESE HOMES TO IMPROVE THE QUALITY OF CARE BY PROVIDING THE BASIC NECESSITIES OF LIFE E.I. NUTRITIOUS FOOD CLOTHING & HEALTHCARE. THESE CHILDREN ARE SUPPORTED THROUGH THE ENTIRE YEAR THROUGH OUR FUNDING. SCHOOL WITH A SMILE.
SCHOOL WITH A SMILE PROGRAM: THE SCHOOL WITH A SMILE PROJECT AIMED AT ENSURING UNINTERRUPTED EDUCATION FOR STUDENTS FORM LOW-INCOME FAMILIES. BY PROVIDING ESSENTIAL SCHOOL SUPPLIES WHILE ALLEVIATING THE FINANCIAL BURDEN ON PARENTS AND PREVENTING SCHOOL DROPOUTS DUE TO POVERTY. THE PROJECT WAS IMPLEMENTED IN COLLABORATION WITH REGISTERED LOCAL NOT FOR PROFIT ORGANIZATION IN SRI LANKA. 1000 STUDENTS WITHIN A POOL OF 50 RURAL SCHOOLS ARE PROVIDED WITH SCHOOL SUPPLIES AT THE BEGINNING OF THE SCHOOL YEAR THESE SUPPLIES WAS SUFFICIENT TO LAST FOR THE ENTIRE SCHOOL YEAR.

What this page can't tell you

  • Whether its work works. A return shows money, not results.
  • Whether anyone audited these figures. The CRA publishes returns as filed, without checking them. Some charities also post audited statements — BookLook compared the largest 200 charities' published statements with their returns (see “How to read this site”).
  • Anything after Dec 31, 2024, its newest return in the published data.
  • Who its directors are. The published data does not include them.
