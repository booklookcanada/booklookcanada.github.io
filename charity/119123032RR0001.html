What it says it does

1) HAPPY MEAL PROGRAM: A PORTION OF EVERY HAPPY MEAL SOLD BY MCDONALD'S RESTAURANTS ACROSS CANADA SUPPORTS CAPITAL EXPANSION AND OPERATING GRANTS FOR RMHC PROGRAMS: RMHC HOUSES (16 ACROSS CANADA): LOCATED RIGHT INSIDE THE HOSPITAL EXTENDS THE COMFORT OF A RONALD MCDONALD HOUSE TO THE HOSPITAL SETTING SERVING BOTH OUT OF TOWN FAMILIES AND LOCAL FAMILIES WHO ARE CARING FOR THEIR SICK CHILD. 2) COIN BOX DONATION PROGRAM: COIN BOX DONATIONS FROM MCDONALD'S GUESTS SUPPORT RMHC PROGRAMS AND FAMILIES ACROSS CANADA. ALL FUNDS ARE COLLECTED BY A THIRD PARTY COLLECTION AGENCY AND ARE THEN DISTRIBUTED TO RMHC PROGRAMS ACROSS THE COUNTRY. 3) CASHLESS DONATION PROGRAM: DONATIONS FROM MCDONALD'S GUESTS AT THE IN-RESTAURANT KIOSK DRIVE THRU AND FRONT COUNTER SUPPORT RMHC PROGRAMS ACROSS THE COUNTRY. 4) RMHC COOKIE PROGRAM: A PORTION OF EVERY RMHC COOKIE SOLD BY MCDONALD'S RESTAURANTS ACROSS CANADA ARE DISTRIBUTED TO ALL RMHC PROGRAMS. 5) NATIONAL PARTNERSHIPS PROGRAM: FUNDING FROM RMHC NATIONAL PARTNERS AND CORPORATE DONORS ARE DISTRIBUTED TO RMHC PROGRAMS ACROSS CANADA. 6) NATIONAL FUNDRAISING: DONATIONS FROM INDIVIDUALS AND FOUNDATIONS ARE DISTRIBUTED TO ALL RMHC PROGRAMS ACROSS CANADA.
