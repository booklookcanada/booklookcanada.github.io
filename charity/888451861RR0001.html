What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

Providing crisis and on-going support guidance and practical help and assistance to individuals in distress over an unexpected or challenging pregnancy. Providing crisis and on-going support guidance and practical help and assistance to parents caring for infants and young children struggling to cope and/or without viable means of support and by extension family members. Providing prenatal parenting and pregnancy loss support classes workshops and programs.
