What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

The Rapha Christian Centre Inc. is a registered charitable organization advancing religion by establishing and maintaining a house of worship with services conducted in accordance with the tenets and doctrines of the Apostolic faith. The charity also operates a Community Centre for the benefit of the public relieves poverty by providing counselling employment assistance and training to unemployed or low skilled individuals and provides literacy classes. In addition the organization receives provincial funding for and operates a program under the EYOW (Enhanced Youth Outreach Worker Program) funded by the Ont. Ministry of Children Community and Social Services.
