Gifts of goods are taken out of the donation side only where the CRA publishes that breakdown (the most recent year), so read this as approximate.

Who runs it — the people it pays

It reported 14 full-time positions and 27 part-time employees (year ended Dec 31, 2024). Total pay for all its positions: $801,760, as filed. The highest pay band it reported was “$40,000–$79,999”. 4 employees were reported in that band. Pay is reported in bands — ranges, not exact salaries. The top band is the highest range with anyone in it: it is not one person's salary, and it is not what everyone there is paid.
