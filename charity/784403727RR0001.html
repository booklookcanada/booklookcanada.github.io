What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

RECOGNIZED & AWARD WOMEN & STUDENTS FOR THEIR OUTSTANDING COMMUNITY SERVICE AND PHILANTHROPY. -PROVIDE SCHOLARSHIPS AND OTHER FORMS OF FINANICAL ASSISTANCE TO STUDENTS TO ADVANCE EDUCATION. - SUPPORT OTHER CHARITIES PROGRAMS TOWARD DISABLED AND LESS FORTUNATE INDIVIDUALS TOWARD THEIR EDUCATION. - MENTAL HELP SUPPORT AND PHONE LINE PROGRAMS IN SEVERAL LANGUAGE FOR STUDENTS WOMENS AND FAMILIES EXPECIALLY NEW COMERS.
COLLABORATION WITH OTHER ORGANIZATIONS AND FOUNDATIONS TOWARDS THEIR EDUCATIONAL PROGRAMS FOR STUDENTS AND WOMEN. - CREATED PROGRAM FOR STUDENTS SUMMITS AND PUBLIC SPEAKING EVENTS TO OVERCOME OBSTACLES IN THEIR LIFE ESPECIALLY MENTAL AND FINANCIAL. - IN PROGRESS TO PUBLISH DOCUMENTARY ALBUM ( PRINTING AND DIGITAL) TO SUPPORT FUND RAISING AND SERVE AS AN INSPIRATIONAL AND GROWTH SUPPORT - IN PROGRESS - CREATING CO-OP OPPORTUNITIES FOR STUDENTS.
