What it says it does

PROVIDES THE SERVICE OF THE ROMAN CATHOLIC CHURCH TO MEMBERS OF THE COMMUNITY. IN THIS RESPECT THE PARISH HOLDS DAILY WORSHIP SERVICES AND PREPARES STUDENTS FOR THE RECEPTION OF SACRAMENTS. PROVIDES PROGRAMS OF PRAYER AND SPIRITUAL GROWTH. PROVIDES EDUCATION FOR SEMINARIANS AND PRIESTS. PROVIDES PASTORAL CARE TO THE NEEDY HOMELESS AND MENTALLY ILL. PROVIDES COMFORT TO THE BEREAVED.
