What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Aug 31, 2024)

Rescue rehabilitation and rehome horses and other animals in need. Promoting welfare of animals (specifically equine) for the benefit of the public by rescuing stray abandoned and or surrendered animals. Horse adoption program to assist in rehoming. To increase public awareness of the abuse of animals specifically equine and to educate the public on responsible management and husbandry of horses. To operate an equine welfare organization to ensure quality care and treatment of horses through intervention education and outreach. To develop a partnership with enforcement agencies in Ontario to provide a reputable facility for the care and rehabilitation of horses under investigation.
Providing critical care facility for horses and animals for Ontario Animal Welfare Services when removal and seizure is necessary.
