THE PURPOSE OF THE CHARITY IS TO RECEIVE AND MAINTAIN A FUND OR FUNDS AND TO APPLY ALL OR PART OF THE PRINCIPAL AND INCOME THEREFROM FROM TIME TO TIME TO CHARITABLE ORGANIZATIONS THAT ARE 'QUALIFIED DONEES' AS DEFIND UNDER THE INCOME TAX ACT (CANADA).
