What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

THE TORONTO PUBLIC LIBRARY FOUNDATION (TPLF) RAISES FUNDS THAT AMPLIFY AND ACCELERATE THE EXCEPTIONAL WORK OF TORONTO PUBLIC LIBRARY (TPL). DONATIONS HAVE A RESOUNDING IMPACT ON THOSE WHO RELY ON TPL'S PROGRAMS SERVICES AND COMMUNITY SPACES FOR EQUITABLE ACCESS TO INFORMATION AND OPPORTUNITIES PARTICULARLY EQUITY-DESERVING COMMUNITIES. STAFF AND VOLUNTEERS ALSO ACTIVELY PROMOTE AND ADVOCATE FOR THE WORK OF TPL DEMONSTRATING THE IMPORTANCE OF THE LIBRARY AS A CORNERSTONE OF DEMOCRACY CIVIC LIFE AND INTELLECTUAL FREEDOM. TPLF WORKS TO ADVANCE TPL'S MISSION TO MAKE US ALL MORE RESILIENT MORE KNOWLEDGEABLE MORE CONNECTED AND MORE SUCCESSFUL.
