What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

OUR PROGRAM IS TO ASSIST IN STARTING AND MAINTAINING STUDENT NUTRITION PROGRAM IN THE SCHOOLS IN CHATHAM-KENT. WE GARNER FUNDS TO ENABLE PROGRAMS TO FUNCTION AND PURCHASE FOOD AND EQUIPMENT. WE ASSIST IN ARRANGING FOR VOLUNTEERS AND TRAININGS TO ENSURE THE QUALITY OF THE PROGRAMS. OUR GOAL IS TO PROVIDE GOOD NUTRITIOUS FOOD FOR CHILDREN AT NO COST TO THE CHILD TO ENABLE THEM TO MAKE BETTER USE OF THEIR EDUCATIONAL OPPORTUNITIES
