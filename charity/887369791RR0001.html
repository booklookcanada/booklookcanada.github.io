What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

Our mission is to glorify God grow in Christ and go with the Gospel. Our vision for ministry is to: • love God with all of our heart soul mind and strength ready to serve Him in the Church - the body of Christ under the guidance of our leaders; • love all with Christ-like love those within the Church and those outside of the Church; • share the truth of Christ's redemption. This mission is carried out through the following means: • hold weekly public services for worship Bible study & prayer; • give out financial support in the community through our established benevolent fund assisting those in need; • operate a community food bank; • operate summer day camps for children and youth in the community; • provide Christian education classes for all ages on Sundays as well as in small groups during the week; • hold weekly recreational kids & youth programs; • provide spiritual & emotional counselling; and • partner with other Canadian charities in Canadian & overseas missionary activities.
