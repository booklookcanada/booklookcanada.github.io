What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Sep 30, 2024)

CAMP SOLELIM OPERATES AN OVERNIGHT SUMMER CAMP AND THE MISSION OF THE ORGANIZATION IS TO PROVIDE JEWISH 14 AND 15 YEAR OLDCAMPERS WITH AN ENGAGING FUN AND SPIRITED SUMMER EXPERIENCE THAT NURTURES JEWISH IDENTITY AND VALUES IN A PLURALISTIC ENVIRONMENT AND FOSTERS KNOWLEDGE OF AND A LOVE FOR ISRAEL. IT PROMOTES CAMPER INDEPENDENCE AND PERSONAL GROWTH AND ENCOURAGES THE DEVELOPMENT OF LEADERSHIP SKILLS. CAMP SOLELIM AIMS TO CREATE A STRONG INCLUSIVE COMMUNITY IN WHICH EACH CAMPER THRIVES.
