What it says it does

THE FOUNDATION HAS A MANDATE TO RECEIVE FUNDS AND CONTRIBUTIONS FROM INDIVIDUALS AND ORGANIZATIONS WITHOUT PUBLIC APPEAL AND TO CONTRIBUTE THOSE FUNDS AT THE DISCRETION OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS TO CHARITABLE ORGANIZATIONS AND INSTITUTIONS IN CANADA THAT SERVE THE AREAS OF CHILDREN IN NEED AND FAMILIES AT RISK.
