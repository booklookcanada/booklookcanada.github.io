What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

THE OBJECTIVES ARE TO: 1. PROVIDE ASSISTANCE TO AND TO ALLEVIATE THE HARDSHIPS OF THE MENTALLY ILL IN PARTICULAR THOSE SUFFERING FROM THE DISEASE OF SCHIZOPHRENIA AND THEIR FAMILIES; 2. FACILITATE COMMUNICATION AMONG THE MENTALLY ILL AND AMONG FAMILIES OF THE MENTALLY ILL TO ASSIST IN EDUCATING THE PUBLIC IN GENERAL AND MENTALLY ILL AND THEIR FAMILIES IN PARTICULAR IN THE NATURE OF MENTAL ILLNESS AND TO ASSIST THE PROFESSIONS IN THE SEARCH FOR AND APPLICATION OF CURES FOR MENTAL ILLNESS; 3. ACCEPT DONATIONS GIFTS LEGACIES AND BEQUESTS.
