THE ART OF TIME ENSEMBLE BRINGS TOGETHER CANADA'S FOREMOST CLASSICAL MUSICIANS WITH SOME OF THE COUNTRY'S GREATEST ACTORS DANCERS AND CHOREOGRAPHERS AS WELL AS JAZZ AND POP MUSICIANS TO PRESENT CONCERTS.
