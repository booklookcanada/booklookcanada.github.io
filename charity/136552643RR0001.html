What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Sep 30, 2024)

THE PRIMARY OBJECTS OF THE CORPORATION ARE: A) TO ESTABLISH MAINTAIN AND OPERATE A NON-PROFIT RECREATIONAL CAMP OR CAMPS WITHIN THE PROVINCE OF ONTARIO FOR CHILDREN WITH LEARNING DISABILITIES. B) TO PROVIDE AN OPPORTUNITY FOR THOSE WITH LEARNING DISABILITIES TO SUCCESSFULLY INTEGRATE INTO SOCIETY WITH THE ASSISTANCE OF AND THROUGH THE INOLVEMENT IN PROGRAMS SUCH AS LIFE SKILLS TRAINING EDUCATIONAL UPGRADING EMPLOYMENT PREPARATION CREATIVE USE OF RECREATION AND LEISURE TIME AND OTHER SPECIALIZED PROGRAMS AS NECESSARY. C) TO DEVELOP AND PROMOTE BETTER UNDERSTANDING OF LEARNING DISABILITIES AND PEOPLE WITH LEARNING DISABILITES WITH A A VIEW TO ENCOURAGING COMMUNITY PARTICIPATION IN THE INTEGRATION OF LEARNING DISABLED PERSONS AS PRODUCTIVE MEMBERS OF SOCIETY.
