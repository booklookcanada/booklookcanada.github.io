What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Aug 31, 2024)

Maranatha Christian Academy is a private school in Windsor ON founded in 1985. We offer quality education from Junior Kindergarten to Grade 12. MCA is ministry inspected and confers the Ontario Secondary School Diploma along with the Maranatha Christian Academy Secondary School Diploma.
