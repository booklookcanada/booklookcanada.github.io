What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

PROVIDING RESPITE PRACTICAL EMOTIONAL AND SPIRITUAL SUPPORT TO CHILDREN AND ADULTS LIVING WITH LIFE THREATENING ILLNESSES AND SUPPORT FOR THEIR FAMILIES IN THE COMFORT OF THEIR OWN HOMES. EMILY’S HOUSE THE FIRST RESIDENTIAL CHILDREN’S HOSPICE IN TORONTO IS COMMITTED TO OPTIMIZING THE QUALITY OF LIVES OF CHILDREN AND THEIR FAMILIES THROUGH PROVISION OF INTEGRATED RESPITE AND PEDIATRIC PALLIATIVE CARE IN A HOME-LIKE SETTING. THESE ARE ACHIEVED THROUGH A SUBSTANTIAL CONTRIBUTION OF VOLUNTEERS REFISTERED NURSES REGISTERED PRACTICAL NURSES PSW’S CHILD LIFE SPECIALIST CASE MANAGEMENT AND CARE GIVER SUPPORT.
