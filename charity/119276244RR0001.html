What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

SPREADING THE GOOD NEWS OF THE GOSPEL OF JESUS CHRIST THROUGH THE CONDUCTING OF WORSHIP SERVICES BIBLE STUDIES SUNDAY SCHOOL AND OTHER ACTIVITY THAT GLORIFIES THE KINGDOM OF GOD. CONTINUALLY WORKING TO MEET THE SPIRITUAL EMOTIONAL AND PHYSICAL NEEDS OF OUR CONGREGATION AND COMMUNITY. AS A CHURCH FAMILY WE HAVE A HEART FOR MISSIONS SUCH AS SAMARITAN'S PURSE OPERATION CHRISTMAS CHILD SPONSORING CHILDREN AND MISSIONARIES IN DEVELOPING COUNTRIES FOCUSING ON HAITI AS OUR MISSION FIELD AND SIERRA LEONE AS A SECONDARY MISSION FIELD. WE ALSO PROVIDE ASSISTANCE TO THOSE IN NEED IN BOTH OUR CONGREGATION AND COMMUNITY. SHILOH COMMUNITY CHURCH UB ALSO SPONSORS CHILDREN AND GROUPS THROUGH CH GLOBAL COMPASSION CANADA HOPE INTERNATIONAL AND GOSPEL FOR ASIA. LIVING HOPE CHRISTIAN CENTRE IS OUR CHURCH PLANT AND IS VERY ACTIVE IN MISSION WORK IN THE COMMUNITY OF SAUGREEN SHORES HOLDING A WEEKLY FOOD BANK AND OFFERING OTHER ASSISTANCE SUCH AS CLOTHING AND PERSONAL SUPPLIES TO THOSE IN NEED.
SHILOH COMMUNITY CHURCH UB IS ACTIVELY WORKING WITH OTHER COMMUNITY CHURCHES TO HOST VACATION BIBLE SCHOOL THAT WILL BE OFFERED TO CHILDREN AND YOUTH IN THE CONGREGATION AND COMMUNITY. SUNDAY SCHOOL IS HELD FOR THE YOUNG CHILDREN DURING THE WORSHIP SERVICES AND IT WELL ATTENDED. THE MEN'S BREAKFAST PROGRAM WITH BREAKFAST FOLLOWED BY FELLOWSHIP AND A SHORT WORSHIP TIME IS CONTINUING TO REACH OUT TO MEN BOTH IN OUR CONGREGATION AND IN THE COMMUNITY. A 'WOMEN'S NIGHT OUT' PROGRAM BEGAN IN THE FALL OF 2024 INVOLVING A GUEST SPEAKER AND WOMEN'S DEVOTIONAL TIME. THIS EVENT IS INVOLVING MANY WOMEN FROM THE COMMUNITY AS WELL AS THE CHURCH.
