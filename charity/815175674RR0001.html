What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

Black Health Alliance centres Black communities in defining the challenges and barriers affecting our health and well-being. We provide a platform for Black people to establish a common voice around systemic problems and we work to co-design and mobilize the tools skills resources and partnerships to implement solutions that improve health outcomes across Canada. Our programming is focused on community engagement research health promotion development of policy solutions strategy development and implementation support activities.
