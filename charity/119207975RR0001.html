What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

Camp Kintail operates a residential children's program. Week long camps are normally offered in July and August for children ages 5-18. Activities include water sports climbing wall high ropes course nature music and drama games arts and crafts campfires chapel faith learning sharing and devotional time. Activities are geared to the age of the campers. During the rest of the year rental opportunities exist for school groups for day and overnight trips and church groups of up to 200 people with options for catering and program. Camp Kintail aslo proved space for weddings and offers retreats. Once a month a monthly worship service called Kirk in the Cedars is offered followed by a community meal.
