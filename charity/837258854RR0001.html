What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

Cornerstone provides shelter to 165 women and supportive housing to 148 women across our five housing communities in Ottawa. To provide emergency shelter affordable housing and support services to women at risk of homelessness; to promote the efficiency and effectiveness of the charitable programs of the Incorporated Synod of the Diocese of Ottawa of the Anglican Church of Canada a registered charity under the Income Tax Act (Canada) by providing and maintaining property and facilities for this purpose and by providing management services thereto; to receive maintain manage and invest a fund or funds and to apply from time to time all or part thereof and the income earned therefrom from time to time for the benefit of the corporation and the Incorporated Synod of the Diocese of Ottawa of the Anglican Church of Canada.
