What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

Opeates Moorelands Camp offering a summer camp experience to 600 boys and girls; Operates After-School Programs during the school year for children between the ages of 6 and 12; -Grenmoble After-School Program - held at Grenoble Public School in Flemingdon Park Monday to Friday; -Thorncliffe Park After-School Program - held at Thorncliffe Park Public School Monday to Friday; -Gateway After-School Program - held at Gateway Public School in Flemingdon Park Monday to Friday; Starting September 2024 After-School programs ran 3 days a week on each location. Operates Virtual Youth Leadership Programs - 1 for young people aged 14 to 15; Operates Youth Leadership Programs - 2 for youth 11 to 13 years of age. Operates a Holiday Sharing Program assisting families over the Christmas season.
