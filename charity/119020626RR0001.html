What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Jun 30, 2024)

WE CARRY ON AN ANNUAL YOUTH HOCKEY TOURNAMENT YOUNG CANADA WEEK. WE PROVIDE PROGRAMS AND DONATIONS THAT BENEFIT BOYS AND GIRLS - SUCH AS AS ARENA ICE TIME AND THE SEAFORTH GIRLS BAND. WE PROVIDE SERVICES TO SENOIRS SUCH AS THE LIONS SENIORS BUS. WE MAKE CONTRIBUTIONS TO GROUPS SUCH AS THE GODERICH LAKE TOWN BAND FOR COMMUNITY BETTERMENT. WE CONTRIBUTE TO ENVIRONMENTAL PROGRAMS SUCH AS THE MAITLAND BRIDGE ASSOCIATION. WE PROVIDE CAMPING FACILITIES FOR YOUTH AND ADULTS AT CAMP KLAHANIE. WE PROVIDE SUPPORT TO HURON AREA NEWCOMERS.
