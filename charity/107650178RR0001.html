What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

In operation since 1970 Lutherwood offers a range of mental health and social service programs in Waterloo Region and Guelph-Wellington County. We provide the following: • We help children and their families work through mental health issues through live-in (residential) school and community-basedtreatment programs; • A variety of employment services for youth and adults; • Services for individuals and families at risk of experiencing homelessness to find suitable housing; • A youth centre including a shelter and various services for homeless youth 12-18 years old; • An assisted living facility that offers various levels of care for older adults.
The Life Launch program was expanded so it can help more youth in crisis giving them a safe place to stay while working towards long-term independent housing that is backed by a network of support and life skills they can always lean on.
