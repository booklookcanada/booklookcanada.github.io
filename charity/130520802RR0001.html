What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Jun 30, 2024)

INTERCEDE INTERNATIONAL WORKS WITH FELLOW BELIEVERS OF LIKE PRECIOUS EVANGELICAL CHRISTIAN FAITH IN CANADA AND IN MANY NATIONS TO CONDUCT MISSIONARY EVANGELISM CHURCH PLANTING CHRISTIAN CHURCH SERVICES AND SCHOOLS AND TEACHING MINISTRIES AMONG ALL NATIONS AND IN CANADA PARTICULARLY AMONG ABORIGINAL TRIBES FOREIGN VISITORS STUDENTS AND REFUGEES. WE ORDAIN AND SEND OUT MISSIONARIES PASTORS AND EVANGELISTS TO MINISTER IN CHURCHES IN CANADA AND IN MANY COUNTRIES. WE CONDUCT OUR MISSIONARY WORK IN PARTNERSHIP WITH INDIGENOUS MISSION AGENCIES AND PROVIDE AID TO VICTIMS OF DISASTER POVERTY AND WAR. WE ALSO PROVIDE AID TO CARRY OUT OUR PROGRAMS OF CHRISTIAN LITERATURE AND BIBLE DISTRIBUTION BIBLE SCHOOLS VOCATIONAL AND EDUCATIONAL PROGRAMS RELIEF AND DEVELOPMENT PROGRAMS INFRASTRUCTURE PROGRAMS AND ASSISTANCE TO PERSECUTED CHRISTIANS. WE ALSO PROVIDE CHILD AND MISSIONARY SPONSORSHIP PROGRAMS AND PUBLISH THE 'INTERCEDE' MAGAZINE 'PRAYERLINE' AND 'GOOD NEWS FOR THE NATIONS' MEDIA PROGRAMS AND CONDUCT 'GATEWAY' AND 'MISSION CAFE' MEETINGS AND SHORT-TERM 'MISSION ACTION TEAMS'. OUR STATEMENT OF FAITH CODE OF CONDUCT AND MISSIONARY PRINCIPLES ARE ESSENTIAL GUIDES TO ALL OF OUR CHARITABLE PROGRAMS.
