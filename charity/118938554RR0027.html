What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Aug 31, 2024)

Developed and delivered high quality non-formal educational programs for girls that offer dynamic flexible training in leadership decision making team building personal growth environmental and international awareness and service to others. Developed resource materials and training programs to train women to deliver non-formal educational programs. Held conferences workshops and other events to train women to deliver non-formal educational programs. Conducted international trips to sister organizations to promote appreciation of different cultures. Provide scholarships to encourage the further education of girls and women.
