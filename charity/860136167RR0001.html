What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

CANADA'S FIRS T AND ONLY REGISTERED MILITARY ACADEMY FOR THE PREPARATION OF STUDENTS FOR POST-SECONDARY ENTRANCE TRADES AND THE FIELD OF WORK OR SERVICE WITH A RICH MILITARY TRADITION. THE ACADEMY SHALL BE BOTH A TEACHING AND AN EXAMINING ACADEMY AND SHALL FURTHER THE PROSECUTION OF ORIGINAL RESEARCH AND INSTRUCTION IN A FULLY BILINGUAL FORMAT USING BOTH OF CANADA'S OFFICIAL LANGUAGES. THE ACADEMY WILL CONTINUE TO MAINTAIN A ONE-OF-A-KIND UNIQUE MILITARY SCHOOL THAT ENCOURAGES SUCCESS A SYSTEM THAT PROMOTES THE DEVELOPMENT OF STUDENTS IN OUR GLOBAL COMMUNITY AND A GATEWAY FOR THE PROMOTION OF ARTS SCIENCES AND LEARNING. THE ACADEMY PRESERVES AND ENHANCE ITS OBJECTIVE AS A MILITARY ACADEMY THAT IS COMMITTED TO ENABLING STUDENT'S ACADEMIC GROWTH. TO FOSTER SPIRITUAL RELIGIOUS AND PASTORAL CARE OF ALL STUDENTS THEIR FAMILIES AND STAFF MEMBERS REGARDLESS OF RELIGIOUS AFFILIATION PRACTICE AND/OR BELIEF. TO ADVANCE THE KNOWLEDGE OF ABILITIES OF STUDENTS BY PROVIDING SCHOLARSHIPS BURSARIES AND PRIZES FOR SCHOLASTIC ADVANCEMENT AND SUCH OTHER FORMS OF ASSISTANCE TOWARDS TUITION.
SUCH REGULATIONS AND REQUIREMENTS AS MAY BE ESTABLISHED FROM TIME TO TIME BY THE GOVERNMENT OF CANADA PROVINCIAL GOVERNMENT AND APPROPRIATE DIRECTORATES. OTHER APPROPRIATE ACTIVITIES PROGRAMS AND COMPONENTS APPROVED BY THE GOVERN-IN-COUNCIL AND IF IT IS BENEFICIAL TO THE ADVANCEMENT AND ATTAINMENT OF EDUCATION AND COMMUNITY SERVICES. SHALL RECOGNIZE AND AWARD EXCELLENCE IN STUDENTS' ACHIEVEMENTS. THE ACADEMY WILL ESTABLISH MAINTAIN AND SUPPORT HISTORICAL EXHIBITS AND STUDIES AS BOTH A TEACHING AN EXAMINING ACADEMY AND SHALL FURTHER THE PROSECUTION OF ORIGINAL RESEARCH. ANY OTHER CHARITABLE PROGRAMS BENEFICIAL TO THE FURTHERANCE ADVANCEMENT AND ATTAINMENT OF THE ACADEMY'S PURPOSES.
