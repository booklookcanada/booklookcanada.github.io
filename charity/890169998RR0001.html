What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

The organization is a lively and devoted church community that convenes multiple times each week for prayer worship and immersive Bible studies. Alongside these core activities the church takes great care in organizing retreats specifically tailored to its families and members fostering valuable moments of togetherness. To effectively carry out its mission the church relies on a dedicated team of employees and volunteers who work across different ministries. These ministries include the Men's Ministry Women's Ministry Sunday School and Youth Activities each serving a distinct purpose within the congregation. Additionally the church extends its services to the community offering spiritual counseling as a means of guidance and support. The church community passionately emphasizes spiritual growth and unity through regular gatherings focused on prayer worship and the study of scripture. By organizing retreats the church provides a serene setting where families and members can deepen their bonds and nurture their faith. With the assistance of dedicated employees and volunteers the Men's Ministry Women's Ministry Sunday School and Youth Activities cater to specific groups within the congregation empowering and guiding individuals in their respective life stages. Furthermore the church plays an active role in the wider community by offering spiritual counseling extending a compassionate and supportive hand to those in need. Through these diverse initiatives the church strives to create an inclusive environment where individuals and families can flourish spiritually and experience the transformative power of faith.
