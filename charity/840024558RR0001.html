What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

COMMUNITY ENGAGEMENT: A PROGRAM TO ENGAGE COMMUNITY ACTION AND INCLUSION FOR DIVERSE COMMUNITIES IN HAMILTON. ADVANTAGE DIVERSITY: A PROGRAM WITH THE GOAL OF CREATING DIVERSE WORK PLACES WITHIN THE CITY OF HAMILTON. BY EVALUATING AND MAKING RECOMMENDATIONS TO EMPLOYERS. DIVERSITY ONBOARD: A PROGRAM THAT WORK IN PARTNERSHIP WITH OTHER SIMILAR ORGANIZATIONS IN ONTARIO TO PROMOTE DIVERSE MEMBERSHIP ON BOARDS OVERSEEING GOVERNANCE ACCOUNTABILITY STRATEGIC AND OPERATIONAL PLANS. WITH THE GOAL OF CREATING MORE INCLUSIVE AND ENGAGED COMMUNITIES.
