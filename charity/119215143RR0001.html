What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Jun 30, 2024)

As an International Roman Catholic religions community of priests and students for the priesthood ( The ' Congregation'). The objectives are to engage in priestly activities without limitations: Educate Evangelize and Teach; Establish Maintain and Operate non-profit residence; provide home and care for aged members of the Congregation; provide education and maintenance of seminarians; furnish and render services of charitable nature to the poor and needed.
