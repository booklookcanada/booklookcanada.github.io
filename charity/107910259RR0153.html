What it says it does

THE PARISH IS DEDICATED TO FOSTERING SPIRITUAL GROWTH THROUGH THE ADVANCEMENT OF ROMAN CATHOLIC RELIGIOUS WORSHIP INSTRUCTION AND MINISTRY TO BOTH PARISHIONERS AND THE COMMUNITY AT LARGE. IN THIS RESPECT THE PARISH: HOLDS WORSHIP SERVICES PROVIDES CATECHESIS AND SACRAMENTAL PREPARATION PROVIDES PASTORAL ARE TO THE SICK DYING AND THOSE IN GRIEF COUNSELS INDIVIDUALS AND FAMILIES IN CRISIS AND ASSISTS THE NEEDY AND HOMELESS IN ITS NEIGHBOURHOOD BY PROVIDING PASTORAL CARE FOOD VOUCHERS AND CLOTHING
