What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

The church family meets regularly on Saundays for the worship of God and the teaching of the Scriptures. During the week it may gather for prayer and Bible study. It seeks to care for and comfort those in the community reaching out to children youth adults and seniors offering personal counseling and community assistance as possible. Summer day camp activities for children also provide community support.
