What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

Provide a welcoming place for worship. Leadership in worship. Education for all ages including Sunday School for all ages. Provide support for local agencies- House of Friendship Mennonite Central Committee Shalom Counselling Compass Centre for Refugee Support Canadian Food Grains Banks Mennonite Economic Development Associates Menno Homes. Every summer run a BMC camp for 1 week that brings together children from our congregation with children from the local community for worship learning and fun. Missions are supported through our giving to Mennonite Church Eastern Canada and through cross-cultural experiences for youth. We encourage and support education through our Mennonite Schools and Colleges. Since we are stewards of God's kingdom BMC has been participating in the Microfit program generating solar power since 2013.
