What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

ARISE Ministry empowers individuals involved in the sex trade to reclaim their lives. This is a ministry of hope offering outreach case management and spiritual care. Case management involves weekly one-on-one meetings that include goal-setting life skills and prevention and awareness training. This program is also offered to at-risk youth and those in recovery seeking some stability. Other on-going programs include virtual street outreach and Pastoral care offering chaplaincy worship and spiritual care. New and developing programming includes preventative outreach with at-risk youth self-defence monthly meal church and an intentional friendship program.
