What it costs to raise a dollar

Across its 2022, 2023, 2024 returns pooled together, it spent $44 on fundraising for every $100 of cash donations it received, as filed. Pooling three returns smooths out one unusual campaign. Gifts of goods are taken out of the donation side only where the CRA publishes that breakdown (the most recent year), so read this as approximate.

Who runs it — the people it pays

It reported 46 full-time positions and 1 part-time employees (year ended Dec 31, 2024). Total pay for all its positions: $3,868,958, as filed. The highest pay band it reported was “$250,000–$299,999”. 1 employee was reported in that band. Pay is reported in bands — ranges, not exact salaries. The top band is the highest range with anyone in it: it is not one person's salary, and it is not what everyone there is paid.

What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

1. WE PROVIDE PEER REVIEWED ALS RESEARCH GRANTS. 2. WE PROVIDE SUPPORT FOR DIRECT CLIENT SERVICES INCLUDING AN EQUIPMENT PROGRAM FOR PEOPLE LIVING WITH ALS IN ONTARIO. 3. WE DEVELOP AND DISTRIBUTE EDUCATIONAL MATERIALS FOR PEOPLE LIVING WITH ALS CAREGIVERS AND HEALTH CARE PROFESSIONALS. 4. WE ADVOCATE ON BEHALF OF PEOPLE LIVING WITH ALS AND RAISE AWARENESS OF AMYOTROPHIC LATERAL SCLEROSIS (ALS) AND SURROUNDING ISSUES.
