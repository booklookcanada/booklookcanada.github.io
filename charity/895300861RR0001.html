What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

BEST BUDDIES CANADA IS A CHARITABLE ORGANIZATION DEDICATED TO ENHANCING THE COMMUNITIES THROUGH ONE-TO-ONE FRIENDSHIPS BETWEEN INDIVIDUALS WITH INTELLECTUAL DISABLITIES AND STUDENTS. BEST BUDDIES ESTABLISHES CHAPTERS AT HIGH SCHOOLS UNIVERSITIES AND COLLEGES ACROSS THE COUNTRY. AT EACH CHAPTER STUDENTS ARE MATCHED WITH INDIVIDUALS WITH INTELLECTUAL DISABILITIES WHO SHARE SIMILAR INTERESTS.
