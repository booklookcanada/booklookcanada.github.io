What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Jun 30, 2024)

Operation Smile Canada Foundation delivers free safe and timely surgery as well as pre- and post-surgical care and comprehensive cleft care for children born and/or living with cleft lip cleft palate and other facial conditions living in the global south. This care is carried out by global volunteer medical professionals. We also specialize in the training and education of medical professionals related to cleft; conduct research on causes and treatment; and educate the public about cleft lip and cleft palate; engaging youth through student leadership programs including social media and fundraising.
