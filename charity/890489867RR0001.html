What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

THE MISSION OF BELMONT HOUSE FOUNDATION IS TO RAISE FUNDS TO SUPPORT THE EFFORTS OF BELMONT HOUSE. THE FUNDS ARE USED TO SUPPORT PASTORAL CARE MUSIC AND ART THERAPY ADDITIONAL CARE STAFFING NEEDS PALLIATIVE CARE AND RECREATIONAL PROGRAMS. ALL PROGRAMS ARE CARRIED OUT BY THE STAFF OF BELMONT HOUSE. FUNDS ARE ALSO GRANTED TO ASSIST WITH RESIDENT SAFETY NEEDS AND TO SUPPORT VITAL EQUIPMENT NEEDS AND CAPITAL UPGRADES.
