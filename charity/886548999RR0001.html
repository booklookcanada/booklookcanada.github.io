What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

YOUTH PROGRAMMES- WE HOST A HIGH SCHOOL STUDENT FROM OVERSEAS (SLOVAKIA FOR 2024 UP COMING NONE IN 2022 OR 2021 DUE TO COVID 19); WE SPONSOR A STUDENT TO STUDY OVERSEAS FOR A YEAR; WE SUPPORT A WEEKEND LEADERSHIP TRAINING PROGRAMME FOR STUDENTS; WE PROVIDE FUNDING AND VOLUNTEERS TO LOCAL FOOD BANKS AND SCHOOL MEAL PROGRAMMES WE ASSIST WITH PLANTING TREES IN LOCAL PARKS. WE DONATE TO LOCAL CHARITIES AND OTHER IN NEED INDIVIDUALS AND ORGANIZATIONS TO ASSIST WITH OPERATIONS AND CAPITAL PROJECTS. WE PROVIDE FUNDING TO INTERNATIONAL PROJECTS SPONSORED BY OUR CLUB OR OTHER INTERNATIONAL ROTARY CLUBS. WE PARTNER WITH OTHER CANADIAN ROTARY CLUBS TO RAISE MONEY FOR ONTARIO BASED CHARITABLE CAUSES.
