What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

We hold regular Sunday Services each Sunday throughout the year. There are special services for New Year Easter Thanksgiving and Christmas days. There are special services with guest speakers invited from local churches. There is Sunday School for kindergarten kids and high school students who meet separately each Sunday conduct own bible classes and carry on other activities. Each group has their own retreat with speakers from within the church.
