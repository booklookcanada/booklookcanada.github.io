What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Nov 30, 2024)

The primary objective of the Foundation is to provide financial support to Canadian qualified charitable religious health and educational organizations. To fulfill this mandate the Foundation provides grants and donations to such organizations enabling them to carry out their activities and serve their communities. The Foundation's funds and donations are invested by its directors in a manner that is reasonable and prudent. The investment strategy aims to generate income which is then utilized to fund grants and donations throughout the year. The Foundation's grants and donations support activities including but not limited to the provision of healthcare services education and training research and development and the promotion of religious values and practices.

What this page can't tell you

  • Whether its work works. A return shows money, not results.
  • Whether anyone audited these figures. The CRA publishes returns as filed, without checking them. Some charities also post audited statements — BookLook compared the largest 200 charities' published statements with their returns (see “How to read this site”).
  • Anything after Nov 30, 2024, its newest return in the published data.
  • Who its directors are. The published data does not include them.
  • Questions worth asking it directly: Do you publish audited financial statements? Who sits on your board this year? What did last year's programs achieve, and how do you know?
