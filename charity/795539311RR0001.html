What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Aug 31, 2024)

Redemption Paws is a dog rescue agency. It serves the primary purpose of assisting at-risk dogs in areas affected by natural disasters to find permanent homes by adoption through a thorough screening process. Prior to adoption each dog receives veterinary care by accredited local veterinarians and temporary care in various supervised foster locations in the GTA and surrounding areas. Redemption Paws also receives fundraising from various Canadian sources through local campaigning activities. The funds are raised to improve the wellness and health conditions of the dogs and to finance the daily charitable operations of the organization.
