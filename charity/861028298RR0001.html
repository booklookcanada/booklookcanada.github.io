What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

WE CENTRE ON RURAL FARMING; OUR GOAL IS TO TRANSFORM AGRICULTURE TO HELP THE POOR IN AFRICA LIFT THEMSELVES TO THE NEXT OF DEVELOPMENT CREATING A BRIGHTER FUTURE FOR THE ORPHANS IN THEIR COMMUNITIES. TO EQUIP THE POOR IN AFRICA AND INDIA ABOUT ORGANIC GARDENING AND NATURAL MEDICINE. TO EMPOWER THOSE IN IMPOVERISHED NATIONALS FROM FOOD RELIANCE TO FOOD SUFFICIENCY. THIS IS DONE BY EQUIPPING SMALL-SCALE FARMERS WITH RESOURCES AND KNOWLEDGE TO FARM ORGANICALLY AND PRODUCTIVELY. TO ALLEVIATE THE STRAIN OF FOOD INSECURITY IN THE POOR ORPHANS IN THE RURAL AREAS BY CREATING THE CAPACITY IN THOSE COMMUNITIES TO CARE FOR THE MOST VULNERABLE OF THE EXTREME POOR.
