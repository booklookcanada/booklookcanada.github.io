What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Jul 31, 2024)

We hold public worship services weekly. Pastoral care is given to those who are sick or elderly or in need. Children young people and adults are given instruction through catechism classes youth groups and Bible Study groups. Benevolence is offered to those in need and we support Christian education. We have rental apartments life lease independent living units and a full care home for our elderly. The care home for our elderly was expanded in the past year. This provides residential housing meals care services and cleaning/laundry services in a home-like environment. Recreational activities are also provided with the help of volunteers. As part of its mandate it also provides terminal care where possible and occasionally respite care. We also support denominational mission activities and provide financial aid to local and international aid programs. We operate a Christian book store.
