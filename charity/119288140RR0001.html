The highest pay band it reported was “$200,000–$249,999”. 3 employees were reported in that band. Pay is reported in bands — ranges, not exact salaries. The top band is the highest range with anyone in it: it is not one person's salary, and it is not what everyone there is paid.

What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

1. Canadian North - provide grants to qualified donees and perform direct program activities to support research and education projects (including a Fellowship program) that increase Northern peoples' ability to participate in and help shape public policy and to ensure a broader understanding of policy issues that affect the North and Indigenous peoples. 2. Fresh Water Resources - provide grants to qualified donees and perform direct program activities to support research and public dialogue to aid in the development of innovative public policies in fresh water management. 3.
