What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

(1) In 2024 we held weekly worship services and children's church class (Prayground). (2) We provide ministry to Men Women Children youth and senior's groups on a weekly basis through group activities. (3) We offer bible discussion groups seasonally. (4) We provide Pastoral care to those in need. (5) We visit shut-ins and the sick. (6) We collect food gift cards unopened toys and knitted articles and donate them to the local food bank women's shelter family and children services of Grand Erie and the homeless. (7) We provided meal support for a holiday dinner for one disadvantaged elementary school. (8) We provide meeting rooms to Four Girl guide groups and S.A.F.E. zone seniors exercise program and Brant Skills Centre ipad training program free of charge. (9) We provide facilities for regular AA meetings at a low cost.
