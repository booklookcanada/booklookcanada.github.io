What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

The Banff Forum's objective is to advance education by hosting an annual forum and events across the country that bring together emerging and established leaders across all sectors and regions to enhance their leadership skills and support their contributions to building a better Canada. Founded in 2002 the Banff Forum is managed by a dedicated staff and volunteer Board of Directors whose members share a passion for advancing public policy and creating a deeply engaged national community. The Banff Forum has become one of Canada's pre-eminent public policy forums in which a diverse group of young Canadian leaders come together to learn about policy issues being faced across the country to enhance their leadership skills and to develop a network of like-minded leaders working to improve the lives of Canadians.
