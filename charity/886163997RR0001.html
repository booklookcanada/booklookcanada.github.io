What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

PROVIDES FINANCIAL SUPPORT TO CANADIAN CONFERENCE OF CATHOLIC BISHOPS AND TO QUALIFIED DONEE ORGANIZATIONS. THE ORGANIZATION PROVIDES FINANCIAL SUPPORT FOR PRIESTLY VOCATIONS AND SCHOLARSHIPS FOR STUDENTS ATTENDING UNIVERSITIES OR COLLEGES FOR THE ENHANCEMENT OF CATHOLIC TEACHING AND FAITH. THE ORGANIZATION ALSO PROVIDES FINANCIAL SUPPORT IN MATTERS AFFECTING LIFE FAMILY MARRIAGE AND SIMILAR PRIORITIES IN BUILDING A CULTURE OF LIFE.
