WE CHARITY CANADA IS IN THE PROCESS OF WINDING DOWN AND CLOSING. HISTORICALLY WE CHAIRTY CANADA HAS OPERATED THE FOLLOWING PROGRAMS. WE VILLAGES: THE INTERNATIONAL PROGRAMMING EFFORTS AIMS AT ENSURING THE CONTINUATION OF PROVIDING SERVICES TO COMMUNITIES IN AFRICA AND LATIN AMERICA THROUGH THE IMPLEMENTATION OF THE FIVE-PILLAR WE VILLAGES DEVELOPMENT MODEL WITH SPECIAL FOCUS ON EDUCATION WATER HEALTH AND FOOD AND SECURITY. THE WE SCHOOL PROGRAM PROVIDES SCHOOLS AND EDUCATORS WITH ONLINE RESOURCES WORKSHOPS AND CURRICULUM FOCUSING ON SERVICE LEARNING EDI MENTAL HEALTH AND WELL-BEING SOCIAL AND EMOTIONAL LEARNING AND TRAUMA-INFORMED PRACTICES.
