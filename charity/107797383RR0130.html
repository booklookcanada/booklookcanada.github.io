What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

The church holds weekly services promoting biblical teachings doctrines and observances as practiced by the Seventh-day Adventist Church. These activities are conducted through church services bible classes and other religious programs. Other church activities include pastoral visitations counseling baptism weddings child dedication funerals community outreach programs evangelistic meetings.
