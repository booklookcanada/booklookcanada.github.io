What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

We exist to lead people into a growing relationship with Jesus Christ one step at a time. We hold weekly worship services (online only since March 2020) and activities for children youth and adults and we foster community through small groups that meet throughout the week. We support the ministries of the Canadian Baptists of Ontario and Quebec (CBOQ) and Canadian Baptist Ministries (CBM) through our prayers and financial gifts. We also partner with local mission groups to reach people in our community and support the work of those partners. We provide pastoral care to members and adherents for life events. We operate Shine On Summer Day Camp a low-cost summer day camp that targets under-resourced families. We offer support to those in our community our city and our world who need help.
