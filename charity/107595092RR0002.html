What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

We are a public library. In addition to the usual circulation materials we offer: large print books for the visually impaired as well as offer eAudio and eBook format through our Overdrive platform; free Internet and Wi-Fi access and training; free admission passes for museums and education centres; volunteer opportunities; adult programs to build community connections; children's literacy programs craft and activities; local school classroom visits for storytime and 'get to know your library' outreach. Recently we have begun a diverse 'Library of Things' collection which includes meters health devices craft kits and outdoor sports equipment.
