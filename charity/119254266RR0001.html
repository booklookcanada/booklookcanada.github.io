Who runs it — the people it pays

It reported 16 full-time positions and 11 part-time employees (year ended Dec 31, 2024). Total pay for all its positions: $984,705, as filed. The highest pay band it reported was “$80,000–$119,999”. 1 employee was reported in that band. Pay is reported in bands — ranges, not exact salaries. The top band is the highest range with anyone in it: it is not one person's salary, and it is not what everyone there is paid.

What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

We have a Guest House where we welcome individuals and groups of all ethnic and faith backgrounds for quiet days meetings and workshops in an atmosphere of prayer and reflection; we have a program of online retreats quiet days and workshops open to all; a discernment program for women; a 'Companions' program in person and online for women who wish to spend time living learning and working with the sisters; an active online prayer and meditation ministry; opportunities for personal discernment and spiritual care; and daily chapel services open to the public. Other work includes work for the Anglican church and other outside ministries; work with Associates and Oblates; and preaching and ordained ministry in the Diocese of Toronto.
