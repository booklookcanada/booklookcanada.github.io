What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

RELIGIOUS SERVICES SOCIAL SUPPORT COUNSELLING PASTORAL VISITS AND FUNERALS ARE OFFERED THROUGHOUT MONTREAL AND THE SURROUNDING AREA. WE ALSO PROVIDE RELIGIOUS EDUCATION AT THE LATIVAN SUMMER CAMP AND ADVOCATE FOR SENIORS. DURING THE COVID-19 PANDEMIC WE INCREASED OUR USE OF SOCIAL MEDIA TO STAY CONNECTED WITH THE CONGREGATION. THIS INITIATIVE WHICH BEGAN AT THE START OF THE PANDEMIC CONTINUED THROUGH 2024. TO ENSURE ACCESSIBILITY FOR AS MANY PARISHIONERS AS POSSIBLE MEETINGS AND SERVICES WERE HELD IN A HYBRID FORMAT BOTH ON ZOOM AND IN PERSON. ADDITIONALLY WE HAVE BEEN FOCUSED ON THE MAINTENANCE AND UPGRADING OF THE CHURCH SUMMER RETREAT.
WHILE NO NEW PROGRAMS WERE LAUNCHED WE FOCUSED ON FINE-TUNING AND ENHANCING THE ONGOING INITIATIVES MENTIONED ABOVE. ADDITIONALLY WE ORGANIZED A FUNDRAISER TO SUPPORT THOSE AFFECTED BY THE ONGOING WAR IN UKRAINE.
