What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

WE PROVIDE WEEKLY PUBLIC WORSHIP SERVICES AS WELL AS OFFER VARIOUS OTHER MINISTRIES THROUGH REGULAR AND SEMI-REGULAR SUNDAY AND MID-WEEK PROGRAMS AND OCCASIONAL SPECIAL EVENTS. THESE PROGRAMS ARE GEARED TOWARDS ALL AGE GROUPS AND GENDERS AND EVERYONE IS WELCOME WHETHER THEY REGULARLY ATTEND OUR CHURCH OR ARE FROM THE COMMUNITY OR SURROUNDING AREA. THESE PROGRAMS INCLUDE BUT ARE NOT LIMITED TO: CHILDREN'S LEARNING GROUPS ON SUNDAY MORNINGS SMALL GROUP BIBLE STUDIES PRAYER MEETINGS FELLOWSHIP GROUPS VACATION BIBLE SCHOOL AND MINISTRY TO RESIDENTS IN THE LOCAL NURSING HOME. IN ADDITION PASTORAL CARE IS PROVIDED TO INDIVIDUALS AS NEEDED. AS A MISSIONS-MINDED CHURCH WE SUPPORT BOTH MISSIONS HERE AT HOME AND ABROAD.
