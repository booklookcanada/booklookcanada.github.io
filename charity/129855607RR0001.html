What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

1. WE FUND PROGRAMS THAT HELP WOMEN TO REBUILD THEIR LIVES AFTER ESCAPING VIOLENCE AND PREVENTION PROGRAMS THAT TEACH YOUTH OW TO CREATE HEALTHY RELATIONSHIPS. 2. WE FUND LIFE-CHANGING PROGRMAS DESIGNED ESPECIALLY FOR LOW-INCOME WOMEN. 3. WE FUND DYNAMIC PROGRAMS FOR GIRLS THAT EXPLORE SCIENCE AND TECHNOLOGY DO PHYSICAL ACTIVITIES BECOME MEDIA-LITERATE LEARN TO THINK CRITICALLY AND TAKE ON LEADERSHIP IN A SUPPORTIVE ALL-GIRL ENVIRONMENT. 4. WE FUND GRASSROOTS ORGANIZATIONS AND PROGRAMS THAT HELP WOMEN AND GIRLS IN CANADA EXCAPE SEX TRAFFICKING AND REBUILD THEIR LIVES AND THAT WORK TO PREVENT TRAFFICKING. 5. WE CONDUCT RESEARCH EVALUATE OUR WORK AND PUBLISH OUR FINDINGS TO ADVANC NEW KNOWLEDGE. 6. WE PROVIDE TRAINING AND SHARE OUR EXPERTISE BY HOSTING FORUMS AND REGIONAL SKILLS INSTITUTES. 7. WE PROVIDE DONOR EDUCATION INITIATIVES TO RAISE AWARENESS OF THE MOST CRITICAL ISSUES FACING WOMEN AND GIRLS IN CANADA.
