What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

WE PROVIDE AN INTEGRATED SET OF SERVICES TO ASSIST CLIENTS IN A HOLISTIC WAY TO RECONNECT WITH OUR COMMUNITY: 1) OUR FOOD PROGRAM PROVIDES THREE DAYS OF GROCERIES CONSISTENT WITH THE CANADA FOOD GUIDE AS WELL AS OTHER SUPPLIES; 2) WE SERVE HOT MEALS THROUGH A TAKE-OUT PROGRAM; 3) WE OPERATE A FOOD DELIVERY PROGRAM FOR LOW INCOME SENIORS AND OTHERS WHO ARE UNABLE TO ACCESS IN PERSON SERVICES.
