OUTSIDE LOOKING IN OFFERS A HIGH SCHOOL ACCREDITED DANCE PROGRAM PROVIDING THE OPPORTUNITY TO INDIGENOUS YOUTH AND THEIR COMMUNITIES TO ENGAGE IN LONG-TERM INTENSIVE EDUCATION AND LIFE SKILL LESSONS THROUGH DANCE. SHOULD YOUTH FULFILL THE ACADEMIC AND ATTENDANCE REQUIREMENTS OF THE PROGRAM YOUTH GET THE OPPORTUNITY TO JOIN OTHER INDIGENOUS YOUTH FROM ACROSS CANADA FOR TWO WEEKS IN TORONTO PREPARING FOR AN ULTIMATE PERFORMANCE ON STAGE TO SHOW OFF THEIR TALENTS AND ABILITIES BEFORE HUNDREDS AT THE SONY CENTRE FOR THE PERFORMING ARTS.
