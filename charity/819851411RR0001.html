What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

AS A CHURCH WE HOLD TWO WEEKLY WORSHIP SERVICES EACH SUNDAY AT 9:30 AM AND 3:30 PM. DURING THE WEEK WE OFFER VARIOUS BIBLE STUDIES FOR ALL AGES WITHIN OUR CONGREGATION. WE ALSO MINISTER TO THE COMMUNITY IN VARIOUS WAYS. WE PARTICIPATE IN THE STREETLIGHT MINISTRIES PROGRAM DOWNTOWN HAMILTON. WE CONTRIBUTE TO FOREIGN MISSION THROUGH COORDINATION WITH OTHER CHURCHES. WE ALSO SEEK TO MINISTER TO THOSE WITH FINANCIAL CONCERNS AND OTHER HARDSHIPS AS WELL AS THE SICK AND LONELY.
