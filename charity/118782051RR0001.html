What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

ALEXANDRA PARK RESIDENTS ASSOCIATION CENTER'S (APCC) MISSION IS TO PROMOTE AND ASSIST THE DEVELOPMENT AND WELL-BEING OF CHILDREN YOUTH AND FAMILIES WITHIN THEIR COMMUNITY. APCC SERVES APPROXIMATELY 1 000 RESIDENTS OF DIVERSE RACIAL AND CULTURAL HERITAGE. OF THOSE SERVED 75% ARE CHILDREN AND TEENAGERS RANGING FROM TODDLERS AND YOUNG ADULTS APPROXIMATELY 23 YEARS OF AGE. THE REMAINING 25% OF THE POPULATION SERVED ARE SENIORS AND ADULTS. FOR OVER 31 YEARS THE CENTER HAS PROVIDED COMMUNITY SOCIAL RECREATION AND DEVELOPMENT PROGRAMS
