What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

We receive and maintain a fund or funds and apply all or part of the principal and income therefrom to qualified donees by way of charitable grants. Following appropriate research and diligence selected qualified donees receive funding to advance religion specifically the Christian faith through leadership development evangelism and church planting projects. Faith-based qualified donees that are relieving adverse economic and social hardships in the majority world may also be supported.
