Who runs it — the people it pays

It reported 69 full-time positions and 151 part-time employees (year ended Mar 31, 2024). Total pay for all its positions: $7,167,246, as filed. The highest pay band it reported was “$120,000–$159,999”. 1 employee was reported in that band. Pay is reported in bands — ranges, not exact salaries. The top band is the highest range with anyone in it: it is not one person's salary, and it is not what everyone there is paid.

What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

Elmira District Community Living provides a variety of supports to adults with a developmental disability. Programs include supported group home living day supports supports for independent living and respite care. Other services include transportation recreation and community agency links.
