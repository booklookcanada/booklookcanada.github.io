What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Jul 31, 2024)

IN CLASS INSTRUCTION: FOR 15-30 CONSECUTIVE WEEKS DURING THE REGULAR SCHOOL YEAR DOANE MUSIC SCHOOL INSTRUCTORS TEACH GROUP INSTRUMENTAL MUSIC LESSONS ( THOUGH THE USE OF THE UKULELE AND SINGING ) TO STUDENTS RANGING FROM GRADE 4-12. THERE ARE 1187 STUDENTS IN 61 CLASSES MEETING EACH WEEK IN SCHOOLS AND NEIGHBOURHOOD HUBS ACROSS THE GTA. CLASSES TAKE PLACE BOTH DURING SCHOOL HOURS AND ON AN EXTRA-CURRICULAR BASIS. TEACHER TRAINING: DOANE MUSIC SCHOOL INSTRUCTIONS ARE TAUGHT THROUGH GROUP WORKSHOPS AND YEAR-ROUND CLASSROOM MONITORING ACCORDING TO THE FORMALIZED CURRICULUM. PERFORMANCE: DOANE MUSIC SCHOOL STUDENTS PERFORM IN THEIR HOME SCHOOL SETTINGS AND AT AN EVENTS OUTSIDE THEIR SCHOOLS.
