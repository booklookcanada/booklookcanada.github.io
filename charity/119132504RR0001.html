Who runs it — the people it pays

It reported 158 full-time positions and 81 part-time employees (year ended Dec 31, 2024). Total pay for all its positions: $12,232,544, as filed. The highest pay band it reported was “$160,000–$199,999”. 1 employee was reported in that band. Pay is reported in bands — ranges, not exact salaries. The top band is the highest range with anyone in it: it is not one person's salary, and it is not what everyone there is paid.
