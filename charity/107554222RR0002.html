What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Jun 30, 2024)

In conjunction with our provincial Charters and Program Delivery Affiliates operating across the country JA Canada develops and delivers practical economic and business education activities for young people through the assistance of volunteers educators and business organizations. Programs can be accessed by teachers (either using volunteers in the classroom or online) parents and students (online) all free of charge. We also host national student events and business competitions.
