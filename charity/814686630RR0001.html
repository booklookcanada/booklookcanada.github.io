What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Jan 31, 2024)

Possibilities House for Children Inc. provides humanitarian aid for impoverished orphaned at risk and abandoned children. In this capacity Possibilities House operates a children's orphanage (in Mexico) and provides each child: 'family style' residential housing food and clean water incidental needs (clothing personal care products...) healthcare support (medical dental...) educational support and learning development programs special needs programs social and emotional development programs (social workers psychologists...) physical development programs local and regional spiritual development programs and education
