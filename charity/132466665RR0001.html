What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

Our motto is 'Founded in Faith Growing In Christ'. In addition to weekly worship services where everyone is welcome we work to show God's love by reaching out to support our community. We support local organizations such as Project Share the Niagara Falls Soup Kitchen and Niagara Mobile Closet. Through our local and national church we support social justice initiatives and the Calls to Action towards reconciliation with Indigenous Peoples. We support Presbyterian World Service and Development which acts at an international level to provide disaster relief development and education. We offer a financial bursary to graduates of the Viktor Sammurtok School in Chesterfield Inlet Nunavut and also sponsor two children through Compassion Canada. In our community we offer a lending library which includes both books and puzzles aimed at reducing cognitive decline during aging. There are many opportunities for collaboration and community building which combat social isolation in seniors.
We have been offering a monthly high tea at no cost to members of the community. This activity is well attended and builds a sense of belonging and fosters well-being.
