PROVIDES THE SERVICE OF THE ROMAN CATHOLIC CHURCH TO MEMBERS OF THE COMMUNITY. IN THIS RESPECT THE PARISH HOLDS DAILY WORSHIP SERVICES AND PREPARES STUDENTS FOR THE RECEPTION OF SACRAMENTS.
