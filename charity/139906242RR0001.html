What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

WE HOLD WEEKLY WORSHIP SERVICES PROVIDE A NURSERY AND SUNDAY SCHOOL. WE ALSO LIVE STREAM THE SERVICE WE SUPPORT 6 DIFFERENT MISSIONS. PROVIDE PASTORAL AND BENEVOLENT CARE AS NEEDED. WE HOLD BIBLE STUDIES FOR MEN WOMEN AND MIXED GROUPS THAT ARE MID WEEK PROGRAMS FOR CHILDREN AND YOUTH OF ABOUT 2 HRS. IN LENGTH THE CHILDREN HAVE 5 VOLUNTEERS AND THE TEENS HAVE 4 VOLUNTEERS THERE IS A WEEKLY DROP IN FOR TEENS OF ABOUT 3 HRS WITH 4 VOLUNTEERS. WE PROVIDE A NON PROFIT CAFE FOR COMMUNITY OUTREACH OPEN 3 TIMES A WEEK FOR 12 HRS WITH 6 VOLUNTEERS. WE PROVIDE A WEEKLY PLAYGROUP FOR MUMS AND TOTS. WE HOST VBS USING 10 VOLUNTEERS AND ABOUT 15 HRS. WE PARTICIPATE AT ART ON THE WATER FRONT WITH A BREAKFAST FROM 7-9 AM THE CAFE IS ALSO OPEN WE MONITOR A KIDS ZONE WE USE 19 ADULT VOLUNTEERS AND 14 YOUTH. WE ALSO HELP WITH MEET ME ON MAIN WITH 6-8 VOLUNTEERS.
