NUMBER OF EXISTING FOSTERS TO YEAR END: 22 The majority of our applications seem to come from along the Great Lakes mostly the Toronto London Guelph Cambridge areas although we are starting to get more from a bit further north and Ottawa areas have increased. Most applications are home owners either retired or close to retirement working from home the rest are condos and apartments dwellers. The majority of applicants at 55 years to 70 years of age. Most of our foster parents adopt as the adoption applicants are given the option of becoming fosters and mostly adopt their foster dogs thus we are always requiring new foster homes. This has continued into and throughout 2024 TRANSPORT PROGRAM The transport program continued to operate to pick up dogs from shelters and bring them to foster homes and to bring them from foster homes to adopters. We work closely now with Critter Cabs a volunteer organization dedicated exclusively to transporting dogs in rescue. VOLUNTEER PROGRAM JRTRO is a completely volunteer run organization we have no paid staff and rely entirely on volunteers for all of our programs. 2024 had 20 new applicants Total number of volunteers at year end 53. SOCIAL MEDIA We currently have 1953 total contacts and 1388 subscribers to our newsletter. MERCHANDISE We once again published a JRTRO calendar.

What this page can't tell you

  • Anything after Dec 31, 2024, its newest return in the published data.
  • Who its directors are. The published data does not include them.
  • Questions worth asking it directly: Do you publish audited financial statements? Who sits on your board this year? What did last year's programs achieve, and how do you know?
