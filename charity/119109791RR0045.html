THE ROMAN CATHOLIC EPISCOPAL CORPORATION FOR THE DIOCESE OF ST. CATHARINES IN CANADA -(ST. ALFRED CHURCH)EXISTS FOR THE PURPOSE OF E ADVANCEMENT OF THE ROMAN CATHOLIC FAITH THROUGH EVANGELIZATION. 1.THE CHURCH PROVIDES DAILY WORSHIP SERVICE FOR A PRAYER AND SPIRITUAL GROWTH THAT MODEL CHRIST'S TEACHINGS; TEACHING CORE VALUES PRINCIPLES AND BELIEFS OF THE ROMAN CATHOLIC CHURCH THROUGH ADULT AND YOUTH RELIGIOUS EDUCATION PROGRAMS PREPARING AND CELEBRATING THE SACRAMENTS OF THE ROMAN CATHOLIC RELIGION. 2.RELIEVING POVERTY THROUGH COMMUNITY OUTREACH AND DONATIONS TO OTHER REGISTERED CHARITIES. 3.TEACHING SOCIAL CONSCIOUSNESS AND AWARENESS BY ENCOURAGING VOLUNTEERISM AND TEACH PARISHIONERS HOW TO MEET SOCIETAL NEEDS THROUGH VOLUNTEER AND CHARITABLE ACTIVITIES. 4.COMFORTING THE BEREAVED VISITING THE SICK AND THE ELDERLY THROUGH PASTORAL CARE TEAMS. 5.PROMOTING VOCATIONS TO THE PRIESTHOOD AND RELIGIOUS LIFE.
