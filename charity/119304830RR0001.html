What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Sep 30, 2024)

WORLD TEAM IS A MISSIONARY ORGANIZATION THAT FOCUSES ON ESTABLISHING CHURCHES. THIS INVOLVES EVANGELISM TEACHING TRAINING AND DISCIPLING INDIVIDUALS IN VARIOUS COUNTRIES. WORLD TEAM PRODUCES AND PROVIDES RESOURCES FOR INDIVIDUALS AND CHURCHES INVOLVED IN CHURCH PLANTING EFFORTS. WORLD TEAM IS ALSO INVOLVED IN TRANSLATION WORK LITERACY DEVELOPMENT AND EDUCATING CHILDREN. ADDITIONALLY WORLD TEAM OFFERS SOME HEALTH CARE RELIEF WORK AND FEEDING PROGRAMS. SHORT-TERM TRIPS TO PERU ARE ALSO CONDUCTED AS PART OF THE OUTREACH PROGRAM.
