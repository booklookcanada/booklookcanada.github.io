What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

ST CLARE’S MULTIFAITH HOUSING SOCIETY PORVIDES TRANSITIONAL AND AFFORDABLE HOUSING FOR FAMILIES AND INDIVIDUALS OF LOW OR MODEST INCOME. ST CLARE’S RESNTS SELF-CONTAINED APARTMENTS TO TENANTS WHO LIVE INDEPENDENTLY. RENT SUBSIDIES ARE PROVIDED TO TENANTS WHO QUALIFY BASED ON FINANCIAL NEED. ST. CLARE’S WORKS WITH PARTNER AGENCIES IN THE COMMUNITY THAT ASSIST PEOPLE WHO ARE HOMELESS OR MARGINALIZED. PARTNER AGENCIES REFER CLIENTS TO LIVE IN APARTMENTS OPRTATED BY ST.CLARE’S.
