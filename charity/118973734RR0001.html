INDIVIDUAL COUNSELLING ADVOCACY AND SUPPORT HOUSING PROGRAM RESPITE CARE HEALTH PROGRAM PARENT-CHILD CENTRE COMMUNITY EDUCATION PROGRAM FOOD PROGRAM JESSIE'S THE JUNE CALLWOOD CENTRE FOR YOUNG WOMEN IS A MULTI-SERVICE RESOURCE CENTRE FOR PREGNANT TEENAGERS YOUNG PARENTS AND THEIR CHILDREN. JESSIE'S HAS DEVELOPED A VARIETY OF PROGRAMS AND SERVICES TO MEET THE SPECIAL NEEDS OF YOUNG FAMILIES THROUGHOUT THE CITY OF TORONTO INCLUDING: HEALTH CARE CHILD CARE AND EDUCATION PREGNANCY AND SUPPORT PROGRAMS INCLUDE COUNSELLING CLINICS PRENATAL CLASSES POST-SECONDARY PLANNING AND SCHOLARSHIPS HOUSING COUNSELLING RESPITE AND DAILY NUTRITIOUS MEALS. THE CENTRE ALSO PROVIDES A COMMUNITY EDUCATION PROGRAM TO EDUCATE HIGH SCHOOL STUDENTS AND TO INCREASE THE AWARENESS AND SENSITIVITY OF PROFESSIONALS WHO WORK WITH YOUNG WOMEN AND YOUNG PARENTS.
