What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

1. Financial support to fund research of Alzheimer's disease and other forms of dementia. 2. Financial and administrative support to local Alzheimer Societies in Ontario that provide education and support services not only to those with Alzheimer's disease and other forms of dementia but to their families and care partners. 3. Development and coordination of support and education programs to support people throughout Ontario.
