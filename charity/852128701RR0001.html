What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

DOGS AND CATS FROM OWNER SURRENDERS ARE PLACED INTIALLY IN FOSTER HOMES AND ARANGED FOR PERMANENT ADOPTION. DOGS AND CATS FROM DOMESTIC ABUSE HOMES ARE HOUSED IN A SAFE ENVIRONMENT AND RETURNED WHEN IT IS SAFE TO DO SO OR PUT UP FOR ADOPTION. EDUCATION OF PUBLIC ON RESCUE FUNCTION.
