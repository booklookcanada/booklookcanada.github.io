What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

Sunbeam Community & Developmental Services is a transfer payment agency funded under the Services and Supports to promote the social inclusion of persons with developmental disabilities act and the Child and Family Services Act of the Province of Ontario. Sunbeam provides residential care treatment and training for individuals with developmental disability and other medical or physical challenges. Services are offered on long term short term parental relief and emergency basis through residential and day program options. Life skills training and therapeutic pool are available to residential and community clients.
