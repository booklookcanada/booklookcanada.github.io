What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

The Lakeridge Health Foundation undertakes various fundraising activities and invests funds the primary purpose of which is to transfer funds to Lakeridge Health (and related community Foundations/Associations) to be used for the purchase of capital equipment capital buildings research and education related to the health and/or social and education purposes of the community.
