What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

WE OPERATE A SHELTER FOR HOMELESS MEN IN ADDITION TO PROVIDING LOW-COST ROOM & BOARD TO THOSE WHO NEED ASSISTANCE. WE PROVIDE SHELTERING/PROGRAMMING FACILITIES FOR 'HOMES FOR SPECIAL CARE'. WE PROVIDE A HALF-WAY HOME FOR 'CORRECTIONS SERVICES CANADA' WE RUN A SOUP VAN FEEDING PROGRAM 365 DAYS A YEAR. THE VEHICLE ALSO SERVES AS AN EMERGECY VEHICLE AIDING THE EMO. WE PROVIDE PASTORAL CARE. WE OPERATE A BREAKFAST PROGRAM AND AN ANTI-BULLYING PROGRAM AT SCHOOLS. WE PROVIDE DAILY LIVING ASSISTANCE TO 'HARD TO HOUSE' INDIVIDUALS.
