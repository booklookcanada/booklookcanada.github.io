What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

KPAS OFFERS A RANGE OF PROGRAMS SERVICES AND SUPPORTS FOR PEOPLE WITH AN AUTISM SPECTRUM DISORDER THEIR FAMILIES AND OTHER SUPPORT PROVIDERS IN SOUTHERN ONTARIO. STARTING OUT OF ONE LOCATION BY A GROUP OF PARENTS THE ORGANIZATION HAS NOW GROWN TO SUPPORT OVER 6 000 PEOPLE WITH AN ASD EACH YEAR IN BOTH A RESIDENTIAL AND COMMIUNTY OUTREACH FORMAT. SUPPRTS AND SERVICES INCLUDE: *NETWORKS OF SPEICALIZED CARE THAT BRING TOGETHER DIFFERENT TEAMS TO PROVIDE SUPPORT TO PEOPLE. *RESPITE OUTREACH PROGRAMS THAT ARE ENTRENCHED IN THE LOCAL COMMUNITIES AND WORK DIRECTLY WITH FAMILIES. *FOUNDATIONS PROGRAMS WHICH INCLUDES VOCATIONAL SKILLS TRAINING *RESIDENTIAL SERVICES FOR SPECIALIZED ACCOMMODATIONS AND THERAPEUTIC TREATMENT CENTRES. *AUTISM EDUCATION AND CAPACITY BUILDING TO ASSIST OTHER SERVICE PROVIDERS IN EFFECTIVELY SUPPORTING PEOPLE WITH AN ASD.
