What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Oct 31, 2024)

Affiliated with The United Church of Canada Camp Quin-Mo-Lac is an overnight summer camp for children of all faiths age 7 to 15 years. Over the months of July and August seven 1-week camps are held to give 1 200+ children and youth an affordable experience of living together in a caring camping environment that promotes tolerance diversity and acceptance. Programs include hiking swimming canoeing archery rock wall climbing high and low ropes orienteering overnight out-tripping crafts music campfires cookouts singsongs camp-wide games special theme days talent shows and vespers to encourage physical activity personal growth and building community and relationships with others in an outdoor setting. An outdoor education program for nearby elementary and high school groups (900+ students in total) is held over the months of May and June. Many of the same activities are offered to these school groups tailored to meet their specific curriculum objectives for outdoor activity and education. Weekend events and rentals for 600 people take place in the shoulder seasons September to April giving groups access to outdoor activities and community building within their group.
