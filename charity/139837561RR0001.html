What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

AT MAGGIE'S TORONTO SEX WORKERS ACTION PROJECT WE PROVIDE COMPREHENSIZE SUPPORT TO CURRENT AND FORMER SEX WORKERS ACROSS TORONTO AND THE GTA. OUR CORE PROGRAMMING INCLUDES STREET OUTREACH CASE MANAGEMENT HARM REDUCTION INITIATIVES AND PUBLIC HEALTH SERVICES LIKE VACCINE AND TESTING CLINICS. WE ALSO OFFER WEEKLY DROP-IN PROGRAMS FOR PARENTS LGBTQ2S+ AND RACIALIZED COMMUNITIES ALONGSIDE ADVOCACY FOR THOSE FACING CRIMINALIZATION STIGMA AND DISCRIMINATION. ROOTED IN HARM REDUCTION MODEL OUR APPROACH EMPHASIZES ANTI-RACISM ANTI-OPPRESSION AND TRAUMA-INFORMED CARE AFFIRMING THAT ALL SEX WORKERS ARE BEST EQUIPPED TO MAKE DECISIONS ABOUT THEIR LIVES WHETHER THEY CHOOSE TO CONTINUE OR EXIT THE INDUSTRY.
