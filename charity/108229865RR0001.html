What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

YWCA works to transform the lives of women girls and gender diverse people who face systemic violence discrimination and poverty in Toronto by operating: -Housing programs including transitional housing for women and children experiencing homelessness violence against women shelters homeless shelters and permanent affordable and supportive housing -Employment and training programs including immigrant settlement labour access life skills training English language development pre-apprenticeship training and employment services -Early childhood education centre -Leadership training for girls and youth including a subsidized summer camp program -Family support programs such as Teen Moms and Here to Help program for women and gender diverse people who have escaped violence -December 6 Fund which provides interest-free revolving loans to survivors of violence -Case management support services and housing for women and gender diverse people who experience mental health issues and/or substance abuse. A housing program in Parkdale in partnership with Neighbourhood Land Trust to provide permanent and supportive housing for women women-led families and gender diverse people.
