What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

To give to registered incorporated charitable organizations which further one or more of the following charitable purposes: 1) provide support services for children youth aged ill and challenged persons that such individuals may require assistance to be more self-sufficient in their lives. 2) Develop co-ordinate and offer programs for children youth aged ill and challenged persons including - social rehabilitative and post-operative support programs.
