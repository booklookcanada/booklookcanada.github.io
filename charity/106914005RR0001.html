What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

PROTECTING THE WELL-BEING OF CHILDREN. CHILD WELFARE/PROTECTION OF CHILDREN UNDER THE CHILD & FAMILY SERVICES ACT. NON-RESIDENTIAL PROGRAMS - TO MONITOR FAMILIES TO ENSURE THE SAFETY AND WELL-BEING OF CHILDREN. TO MAKE DECISIONS ON PLACE OF CARE AND REMOVAL OF CHILDREN FROM FAMILY HOME. TO PROTECT THROUGH THE COURT SYSTEM OF OR WITH FAMILY MEDICATION TO PROVIDE ACCESS AS REQUIRED. RESIDENTIAL PROGRAMS - TO PROVIDE HOUSING CLOTHING MEDICAL CARE DAY TO DAY EXPENSES AND SUPPORT OF CHILDREN IN THE SOCIETIES CARE.
