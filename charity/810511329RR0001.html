What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

THE PURPOSE OF OAK RIDGES HOSPICE IS TO BRING COMPASSIONATE CARE TO THE COMMUNITY THROUGH A TRANQUIL HOME-LIKE SETTING WHERE PEOPLE WITH A TERMINAL ILLNESS CAN LIVE THEIR FINAL DAYS WITH DIGNITY AND COMFORT. THE ORGANIZATION WAS FOUNDED IN 2016 AND IS A REGISTERED CHARITY UNDER THE INCOME TAX ACT. THE HOSPICES ACTIVELY CARES FOR INDIVIDUALS IN THEIR FINAL DAYS AND UNDERTAKES FUNDRAISING ACTIVITIES TO ASSIST IN SUPPORTING ITS OPERATIONS.
