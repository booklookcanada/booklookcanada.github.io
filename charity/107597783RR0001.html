What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

THE OPERATION OF A PUBLIC LIBRARY FOR ALL MEMBERS OF THE COMMUNITY SEASONAL PATRONS AND VISITORS WHILE PROMOTING LITERACY AND COMPREHENSION. THE LIBRARY OFFERS ALL MATERIALS FREE OF CHARGE INCLUDING WELL ESTABLISHED COLLECTIONS OF FICTION NON-FICTION INDIGENOUS AND PERIODICALS IN BOTH ENGLISH AND FRENCH. FREE IFI AND COMPUTERS USE PHOTOCOPYING AND LAMINATING. WE HOLD MONTHLY CHILDREN'S CRAFT NIGHTS & POPCORN MOVIE NIGHTS. WE PARTICIPATE IN THE TD SUMMER READING PROGRAMS AND 'GAMES NIGHTS' AS PART OF OUR SUMMER PROGRAMMING FOR CHILDREN. WE OFFER TECHNOLOGY LESSONS THROUGH THE TIMISKAMING LITERACY COUNCIL. THE LIBRARY CONTINUED WITH THE 'SAUSAGE FUNDRAISER' SELLING CHOCOLATE BARS AND HELD OUR 30TH ANNUAL CHRISTMAS CRAFT AND BAKE SALE. WE DONATE OF A BURSARY TO GRADUATING STUDENTS IN BOTH THE ENGLISH AND FRENCH HIGH SCHOOLS.
IN 2024 WE PRESENTED A 'REPTILE ADVENTURE CAMP' EVENT FOR THE COMMUNITY. SENT OUR 'VALENTINES FOR VET'S'. HELD MONTHLY MOVIE EVENTS WITH THE 'PAVILION WOMEN'S CENTRE AND OFFERED FREE NEW CLOTHING FOR MEMBERS OF OUR COMMUNITY. WE WERE A 'TACKLESHARE LOANER SITE'. THE LIBRARY HELD A REMEMBRANCE DAY MOVIE AFTERNOON PRESENTED THROUGH THE NATIONAL FILM BOARD OF CANADA.
