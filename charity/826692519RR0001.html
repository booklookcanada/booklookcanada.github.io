What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

The Ministry operates without a physical location. It simply provides outreach to the community by operating a FREE clothing room. We also minister to nursing and retirement homes. There is also a prison ministry sharing the gospel and doing counselling. Any donations given to the Board of Directors are distributed directly to the community. Grocery gift cards are distributed as we are made aware of the need.

What this page can't tell you

  • Anything after Dec 31, 2024, its newest return in the published data.
