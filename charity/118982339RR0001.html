What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended May 31, 2024)

The Kingston Symphony Association performed orchestral concerts both in person and online for the enjoyment by and enrichment of residents in Kingston and the surrounding region. The Kingston Symphony presented education and outreach programs for students and for community members.
