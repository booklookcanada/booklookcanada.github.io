What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

THE GROVE THEATRE PROJECT IS BUILDING A COMMUNITY INFRASTRUCTURE SPACE ON PROPERTY OWNED BY THE CITY OF KAWARTHA LAKES FOR THE PURPOSE OF PROVIDING THEATRE PROGRAMS AND COMMUNITY EVENTS. THE FENELON FALLS HORTICULTURAL FUND IS TO FUND THE EDUCATION AND PROMOTION OF GARDENING AS WELL AS ACTIVITIES THAT TAKE PLACE ON PUBLIC PROPERTY IN THE CITY OF KAWARTHA LAKES. THE FENELON FALLS COMMUNITY FUND AND THE BOBCAYGEON COMMUNITY FUND IS TO SUPPORT THE CHARITABLE ACTIVITIES IN BOBCAYGEON AND AREA IN THE CITY OF KAWARTHA LAKES. LEGACY FUNDS FROM LOCAL FAMILIES TO SUPPORT SCHOLARSHIPS TO SECONDARY AND POST-SECONDARY INSTITUTIONS. A FUND MISHKODEH CENTRE FOR INDIGENOUS KNOWLEDGE FUND TO SUPPORT THE ACQUISITION OF PROPERTY AND THE DEVELOPMENT OF INCLUSIVE LAND BASED INDIGENOUS EDUCATION AND OTHER PROGRAMS IN BOBCAYGEON THROUGH OTHER QUALIFIED DONEES.
THE OMEMEE COMMUNITY FUND SIMILAR TO THE BOBCAYGEON COMMUNITY FUND TO SUPPORT THE CHARITABLE ACTIVITIES WITHIN SMALLER TOWNSHIP IN THE CITY OF KAWARTHA LAKES. A FUND (KEN FOUND ART FUND) TO ASSISTING IN THE PROMOTION VISIBILITY AND ACQUISITION OF LOCAL ART SUPPORTING A HEALTHLY RURAL CREATIVE ECONOMY. A FUND (100 MEN WHO CARE) TO SUPPORT CHARITABLE GIVING CIRCLES EVENTS SUPPORTING QUALIFIED DONEES. A FUND PLANT KAWARTHA SUCCEEDING THE CANOPY PROJECT CONTINUING THE PLANTING OF NEW TREES OR ENCOURAGING BIODIVERSITY ON PUBLIC PROPERTY IN THE CITY OF KAWARTHA LAKES.
