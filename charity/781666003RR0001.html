What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

Radical Connections transforms healthcare together with artists and musicians. Connecting the arts and health improves quality of life for residents patients and everyone working in and visiting healthcare settings. Our programs and activities are crafted and executed to achieve the following charitable purposes: 1. Expand and foster a global community of people working in this cross-sectoral field of arts and health to humanize healthcare and improve wellbeing both for people in care and caregivers. 2. Arts activities beneficial to the community 3. Relieving conditions attributable to being aged (and other conditions in healthcare) 4. Advancement of community arts education
