What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Jun 30, 2024)

1) TO ADVANCE THE PUBLIC'S APPRECIATION AND THE PARTICIPATION OF WOMEN IN THE LITERACY ARTS BY ESTABLISHING AND ADMINISTERING A PERIODIC AWARD TO PROMOTE HIGH-QUALITY WORKS OF LITERATURE BY WOMEN IN NORTH AMERICA; PROVIDING PUBLICLY AVAILABLE SCHOLARSHIPS BURSARIES AND OTHER FORMS OF FINANCIAL ASSISTANCE TO WOMEN WRITERS TO SUPPORT THE PRODUCTION OF HIGH-QUALITY LITERACY WORKS; OFFERING MENTORING PROGRAMS FOR WOMEN IN THE LITERARY ARTS; AND OFFERING SEMINARS CONFERENCES AND WORKSHOPS ON A VARIETY OF TOPICS RELATED TO WOMEN IN LITERATURE FOR THE BENEFIT OF WOMEN WRITERS AND THE GENERAL PUBLIC. 2) TO CONDUCT RESEARCH ON A VARIETY OF TOPICS RELATED TO WOMEN AND GENDER ROLES IN THE LITERARY ARTS AND TO DISSEMINATE THE RESULTS OF SUCH RESEARCH TO THE GENERAL PUBLIC. 3) TO RECIEVE AND MAINTAIN A FUND OR FUNDS AND TO APPLY ALL PARTS OF THE PRINCIPAL AND INCOME THEREFORE FROM TIME TO TIME TO QUALIFIED DONEES.
