What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

THE OUTPATIENT DIVISION OFFERS HABILITATION AND REBHABILITATION SERVICES TO CHILDREN WITH LONG-TERM PHYSICAL DISBILITIES/OR COMMUNICATION IMPARIMENT. SUPPORT SERVICES DIVISION PROVIDES RESPITE CARE TO CHILDREN WITH PHYSICAL DISABILITIES. SPECIAL SERVICES AT HOME PROVIDES PROGRAMMING FOR CHILDREN WITH PHYSICAL &/OR DEVELOPMENTAL HANDICAPS. CHILD DEVELOPMENT DIVISION PROVIDES ASSESSMENT AND INTERVENTION SERVICES TO FAMILIES THROUGH INFANT DEVELOPMENT & KIDS FIRST. APPLIED BEHAVIOUR ANALYSIS PROVIDES INTERVENTIONS FOR CHILDREN AND EDUCATION FOR PARENTS AND CAREGIVERS AND FOCUSES ON ONE AREA OF A CHILD'S SPECIFIC BEHAVIOR GOALS AT A TIME- PROGRAM WORKS THOUGH SPECIFIC TREATMENT IN SMALL GROUP OR INDIVIDUAL SESSIONS.
