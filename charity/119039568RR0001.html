What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

Boost is a creative community response to child abuse investigations. A partnership among local community and government agencies it brings together all professionals involved in child abuse cases under one roof for a coordinated interdisciplinary response to child abuse victims in Toronto. Programs include Prevention & Education Advocacy Assessment-Directed Therapy Child victim Witness Support and Coordination. Child Victim Witness Support program extends beyond Toronto's borders to other regions in Ontario.
