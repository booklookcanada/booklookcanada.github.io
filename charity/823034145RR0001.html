What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

ICC HOSTS CITIZENSHIP CEREMONIES ACROSS CANADA. THESE COMMUNITY CEREMONIES CELEBRATE CANADA'S NEWEST CITIZENS AND OFFER UNIQUE OPPORTUNITIES FOR THEM AND MEMBERS OF THEIR COMMUNITIES TO REFLECT ON WHAT CANADIAN CITIZENSHIP MEANS. CANOO IS A MOBILE APP THAT PROVIDES NEW CANADIAN CITIZENS AND PERMANENT RESIDENTS FREE ADMISSION TO OVER 1400 OF CANADA'S BEST CULTURE AND NATURE ATTRACTIONS AS WELL AS VOLUNTEERING OPPORTUNITIES PREMIUM ACCESS TO SPECIAL EVENTS AND OTHER VALUABLE BENEFITS TO HELP THEM FEEL AT HOME IN CANADA. CONOO IS AVAILABLE FOR FREE ON THE APPLE STORE AND GOOGLEE PLAY STORE. THE ICC ALSO CONDUCTS PUBLIC POLICY RESEARCH INTO PRESSING QUESTIONS OF CITIZENSHIP AND IMMAGRATION. OUR RESEARCH IS NOVEL PRACTICAL AND ACTION-ORIENTED.
