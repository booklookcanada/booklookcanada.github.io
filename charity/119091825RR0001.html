What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

The Peterborough Regional Health Centre Foundation raises funds and manages donor gifts for the purpose of supporting outstanding patient care at the Peterborough Regional Health Centre. This includes raising funds for identified capital priorities and for the purchase of new medical equipment to hasten diagnosis and treatment of diseases shorten wait times and support the education and training of PRHC's clinical leaders to ensure great care is available at our regional hospital.
