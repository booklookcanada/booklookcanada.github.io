What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

THE PURPOSE OF THIS CHARITY IS TO PROMOTE JEWISH KNOWLEDGE AWARENESS AND PRACTICE TO BOTH YOUNG AND OLD ACHIEVE AND MAINTAIN JEWISH IDENTITY AMONG THE MEMBER OF THE COMMUNITY UNIVERSITY STUDENTS AND VISITORS BY PROVIDING EDUCATIONAL SPIRITUAL AND RELIGIOUS SERVICES MEETING THEIR JEWISH NEEDS. THE CHARITY OFFERS HEBREW SCHOOL OF THE ARTS AND PROGRAMS FOR THE CHILDREN AFTERNOON TEEN CLASSES LECTURES/SEMINARS AND SHABBAT MEALS FOR UNIVERSITY/COLLEGE STUDENTS ADULT EDUCATION AND MINYAN SERVICE THROUGHOUT THE YEAR. HOLIDAY PROGRAMS AND PACKAGES INCLUDING HIGH HOLIDAY SERVICES SUKKOT BASH MENORAH LIGHTING/PARTY'S TUBISHVAT PURIM BANQUESTS PASSOVER SEDERS LAHBOMER OUTINGS SHAVUOT READINGS ON A YEARLY BASIS. THROUGHOUT THE TOURIST SEASONS THE CHARITY HOLDS DAILY MINYAN SERVICES AM/PM KOSHER RESTAURANT ARRANGES TRIPS FOR EDUCATIONAL SCHOOL/CAMPS PROVIDING THEIR ACCOMODATIONS FOOD AND ACTIVITIES WHILE INFUSING IT WITH SPIRITUALITY AND MEANING. OUTREACH LIFECYCLE EVENTS AND SIMPLY BEING A SOURCE OF SPIRITUAL MATERIAL AND SOCIAL SUPPORT FOR THE JEWISH COMMUNITY CONTINUE TO BE OUR FOCUS.
