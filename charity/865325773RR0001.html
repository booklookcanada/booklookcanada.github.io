What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

1. Spay/neuter program and microchip for abandoned lost and feral cats in the community. 2. Foster care program for abandoned and lost cats in our community while waiting for a permanent home. 3. Adoption program for abandoned and lost cats and kittens in our community. 4. Commitment of the heart program: provided housing and medical care for special needs and elderly cats that may be difficult toadopt into a permanent home. VOLUNTEERS ARE USED IN THE FOLLOWING CAPACITIES: TWICE DAILY CLEANING OF LIVING SPACE OF CATS AT THE MAIN FOSTERHOME: FEEDING OF THE CATS; TRANSPORTATION OF THE CATS TO MEDICAL APPOINTMENTS; CO-ORDINATE RESCUE OF CATSNEEDING ATTENTION MEET WITH POTENTIAL ADOPTING FAMILIES TO ANSWER QUESTIONS AND MONITOR ADOPTION PROCESS;FOSTERING OF CATS IN THEIR OWN HOMES UNTIL READY FOR ADOPTION; SOCIAL MEDIA TO PROMOTE CHARITY INCLUDINGWEBSITE AND OTHER ADMINISTRATIVE DUTIES. CURRENTLY WE HAVE 40-45 VOLUNTEERS THAT CONTRIBUTE ANYWHERE FROM 1HOUR TO 20 HOURS PER WEEK
