What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

TRINITY UNITED CHURCH PROVIDES A PLACE OF WORSHIP FOR THE COMMUNITY AND A MEETING PLACE FOR CHILDREN'S AND ADULT'S GROUPS OPERATING IN THE COMMUNITY(SHUFFLEBOARD EUCHRE COMMUNITY MEALS EXERCISE GROUPS CRAFT GROUPS AND OTHER FELLOWSHIP GATHERINGS). THE CHURCH ALSO SERVICES THE RELIGIOUS NEED OF THE COMMUNITY INCLUDING SERVICES TO SHUT-INS PEOPLE IN THE HOSPITAL AND THE ELDERLY. THE CHURCH HOSTS A MONTHLY COMMUNITY MEAL NIGHT WHEREBY THE GENERAL PUBLIC ARE ENCOURAGED TO SHARE A MEAL WITH OTHERS FREE OF CHARGE. THE COMMUNITY MEAL IS NON-DENOMINATIONAL. EXERCISE GROUPS MEET BI-WEEKLY AND CONTINUE TO BE POPULAR. THE ROYAL CONSERVATORY OF MUSIC USES THE CHURCH AS ONE OF ITS TESTING CENTRES FOR CHILDREN AND ADULTS IN THE COMMUNITY. THE CHURCH ALSO OPERATES A SOUP PROGRAM FOR THE NEEDY AND HOMELESS COMMUNITY WHERE INDIVIDUALS AND FAMILIES CAN SEEK FOOD AND OTHER NEEDS.
WE ARE DEVELOPING NEW ACTIVITIES AND PROGRAMS TO INCREASE THE ACTIVE MEMBERSHIP. SOME EXAMPLES INCLUDE POTLUCKS CELEBRATIONS AND GET TOGETHERS.
