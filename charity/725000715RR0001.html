Who runs it — the people it pays

It reported 8 full-time positions and 11 part-time employees (year ended Dec 31, 2024). Total pay for all its positions: $418,970, as filed. The highest pay band it reported was “$40,000–$79,999”. 4 employees were reported in that band. Pay is reported in bands — ranges, not exact salaries. The top band is the highest range with anyone in it: it is not one person's salary, and it is not what everyone there is paid.

What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

The charity's purpose is to relieve poverty and promote health equity by operating a free or low-income dental clinic to improve overall health for people who are living on low incomes in London Ontario. Clinic operations commenced on January 4 2021. Dental care services continue to be delivered by a team of employees and trained volunteers.
