What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

First the Foundation brings donors to the table as community builders helping them formulate and realize their philanthropic goals matching their interest and concerns with community needs and organizations and finding innovative and responsible ways to give their investments lasting impact. Second the Foundation takes the broadest view of what a community is and what it needs to succeed. This is why grants from the Foundation support everything from shelter sustenance and care for those in need to recreation the arts and the environment. Third the Foundation takes the long term view for the community identifying long term needs and opportunities and investing through grants in sustainable solutions helping our communities become resilient resourceful and ready for the future. Mississauga Vital Signs: The Foundation participates in this national initiative which provides donors organizations and the broader community with information in key quality of life indicators helping the community identify area of strength as well as opportunities to invest resources for greater impact.
