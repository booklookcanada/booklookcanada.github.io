What it says it does

THE FOUNDATIONS PURPOSE IS TO FUND OTHER REGISTERED CHARITIES THAT HAVE THE PURPOSE TO SUPPORT ANIMAL WELFARE MEDICAL AND SCIENTIFIC RESEARCH JEWISH ORGANIZATIONS AND UNDERTAKE ANY OTHER PURPOSE AS SELECTED BY THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS IN COMPLIANCE WITH THE PERMISSIBLE ACTIVITIES FOR A 501(C)(3) ENTITY AS PRESCRIBED BY THE INTERNAL REVENUE CODE.
