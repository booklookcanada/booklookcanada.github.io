What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

CANADIAN TIRE JUMPSTART CHARITIES (JUMPSTART) HELPS FINANCIALLY DISADVANTAGED CHILDREN GAIN THE BENEFITS ASSOCIATED WITH PARTICIPATING IN SPORTS AND RECREATIONAL ACTIVITIES BY PROVIDING FUNDING TO ASSIST WITH COST OF REGISTRATION EQUIPMENT AND TRANSPORTATION. JUMPSTART ALSO FUNDS ACCESSIBILITY GRANTS AND BUILDS ACCESSIBLE PLAYSPACES TO ALLOW KIDS OF ALL ABILITIES TO BE INVOLVED IN SPORTS AND RECREATION. JUMPSTART ALSO HAS A GENDER EQUITY PORTFOLIO WHICH INCLUDES A LEADERSHIP PROGRAM AIMED AT BRINGING TOGETHER YOUNG FEMALE ATHLETES AND THEIR COACHES TO DEVELOP LEADERSHIP SKILLS GAIN CONFIDENCE AND BUILD LASTING RELATIONSHIPS THAT CONTRIBUTE TO GENDER EQUITY WITHIN THEIR COMMUNITY AND SPORT ORGANIZATION.
