What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

The Charity continues to sponsor the Amazing Love School Project in Nakyere near Iganga in Eastern Uganda. Amazing Love School provides Education opportunity to children in the Neighborhood and beyond. These children would not have the opportunity for Education without this project. The project started with the construction of classrooms for Primary level Education and has now expanded to include a Library Solar Power and Water. The Charity will also continue to work in partnership with other Charities to sponsor Community based Development Projects in Uganda
The Charity will focus on the Classroom expansion and student accommodation sponsorship for Amazing Love School in Nakyere Eastern Uganda. The Charity will also continue to work in partnership with VIVA Church (Oshawa-Canada) in sponsoring new Community based Development initiatives in Iganga Eastern Uganda being Supervised by Life Church Limited.
