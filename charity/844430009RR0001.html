What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Jun 30, 2024)

THE PUBLIC POLICY FORUM BUILDS BRIDGES AMONG DIVERSE PARTICIPANTS IN THE POLICY-MAKING PROCESS AND GIVES THEM A PLATFORM TO EXAMINE ISSUES OFFER NEW PERSPECTIVES AND FEED FRESH IDEAS INTO CRITICAL POLICY DISCUSSIONS. WE BELIEVE GOOD POLICY IS CRITICAL TO MAKING A BETTER CANADA-A COUNTY THAT'S COHESIVE PROSPEROUS AND SECURE. WE CONTRIBUTE BY: - CONDUCTING RESEARCH ON CRITICAL ISSUES - CONVENING ROUNDTABLES TO FOSTER HONEST DIALOGUE - RECOGNIZING EXCEPTIONAL LEADERS OUR APPROACH - CALLED INCLUSION TO CONCLUSION - BRINGS EMERGING AND ESTABLISHED VOICES TO POLICY CONVERSATIONS WHICH INFORM CONCLUSIONS THAT IDENTIFY OBSTACLES TO SUCCESS AND PATHWAYS FORWARD. PPF IS AN INDEPENDENT NON-PARTISAN CHARITY WHOSE MEMBERS ARE A DIVERSE GROUP OF PRIVATE PUBLIC AND NON-PROFIT ORGANIZATIONS.
