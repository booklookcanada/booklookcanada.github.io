What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Jun 30, 2024)

ENACTUS CANADA OPERATES PROGRAMS ON VARIOUS UNIVERSITY AND COLLEGE CAMPUSES ACROSS THE COUNTRY. GUIDED BY ACADEMIC ADVISORS AND BUSINESS EXPERTS THE STUDENT LEADERS OF ENACTUS CANADA CREATE AND IMPLEMENT COMMUNITY EMPOERMENT PROJECTS AND BUSINESS VENTURES IN COMMUNITIES COAST TO COAST THAT AIM TO USE BUSINESS AND ENTREPRENEURSHIP TO IMPROVE THE LIVELIHOODS OF THOSE IN NEED. TO CELEBRATE THE ENTREPRENEURIAL SPIRIT OF YOUNG CANADIANS ENACTUS CANADA RUNS THE STUDENT ENTREPRENEUR NATIONAL COMPETITION. THE STUDENT ENTREPRENEUR NATIONAL COMPETITION ENHANCES THE SUCCESS OF FULL-TIME STUDENTS OPERATING BUSINESSES WHO ARE CREATING JOBS AND ADNVACING THE CANADIAN ECONOMY. ENACTUS AS AN EXPERIENTIAL LEARNING PLATFORM HELPS STUDENTS UNLEASH THEIR ENTREPRENEURIAL SPIRIT AND DEVELOP THE TALENT AND PERSPECTIVE ESSENTIAL TO LEADERSHIP IN OUR EVER CHANGING WORLD.
