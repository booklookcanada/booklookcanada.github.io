What it says it does

THE FOUNDATION CONTINUES TO PROVIDE SCHOLARSHIPS TO POST-SECONDARY STUDENTS IN AFRICA PROVIDES GRANTS TO ASHESI UNIVERSITY TO CARRY OUT ACTIVITIES RELATED TO HEALTH COLLABORATIVE AND EDUCATION COLLABORATIVE. THE HEALTH COLLABORTIVE WITH ASHESHI UNIVERSITY SUPPORTS STUDENTS AND ALUMNI ENTREPRENEURS TO BUILD VENTURES IN THE HEALTHCARE ECOSYSTEM AND TO WORK ALONGSIDE THE HEALTHCARE SYSTEM AND OTHER EDUCATION INSTITUTIONAL ACTORS TO CO-CREATE SOLUTIONS THROUGH A SERIES OF PROGRAMS. THE EDUCATION COLLABORATION WITH ASHESHI UNIVERSITY SUPPORTS STUDENTS BY PROVIDING WORKSHOPS TRAINING PROGRAMS AND CONFERENCES ON SUBJECTS RELATED TO TEACHING AND POST-SECONDARY EDUCATION IN AFRICA. THE FOUNDATION CONTINUES TO SUPPORT THE DEVELOPMENT OF A CO-ED DORMITORY BUILDING AT ASHESI UNIVERSITY CONSISTING OF SIX BUILDINGS LINKBY BY CORRIDORS AND COURTYARDS WHICH IS EXPECTED TO HOUSE 264 UNIVERSITY STUDENTS. THE FOUNDATION PROVIDES FUNDING TO THE ENTREPRENUERSHIP CENTRE AT ASHESHI UNIVERSITY TO ADVANCE EDUCATION BY PROVIDINT ENTREPRENEURIAL TRAINING AND RESOURCES TO RECENT GRADUATES.
