What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

Transitional residence for women paroled from federal & provincial prisons satellite apartments to accommodate sentenced women with children -Individual and group counselling life skills practical assistance & resources to women in the residence. -Outreach counselling & community reintegration support. -Counselling programs including substance use parenting partner assault response healing from abuse shoplifting fraud -Direct accountability program for individuals with first time non violent minor crimes operating out of college park courthouse -Counselling and release planning for women in the prison system – volunteer program community outreach -Pre-employment job development program -TREM Trauma Recovery & Empowerment Model to support survivors & their families to prevent & address gender-based violence. -Homes for Good: using evidence-based CTI model homeless women leaving the correctional facility Vanier Centre for women will be supported. - Feminist Response and Recovery (We Learn) from WAGE - Record Suspension Program - One on One assisting with submitting a pardon application to the national parole Board of Canada.
