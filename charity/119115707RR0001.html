What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Apr 30, 2024)

Renison University College is a college teaching in the arts and social sciences affiliated with the University of Waterloo. Renison offers courses in the departments of Social Development Studies and Culture and Language studies. We offer post graduate programs in the school of Social Work. In addition to degree courses and programs Renison also runs a student residence and a centre for community and professional education. In addition to teaching our charitable programs include issuing scholarships and bursaries to students as well as providing the opportunity to expand their spiritual journeys through our Ministry Centre and Chapel.
