What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

ONGOING PROGRAMS: WE OBTAIN NON-PERISHABLE FOOD FROM RECLAMATION COMPANIES DONATIONS AND FOOD DRIVES. THE FOOD IS SORTED AND STORED THEN DISTRIBUTED TO OUR MEMBER AGANCIES ON A FAIR SHARE BASIS. MEMBER AGENCIES ARE FOOD BANK/CUPBOARDS ETC. THAT PROVIDE FOOD TO NEEDY INVIDIDUALS IN THE CITY OF KAWARTHA LAKES. OTHER ACTIVITIES INCLUDE PROMOTION OF AWARENESS AND ITS IMPACT. WE ALSO PURCHASE/GROW AND DISTRIBUTE PERISHABLE FOOD INCLUDING FRUIT AND VEGETABLES TO ENHANCE NUTRITIONAL VALUE OF FOOD AVAILABLE IN LOCAL SCHOOL BREAKFAST PROGRAMS AND FOR FAMILIES SERVED BY OUR MEMBER AGENCIES.
