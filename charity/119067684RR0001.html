What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

THE ADVANCEMENT OF ROMAN CATHOLIC RELIGIOUS WORSHIP INSTRUCTIONS AND MINISTRY. IN THIS RESPECT THE CONGREGATION HOLDS WORSHIP SERVICES BOTH DAILY AND ON WEEKENDS. PREPARES AND INSTRUCTS INDIVIDUALS FOR THE RECEPTION OF SACRAMENTS. ORGANIZES SPECIAL DAYS OF RETREAT. COUNSELS ADULTS CONTEMPLATING MARRIAGE. ASSISTS THE SICK DYING AND THOSE IN GRIEF. PROVISION OF A SOCIETY MINISTRY - THE CONGREGATION ASSISTS THE NEEDY HOMELESS AND MENTALLY ILL ITS NEIGHBOURHOOD BY PROVIDING FOOD VOUCHERS CLOTHING ETS. ASSISSTING IN THE REFERRAL OF INDIVIDUALS AND FAMILES IN CRISIS TO RELEVANT SOCIAL AGENCIES.
