1. EDUCATION: WE COLLABORATE WITH LOCAL EDUCATION AUTHORITIES PROVIDING ACCESS TO QUALITY EDUCATION FOR CHILDREN AND YOUTH. OUR INITIATIVES INCLUDE PRESCHOOL PRIMARY CLASSES VOCATIONAL TRAINING AND SUPPORT FOR STUDENTS RE-ENTERING EDUCATION AFTER CONFLICTS OR CRISIS WITH A SPECIAL FOCUS ON EDUCATION DURING EMERGENCIES. 2. HEALTH: PARTNERING WITH LOCAL AUTHORITIES WE STRENGTHEN HEALTHCARE SYSTEMS. WE TRAIN COMMUNITY HEALTHCARE WORKERS TO DELIVER EFFECTIVE CARE TO CHILDREN ADOLESCENT GIRLS AND WOMEN. OUR EFFORTS ENCOMPASS REPRODUCTIVE HEALTH SERVICES HIV TUBERCULOSIS MALARIA AND COVID-19 PREVENTION AND TREATMENT. WE ADVOCATE FOR INDIVIDUALS' RIGHT TO HEALTHCARE SERVICES. 3. PROTECTION FROM VIOLENCE: WE IMPLEMENT PROGRAMS ENSURING THE SAFETY OF CHILDREN PARTICULARLY GIRLS BY COMBATING PRACTICES LIKE CHILD MARRIAGE AND GENDER-BASED VIOLENCE. YOUTH LEADERSHIP AND ECONOMIC EMPOWERMENT: OUR PROGRAMS EMPOWER CANADIAN YOUTH (AGES 14-24) TO ENGAGE WITH GLOBAL ISSUES AND ENHANCE THEIR ADVOCACY SKILLS. INTERNATIONALLY WE OFFER FINANCIAL LITERACY TRAINING SAVINGS AND LOAN GROUPS AND CHALLENGE HARMFUL IDEOLOGIES HINDERING GENDER EQUALITY ALONGSIDE COMMUNITIES AND YOUTH-LED MOVEMENTS. 5. HUMANITARIAN RESPONSE AND RESILIENCE: IN EMERGENCIES WE PROVIDE LIFE SAVING FOOD AID CASH VOUCHERS WATER AND PROTECT CHILDREN BY CREATING SAFE SPACES.
