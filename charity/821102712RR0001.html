What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

Workshops - serving type 1 diabetic experiential education / support. Family Camps - Serving type 1 diabetic families with experiential education /support. At-Risk Youth - Serving type 1 diabetic teens who are struggling to reach standards of health despite numerous efforts of traditional intervention. Extreme Adventures - Serving type 1 diabetics by providing safe and responsible support to extend their range of abilities and utilize a large challenge to find motivation and empowerment that transforms their everyday. Diabetes Sports Camp -is a comprehensive individualized and innovative approach to the support of exercise for children and teens with type 1 diabetes (T1D). It builds on the framework of existing camp programming by integrating I Challenge Diabetes' expert mentorship to demonstrate healthy behavior's in exercise/insulin control. Support in tracking identifying and problem solving challenges with insulin and exercise will increase participants' physical literacy and safety to promote a long active lifestyle.
