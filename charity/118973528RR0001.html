What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

THE JEAN TWEED CENTRE IS THE LARGEST NON-MEDICAL COMMUNITY-BASED SUBSTANCE ABUSE AND PROBLEM-GAMBLING TREATMENT FACILITY FOR WOMEN AND THEIR FAMILIES IN ONTARIO. WE OFFER RESIDENTIAL DAY AND OUTPATIENT PROGRAMS FOR WOMEN AND THEIR FAMILIES. OUR KEY PROGRAMS ARE: SUBSTANCE ABUSE AND PROBLEM GAMBLING; TRAUMA; CONTINUING CARE; SUPPORTIVE HOUSING; FAMILY AND CHILDREN'S SERVICES.
