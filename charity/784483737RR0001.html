What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

1. OUTREACH PROGRAM: THIS PROGRAM PROVIDES SUPPLIES FOOD COUNSELLING AND REFERRAL TO OTHER AGENCIES AS NEEDED TO VULNERABLE INDIVIDUALS AND FAMILIES IN NEW TECUMSETH & SURROUNDING AREAS. 2. EMERGENCY OVERNIGHT SHELTER PROGRAM (NOV-APR) - OFFERS OVERNIGHT SHELTER TO INDIVIDUALS AND COUPLES EXPERIENCING HOMELESSNESS WITH NO HOUSING ALTERNATIVES. AS A SHORT-TERM HOUSING-FOCUSED SHELTER THE PROGRAM IS INTENDED TO BRIDGE THE GAP BETWEEN ENTERING HOMELESSNESS AND MOVING INT SAFE AND SUSTAINABLE HOUSING. 3. COMMUNITY DINNER PROGRAM - THE COMMUNITY DINNER PROGRAM IS RUN DAILY DURING THE REGULAR OPERATING SEASON (WINTER). THE PROGRAM IS OPEN TO ANYONE IN THE COMMUNITY LOOKING FOR A WARM MEAL AND SOME FRIENDSHIP ACCOMMODATING UP TO 12 PARTICIPANTS IN THE SPACE AT A TIME. INTAKE WORKERS ARE ON SITE TO PROVIDE RESOURCES AND SUPPORT UPON REQUEST.
