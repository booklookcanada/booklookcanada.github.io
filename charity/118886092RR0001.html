What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

THE DISTRICT OF TORONTO WORKS WITH CHILDREN AND YOUTH REGARDING ALCOHOL AND DRUG CONCERNS THROUGH YOUTH RETREATS RALLIES AND DAY CAMPS. GENERAL MEMBER AND GUEST MEETINGS ARE HELD 4-6 TIMES A YEAR TO INFORM MEMBERS OF THE ORGANIZATION’S ACTIVITIES AND TO ALLOW SPECIAL SPEAKERS TO BE BROUGHT IN. DONATIONS ARE GIVEN TO ORGANIZATIONS THAT HELP THOSE WITH ADDICTIONS OR ADDICTION AWARENESS AS WELL AS THOSE AIDING IN HELPING THOSE IN NEED.
