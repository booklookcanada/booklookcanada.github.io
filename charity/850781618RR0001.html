What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended May 31, 2024)

WE ASSIST WITH THE PURCHASE OF MEDICAL EQUIPMENT AND MEDICAL RESEARCH THROUGH GRANTS TO HOSPITALS LONG TERM CARE FACILITIES AND SPECIALIZED HEALTH ORGANIZATION. WE SUPPORT COMMUNITY SERVICES THROUGH GRANTS T NURSING HOMES HOSPICES AND EMERGENCY SERVICES. WE PROVIDE COMFORT AND ENTERTAINMENT TO THE VETERANS RESIDING AT SUNNYBROOK HEALTH SCIENCES CENTRE IN TORONTO ONTARIO. WE PURCHASE AND GIVE OUT APARTMENT KITS (BEDDING TOWELS DISHES COOKWARE ETC.) TO VETERANS THAT WERE PREVIOSLY HOMELESS AND ARE NOW GETTING A ROOM OR APARTMENT WE PARTNER WITH LOCAL COLLEGES TO PROVIDE DENTAL CARE TO HOMELESS VETERANS. WE GIVE BURSARIES TO STUDENTS FOR POST SECONDARY EDUCATION.
