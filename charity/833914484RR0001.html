What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

Community Food Centres Canada (CFCC) builds and supports vibrant food-focused organizations in low-income communities across the country that bring people together to grow cook share and advocate for policies that improve the health and well-being of marginalized Canadians. We provide stable funding resources and a proven program model to local Affiliate Community Food Centres so they can operate responsive and impact driven Community Food Centres. We also work with the broader food movement (providing training leadership grants) to build greater capacity for impact and to empower communities to work toward a healthy and fair food system.
