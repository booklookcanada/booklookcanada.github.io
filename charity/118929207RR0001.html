What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

1. We conduct research projects in key target areas of Education Work & Economics Communities (formerly Social Cities) Families and Law. 2. We research publish and distribute a variety of journals and briefs targeted for the educational benefit of the general public Christian and business leaders students government leaders academics and lawyers. 3. We publish Comment Magazine which serves Christian leaders and culture makers and electronically publish Convivium magazine connecting people with faith in common life. 4. We host conferences events and round tables to bring public attention to our research and programs. 5. We operate the Cardus Religious School initiative in cooperation with multiple universities in North America. 6. We operate the Cardus Religious Freedom Institute designed to research Canadian religious freedom issues for the benefit of all Canadians government and interested institutions and to strengthen the national network of religious freedom participants. 7. Spirited citizenship conducts public opinion research and analysis into faith and the faithful in Canadian public life. The research findings are used to stimulate public conversation focused on faith motivated activities and organizations.
