UNITED WAY ALSO PROVIDES DIRECT COMMUNITY SERVICE BY PARTICIPATING IN AND/OR FACILITATING DISCUSSIONS AMONG LOCAL ORGANIZATIONS TO PROMOTE THE ENHANCEMENT AND EXPANSION OF SOCIAL SERVICES IN THE COMMUNITY.
