Who runs it — the people it pays

It reported 48 full-time positions and 6 part-time employees (year ended Dec 31, 2024). Total pay for all its positions: $5,903,081, as filed. The highest pay band it reported was “$350,000 and over”. 1 employee was reported in that band. Pay is reported in bands — ranges, not exact salaries. The top band is the highest range with anyone in it: it is not one person's salary, and it is not what everyone there is paid.

What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

AS CANADA'S LARGEST CHILD AND YOUTH SERVING ORGANIZATION BOYS AND GIRLS CLUBS OF CANADA PROVIDES VITAL PROGRAMS AND SERVICES TO ALMOST 150 000 YOUNG PEOPLE IN OVER 600 LOCATIONS ACROSS CANADA. DURING CRITICAL OUT-OF-SCHOOL HOURS OUR CLUBS OFFER SAFE SPACES WHERE YOUNG PEOPLE CAN EXPLORE THEIR INTERESTS DEVELOP THEIR STRENGTHS AND REALIZE POSITIVE OUTCOMES IN SELF EXPRESSION ACADEMICS HEALTHY LIVING PHYSICAL ACTIVITY MENTAL HEALTH AND MORE. OUR TRAINED STAFF AND VOLUNTEERS HELP YOUNG PEOPLE BUILD THE CONFIDENCE AND SENSE OF BELONGING THEY NEED TO OVERCOME BARRIERS FORM POSITIVE RELATIONSHIPS AND MATURE INTO RESPONSIBLE CARING ADULTS.
