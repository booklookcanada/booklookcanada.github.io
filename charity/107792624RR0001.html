What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

During the past year Off Lake Bible Church held weekly Sunday morning worship services children's Sunday School as well as weekly Bible study and prayer meetings with the main goal of sharing the Gospel of Jesus Christ throughout the local community and the World at large. Off Lake Bible Church provided financial support to other Qualified Donees who spread the gospel of Jesus Christ and provided aid relief to individuals and groups in Canada and other parts of the world. Off Lake Bible Church hosted an AWANA Kids Club ('Approved Workmen Are Not Ashamed' as taken from 2 Timothy 2:15) on a weekly basis during the school year. Children from ages 3-10 were enrolled in the Club and through memory work Bible studies and other fun activities the children were taught a Biblical worldview by building up their Bible knowledge and instilling in them a love and respect for God and His Word.
In the upcoming year we will be offering week-long faith-based wilderness canoe trips. The primary goal is to share the gospel with youth while experiencing god creation in a camping/canoe setting.
