What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

THE CENTRE PROVIDES A SAFE PLACE TO HELP HEAL END CHILD ABUSE FOR NIAGARA’S CHILDREN AND YOUTH WHO HAVE EXPERIENCED ALLEGED PHYSICAL ABUSE SEXUAL ABUSE BECOME THE TARGETS OF INTERNET LURING OR WERE THE UNWILLING WITNESS OF VIOLENCE. THE CENTRE OFFERS MANY PROGRAMS AND SERVICES SUCH AS PLAY THERAPY ONE TO ONE COUNSELLING SOCIAL SUPPORT PEACEFULL WARRIORS – YOGA AND MEDITATION THERAPY SUMMER CAMPS AND MORE. THE CENTRE ALSO HOST 4 ANNUAL EVENTS THE SPOTLIGHT GALA AMAZING RACE NIAGARA KIDS GOLFING FOR KIDS AND THE NIAGARA CYCLING MARATHON. ALL EVENTS AND PROGRAMS ARE FUNDED THROUGH DONATIONS GRANTS AND GIFTS.
