What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

Hospice Mississauga provides physical emotional and spiritual support to those living with terminal illness and their family members. We support clients in their homes or care facilities. There are no direct costs to clients for our services. Our services are: 1) Care Coordination 2) Visiting Volunteer Program 3) Day Program 4) Spiritual Care 5) Bereavement Support 6) Children's Grief Program - Help Us Understand Grief (H.U.U.G.) 7) Volunteer Recruitment and Training and 8) Wellness program including Therapeutic Touch Reiki and Aromatherapy therapies to clients and care givers. 9) 24/7 Palliative Care Helpline
