What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

The charity relies on corporate donations donations from online charities and private donations to buy clothing for needy children young adults older adults in need and families in need in the community. The charity works with local schools to help provide clothing and footwear to those in need. The charity does not sell anything. There are approximately 4 to 15 volunteers to help shop sort compile and deliver clothing which requires anywhere from 20 to 50 hours each sometimes more.
