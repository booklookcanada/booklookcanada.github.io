THE CANADIAN FRIENDS OF THE HEBREW UNIVERSITY (CFHU) PROVIDED FUNDING TO THE HEBREW UNIVERSITY OF JERUSALEM WHICH IS A REGISTERED FOREIGN UNIVERSITY AND A QUALIFIED DONEE TO CARRY OUT POST SECONDARY EDUCATIONAL & RESEARCH ACTIVITIES AS WELL AS PROVIDING SCHOLARSHIPS AND RESEARCH GRANTS. CFHU ALSO RECRUITED STUDENTS FROM CANADA TO STUDY AT THE HEBREW UNIVERSITY OF JERUSALEM.
