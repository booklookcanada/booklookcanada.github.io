What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

PROVIDES THE SERVICE OF THE ROMAN CATHOLIC CHURCH TO MEMBERS OF THE COMMUNITY. IN THIS RESPECT THE PARISH HOLDS DAILY WORSHIP SERVICES AND PREPARES STUDENTS FOR THE RECEPTION OF SACRAMENTS. PROVIDES PROGRAMS OF PRAYER AND SPIRITUAL GROWTH. PROVIDES EDUCATION FOR SEMINARIANS AND PRIESTS. PROVIDES PASTORAL CARE TO THE NEEDY HOMELESS AND MENTALLY ILL. PROVIDES COMFORT TO THE BEREAVED. PREPARES ADULTS FOR MARRIAGE. PROVIDES MARRIAGE AND FUNERAL SERVICES. ORGANIZES SPECIAL DAYS OF PRAYER AND SPIRTUAL GROWTH. ASSISTS THE SICK AND DYING. REFERS INDIVIDUALS AND FAMILIES TO RELEVANT SOCIAL SERVICE PROGRAMS AVAILABLE TO THEM.
