What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended May 31, 2024)

THE PADDY WALKER HERITAGE SOCIETY CARRIES ON THE OPERATION OF THE HISTORICAL WALKER HOUSE INN LOCATED IN KINCARDINE ONTARIO. THE BUILDING HOUSES ARTIFACTS FROM THE TIME PADDY WALKER IMMIGRATED TO CANADA IN 1850 TO PRESENT DAY. THE MUSEUM AND HERITAGE CENTRE'S GOAL IS TO PRESERVE OUR ANCESTOR'S LEGACIES. THE MUSEUM IS OPEN TO THE PUBLIC FOR GUIDED AND SELF-GUIDED TOURS FROM JUNE TO LABOUR DAY WEEKEND AND BY SPECIAL APPOINTMENT THE REST OF THE YEAR. WE HIRE LOCAL STUDENTS TO STAFF THE MUSEUM DURING THE SUMMER. THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS IS ENTIRELY VOLUNTEER AND LOOKS AFTER THE UPKEEP OF THE BUILDING AND GROUNDS. OUR MANY VOLUNTEERS WORK ON EXHIBITS FUNDRAISING AND PUBLIC EVENTS.
WE WERE FORTUNATE THIS YEAR TO HAVE A YOUNG ADULT WITH HER MASTER'S DEGREE IN MUSEUM MANAGEMENT WORK FOR US THIS SUMMER WE ARE CURRENTLY WORKING AT FUNDRAISING TO FINANCIALLY SUPPORT HER EMPLOYMENT AS A CURATOR AND EVENT ORGANIZER IN THE COMING YEAR.
