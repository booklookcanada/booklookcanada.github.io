What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

Moppet Parent Participating Preschool of Kitchener is a cooperative preschool program for children that provides a stimulating learning environment and prepares them for entry into elementary school. The children's family members are classroom helpers during class time supporting the teaching staff. Family members also hold executive positions and do a range of committee work to support the activities of the preschool.
