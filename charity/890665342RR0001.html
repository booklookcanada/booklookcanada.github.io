What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

TO PROVIDE A SAF AND SUPPORTIVE ENVIRONMENT FOR YOUNG PEOPLE THAT OFFERS A SENSE OF COMMUNITY SPIRIT AND PRIDE AND PROMOTES FAITH AND TRUST IN OTHERS. TO PROVIDE OPPORTUNITIES FOR RURAL YOUTH THROUGH UNCONDITIONAL ACCEPTANCE TO PROGRAMS AND ACTIVITIES. TO OFFER A DELIVERY APPROACH THAT DECREASES RISK FACTORS ASSOCIATED TO LIFE'S CHANGES. TO ESTABLISH AND ACHIEVE GOALS FOR SELF-IMPROVEMENT THROUGH OPPORTUNITIES TO PARTICIPATE IN SOCIAL RECREATION CREATIVE AND EDUCATIONAL ACTIVITIES. TO MAINTAIN A STRONG RAPPORT AND CONSTANT COMMUNICATION WITH THE COMMUNITY. SCHOOLS CHURCHES AND OTHER SERVICE AGENCIES TO HELP BETTER SERVE OUR YOUNG PEOPLE. TO PROVIDE COUNSELING REFERRALS AND ADVOCACY UPON REQUEST.
