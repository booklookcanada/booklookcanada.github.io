What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

FIREFIGHTERS WITHOUT BORDERS IS A CHARITY DEDICATED TO HELPING FIRE DEPARTMENTS AROUND THE WORLD GET A 'HAND-UP' SO THEY ARE PREPARED TO DEAL WITH THE NEXT EMERGENCY - BIG OR SMALL. AS EACH PROGRAM IS SPECIFIC TO THE NEEDS OF THE COMMUNITY AND THEIR FIRE DEPARTMENT WE WORK CLOSELY WITH THE TEAM ON THE GROUND TO BUILD A STRATEGIC PLAN ADDRESSING THE COMMUNITIES NEEDS AND GOALS BE IT TRAINING EQUIPMENT OR A MIX OF BOTH. FIREFIGHTERS WITHOUT BORDERS ENSURE THAT ALL EQUIPMENT DONATED FOR THE PROJECT IS SAFE AND IN GOOD WORKING ORDER AND THAT THEY HAVE THE MEANS TO ENSURE IT REMAINS THAT WAY ONCE WE LEAVE. OUR TRAINING COURSES ARE ALL TAUGHT TO A RECOGNIZED INDUSTRY STANDARD IN A TRAIN THE TRAINER FORMAT TO ENSURE THEY HAVE LASTING IMPACT AND CAN BE EXTENDED OUT PAST THE IMMEDIATE GROUP TO A MUCH BROADER TEAM OF FIREFIGHTERS.

What this page can't tell you

  • Whether its work works. A return shows money, not results.
  • Whether anyone audited these figures. The CRA publishes returns as filed, without checking them. Some charities also post audited statements — BookLook compared the largest 200 charities' published statements with their returns (see “How to read this site”).
  • Anything after Mar 31, 2024, its newest return in the published data.
  • Who its directors are. The published data does not include them.
  • Questions worth asking it directly: Do you publish audited financial statements? Who sits on your board this year? What did last year's programs achieve, and how do you know?
