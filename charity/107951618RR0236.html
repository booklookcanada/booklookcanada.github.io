What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

CHURCH MINISTRY - WE HOLD WORSHIP SERVICES WEEKLY WE PROVIDE PASTORAL CARE WE PROVIDE MINISTRIES FOR CHILDREN ON A WEEKLY BASIS AND ADULT MINISTRY ON A MONTHLY BASIS. WE DONATE FUNDS TO OUR HEADQUARTERS FOR OVERSEAS MISSIONS. WE PERFORM MARRIAGE CEREMONIES. FAMILY SERVICES - WE RUN A FOOD BANK WE PROVIDE EMERGENCY TRANSPORTATION WE RUN A MONEY MANAGEMENT PROGRAM. WE PROVIDE THE OPPORTUNITY FOR UNDERPRIVILEGED CHILDREN TO GO TO CAMP. WE PROVIDE CLOTHING AND FURNITURE TO THOSE IN NEED VIA VOUCHERS TO OUR THRIFT STORES. THRIFT STORES - WE OPERATE 3 THRIFT STORES WHICH PROMOTE THE RECYCLING OF GOOD UNWANTED CLOTHING AND FURNITURE. WE PROVIDE CLOTHING AND FURNISHNGS FOR VICTIMS OF FIRE. ANY PROFIT FROM THE STORES GOES TO OUR FAMILY SERVICES PROGRAMS.
