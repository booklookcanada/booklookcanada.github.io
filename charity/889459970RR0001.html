What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Jun 30, 2024)

EduDeo Ministries is an international development organization advancing quality Christ-centred education so children in the majority world learn to live as Christ. We believe that a quality education integrated with the love of Jesus Christ is a pathway to whole-life transformation not just for children but also their communities at large. When a child has access to a quality education they also access opportunities to build a career give back to their community combat material poverty and so much more. When that education is built upon the Good News of Jesus everything they do will be driven by a heart of Christ-like love and service for their neighbours and communities. We partner long-term with locally-led school associations and organizations in the majority world to invest in their teachers schools and students. We promote sustainable schools by investing in three main ways: 1) Professional development opportunities for educators. 2) Construction of schools and related projects. 3) Student supports that help defray the cost and improve the quality of education. To accomplish this we promote the following programs: a) Walking Together: offering professional development and training for Christian educators and administrators in partner schools. b) co-Impact Teams: assisting in the construction and/or repair of partner school facilities. c) The Neighbourhood: helping defray the cost of education maintain and improve classrooms and supply additional educational materials to partner schools. d) Canadian Christian Schools: supporting education initiatives within Canada that extend learning opportunities to global Christian education networks
