What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

Creation of community-based media arts education programs for all ages especially for youth and seniors and people with disabilities (emotional or physical) or who are financially disadvantaged. Media arts educational program and events or all ages especially for seniors and youth who experience vulnerabilities and economic barrriers. -Film Camps for Children and Teens -Senior Drop-Ins -Movie Nights -Art Programs -Film Museum Collection of Materials and more
We are renovating our facility which was formerly a church owned by the Downtown Mission in Windsor. The facility is being totally renovated and upgraded (23 000 sq.ft) to support year round programs for youth seniors as well as unemployed/underemployed adults. Museum archives will also be a part of the facility so we continue to collect goods.
