What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

The social support services and programs continue to be; emergency food-bank baby food cupboard information and referral services adult drop-ins cooking programs children clothing giveaways fruit and vegetable give-away school supply give-away. The educational support programs continue to be; pre-school readiness homework clubs computer/internet access bursary fund and youth council. The recreational programs continue to be; sports night summer camps park program youth leadership programs drop ins and outings afterschool activities. In addition to our regular programs we also implement a few events throughout the year. Some of these events are Community Clean Up where families are provided with equipment and supplies to assist in a neighbourhood clean up. Open House where families are invited to the Community House to learn about the various partners and services available to them followed by a BBQ. The last event in the summer Cultural Festival where the community can engage in special activities in a nearby park. There are various cultural activities done through arts dance music and food. This is organized with tenants and the Community House.
