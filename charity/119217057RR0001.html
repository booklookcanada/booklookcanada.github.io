What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

THE FOUNDATION CARRIES ON AN EXTENSIVE SCHOLARSHIP PROGRAM. ELIGIBLE CANDIDATES ARE INTERVIEWED BY THE FOUNDATION OFFICERS AND SCHOLARSHIPS ARE AWARDED BY THE FOUNDATION. THE SCHOLARHSHIP FUNDING IS DONE DIRECTLY WITH THE UNIVERSITIES OR COLLEGES. AT THE PRESENT TIME OVER 100 ELIGIBLE STUDENTS ARE RECEIVING UP TO $5 500 PER YEAR FOR STUDIES. THE BALANCE OF THE FOUNDATION'S DONATIONS ARE DIRECTED TO HEALTH CARE SOCIAL WELL-BEING AND VARIOUS OTHER LOCAL COMMUNITY PROJECTS IN NIAGARA.
