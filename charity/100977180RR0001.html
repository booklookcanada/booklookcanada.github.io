What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

THE CHILD WITNESS CENTRE PROVIDES SUPPORT EDUCATION AND ADVOCACY FOR CHILDREN AND YOUTH WHO ARE OR MAY BECOME VICTIMS OR WITNESSES OF ABUSE OR CRIME. OUR ADVOCATES AT THE WATERLOO REGION CHILD AND YOUTH ADVOCACY CENTRE WORK AS PART OF A MULTIDISCIPLINARY TEAM TO ENSURE YOUNG PEOPLE AND THEIR FAMILIES ARE SUPPORTED DURING A CRIMINAL INVESTIGATION AND BEYOND REGARDLESS OF WHETHER CHARGES ARE LAID. OUR CHILD WITNESS PROGRAM PROVIDES COURT PREPARATION AND ACCOMPANIMENT ADVOCACY AND SUPPORT WHEN CRIMINAL CHARGES HAVE BEEN LAID FOR CHILDREN AND YOUTH WHO ARE VICTIMS OR WITNESSES OF A CRIME AND ARE INVOLVED IN THE CRIMINAL COURT SYSTEM IN WATERLOO REGION GUELPH AND WELLINGTON COUNTY
