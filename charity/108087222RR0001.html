Who runs it — the people it pays

It reported 201 full-time positions and 419 part-time employees (year ended Mar 31, 2024). Total pay for all its positions: $27,841,292, as filed. The highest pay band it reported was “$80,000–$119,999”. 2 employees were reported in that band. Pay is reported in bands — ranges, not exact salaries. The top band is the highest range with anyone in it: it is not one person's salary, and it is not what everyone there is paid.

What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

COMMUNITY LIVING LONDON PROVIDES A WIDE VARIETY OF SUPPORTS TO ADULTS AND CHILDREN WITH DEVELOPMENTAL DISABILITIES. THESE INCLUDE: ACCOMODATION FOR ADULTS; JOB CREATION AND SUPPORT FOR INDIVIDUALS IN COMPETITIVE EMPLOYMENT; COMMUNITY ACCESS SUPPORTS INCLUDING RECREATIONAL LEISURE VOLUNTEERISM AND LIFE LONG LEARNING; DEVELOPMENT OF SELF ADVOCATE PROGRAMS; RESPITE SUPPORTS; FAMILY SUPPORT.
