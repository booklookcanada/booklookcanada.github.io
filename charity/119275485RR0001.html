What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

TO PROVIDE ASSISTANCE TO EQUIP AN UNEMPLOYED PERSON WITH SKILLS TO FIND A JOB INCLUDING COURSES IN RESUME PREPARATION & COMPLETION OF JOB APPLICATION. TO PROVIDE EMPLOYMENT PLANNING AND PREPARATION INCLUDING INDIVIDUAL CAREER COUNSELLING. TO PROVIDE LIFE SKILLS CAREER EXPLORATION AND VOCATIONS ASSESSMENTS TO HELP INDIVIDUALS ESTABLISH REALISTIC GOALS. TO ASSIST ADULTS LACKING LITERACY SKILLS THRU CLASSROOM INSTRUCTIONS. OPERATING A FOOD BANK FOR THE NEEDY INCLUDING DISTRIBUTION OF FOOD TO LOCATIONS WITHIN ESSEX COUNTY. OPERATING A COMMUNITY KITCHEN EMPLOYING CHEFS AND STUDENTS IN A WORK PROGRAM TO FEED CHILDREN AND THE ELDERLY.
