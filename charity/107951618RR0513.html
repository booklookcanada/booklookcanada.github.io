What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

THE CENTRE OF HOPE IS A MULTIFACETED FACILITY WITH PROGRAMMING THAT RESPONDS TO THE EMOTIONAL SPIRITUAL AND PHYSICAL NEEDS OF THE CLIENTS THAT WE SERVE. THE CENTRE OF HOPE PROVIDES EMERGENCY SHELTER SERVICES TO MEN WOMEN AND YOUTH WHO ARE EXPERIENCING HOMELESSNESS. OUR COMMUNITY SERVICES DEPARTMENT OPERATES A FOOD BANK THAT PROVIDES EMERGENCY FOOD ASSISTANCE AND OFFERS MULTIPLE HOUSING SUPPORT SERVICES PROGRAMS THAT INCLUDE A RENT BANK AND AN EMERGENCY ENGERY ASSISTANCE PROGRAM. THE CENTRE OPERATES AN 18 BED WITHDRAWAL MANAGEMENT PROGRAM TO ASSIST BOTH MEN AND WOMEN SEEKING SOBRIETY IN A SAFE ENVIRONMENT. THE CENTRE OF HOPE PROVIDES PASTORAL AND SPIRITUAL MINISTRY TO BOTH OUR CLIENTS STAFF AND MEMBERS OF THE COMMUNITY THROUGH OUR CHAPLAINS AND VARIOUS SPIRITUAL CARE PROGRAMS.
