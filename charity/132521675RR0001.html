What it costs to raise a dollar

Gifts of goods are taken out of the donation side only where the CRA publishes that breakdown (the most recent year), so read this as approximate.

What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Jul 31, 2024)

To promote and establish Christian education in Ontario and provide a Christ-centered program of education to Christian families in Beamsville Binbrook Campden East Hamilton Fenwick Fonthill Grimsby St. Anns Smithville Stoney Creek Welland and Wainfleet. Providing daily instruction to children from JK to Grade 8.
