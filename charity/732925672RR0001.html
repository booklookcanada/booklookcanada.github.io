ALL PROGRAMS ARE VOLUNTEER LED AND WERE ATTENDED BY PASTORS MINISTRY LEADERS PASTORS' SPOUSES AND OR MISSIONARIES. 1) UNFINISHED BUISNESS: 14 WEEK GROUPS BASED ON FORMATIONAL PRAYER (HEALING CARE MINISTRIES MODEL). PARTICIPANTS ADDRESS BAGGAGE FROM THE PAST HURTFUL EXPERIENCES IN LIFE AND MINISTRIES THAT RESULT IN A VARIETY OF UNHEALTHY WAYS OF RELATING TO OTHERS. PRAYER SCRIPTURE) 2) INDIVIDUAL PASTORAL CARE AND MENTORING: SESSIONS PROVIDED ONE OR MORE OF TH FOLLOWING - ASSESMENT OF NEEDS EDUCATION AND RESOURCES OPPORTUNITIES FOR SELF- REFLECTION AND SPIRITUAL DEVELOPMENT PRAYER AND REFERRAL TO APPROPRIATE THRIVE PROGRAMS OR OTHER SERVICE PROVIDERS. 3) SMALL GROUP PASTORAL CARE AND MENTORING: GROUP SESSIONS PROVIDED SUPPORT AND ENCOURGEMENT OPPORTUNITIES FOR SELF-REFLECTION SPIRITUAL DEVELOPMENT AND PRAYER. 4) INDIVIDUAL FORMATIONAL PRAYER: IN-PERSON OR VIRTUAL CONFIDENTIAL SESSIONS BASED ON FORMATIONAL PRAYER (HEALING CARE MINISTRIES MODEL) PROVIDING EDUCATIONAL AND SPIRITUAL PRACTICES AND HELPING PARTICIPANTS FIND RESOLUTION TO PAST TRAUMA. 5) SOUL FOCUS: 1/2 DAY RETREATS PROVIDED OPPORTUNITY TO CULTIVATE A DEEPER WALK WITH GOD AND PRACTICE PRINCIPLES OF SOUL CARE THROUGH TEACHING PERSONAL REFLECTION TIME GROUP SHARING AND PRAYER
