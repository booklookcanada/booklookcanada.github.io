What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

CDI IS A MULTI-SERVICE CHARITABLE ORGANIZATION THAT OFFERS EARLY YEARS AND CHILDCARE SERVICES MENTAL HEALTH AND COUNSELLING SERVICES AND PROGRAMS THAT ADDRESS GENDER-BASED VIOLENCE. UNIFIED UNDER ONE AGENCY CDI'S DIVERSE PROGRAMMING FOCUSES ON IMPROVING THE MENTAL HEALTH AND WELL-BEING OF CHILDREN AND YOUTH AGES 0 TO 18. CDI'S PROGRAMS AND SERVICES ARE GUIDED BY A STRENGTHS-BASED APPROACH PROVIDING CHILD-CENTRIC AND FAMILY- FOCUSED PROGRAMMING TO ENSURE THAT EVERYONE RECEIVES THE TAILORED SUPPORT AND TOOLS THEY NEED TO BUILD LIFE-LONG SKILLS POSITIVE MENTAL HEALTH AND RESILIENCE. PROGRAMS INCLUDE CHILDCARE SERVICES FAMILY AND GROUP COUNSELING SOCIAL EMOTIONAL AND BEHAVIOURAL COUNSELLING SERVICES DROP-IN PARENT/CHILD PROGRAMS SCHOOL-BASED TREATMENT PROGRAMS CHILDREN'S SOCIAL SKILLS GROUPS CONSULTATION TO CHILDCARE CENTRES REGARDING CHILDREN WITH SPECIAL NEEDS TRAUMA-INFORMED COUNSELING TRANSITIONAL AND HOUSING SUPPORTS FOR WOMEN AND CHILDREN WHO HAVE EXPERIENCED GENDER-BASED VIOLENCE. IN 2023-24 CDI SERVED 4 792 CHILDREN AND YOUTH THROUGH ITS TORONTO-BASED CLINICAL SERVICES AND EARLY YEARS AND CHILD CARE SERVICES. OF CLIENTS WHO COMPLETED TREATMENT THROUGH CDI'S ONTARIO MINISTRY OF HEALTH-FUNDED CLINICAL SERVICES MORE THEN 85% REPORTED POSITIVE OUTCOMES AT DISCHARGE AND EXPRESSED SATISFACTION WITH SERVICES RECEIVED.
WHILE CDI IS CURRENTLY DEVELOPING NEW PROGRAMS TO PILOT FUTURE YEARS NO NEW PROGRAMS HAVE BEEN OFFERED IN 2023-24.
