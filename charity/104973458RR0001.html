What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

SPRINT Senior Care is a not-for-profit neighborhood agency providing home support and community services to seniors in North Toronto. SPRINT purpose is to sustain independent living; encourage participation in community life; enhance the caring and capacity of family friends and neighbor; and foster strong communities. Programs consist of Meals on Wheels community dining transportation telephone assurance support group client intervention and assistance respite care home care homemaking home help case management social work adult day program assisted living and dementia care homes.
