What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Oct 31, 2024)

RKY CAMP PROGRAMS FOSTER TEAMWORK CONFIDENCE AND RESILIENCY AMONGST THE CHILDREN AND YOUTH WHO ATTEND OUR PROGRAMS YEAR-ROUND. IT IS OUR GOAL THAT STAFF AND PARTICIPANTS WHO EXPERIENCE AN RKY PROGRAM WILL LEAVE AS BETTER FRIENDS CITIZENS AND PEOPLE. RKY ACCOMPLISHES THIS THROUGH THE FOLLOWING PROGRAM OFFERINGS: - EDUCATIONAL RECREATIONAL AND LEADERSHIP PROGRAMS FOR YOUTH FAMILIES SCHOOLS AND COMMUNITY GROUPS DURING THE FALL WINTER AND SPRING MONTHS. - TRADITIONAL SUMMER CAMP PROGRAMS INCLUDING ARCHERY CANOEING KAYAKING OUT-TRIPPING OUTDOOR LIVING SKILLS ARTS AND CRAFTS MUSIC CHALLENGE COURSE (CLIMBING) LOW ROPES AND CREATIVE ALL CAMP PROGRAMS. WE PROVIDE FINANCIAL ASSISTANCE FOR PARTICIPANTS WHOSE FAMILIES ARE UNABLE TO PAY THE REGULAR REGISTRATION FEE. RKY ALSO PROVIDES INTEGRATION OPPORTUNITIES FOR CHILDREN AND YOUNG ADULTS WITH DEVELOPMENTAL AND PHYSICAL DISABILITIES. RKY WAS FOUNDED BY THE KINGSTON YMCA AND THE ROTARY AND KIWANIS CLUBS OF KINGSTON ONTARIO. THE CAMP IS LOCATED IN PARHAM ONTARIO.
