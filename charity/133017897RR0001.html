What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

PROVIDES EMERGENCY SHELTER AND SUPPORT TO HOMELESS WOMEN. SUPPORT WORKERS PROVIDE ONGOING SUPPORT AND ASSIST WOMEN WITH THEIR PLANS TO LIVE INDEPENDENTLY. IN ADDITION TO STAFF SUPPORT THE RESIDENTS RECEIVE OUTREACH SERVICES FROM SUCH AGENCIES AS STREET HEALTH LEGAL ASSISTANCE OF WINDSOR AND HOUSING INFORMATION SERVICES. DAY PROGRAMS FOR WOMEN INCLUDE; INDIVIDUAL COUNSELLING THERAPEUTIC SUPPORT GROUPS AND FOOD BANK.
