What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

I. TO PREACH AND ADVANCE THE TEACHINGS OF THE CHRISTIAN FAITH AND THE RELIGIOUS TENETS DOCTRINES OBSERVANCES AND CULTURE ASSOCIATED WITH THAT FAITH II. TO ESTABLISH MAINTAIN AND SUPPORT A HOUSE OF WORSHIP WITH SERVICES CONDUCTED IN ACCORDANCE WITH THE TENETS AND DOCTRINES OF THE CHRISTIAQN FAITH. III. TO SUPPORT AND MAINTAIN MISSIONS AND MISSIONARIES IN ORDER TO PROPAGATE THE CHRISTIAN FAITH. IV. TO ENCOURAGE AND SUPPORT CHRISTIAN EDUCATION AT THE ELEMENTARY HIGH SCHOOL AND/OR POST-SECONDARY EDUCATION LEVEL BY PROVIDING TUITION ASSISTANCE TO ELIGBLE INDIVIDUALS OR FAMILIES V. TO RELIEVE POVERTY BY PROVIDING FOOD AND OTHER BASIC NECESSITIES OF LIFE TO INDIVIDUALS OR FAMILIES WHO ARE POOR OF LOW INCOME OR IN NEED. VI. TO DO ALL SUCH THINGS AS ARE INCIDENTAL AND ANCILLARY TO THE ATTAINMENT OF THE ABOVE PURPOSES.
