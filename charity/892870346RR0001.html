What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

We provide social services and humanitarian aid to Chatham-Kent and in several countries around the world. Loads of Love operates a 17 000 sq.ft. warehouse where it receives by donation - clothing medical supplies furniture etc. We sort and package humanitarian aid for distribution. We network with social services and other community agencies to provide beds clothing and other household items for those in need. We operate a thrift store. We have over 95 volunteers on a rotating bases who help with sorting packing thrift store and the referral programs. We support the Lambton-Kent Board of Education's co-op program by accepting 1 to 3 co-op students per term. We enable those requiring community service hours to fulfill their time at Loads of Love. We continue to provide support for orphanages church planting schools seniors and missionaries in countries such as Ukraine Zambia Cuba Honduras Nicaragua. Children of Hope provides support to children in Indonesia and Ukraine.
