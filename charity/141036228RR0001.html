What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

OVERALL MISSION IS TO ENRICH THE QUALITY OF LIFE OF THE PEOPLE OF THE COMMUNITY BY SUPPORTING CHARITABLE EDUCATIONAL AND CULTURAL ORGANIZATIONS. THIS IS DONE BY PROVIDING THE EXPERTISE NEEDED TO MANAGE AND INVEST ENDOWMENT FUNDS IN ORDER THAT THOSE FUNDS PROVIDE AN ONGOING SOURCE OF FUNDING FOR VARIOUS CHARITIES. AS WELL MANAGEMENT IS PROVIDED FOR RESTRICTED FUNDS TO ENSURE THAT GRANT RECIPIENTS MEET THE REQUIREMENTS OF RESPECTIVE DONORS.
