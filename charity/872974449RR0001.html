What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Jun 30, 2024)

We have returned to carry out face to face ministries such as evangelistic work fellowship activities on campus and student leadership training camps. We have maintained some online programs such as Spiritual Formation retreats and Summer Bible Courses. We continue to set up Bible study classes for high school students who came from China teaching them English through learning God's word. We run weekly gatherings for international students in Canadian universities and colleges.
We carried out the same ministries through different means.
