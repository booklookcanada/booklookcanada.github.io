WE PROMOTE PUBLIC AWARENESS IN CANADA ABOUT THE PROBLEMS OF SEX TRAFFICKING AND OTHER FORMS OF SEXUAL EXPLOITATION OF CANADIAN CHILDREN AND YOUTH THROUGH THE DISSEMINATION OF INFORMATION. - WE OFFER CURRICULUM AND COACHING IN CANADA TO HELP COMMUNITY MEMBERS START GIRLS CLUBS THAT PROVIDE EDUCATION TO PREVENT SEXUAL EXPLOITATION. - WE OPERATE A FOSTER HOME IN NEPAL TO CARE FOR CHILDREN AND YOUTH WHO HAVE LOST THE SUPPORT OF THEIR FAMILIES. - WE PROVIDE SAFE AND SUPPORTED HOUSING TO PROMOTE THE HEALTH SAFETY AND WELLBEING OF FEMALE POST-SECONDARY STUDENTS IN PETERBOROUGH ONTARIO. - WE ADVOCATE FOR CHANGES IN PUBLIC AND PRIVATE SECTORS TO ADDRESS AND PREVENT SEXUAL EXPLOITATION OF CANADIAN CHILDREN AND YOUTH.
