The highest pay band it reported was “$80,000–$119,999”. 1 employee was reported in that band. Pay is reported in bands — ranges, not exact salaries. What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Sep 30, 2024)

The charity operated a store selling used goods which are generally received by it as non-receipted donations. This helps reduce the amount of usable goods sent to landfill and also assists primarily lower income people by selling these goods at low prices. The charity also provides scholarships to students to assist them in pursuing post-secondary educations in enviromental related studies.
