What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

THE ORGANIZATION'S MISSION PROVIDES FOR AN OPEN SUPPORTIVE ENVIRONMENT FOR CHILDREN YOUTH AND ADULTS IN INNER-CITY TORONTO WITH ACCESS TO FREE ART INSTRUCTION AND SUPPLIES.
EFFECTIVE 2024 ARTHEART HAS MOVED AWAY FROM A STUDIO ART BRICKS AND MORTOR FACILITY AND NOW PROVIDES MOBILE ARTS INSTRUCTOON AND SUPPLIES WHERE STAFF ATTEND COMMUNITY LOCATIONS ON-SITE TO FOCUS ON THOSE MOST IN NEED OF ART THEROPY AND SERVICES.
