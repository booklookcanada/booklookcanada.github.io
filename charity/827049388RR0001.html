What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

HIGH NOTES AVANTE PRODUCTIONS INC. IS A NOT-FOR-PROFIT ORGANIZATION WHOSE OBJECTIVES INCLUDE TO RELIEVE CONDITIONS ASSOCIATED WITH MENTAL ILLNESS BY OFFERING ART PRODUCTIONS DIRECTED TOWARDS THE ALLEVIATION OF LONELINESS AND ISOLATION AS WELL AS TO REDUCE THE ASSOCIATED STIGMA. THE ORGANIZATION ALSO PROMOTES MENTAL HEALTH BY PROVIDING INFORMATION FROM MENTAL HEALTH PROFESSIONALS AND TESTIMONIALS FROM ARTISTS WELL-KNOWN PERSONALITIES AND OTHERS WHO SUFFER OR HAVE SUFFERED FROM MENTAL ILLNESS.
