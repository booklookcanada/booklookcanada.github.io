The highest pay band it reported was “$40,000–$79,999”. 7 employees were reported in that band. Pay is reported in bands — ranges, not exact salaries. What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

FALL FOR DANCE NORTH FESTIVAL INC WAS FOUNDED TO PRODUCE AN ANNUAL DANCE FESTIVAL FALL FOR DANCE NORTH(FFDN). THE MISSION OF FFDN IS TO PRESENT DIVERSE DANCE PROGRAMS MADE UP OF THE BEST OF ESTABLISHED AND EMERGING DANCE TALENT FROM TORONTO ACROSS CANADA AND AROUND THE WORLD AND CREATE AN ENVIRONMENT THAT EMBRACES DANCE LOVERS ENGAGES NEW AUDIENCES AND MAKES DANCE ACCESSIBLE TO ALL WITH ITS AFFORDABLE TICKET PRICING. THE FESTIVAL'S INCLUSIVE ATMOSPHERE ALSO OFFERS A UNIQUE ENVIRONMENT IN WHICH TO PROMOTE INTERACTION AMONG ARTIST STUDENTS AND AUDIENCE -EXCHANGES WITH FFDN FOSTERS THROUGH AN ARTIST SHOWCASE MASTER CLASSES THROUGHOUT THE CITY OPEN REHEARSALS AND PRE/POST PERFORMANCE DISCUSSIONS WITH ARTISTS AND CHOREOGRAPHERS.
