It reported 16 full-time positions and 63 part-time employees (year ended Oct 31, 2024). Total pay for all its positions: $1,016,449, as filed. The highest pay band it reported was “$40,000–$79,999”. 6 employees were reported in that band. Pay is reported in bands — ranges, not exact salaries. What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Oct 31, 2024)

A) REACH A CHILD - CHILDREN'S PROGRAMS FOR THOSE AGED 5-14 LIVING IN TORONTO COMMUNITY HOUSING; THEY ARE NURTURED IN A SAFE ENVIRONMENT AT OUR AFTER SCHOOL PROGRAMS AND SEASONAL CAMPS WITH THE AIM TO EQUIP CHILDREN WITH THE TOOLS NECESSARY FOR SOCIAL ACADEMIC AND SPIRITUAL SUCCESS; MENTORED BY CARING ADULTS PROVIDING ACCEPTANCE FOR THE CHILDREN AND THEIR NEEDS. B) RAISE A LEADER - LEADERSHIP DEVELOPMENT FOR THOSE AGED 15-19 LIVING IN TOROTNO COMMUNITY HOUSING; PROVIDING MEANINGFUL VOLUNTEER AND EMPLOYMENT OPPORTUNITIES FOR YOUNG LEADERS (AKA-STREETLEADERS) WITH THE AIM TO CONTINUED DEVELOPMENTAL OF SOCIAL ACADEMIC AND SPIRITUAL SUCCESS AS WELL AS DEVELOPING THEIR PROGRAM LEADERSHIP AND MENTORING SKILLS. C) RESTORE COMMUNITY - LEADERSHIP OPPORTUNITIES FOR THOSE AGED 20-25 LIVING IN TORONTO COMMUNITY HOUSING TO DIRECT AND OVERSEE PROGRAMS CONTINUING IN THEIR LEADERSHIP AND MENTORING SKILLS AS WELL AS SUPPORTING THE FAMILIES OF THE PARTICIPANTS IN PROGRAMS. D) FAMILY SUPPORT - PROVIDING SUPPORT THROUGH NETWORKING AND ADVOCACY FOR FAMILIES OF PARTICIPANTS IN PROGRAMS ASSISTING THEM IN FINDING RESOURCES TO MEET THEIR NEEDS
