What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

THE ORGANIZATION CARRIES OUT RELIGIOUS SOCIAL AND CULTURAL PROGRAMS AND ACTIVITIES FOR THE JEWISH COMMUNITY IN DOWNTOWN TORONTO Chabad runs a vibrant lineup of community programs including adult education classes Hebrew school for youth mezuzah installations and hospital visitation. During major Jewish holidays such as Passover and the High Holy Days they organize full seders festive dinners holiday services and fundraising campaigns to engage and support the broader community. With special initiatives like Chabad Young Professionals events and Torah classes they cultivate meaningful social and spiritual connections for Jews of all ages and backgrounds. Canada
