What it says it does

WE ENGAGE WITH CHURCHES TO PROVIDE TEMPORARY HOUSING FOR NEW ASYLUM SEEKERS AND PROVIDE EDUCATION ON REFUGEE REALITIES. WE SUPPORT EDUCATION TO REFUGEE YOUTH AND CHILDREN AS WELL AS TRAUMA COUNSELLING AND LIVELIHOODS TRAINING FOR THOSE WHO ARE FORCIBLY DISPLACED. WE PROVIDE SUPPORT FOR VULNERABLE REFUGEE WOMEN IN THE MIDDLE EAST AND MEDICAL AID EDUCATION AND EMERGENCY RELIEF TO REFUGEE POPULATIONS IN SPECIFIC REGIONS IN THE MIDDLE EAST AND AFRICA.
