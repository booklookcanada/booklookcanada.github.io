What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

The Grey Bruce Pregnancy Centre is a community base service composed of inter-denominational Christians whose aim is to minister to women and their partners with unplanned pregnancies with the love mercy and compassion of Jesus Christ. The following free confidential services are provided: friendship support referrals to professional services accurate information regarding abortion adoption and prenatal life/care pregnancy testing maternity clothing baby clothing formula diapers new car seats new strollers other baby equipment post-abortion counselling unconditional love and the teaching and advancement of the gospel of Jesus Christ via example (and word when appropriate).
