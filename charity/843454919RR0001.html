What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

Ezra Canada responds to the plight of the Jewish people worldwide who suffer oppression and indignity on the basis of their race by: 1. Providing humanitarian aid and relief to alleviate poverty sickness distress and danger experienced by the Jewish people living in oppressive situations and 2. Assisting Jewish people in the process of escaping oppressive environments and returning to lives of dignity and hope in their homeland of Israel.
