What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

We operate a religious school of instruction on Islam for children youth and adults. We serve over 150 children and youth from the Flemingdon Park and Thorncliffe Park neighbourhoods in Toronto. These areas are high priority neighbourhoods and are well known in Canada for their high refugee and new immigrant populations. We have more than 10 dedicated volunteers who support our daily activities as well as special programming throughout the year.
