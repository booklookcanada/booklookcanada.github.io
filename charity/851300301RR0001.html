What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

THE PURPOSE OF THE FOUNDATION IS TO RECEIVE AND MAINTAIN FUNDS AND APPLY ALL OR PART THERE OF AND/OR THE INCOME THEREFORM TO FUND REGISTERED CHARITABLE ORGANIZATIONS IN THE GREATER TORONTO AREA ('GTA') THAT ENGAGE IN CHARITABLE ACTIVITIES SUPPORTING THE HEALTHY DEVELOPMENT OF INDIVIDUALS CHILDREN AND COMMUNITIES IN THE GTA THROUGH PREVENTION PROTECTION COUNSELLING EDUCATION AND OUTREACH ALL WITHIN THE CONTECT OF JEWISH VALUES
