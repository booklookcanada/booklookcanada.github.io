What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

Weekly worship services are held. Ministries for men women youth & children are held weekly. Prayer services are held daily. We reach out to the people in our community. We minister to the needs of our people and the community. Councelling is provided. Courses are offered for New Believers Membership & Baptism. Video sermons are available for each service through the church website and church YouTube channel. Revive Niagara started in 2022 and reaches out to the people in the Niagara Region to lift Jesus higher. Along with this a branch was formed called Worship in the Wild which is a day of worship & celebration and was held at Safari Niagara in 2024.
Another branch of Revive Niagara was introduced called Women Walking Worth: Connect where women gather together to lift up and encourage one another.
