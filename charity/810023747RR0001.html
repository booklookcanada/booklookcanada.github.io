Who runs it — the people it pays

It reported 881 full-time positions and 799 part-time employees (year ended Mar 31, 2024). Total pay for all its positions: $147,774,381, as filed. The highest pay band it reported was “$350,000 and over”. 6 employees were reported in that band. Pay is reported in bands — ranges, not exact salaries. The top band is the highest range with anyone in it: it is not one person's salary, and it is not what everyone there is paid.

What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

SPECIALIZED MENTAL HEALTH HOSPITAL PROVIDING TERTIARY CARE MENTAL HEALTH PROGRAMS FOR INPATIENTS AND OUTPATIENTS. - OPERATES SATELLITE LOCATIONS IN THE COMMUNITY AND THROUGH OTHER REGIONAL PROGRAMS AND PARTNERSHIPS. IN ALL PROGRAMS ONTARIO SHORES PROVIDES ASSESSMENT STABILIZATION TREATMENT REHABILITATION TRANSITION AND COMMUNITY REINTEGRATION. - TEACH PATIENTS ABOUT THEIR ILLNESSES. - ASSIST PATIENTS TO ENHANCE THEIR INDEPENDENCE AND COPING SKILLS. - HELPS PATIENTS DEVELOP TOOLS NECESSARY TO PROSPER IN THE COMMUNITY.
