What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended May 31, 2024)

TO ADVANCE EDUCATION BY PROVIDING PUBLICLY AVAILABLE SCHOLARSHIPS BURSARIES AND OTHER FORMS OF FINANCIAL ASSISTANCE TO STUDENTS ATTENDING JEWISH POST-SECONDARY SCHOOLS. TO RELIEVE POVERTY BY PROVIDING BASIC NECESSITIES OF LIFE INCLUDING FOOD CLEAN WATER CLOTHING AND/OR SHELTER TO INDIVIDUALS OR FAMILIES WHO ARE IN NEED. TO PROMOTE HEALTH BY PROVIDING MEDICAL TREATMENT FUNDING TO INDIVIDUALS IN NEED. TO UNDERTAKE ACTIVITIES ANCILLARY AND INCIDENTAL TO THE ATTAINMENT OF THE ABOVE CHARITABLE PURPOSES. TO RECEIVE AND MAINTAIN A FUND OR FUNDS AND TO APPLY ALL OR PART OF THE PRINCIPAL AND INCOME THEREFROM FROM TIME TO TIME TO QUALIFIED DONEES AS DEFINED IN SUBSECTION 149.1(1) OF THE INCOME TAX ACT.
