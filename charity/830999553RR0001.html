What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Aug 31, 2024)

PCA was incorporated on November 7 2007 and received its charitable status on May 7 2008. It is committed to provide an excellent Christ centered education from JK through grade 12 in a nurturing environment resulting in students who live to glorify God. Academic spiritual and social programs are designed to provide well rounded training to students. Academic programs are designed to meet the requirements of the Ministry of Education of Ontario. Extra curricular programs include chapel house system music arts athletics missions academic competitions debate clubs and schools trips.
