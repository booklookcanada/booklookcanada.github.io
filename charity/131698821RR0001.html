What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

Visit local Correctional Institutions to provide pre-release planning to inmates. Assist persons in securing affordable housing. Provide ongoing counselling support advocacy and referral services. Attend court and supervise individuals on pre-trial release Conduct group sessions in Anger Management and Cognitive Behavioural Interventions. Provide an alternative to custody for youth through the Youth Justice Committee Assist accused with referrals to programming community service work and charities to make restitution. Assist chronically homeless persons secure permanent housing. Provide Peer support to individuals living with mental health and addictions. Supporting clients navigating services upon release from an institution.
