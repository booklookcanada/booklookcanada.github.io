What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Apr 30, 2024)

LAY-UP IS WORKING ON THE FUTURE OF ACCESS TO BASKETBALL THROUGH COST-FREE LEADERSHIP BASKETBALL PROGRAMS IN TORONTO'S PRIORITY COMMUNITIES. LAY-UP'S MISSION IS TO EMPOWER YOUTH WITH CONFIDENCE LIFE SKILLS AND TO SUPPORT THEIR POSITIVE DEVELOPMENT. HIGH QUALITY BASKETBALL IS USED AS A PLATFORM TO DELIVER MEANINGFUL CUSTOMIZED ON AND OFF COURT PROGRAMMING THAT WILL EQUIP AND INSPIRE YOUTH TO OVERCOME BARRIERS IN ACADEMIA LIFE SKILLS DEVELOPMENT AND HEALTHY LIVING. LAY-UP OFFERS SUMMER CAMPS AND SCHOOL YEAR PROGRAMS TO CHILDREN AGES 6-14 AS WELL AS PROGRAMS AND PERSONAL DEVELOPMENT THROUGH EMPLOYMENT TO TEENS AND YOUNG ADULTS.
