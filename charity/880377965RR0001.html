What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Jun 30, 2024)

THE CHARITY HAS THREE BROAD MANDATES TO SUPPORT COMMUNITY PROJECTS IN THE NEW TECUMSETH AREA INTERNATIONAL ROTARY SERVICE PROJECTS AND VOCATION / YOUTH PROJECTS. LOCAL YOUTH ARE SPONSORED TO ATTEND LEADERSHIP PROGRAMS. THE CHARITY ORGANIZES THE POTATO FESTICAL AND SANTA CLAUS PARADES IN ALLISTON. THE CHARITY SUPPORTS GLOBAL PROJECTS IN THE ROTARY SIX AREAS OF FOCUS BEING DISEASE PREVENTION WATER & SANITATION MATERNAL & CHILD HEALTH LITERACY ECONOMIC DEVELOPMENT AND PEACE.
THE CHARITY IS FINANCIALLY SUPPORTING THE LOCAL HOSPITAL REDEVELOPMENT. THE CHARITY SUPPORT LOCAL YOUTH THROUGH TRAINING PROGRAMS ON LEADERSHIP AND DRUG AWARENESS.
