Who runs it — the people it pays

It reported 7 full-time positions and 6 part-time employees (year ended Dec 31, 2024). Total pay for all its positions: $676,378, as filed. The highest pay band it reported was “$120,000–$159,999”. 1 employee was reported in that band. Pay is reported in bands — ranges, not exact salaries. The top band is the highest range with anyone in it: it is not one person's salary, and it is not what everyone there is paid.

What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

CORE PROGRAMS ARE LAND PROTECTION STEWARDSHIP AND EDUCATION. GBLT SEEKS TO PROTECT LAND FOR THE PURPPOSES OF CONSERVATION STEWARDSHIP ENSURES THE CONTINUED PROTECTION OF THE PROPERTIES OWNED OR MANAGED BY THE GBLT. EDUCATION IS PROVIDED THROUGH COMMUNITY-BASED ACTIVITIES INCLUDING NEWSLETTERS EVENTS FOCUSED ON EDUCATION (E.G. NATURE WALKS) AND PARTICIPATING IN COMMUNITY ORGANIZED EVENTS.
