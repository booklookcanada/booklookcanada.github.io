What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

THE CHURCH IS A RELIGOUS ORGANIZATION AFFILIATED WITH THE APOSTOLIC CHURCH OF PENTECOST. THE CHURCH OPERATES ITS PHYSICAL BUILDINGS SIMILAR TO A COMMUNITY-BASED CENTRE WHERE NOT-FOR-PROFIT ORGANIZATIONS CAN USE THE FACILITIES TO PROMOTE GROWTH OF THE COMMUNITY AND THE NEIGHBOURHOOD. BESIDES THE LOCAL COMMUNITY GROWTH THE CHURCH ALSO PROVIDES MISSIONARY WORK THROUGHOUT THE DISTRICT AND SUPPORTS SEVERAL INTERNATIONAL DEVELOPMENT PROJECTS.
