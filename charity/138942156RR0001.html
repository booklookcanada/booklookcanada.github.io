It reported 13 full-time positions and 18 part-time employees (year ended Dec 31, 2024). Total pay for all its positions: $929,075, as filed. The highest pay band it reported was “$40,000–$79,999”. 9 employees were reported in that band. Pay is reported in bands — ranges, not exact salaries. The top band is the highest range with anyone in it: it is not one person's salary, and it is not what everyone there is paid.

What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

MARILLAC PLACE OFFERS HOUSING TO INDEPENDENT WOMEN 16 AND UP WHO ARE PREGNANT WHO HAVE THEIR CHILD(REN) IN THEIR CARE OR WHO ARE ATTEMPTING TO REGAIN CARE OF THEIR CHILD(REN). MARILLAC PLACE CAN ACCOMMODATE CHILDREN UP TO TWO YEARS OF AGE. RESIDENCY IS PERMITTED FOR UP TO 12 MONTHS. MARILLAC PLACE ALSO PROVIDES OUTREACH SERVICES TO WOMEN WHO HAVE MOVED ON TO INDEPENDENT LIVING AND ADVOCACY SERVICES TO ENSURE THAT THE RIGHTS OF THE WOMEN AND CHILDREN ARE RECOGNIZED.
