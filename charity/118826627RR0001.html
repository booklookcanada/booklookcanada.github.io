What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

We hold regular services of worship and other learning and fellowship opportunities for all ages. We financially support other registered charities that are involved in missionary activities relief and development evangelism and Christian education. We provide used clothing and household items inexpensively as a ministry outreach. We provide church services to retirement homes nursing homes and the local hospital as requested. We financially support other community charitable organizations. We have over 90 volunteers that help to carry on the work that we do.
