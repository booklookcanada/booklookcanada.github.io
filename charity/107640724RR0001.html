What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

IN HOUSE AND ONLINE PROGRMS: LOGOS CHRISTIAN FAMILY CHURCH IS A RELIGIOUS NON-PROFIT CHARITABLE ORGANIZATION THAT IS AFFILIATED WITH THE PENTECOSTAL ASSEMBLIES OF CANADA (PAOC) AS A DENOMINATION. OUR PRIMARY PURPOSE IS TO MINISTER TO THE SPIRITUAL NEEDS TO THOSE WITHIN OUR COMMUNITY. WE HOLD REGULAR WEEKLY WORSHIP SERVICES MID-WEEK BIBLE STUDIES CHRISTIAN EDUCATION FOR YOUTH CHILDREN YOUNG ADULTS AND YOUNG MARRIEDS IN ORDER TO ENCOURAGE AND GROW PEOPLE IN THEIR CHRISTIAN FAITH. MINISTERIAL EVENTS ARE ALSO ORGANIZED TO ENCOURAGE FELLOWSHIP AND FURTHER SPIRITUAL GROWTH. LOGOS CHRISTIAN FAMILY CHURCH CONDUCTS WEDDINGS FUNERALS BAPTISMS BABY DEDICATIONS AND PROVIDES PASTORAL CARE TO ITS CHURCH MEMBERS ADHERENTS MEMBERS OF THE COMMUNITY AS WELL AS TO LOCAL HOSPITALS SHUT-INS AND SENIORS HOMES. OUR PROGRAMMING IS HEAVILY CONTINGENT ON VOLUNTEERS WHO WORK IN THE CHURCH AS; TEACHERS HELPERS USHERS GREETERS NURSERY WORKERS DEACONS WORSHIP LEADERS MUSICIANS HOSPITALITY TEAMS MEDIA SOUND TECHNICIANS CREATIVE ARTS SPECIALIST AND ADMINISTRATORS.
