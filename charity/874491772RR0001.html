What it says it does

ADVOCATE AGAINST DISCRIMINATION TOWARDS THE 2SLGBTQ2+ AND OTHER MARGINALIZED COMMUNITIES AT ALL LEVELS OF THE UNITED CHURCH OF CANADA (UCC) AND IN OTHER ORGANIZATIONS ASSOCIATED WITH THE UCC (EG. LONG TERM CARE HOMES CONFERENCES CENTRES UNIVERSITY CHAPLAINCIES). VOLUNTEERS WORK CLOSELY WITH CHURCHES/ORGANIZATIONS TO FACILITATE THE TRAINING PROGRAM. WHEN ALL REQUIREMENTS HAVE BEEN COMPLETED THE CHURCH ORGANIZATION IS ACCREDITED AS AFFIRMING MINISTRY. VOLUNTEERS DEVELOP POLICIES AND LITURGIES FOR USE THROUGHOUT THE UCC THAT CELEBRATE THE GIFTS AND CONTRIBUTIONS OF MARGINALIZED PEOPLE AND RECOGNIZE SIGNIFICANT DATES SUCH A PRIDE MONTH AND TRANSGENDER DAY OF REMEMBRANCE.
