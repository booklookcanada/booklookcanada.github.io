What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Aug 31, 2024)

SKILLS FOR LIFE ALIGNS WITH THE 'MINDSET GROWTH MODEL' WHICH AMPLIFIES THE IMPORTANCE OF CONTINUOUS LEARNING AND SELF-DISCOVERY. THE CONCEPT WAS FOUNDED ON THE PRINCIPLES OF EXPLORING CONFRONTING AND MOVING PAST FAILURE TO BUILD ON KEY TRANSFERABLE SKILLS THAT CAN BE PRACTICALLY APPLIED TO DAILY LIFE. THEY ORGANIZE WORKSHOPS EVENTS AND OTHER INITIATIVES TO FILL THE GAP BETWEEN TRADITIONAL LEARNING AND PRACTICAL SKILLS. WE ARE DETERMINED TO HELP KIDS FROM ALL WALKS OF LIFE REALIZE THE TOOLS THEY HAVE WITHIN THEMSELVES TO PURSUE THEIR DREAMS.
