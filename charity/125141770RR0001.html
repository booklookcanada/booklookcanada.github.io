What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

Our ministries include weekly worship services Sunday school adult youth/children ministries Bible Study Groups sports ministries outreach program retreat as well as heritage program (T.H. Chan Memorial Chinese School). We provide pastoral care support the community food bank and out-of-the-cold programs. We donate funds to denomination headquarters (The United Church of Canada) and raise money for other qualified donees for national/local missionary activities and any emergency appeal if necessary. Owing to COVID pandemic our church holds worship services virtually and in-person.
Our church opened its facilities for activities initiated by the UCC 'Canada-Hong Kong United Heart-to-Heart' project. The goal of this project is to attract and welcome new immigrants through activities such as sports board games live band concert and the like. This initiative provided opportunities for new immigrants to get to know each other build relationships and learn about The United Church of Canada and our church.
