What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

The parish is dedicated to fostering spiritual growth through the advancement of Roman Catholic religious worship instruction and ministry to both parishioners and community at large. In this respect the parish holds worship services on weekends provides catechesis and sacramental preparation provides pastoral care to the sick dying and those in grief gives spiritual guidance to the individuals and families organizes special days for parish retreats and activities and assists the needy and homeless in its neighborhood by providing pastoral care.
