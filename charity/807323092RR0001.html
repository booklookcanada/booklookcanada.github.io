What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Aug 31, 2024)

The KSO performs an international repertoire including Canadian contemporary music and offers subscription series at the Flato Markham Theatre the Meridian Arts Centre and the CBC Glenn Gould Studio in addition to community outreach and family concerts educational programs and activities for children senior citizens and people with disabilities. Through a pursuit of innovation and technology the KSO seeks to reduce the impact of its operations on the environment.
