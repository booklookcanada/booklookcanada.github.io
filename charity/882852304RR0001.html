What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

THE ORGANIZATION PROVIDES A FULL RANGE OF OUTREACH SUPPORT COUNSELLING TRAINING AND REFERRAL SERVICES RELATING TO RESETTLEMENT AND INTEGRATION TO ASSIST LAWFULLY ADMITTED REFUGEES AND IMMIGRANTS INTEGRATE INTO CANADIAN SOCIETY. IN ADDITION THE ORGANIZATION PROVIDES PROGRAMS AND SERVICES DESIGNED TO ASSIST LAWFULLY ADMITTED POOR JEWISH IMMIGRANTS IN MEETING BASIC FINANCIAL SOCIAL LEGAL AND OTHER REQUIREMENTS OF IMMIGRATION.
THE ORGANIZATION OFFERS SETTLEMENT SUPPORT TO ALL IMMIGRANTS EXPERIENCING VULNERABILITY REGARDLESS OF THEIR EDUCATION PROFESSIONAL BACKGROUNDS ENGLISH LANGUAGE PROFICIENCY OR ECONOMIC CIRCUMSTANCES. IT PROVIDES TAILORED SERVICES TO NEWCOMERS INCLUDING WORKSHOPS ON ONTARIO’S EDUCATION SYSTEM CANADIAN CULTURE AND CITIZENSHIP PREPARATION. IN ADDITION TO SUPPORTING NEWCOMERS FROM AROUND THE WORLD REGARDLESS OF FAITH OR COUNTRY OF ORIGIN THE ORGANIZATION OFFERS FREE SETTLEMENT AND LANGUAGE SERVICES. IT IS INCLUSIVE 2SLGBTQ+ FRIENDLY AND SERVES ALL POPULATIONS. WITH A DIVERSE AND MULTILINGUAL STAFF THE ORGANIZATION ENSURES ACCESSIBILITY BY PROVIDING SERVICES IN A VARIETY OF LANGUAGES
