What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

Trusted since the 1940s Canadian Hearing Services provides industry-leading services products and programs to Deaf and hard-of-hearing Canadians to ensure barrier-free access and increased quality of life. It is an independent registered non-profit organization that reinvests proceeds from product and program sales back into community services the focus of the organization. For more information about CHS services visit www.chs.ca or call 1-866-518-0000.
