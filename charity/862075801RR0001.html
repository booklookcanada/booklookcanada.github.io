THE MAJOR PROGRAM OF SDRJ IS THE COMMUNITY JUSTICE CONFERENCE (CJC) WHICH TAKES PLACE IN A SAFE CONTROLLED SETTING IN THE COMMUNITY. THE VICTIMS(S) ACCUSED AND THEIR RESPECTIVE GUARDIANS AND/OR SUPPORT PEOPLE ARE BROUGHT TOGETHER WITH TRAINED ACCREDITED FACILITATORS TO DISCUSS THE OFFENCE ITS EFFECTS AND TO JOINTLY DECIDE HOW TO RIGHT THE WRONG THAT HAS BEEN DONE.
