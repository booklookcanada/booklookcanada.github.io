What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

The charity War Child Canada helps families and communities in Afghanistan the Democratic Republic of Congo South Sudan Sudan Uganda and Yemen through education youth and women’s livelihoods support and other vital services. The charity helps children by providing safe spaces to play learn and seek support. The charity helps children who have been forced to leave school to catch up on their education and return to learning. The charity helps survivors of conflict through counselling services and activities that address trauma and support recovery. The charity helps communities by providing essential educational materials and supplies. The charity helps protect the rights of women and children by educating and training communities and local leaders. The charity helps families grow their livelihoods by providing farming supplies—such as seeds tools and livestock—and by offering training in sustainable agriculture and livestock management. The charity helps young people resolve disputes peacefully by teaching conflict resolution skills and promoting social cohesion. The charity helps young adults and women start and grow small businesses through vocational training and economic support. The charity helps deliver high-quality programs by partnering with local and international agencies
