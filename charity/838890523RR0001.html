TO PROVIDE PRIMARY HEALTHCARE SERVICES ACCESS TO EXPERTISE OF ALLIED MULTI-DISCIPLINARY HEALTHCARE PROFESSIONALS AND AN EDUCATION FACILITY TO DELIVER PREVENTATIVE AND HEALTH EDUCATION WITH THE GOAL OF IMPROVING THE QUALITY OF HEALTHCARE PROVIDED TO PATIENTS. THE ORGANIZATION ALSO OFFERS A WALK-IN CLINIC FOR EPISODIC AND NON-URGENT CARE WITH THE GOAL OF REDUCING UNNECESSARY EMERGENCY DEPARTMENT VISITS.
