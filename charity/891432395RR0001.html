What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

THE FOUNDATION PROVIDES GRANTS TO GROUPS WITH CHARITABLE REGISTRATION NUMBERS PRIMARILY IN THE GREATER KINGSTON AND SURROUNDING AREA. THESE GRANTS ARE AWARDED TO ENCOURAGE COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT IN AREAS SUCH AS HEALTH AND SOCIAL SERVICES ARTS AND CULTURE ENVIRONMENTAL INITIATIVES AND COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT. THE FOUNDATION PLAYS A LEADERSHIP ROLE IN THE COMMUNITY IN AREAS SUCH AS COMMUNITY CONSULTATION.
