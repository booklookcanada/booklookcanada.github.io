What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Jun 30, 2024)

WCS CANADA PROVIDES EVIDENCE-BASED EXPERT GUIDANCE AND POLICY RECOMMENDATIONS TO EMPOWER DECISION-MAKERS TO TAKE TRANSFORMATIVE ACTION FOR WILDLIFE ECOSYSTEMS AND PEOPLE. THROUGH OUR OWN RESEARCH ACCUMULATED EXPERTISE AND UNDERSTANDING OF SOCIO-ECOLOGICAL CONTEXTS WE STAND AS A TRUSTED VOICE ON CONSERVATION AND CLIMATE SCIENCE. WCS CANADA PLAYS A UNIQUE ROLE IN THE CONSERVATION LANDSCAPE OF CANADA. WE WORK AT THE INTERSECTION OF BIODIVERSITY CLIMATE CHANGE AND HEALTH IN PRIORITY LANDSCAPES AND SEASCAPES WHERE WE HAVE HAD A LONG-TERM FIELD PRESENCE AND WHERE THERE IS HIGH POTENTIAL FOR DURABLE CONSERVATION GAINS. WE HAVE SEVERAL NATIONAL INITIATIVES TO COMPLEMENT AND EXPAND OUR GEOGRAPHICALLY FOCUSED WORK THAT ADDRESS ISSUES SUCH AS BIODIVERSITY POLICY WILDLIFE HEALTH KEY BIODIVERSITY AREAS CLIMATE CHANGE AND TRAINING YOUNG CONSERVATION SCIENTISTS. OUR DEDICATION TO BRIDGING SCIENCE AND ACTION HAS LED US TO COLLABORATE CLOSELY WITH INDIGENOUS COMMUNITIES AND GOVERNMENTS NURTURING A DEEPER UNDERSTANDING OF NATURE THAT LEADS TO SAFEGUARDING PLACES SPECIES AND ECOSYSTEMS. WCS CANADA'S UNIQUE BLEND OF ON-THE-GROUND FIELD PROGRAMS AND NATIONAL INITIATIVES WIELDS INFLUENCE SHAPING CONSERVATION POLICIES AND PRACTICES ACROSS THE COUNTRY AND GLOBALLY. OUR SCIENTIFIC EXPERTISE GUIDES KEY DECISIONS FROM GLOBAL AGREEMENTS TO CRUCIAL LOCAL LAND USE CHOICES MAKING US A TRUSTED ADVISOR TO DECISION-MAKERS AT ALL LEVELS.
