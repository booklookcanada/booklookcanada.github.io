What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

THE CHARITY IS A CHRISTIAN MISSIONARY ORGANIZATION WHICH RECRUITS MISSIONARIES TO ESTABLISH INDIGENOUS CHURCHES AND PROCLAIM THE GOSPEL OF THE LORD JESUS CHRIST. THE CHARITY RECEIVES AND MAINTAINS FUNDS FOR CHARITABLE PURPOSES AND IN PARTICULAR FOR THE BAPTIST CHURCH IN PROPEGATION OF THE GOSPEL. THE MISSIONARIES TEACH CONDUCT PUBLIC MEETINGS OF A RELIGIOUS AND EVANGELISTIC NATURE TO SPREAD THE GOSPEL AND TRAIN INDIVIDUALS IN THE WORD OF GOD AND BIBLICAL PRINCIPLES.
