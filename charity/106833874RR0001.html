Who runs it — the people it pays

It reported 498 full-time positions and 749 part-time employees (year ended Mar 31, 2024). Total pay for all its positions: $77,812,897, as filed. The highest pay band it reported was “$200,000–$249,999”. 2 employees were reported in that band. Pay is reported in bands — ranges, not exact salaries. The top band is the highest range with anyone in it: it is not one person's salary, and it is not what everyone there is paid.

What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

Cambrian College is Northern Ontario’s largest college with more than 100 programs including degree-level programs. Cambrian College is also a global college of choice with students from more than 60 countries. Cambrian’s main campus is in Greater Sudbury with satellite centres in Espanola and Little Current.
