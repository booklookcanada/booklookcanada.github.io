What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

BURLINGTON COMMUNITY FOUNDATION (BCF) WAS ESTABLISHED IN 1999 BY AND FOR THE PEOPLE OF BURLINGTON. BCF EXERCISES LEADERSHIP BY IDENTIFYING THE HIGHEST PRIORITY NEEDS IN THE COMMUNITY AND ACTS AS A CATALYST FOR ACTION BY CONVENING MULTI-SECTOR PARTNERS AND DONORS WHO HAVE THE DESIRE TO HELP ADDRESS THEM. THROUGH THE EXPRESSED LEGACIES OF BURLINGTON'S GENEROUS PARTNERS AND DONORS WE CREATE PERMANENT IMPROVEMENTS IN OUR COMMUNITY. TOGETHER WE PROVIDE GRANTS TO SUPPORT MEANINGFUL LOCAL CAUSES IN AREAS SUCH AS HEALTH EDUCATION YOUTH RECREATION ARTS/CULTURE AND THE ENVIRONMENT. IN DOING SO WE ASSIST THOSE MOST VULNERABLE IN OUR COMMUNITY.
