What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

HURONIA COMMUNITY FOUNDATION IS A LOCALLY RUN PUBLIC FOUNDATION THAT BUILDS AND PRUDENTLY OVERSEES ENDOWMENTS TO SUPPORT CHARITABLE ACTIVITIES IN THE GEOGRAPHIC AREA IN AND AROUND THE MUNICIPALITIES OF MIDLAND PENETANGUISHENE TAY TINY ELMVALE WASAGA BEACH AND COLLINGWOOD ONTARIO. THE FOUNDATION PROVIDES AN OPPORTUNITY FOR DONORS TO ENSURE SUPPORT OF THEIR FAVOURITE CHARITY IN PERPETUITY OR TO USE THE FOUNDATIONS EXPERTISE TO IDENTIFY AREAS OF GREATEST NEED. THE FOUNDATION SUPPORTS A BROAD SPECTRUM OF INTERESTS INCLUDING ARTS & CULTURE HEALTH EDUCATION SPORTS & RECREATION SOCIAL SERVICES AND THE ENVIRONMENT.
