WE HOLD UPLIFTING SERVICES WEEKLY WITH A KIDS PROGRAM USING LOTS OF MUSIC AND SONG. WE OFFER PASTORAL CARE TO SENIORS AND ANY WHO REQUEST IT. WE HOLD WEDDINGS BAPTISMS AND FUNERALS.
