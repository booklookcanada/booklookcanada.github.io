What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

Mission Services of London is a Christian faith-based social service agency delivering services through five branches. The Men's Mission provides emergency shelter food referrals to other services transitional housing and more to men experiencing homelessness. Rotholme Family Shelter provides emergency shelter food crisis intervention assistance seeking permanent housing and much more to families (men women and children) experiencing homelessness. Quintin Warner House provides live-in addiction treatment for men. Community Mental Health Programs provide the following services to those coping with mental illness: street outreach crisis intervention case management life skills coaching and more. The Mission Store operates a charity store selling donated used clothing and household items at modest prices; it also operates a voucher program that provides free clothing and household items to individuals and families; in addition to providing free children's clothing to elementary schools in the area.
