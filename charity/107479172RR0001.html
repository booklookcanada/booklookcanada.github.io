What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

HERITAGE PARK ALLIANCE CHURCH FUNDS THE WORK OF OUR DENOMINATIONAL HEADQUARTERS THE CHRISTIAN & MISSIONARY ALLIANCE IN CANADA BY DONATING TO THE SUPPORT OF CANADIAN AND OVERSEAS MISSIONARY ACTIVITIES. WE PREACH AND TEACH THE GOSPEL OF JESUS CHRIST THROUGH PUBLIC WORSHIP SERVICES BOTH IN-PERSON AND VIA THE INTERNET. WE ACTIVELY SEEK TO MEET THE SPIRITUAL NEEDS OF OUR SERVICE AREA AND PROVIDE PASTORAL CARE AND COUNSELLING TO THOSE SEEKING IT. WE PROVIDE WEEKLY MINISTRIES FOR CHILDREN YOUTH YOUNG ADULTS WOMEN AND MEN AND MONTHLY ACTIVITIES FOR SENIORS. WE OFFER A MENTORSHIP PROGRAM FOR YOUTH AND ADULTS TO FOSTER SPIRITUAL RELATIONAL AND PERSONAL GROWTH. ALL OF THESE PROGRAMS AND MINISTRIES ARE MADE POSSIBLE WITH THE ASSISTANCE OF OUR 120 VOLUNTEERS. WE ADMINISTER A BENEVOLENCE PROGRAM PROVIDING FINANCIAL & PRACTICAL ASSISTANCE TO THE NEEDY. THE BENEVOLENCE PROGRAM INCLUDES DONATIONS TO QUALIFIED DONEES THAT WORK WITH PEOPLE IN NEED. WE PROVIDE HOSPITAL & NURSING HOME VISITATION AS NEEDED. WE CONDUCT WEDDINGS & FUNERALS. WE OPERATE A CHILDREN'S SUMMER CAMP FOR RECREATION & RELIGIOUS TEACHING.
IN 2024 A NEW CAREER ADULTS PROGRAM WAS STARTED. THIS WAS DESIGNED TO HELP INDIVIDUALS WHO ARE AGES 25-39.
