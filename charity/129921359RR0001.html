What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

St. Cuthbert's provides weekly worship services pastoral care a music program. It offers Christian education to children and youth. It supports the work of the Presbyterian Church in Canada and its agency Presbyterian World Service and Development. It serves and supports the local Hamilton community through its membership and involvement with True City a partnership with mostly inner-city churches. It provides food vouchers to the hungry in partnership with Westdale churches. It works with other Presbyterian churches to support refugees arriving in Hamilton. It contributes financially to local service agencies. It supports a church congregation in Uganda who care for orphans and provide for their education.
