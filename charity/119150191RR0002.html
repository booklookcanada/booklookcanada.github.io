What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

During 2024 the Charity ran 13 overnight camps (7 of them were week long 5 of them were weekend events and one was 5 days long). In addition the Charity ran 4 weeklong day camps. The camps were for children 5 and under youth teens young adults women families and seniors. The Charity also made our facilities available for like minded groups who did the same thing we did - run camps for individuals of all ages. A total of 35 groups took advantage of this availability. Most of our work was accomplished through the hiring of seasonal summer staff. We had just over 96 volunteers who gave a total of 2233 hours of effort.
There were no new programs introduced in 2024.
