What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

WE PROVIDE SUPPORT AND SERVICES FOR SALVATION ARMY MINISTRY UNITS IN ONTARIO. WE RUN SUMMER CAMPS FOR RECREATION AND RELIGIOUS PURPOSES. WE PROVIDE PASTORAL CARE TO RESIDENTS OF LONG-TERM CARE FACILITIES AND OTHERS. WE OPERATE AN EMERGENCY VEHICLE THAT PROVIDES ASSISTANCE TO EMERGENCY SERVICES AND DISTRIBUTION FOOD & CLOTHING TO HOMELESS. WE PROVIDE MINISTRIES FOR WOMEN MEN YOUTH CHILDREN AND OPERATE A FAMILY TRACING PROGRAM. WE OPEARATE CHILD CARE CENTRES. WE HOLD SENIOR CARE PROGRAMS ON A WEEKLY BASIS. WE PROVIDE RESIDENTIAL AND COUNSELLING SERVICES FOR INDIVIDUALS WITHIN THE YOUTH JUSTICE SYSTEM.
