What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

THE CHARITY DISTRIBUTES MONEY TO NEEDY INDIVIDUALS OR FAMILIES TO MEET THEIR BASIC DAILY NEEDS SUCH AS RENT CLOTHES & FOOD. IN ADDITION THE CHARITY DISTRIBUTES MONEY TO NEEDY INDIVIDUALS OR FAMILIES TO ASSIST WITH MEDICAL EMERGENCIES OR CONDITIONS WHERE INSUFFICIENT FINANCIAL MEANS EXIST. THIS INCLUDES THE PROVISION OF FINANCIAL ASSISTANCE TO PAY FOR THE MEDICAL EXPENSES OF NEEDY APPLICANTS FROM OUTSIDE OF CANADA WHO COMES TO TORONTO FOR URGENT CARE.
