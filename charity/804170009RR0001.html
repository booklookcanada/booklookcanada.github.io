THE BOOK CLUB OFFER INTELLECTUAL STIMULATION TO INMATES IN AN ENVIRONMENT WITH LIMITED CHOICES. CHILDREN OF INMATES READING PROGRAM (CHIRP) TO BUILD AND ENHANCE A HEALTHIER PARENT/CHILD RELATIONSHIP DEVELOP LITERACY AND LISTENING SKILLS INCREASE VOCABULARY AND ATTENTION SPANS FOR CHILDREN AND PROMOTE A LOVE OF LEARNING IN CHILDREN THROUGH THE CONSISTENT PRESENCE OF A PARENT AND BOOKS.
