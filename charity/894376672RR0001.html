PRIMARY FUNDRAISING VIA NEVADA TEAR-OFF TOCKET SALES IN ELGIN COUNTY. RESTORATION OF HISTORIC LAKE FREIGHTER WHEELHOUSE HAS BEEN INSTITUTED AFTER LAYOFF DUE TO COVID PANDEMIC.
