What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 30, 2024)

Fernie Youth Services provides residential and non-residential treatment and support to at risk youth in the Greater Toronto Area. During the year it operated 1 group home and 1 special education classroom. Through Youth Transition Services Fernie Youth supports community educational and family reintegration services to young persons with open or secure custody/detention status through direct service coordination advocacy and consultation. Fernie Youth also provides employment skills training and transitional housing for at risk youth aged 18 to 24.
