What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

Oakville Family YMCA is a charity dedicated to strengthening the foundations of communities. This is done by nurturing the potential of children teens and young adults; promoting healthy living; and fostering social responsibility. Our focus on inclusiveness and accessibility means we serve people of all ages backgrounds and abilities through all stages of life. Through YMCA financial assistance programs the YMCA is accessible to all. Our core offerings include: Child Care Camping Aquatics Health & Fitness Education Volunteer Development Community Initiatives and Opportunities to Give. In 2024 volunteers contributed 2 544 hours to assist the YMCA of Oakville in carrying out its service delivery activities. The value of financial assistance provided to families was $319 074.
