What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

CENTRETOWN CHURCHES SOCIAL ACTION COMMITTEE (CCSAC) OPERATES THE CENTRETOWN COMMUNITY FOOD CENTRE WHICH IS A FOOD BANK SERVING THOSE IN NEED IN CENTRAL OTTAWA. IN 2024 OVER 18 000 INDIVIDUALS WERE SERVED AN INCREASE OF 46% OVER 2023. CCSAC ALSO PROVIDES FUNDS AND SUPPLIES TO SMALL CHARITIES THAT SERVE THE VULNERABLE IN CENTRAL OTTAWA.
IN 2024 THE CENTRETOWN COMMUNITY FOOD CENTRE INCREASED ITS OPERATIONS FROM 3 DAYS TO 4 DAYS PER WEEK TO MEET THE INCREASE IN DEMAND.
