What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

Worship services weekly in person/Facebook - all ages. Bible Studies- local Long Term Care Facilities in person depending on facility restrictions. Pastoral Care those in Hospital Long Term Care & shut-ins via phone/in person. Ladies and Men's groups every 4 months Sunday School Sunday for ages 3- 10 during our church service as needed. Bible Studies - monthly. We financially support overseas missionary activities donating to CBM missionary relief and development in other countries support local food bank local youth centre CBOQ to help aid retired ministers and families - gifting funds which is our denomination headquarters a registered charity Big Brothers/Big Sisters The Daily Bread Canadian Bible Society The Gideon's ShareWord Galcom Inc. We support financially the working poor homeless widows widowers children and elderly in our community. We may buy groceries pay bills or give them gift cards to enable them to have self-esteem by getting items they need for themselves. Christmas we give food baskets to families who would not be able to do so support financially local Angel Tree program local persons in need. Volunteers who drive people to Dr visits prepare food baskets/deliver them buy supplies for local food bank call individuals to check in on them. Have donation baskets so individuals can donate food to local food bank. Worship song practices.
Nursery as needed. Building improvements/expansion committee. Provide funding- student -Haiti- go to university. Started making preparations for some members of our congregation to go on a mission trip to Jamacia.
