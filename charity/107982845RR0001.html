What it says it does

OFFER A COMPREHENSIVE PROGRAM OF CAREER-ORIENTED POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION AND TRAINING TO ASSIST INDIVIDUALS IN FINDING AND KEEPING EMPLOYMENT TO MEET THE NEEDS OF EMPLOYERS AND THE CHANGING WORK ENVIRONMENT AND TO SUPPORT THE ECONOMIC AND SOCIAL NEEDS OF THEIR LOCAL AND DIVERSE COMMUNITIES. WE UNDERTAKE A RANGE OF EDUCATIONAL-RELATED AND TRAINING-RELATE ACTIVITIES INCLUDING A) ENTERING INTO PARTNERSHIPS WITH BUSINESS INDUSTRY AND OTHER EDUCATIONAL INSTITUTIONS B) ADULT VOCATIONAL EDUCATION AND TRAINING C) BASIC SKILLS AND LITERACY TRAINING D) APPRENTICESHIP IN-SCHOOL TRAINING E) APPLIED RESEARCH WE PROVIDE FINANCIAL ASSISTANCE TO FLEMING STUDENTS THROUGH THE DISTRIBUTION OF BURSARIES AND SCHOLARSHIPS.
