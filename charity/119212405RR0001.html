What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

THE FOUNDATION PROVIDES FINANCIAL ASSISTANCE IN THE FORM OF GRANTS AND LOANS TO SUPPORT THE ANGLICAN CHURCH OF CANADA. THIS SUPPORT IS GIVEN TO HELP FINANCE INNOVATIVE MINISTRIES AND PROGRAMS TO AWARD BURSARIES FOR THEOLOGICAL EDUCTION AND TO ASSIST IN THE CONSTRUCTION AND MAINTENANCE OF CHURCH PROPERTIES. THE FOUNDATION'S WORK IS MADE POSSIBLE THROUGH THE DEVOTION GENEROSITY AND COLLECTIVE VISION OF HUNDREDS OF MEMBERS AND DONORS ACROSS CANADA. THE FOUNDATION ALSO ADMINISTERS VARIOUR TESTAMENTARY AND OTHER ENDOWMENTS AND TRUSTS ESTABLISHED TO PROVIDE FUNDS FOR VARIOUS CHARITABLE PURPOSES.
