OTTAWA NETWORK FOR EDUCATION FUELS KIDS AND IGNITES LEARNING EVERY SCHOOL DAY. OUR MISSION IS TO INSPIRE AND SUPPORT EVERY CHILD'S LEARNING JOURNEY IN REACHING THEIR FULL POTENTIAL BY CONNECTING COMMUNITY RESOURCES TO HELP K-12 STUDENTS SUCCEED. WORKING WITH THEIR FOUR PUBLIC SCHOOL BOARDS IN OTTAWA OUR PROGRAMS ARE OFFERED IN ENGLISH AND FRENCH.
