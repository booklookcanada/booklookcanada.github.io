What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

Epilepsy Ontario provides information support and counseling for people living with Epilepsy. In addition the organization provides the Summerfest Camp program and provincially focused education and training to community stakeholders and access to a resource centre. Extensive awareness activities include the weekly enews Voices of Epilepsy various online and print publications and stakeholder presentations March Awareness Month and Purple Day activities. Facilitates ongoing support to new chapters and existing local epilepsy agencies. Distributes brochures to families medical offices and individuals seeking information. Disseminates information via the website online media advocacy programs and other events. Epilepsy Information Specialist - accessible by phone and internet in communities where there is no local epilepsy agency provides support and information to assist people to better understand their condition and as well as provides tools and resources to navigate the health care system. Also provides specialized information support to local agencies.
