What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

U-Link Patient and Family Fund - Providing financial support for families who have to travel to access an early phase clinical trial when they have a child with cancer. Kindred Cares Grant- Funding for residential hospice programs and pediatric palliative care research projects and programs in Ontario. Youth Mental Health Grant. Treats & Treasures Cart Program - We provide funding to hospitals to run a treats & treasures cart program for kids undergoing cancer treatment. We have been doing this since 2021. And continue to expand into more hospitals across Canada.
Family Support Packages - providing families with a package of gift cards to help with groceries and other everyday costs when they have a child in cancer treatment. Housing Support Program - providing families with housing support. To qualify a review of a family's financial situation (by a licensed insolvency agent and mortgage specialist) must be completed. If it is deemed appropriate $5000 is provided by the Foundation to go towards a mortgage payment or rent. The family must have a child undergoing cancer treatment.
