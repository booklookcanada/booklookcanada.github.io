What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

THERE ARE REGULAR WORSHIP MEETINGS AND ACTIVITIES TO SUPPORT OUR OBJECTIVES AT BOTH LOCATIONS. THE WOMEN MEET ON A REGULAR BASIS AS THE UNITED CHURCH WOMEN'S GROUP. THE BRUCE BELL CHOIR AND VOCAL CHOIR ARE AN INTEGRAL PART OF THE ORGANIZATION. MISSION AND SERVICE AND OUTREACH COMMITTEES SUPPORT THE COMMUNITY. THE OBJECTIVES OF THE PASTORAL CHARGE (SAUBLE BEACH AND ST ANDREW'S CHURCHES) ARE: 1. TO PROVIDE RESIDENT OF THIS FARM AND RESORT AREA WITH CHRISTIAN GUIDANCE AND A PLACE FOR WORSHIP. 2. TO CONDUCT RELIGIOUS AND SOCIAL ACTIVITIES FOR ALL AGES WITHIN THE CHURCH HALL. 3. TO SUPPORT LOCAL AND INTERNATIONAL CHARITIES THROUGH OUR MISSION AND SERVICE AND OUTREACH PROGRAMS.
IN 2024 OUR SAUBLE BEACH CONGREGATION VOTED TO BECOME AN AFFIRMING CONGREGATION THEREBY ENCOURAGING THE ACTIVE PARTICIPATION OF PEOPLE REGARDLESS OF THEIR AGES HEALTH CONDITIONS RACES GENDERS GENDER IDENTITY SEXUAL ORIENTATIONS FAMILY DIVERSITY ABILITY ECONOMIC CIRCUMSTANCES RELIGION SPIRITUAL AND ETHNIC BACKGROUND.
