What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Aug 31, 2024)

Grace Cafe operates a street mission and soup kitchen providing free lunch meals to the underprivileged in the City of St Thomas and area. All services carried out with over 8 500 hours from community volunteers donations from the community at large individuals and corporate. Excess food is distributed to the clients as available along with seasonal clothing and household items as available. Grace Cafe is operating an addictions resource outreach to the community to the homeless who are in addiction and to the community of St Thomas and area to provide information on addictions to provide assistance in accessing addiction services including rehabilitation treatment programs and to assist in related issues such as aftercare housing mental health services medical and other related issues to addiction.
