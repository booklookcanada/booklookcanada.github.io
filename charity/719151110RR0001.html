What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

Khalil Health Center Canada is a psychological and spiritual wellness center. Khalil Center specializes in spiritually integrated mental health care. Some of the services provided by Khalil Center Canada includes: individual/family counseling career counseling medication management education services and spiritual services. Khalil Center’s approach emphasizes the following: physical reconstruction behavioral reformation and spiritual elevation. Khalil Center utilizes faith–based approaches rooted in Islamic theological concepts whilst also integrating the science of psychology towards addressing social psychological communal and spiritual health. Khalil Center also provides subsidized mental health services through charitable contributions to the community. Khalil Health Center Canada is designed to provide greater access to community oriented mental health services and in particular address behavioral healthcare disparities. Khalil Center Canada also provides their service in multiple languages and is culturally tailored to meet the needs of ethnic diversity. Treatment is provided to any individual and their ability to pay isn’t taken into consideration. Our goal is to provide treatment to anyone irrespective of their race culture or financial capability. Additionally direct intervention mental health services are also provided on-site which includes the employment of registered psychotherapists GP psychotherapists psychiatrists and clinical psychologists. Subsidized Clinical Treatment: Provide ongoing subsidized mental health care through direct Counselling and Psychotherapy services. This was possible through the support of partners in the community who subsidized the cost of treatment for referrals. Community Education: We also carry out various psycho-educational workshops and events on different mental health topics. such as 'Coping with mental health during the pandemic' 'Emotional Intelligence' and 'Coping with Anxiety' . Khalil Center Toronto has also started offering in-house peer support groups with a therapist facilitating the group on different topics.
