What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Aug 31, 2024)

THE HASTINGS AND PRINCE EDWARD LEARNING FOUNDATION IS THE CHARITABLE ARM OF THE HASTINGS AND PRINCE EDWARD DISTRICT SCHOOL BOARD. IT WAS ESTABLISHED FOR THE PURPOSE OF RAISING FUNDS THAT WILL PROMOTE SUCCESS AND ENHANCE EDUCATIONAL ACTIVITIES FOR ALL STUDENTS IN THE HASTINGS AND PRINCE EDWARD DISTRICT. THE ON-GOING PROGRAMS ARE: FOOD FOR LEARNING THE FOOD SHARING PROJECT LEAD AGENCY OF THE SCHOOL NUTRITION PROGRAM (SNP) STUDENT EMERGENCY FUND AND SHARING CUPBOARDS. AS THE LEAD AGENCY OF MYCS THE LEARNING FOUNDATION HAS THE RESPONSIBILITY FOR THE ADMINISTRATION OF SCHOOL NUTRITION PROGRAMS IN A 5 COUNTY AREA.
