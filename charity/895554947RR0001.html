What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Sep 30, 2024)

Medical Ministry International has been providing spiritual and compassionate health care in this world of need. Medical Ministry International has made lasting impact in 41 countries from addressing medical and surgical needs of communities especially unreached populations to establishing and equipping hospitals and clinics launching specialized healthcare services like physical therapy vision and ENT and developing leaders who continue to serve the next generation. Today we directly serve 14 countries by facilitating volunteer participants including doctors nurses other health care professionals and general helpers on project teams to equip empower and expand the reach of Christ-centered healthcare worldwide. Value of health care services provided that were delivered to people in need on Project Teams and local initiatives: $33.02 million. Services were delivered through the following programs: Vision Care Program $12.9 M Dental Care Program $6.1 M Primary Care and Prevention Program $8.9 M Surgical Care Program $3.6 M Education and Nutrition Program $1.5 M Physical Therapy Program $0.02 M Value of health care services provided by the network of Health Centers with whom Medical Ministry International partners: $4.5 million. Total value of services $37.52 million. Countries include Bolivia Cambodia Colombia Dominican Republic Ecuador Ghana Honduras India Indonesia Mexico Nepal Peru Philippines Thailand Ukraine.
