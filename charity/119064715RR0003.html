What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

The Advancement of Roman Catholic (RC) Religion by the formation administration & support of the priests and religious brothers of the OMI Congregation (hereinafter referred to as Oblates); by establishing operating & maintaining places of worship engaging in pastoral activities; Advancement of Education by providing schools & facilities and academic & religious instruction in the doctrine & teachings of the RC Church; Relief of Poverty by caring for the poor & sick & by providing on-going care maintenance & support of the retired & infirmed Oblates & by providing for missions established & operated by the corporation outside of Canada; Funding Other Qualified Donees which are in pursuit of purposes consistent with the purposes of the corporation. The charity had no income and expenses in the fiscal year 2024. However it sometimes receives donations such as estate funds from donors.
