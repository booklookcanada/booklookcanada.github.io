What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Jan 31, 2024)

THE PURPOSE OF THE CORPORATION IS TO PROMOTE THE EFFICIENCY MORALE ESPRIT DE CORPS AND REINTEGRATION OF THE ACTIVE AND RETIRED MEMBERS OF THE CANADIAN SPECIAL OPERATIONS FORCES (CANSOF) INTO CIVILIAN LIFE IN ORDER TO ADVANCE THE SAFETY AND SECURITY OF CANADA BY: 1) PROVIDING FINANCIAL ASSISTANCE TO ACTIVE AND RETIRED MEMBERS OF THE CANSOF AND THEIR FAMILIES OR REFERRALS TO OTHER AGENCIES; 2) AWARDING SCHOLARSHIPS OR BURSARIES TO FAMILIES OF ACTIVE AND RETIRED MEMBERS OF THE CANSOF FOR POST-SECONDARY EDUCATION AND PROFESSIONAL AND VOCATIONAL TRAINING; 3) PROVIDING INFORMATION SESSIONS AND MENTORSHIP OPPORTUNITIES TO ASSIST ACTIVE AND RETIRED MEMBERS OF THE CANSOF WHO ARE RE-ENTERING THE WORK-FORCE; AND 4) ESTABLISHING AND OPERATING A MUSEUM TO EXHIBIT THE ARTIFACTS HISTORY AND TRADITIONS OF THE CANSOF AND TO EDUCATE THE CANADIAN PUBLIC ABOUT THE HISTORY AND LEGACY OF THE CANSOF
