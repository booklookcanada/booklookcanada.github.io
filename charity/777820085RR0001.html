What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

The Foundation collected goods in kind and received cash donations that it provided to qualified donees. The donations were used to support families of Canadian military personnel. The Foundation undertakes several programs which promote and uphold the morale of the Canadian Armed Forces by raising the public's awareness and appreciation for members of the miliary and their families. Additionally the Foundation provides financial assistance to Canadian Armed Forces and Veteran families in need of food security housing childcare and mental health resources.
