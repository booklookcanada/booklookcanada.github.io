What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

We operate an emergency grocery program (food bank) three days/week using a choice based model. We offer two community meals weekly on a drop-in basis. We deliver social justice workshops to school-aged children and youth as well as to groups of adults. We provide employment training secondary and post-secondary placements for youth. We convene a city-wide nonprofit network focused on food security. We offer 1:1 support and referral services on a drop-in and appointment basis. We deliver cohort-based peer leadership and skills development programming. In 2024 195 volunteers offered 8 600 hours of service in support of our programs.
