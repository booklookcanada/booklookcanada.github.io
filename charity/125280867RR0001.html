WE SPEND THE YEAR MEETING AND PLANNING FOR OUR ANNUAL FALL FAIR HELD IN SEPTEMBER EACH YEAR. THE FAIR GIVES THE COMMUNITY THE OPPORTUNITY TO DISPLAY LIVESTOCK HORSES FIELD CROPS AND GRAIN.
