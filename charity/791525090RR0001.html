What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Aug 31, 2024)

The Gathering—Weekly Family Fellowship and Bible Study; Community Outreach and PlayDays—Activity-based programs in low income housing complexes; Bible studies and Spiritual Growth—Trauma informed literacy building culturally relevant Bible lessons for individuals and groups; Counselling Services—Clinical psychotherapy and counselling; Family Strengthening—Whole life healing for families through psychotherapy and attachment-theory-based activities; Compass—Crisis response team to northern communities; Competent Caring workshops—Training for individuals who are in supportive roles; LifeBuild—Life development and skills training; Women Restored—Supporting women impacted by domestic violence; LifeBuild Parenting—parenting series for Indigenous families; Cooking For Life—Teaching nutritional cooking with limited resources; Camps—Summer camps for Children youth and family; Youth retreats—Land-based programs for Indigenous youth; Friday Outreach—ministry to street adjacent; Learning Centre—Bible training that meets people where they are and apprentices them into leadership; Next Steps—equipping the street involved to take the next steps to independence and health; Enhance—Life skills and safety-in-the-city training for Indigenous youth.
