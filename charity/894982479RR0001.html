What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

CityKidz exists to increase resiliency and inspire big dreams for Canadian children living in low-income communities by providing inspirational experiences and nurturing personal relationship one child at a time. Addressed by mission through child focused programs which impact over 1 200 children each week in Hamilton Ontario alone. These programs include weekly home visits to every one of the children on the active roster and two additional fast paced values based programs - one for children aged 6-11 and the second for children aged 12-17. Additional projects which support our mission include activities such as CityKidz Connects CityKidz Cares Food and Nutrition CityKidz Online Summer to Remember CityYouth and Gift of Christmas.
