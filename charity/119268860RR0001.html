Who runs it — the people it pays

It reported 8 full-time positions and 3 part-time employees (year ended Mar 31, 2024). Total pay for all its positions: $310,431, as filed. The highest pay band it reported was “$120,000–$159,999”. 1 employee was reported in that band. Pay is reported in bands — ranges, not exact salaries. The top band is the highest range with anyone in it: it is not one person's salary, and it is not what everyone there is paid.

What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

TMHF DONATES FUNDS TO QUINTE HEALTH CARE TRENTON MEMORIAL TO PURCHASE MEDICAL EQUIPMENT FINANCE DEVELOPMENT OF NEW HOSPITAL FACILITIES AND SUPPORT VARIOUS HEALTHCARE AND EDUCATIONAL PROGRAMS. TMHF ALSO MANAGES RESTRICTED AND ENDOWMENT FUNDS AND TRANSFERS THE ANNUAL INVESTMENT INCOME TO SUPPORT PROGRAMS AT QUINTE HEALTH CARE TRENTON MEMORIAL. THROUGH THE KAY STAFFORD MEMORIAL FUND TMHF SUPPORTS LOCAL HEALTHCARE PROGRAMS AND INITIATIVES FOR THE BETTERMENT OF THE COMMUNITY.
