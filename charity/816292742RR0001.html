What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

(1) Providing emergency financial assistance to current or former RCMP members employees and their dependents. (2) Providing financial assistance to RCMP applicants who are members of an under-represented population within the RCMP. (3) Providing maintenance for memorials for RCMP members killed while on duty. (4) Providing grief support for current or former RCMP members employees and their dependents on the death injury or displacement of serving RCMP members in the performance of their duties. (5) Providing relieve for conditions associated with disabilities for former RCMP member and dependents who by reason of their disability are in need of residential modifications or mobility assistance. (6) Providing grants to qualified donees. All programs and activities are conducted by volunteers with the exception of contracted services for specific activities such as construction of plaques or research in relation to medical matters e.g. PTSD
