What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

A. Hold Worship Services every Sunday. Currently in Sherwood Community Centre- Room 3 located in6355 Main St W Milton ON L9T2Y1. We rent this facility for $520.14 – 866.90 per month. B. In our worship services we worship God through various means like prayer praise and worship songs collecting our tithes and offerings and preaching of Christian doctrines from the Bible. C. In our preaching services the Pastors/Elders discuss the Christian doctrines based on the Bible. Our doctrines are found in the Statement of Faith Section of our By-laws. The Pastors/Elders qualifications is primarily based in the Bible as provided in 1 Timothy 3:1-13 and Titus1:5-9. The lead pastor of Milton Christ-Centered Church took his Masters in Ministry in Asia-Pacific Institute of International Studies in Singapore. D. We also promote Christianity by preaching the Good News of God’s love to our friends and relatives in Home Bible Study settings and this caters to members and non-members. By preaching the Good News to non-members we aim to positively influence the community to be Godly and law-abiding citizens. i. We have 3 Bible Study group all in Milton ON. ii. These Bible Study groups are held every Thursdays every other Saturdays and once every 3 months held in the homes of the members. iii. Each Bible Study Group is usually composed of 10-15 people. iv. Each Bible Study Group is led by either the Pastor or the assigned member. v. There is no cost involved in the Bible study group other than the meal that is shared after the study. E. We conduct Ceremonies. i. Baptism for new believers. Baptism is one of the New Testament ordinances adhered to by Milton Christ-Centered Church as directed by the scripture in Acts 2:41-42 and other related Bible verses like Matthew 28:19-20 as fundamental steps of obedience and as requirements for church membership. ii. Child dedication. Child dedication is an act of parent presenting the child to the giver who is God and praying for the child. Psalm 127:3 iii. Church Wedding Milton Christ-Centered Church believes and uphold the sanctity and exclusivity of marriage as being holy God-willed and blessed union of a man and woman who are joined together in their humanity and in their spirituality sharing in the life of the Holy Trinity. Gen 1:27; Gen 2:18-25 The church intends to conduct church wedding in the future as soon as the Pastor Pastor satisfies all the government requirements.
