It reported 2 full-time positions and 8 part-time employees (year ended Mar 31, 2024). Total pay for all its positions: $278,431, as filed. The highest pay band it reported was “$80,000–$119,999”. 1 employee was reported in that band. Pay is reported in bands — ranges, not exact salaries.
