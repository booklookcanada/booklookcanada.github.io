What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

The museum operates as a community museum and archives for the benefit and use of the public We collect preserve research exhibit and interpret objects which demonstrates the founding settlement diversity and development of the community of Dundas and surrounding area. We offer educational programming of and historical nature for all ages. We support and partner with other community organizations in support of heritage projects. We have developed and offer a catalogue of education programs that meet Ontario Curriculum guidelines. We have developed and offer a catalogue of programs and education opportunities for seniors of all abilities. We offer exhibition space and opportunities for local artists to show and sell their works.
