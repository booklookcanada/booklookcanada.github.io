TO ESTABLISH AN ORTHODOX JEWISH HIGH SCHOOL FOR BOYS DEVOTED TO THE STUDY OF THE LAWS TENETS AND DOCTRINES OF THE JEWISH FAITH. TO OPERATE A HOUSE OF RELIGIOUS PRAYER AND WORSHIP WHICH ADHERES TO AND COMPLIES WITH THE LAWS CUSTOMS AND TRADITIONS OF ORTHODOX JUDAISM AS INTERPRETED BY THE CHASIDIC JEWISH MOVEMENT KNOWN AS CHABAD LUBAVITCH.
