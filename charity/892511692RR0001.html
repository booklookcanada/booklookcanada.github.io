What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

OBC delivers experiential education programs that cultivate resilience leadership connections and compassion through inspiring journeys of self discovery in the natural world. The majority of our programs take place while travelling through wilderness settings throughout Canada's provinces & territories. We also run urban programs for inner city youth out of our centres in Toronto & Vancouver. Activities include; canoeing sea kayaking white water canoeing trekking and hiking mountaineering rock climbing snow shoeing & winter camping. Programs are accessible to the general public schools community and other charitable organizations. Outward Bound Canada delivers community programs working with a number of diverse and vulnerable populations including Indigenous Peoples underprivileged youth & new Canadians; our programs also support employability for young struggling adults through interpersonal skill development and tools related to self efficacy.
Outward Bound Canada is continually modifying our programs to be more inclusive and to meet the specific needs of our participants.
