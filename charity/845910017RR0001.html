What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

We continue to provide the public with exposure to the music of a Scottish Pipe and Drum Band by participating in community events and parades. The band also promotes Scottish heritage by offering the community with free music lessons. During 1993 the band has been able to play in parades and concerts in the community with all restrictions fully lifted.
