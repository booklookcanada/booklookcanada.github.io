What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

Children's Mental Health Programs including Section 23 School FASD Treatment Program Trauma Treatment Intensive Community Services Early Intervention School Based Programs Respite Learning Assessments and Resources. Individual Couple Family and Group Counselling including Quick Response and Single Session Counselling. Outreach Services in two Neighbourhoods to assist low income families cover basic needs. Newcomers Program aimed at aligning Mental Health Supports for Government Assisted Refugees in Waterloo Region. Pathways to Education site aimed at reducing poverty and its effects by lowering high school drop-out rates and increasing access to post secondary education. Lead Agency of Connectivity KW where local Health and Social Service Agencies meet to collaboratively and proactively address situations of elevated risk and support individuals to access required services. Home of the Family Violence Project of Waterloo Region.
Due to Merger with Kitchener Waterloo Counselling Services - expanded Counselling including 2SLGBTQIA+ and Relationship Supports and supports to Victims and Survivors of Abuse and Walk-in Counselling Services. Due to Merger with Saint Monica House - expanded programs for Pregnant and Parenting Youth and their Children a 15 Unit Dedicated Supporting Housing Complex at Ainslie St in Cambridge.
