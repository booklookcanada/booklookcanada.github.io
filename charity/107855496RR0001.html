What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

Weekly worship services prayer meetings bible studies etc. Youth/Young adults/children's meetings held on a regular basis. Also groups geared to different groups i.e men's women's moms etc.. A weekly soup kitchen is available as well as groceries (dry-goods) for those in need free of charge. A clothing ministry is also available to those in need free on charge. We also provide ministry (food clothing) to the homeless and those living in temporary housing situations. Support of overseas missionaries through Elim Fellowship Canada as well as to India where we support an orphanage housing up to 100 orphaned children and their caregivers. Prayer and ministry in office daily and throughout the week preparing sermons and providing support and oversight to congregation. General care to members of the congregation provided on an as-need basis. Many volunteers carry out various weekly programs. Hours of volunteers are too numerous to tally.
