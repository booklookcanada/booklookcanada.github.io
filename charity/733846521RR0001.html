What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

THE CHURCH IS A RELIGIOUS ORGANIZATION THE PRIMARY PURPOSE OF WHICH IS TO PROVIDE A PLACE OF WORSHIP FOR ITS CONGREGATION. THE CHURCH OFFERS SPIRITUAL SERVICES TO CONGREGANTS IN THE BURLINGTON AND SURROUNDING AREA. THE SERVICES INVOLVE CELEBRATING THE HOLY LITURGY MULTIPLE TIMES PER WEEK CONDUCTING A WEEKLY BIBLE STUDY AS WELL AS OFFERING SPIRITUAL MEETINGS FOR THE YOUTH AND THE GENERAL CONGREGANTS. THE CHURCH STRIVES TO OFFER A BROAD SET OF SPIRITUAL SERVICES TO THE COMMUNITY AND PLANS TO EXPAND THOSE SERVICES IN THE NEXT FEW YEARS.
