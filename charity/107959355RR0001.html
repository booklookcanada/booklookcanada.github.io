What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

Scarborough Chinese Baptist Church is a Christian church of Baptist denomination located in the city of Toronto. The church is a member of the Canadian Baptists of Ontario and Quebec. The church missions and services include: - Sunday worship services conducted in Cantonese English and Mandarin. - Sunday School classes - Fellowship groups - Seminars and Christian teachings - Summer camps for children and youth. - Community services (e.g. ESL classes) and evangelistic activities.
