What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

TORONTO CHESED A HUMANITARIAN ORGANIZATION BASED IN THE GTA PROVIDES ASSISTANCE TO FAMILIES AND INDIVIDUALS IN NEED. WE WORK TOWARDS LONG-TERM SOLUTIONS WHICH INCLUDE FINANCIAL FAMILY AND CAREER PLANNING. TORONTO CHESED PLAYS AN INTEGRAL ROLE IN ADDRESSING NEEDS THAT MAY NOT FIT MORE IMMEDIATE AND REQUIRE SWIFT ACTION WITHOUT A LENGTHY APPLICATION PROCESS. OUR GOAL IS TO BOTH ELEVATE THE SHORT TERM ISSUES WHILE ALSO PROVIDING GUIDANCE AND SUPPORT TO MAINTAIN SUSTAINABLE SOLUTIONS.
