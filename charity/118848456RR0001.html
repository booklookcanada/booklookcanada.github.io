What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

LOCATED IN OTTAWA YOUVILLE CENTRE IS A NON-PROFIT REGISTERED CHARITY THAT SERVES ADOLESCENT MOTHERS AND THEIR CHILDREN. YOUVILLE'S TRAUMA-INFORMED HOLISTIC PROGRAMS AND SERVICES INCLUDE: CRISIS INTERVENTION INTENSIVE MENTAL HEALTH THERAPY AND TREATMENT ADDICTION COUNSELLING COLLABORATIVE PROBLEM SOLVING A LISCENSED CHILD DEVELOPMENT PROGRAM WITH A FOCUS ON INFANT MENTAL HEALTH ATTACHMENT-BASED PARENTING PROGRAMS WITH INTERVENTION AND TREATMENT AND A FULLY ACCREDITED SECONDARY SCHOOL. FOR MORE THAN A QUARTER CENTURY YOUVILLE CENTRE HAS BEEN AN INNOVATIVE CENTRE THAT INSPIRES EDUCATES AND NURTURES YOUNG MOTHERS AND THEIR CHILDREN TO UTILIZE THEIR STRENGTHS AND ACHIEVE THEIR GOALS.
