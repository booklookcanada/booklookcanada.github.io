What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Sep 30, 2024)

The Centre for Civic Engagement (CCE) funded the commissioning an extensive body of expert analysis on topics related to law global affairs economics trade technology the environment and culture. These documents were written by leading authorities in their respective fields including academics and scholars practitioners and area experts. The papers were published on website funded by the CCE and made available to the public free of charge. CCE also helped convene a series of public lectures on topics related to law global affairs economics trade technology the environment and culture. These events featured CCE invited experts lecturing about their areas of expertise interacting with participants and answering audience questions.
