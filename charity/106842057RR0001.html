What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Jul 31, 2024)

MUSICOUNTS IS A MUSIC EDUCATION CHARITY THAT SUPPORTS URBAN AND RURAL COMMUNITY ORGANIZATIONS AND SCHOOLS THROUGHOUT CANADA. THIS IS DONE BY WAY OF DONATIONS OF MUSICAL INSTRUMENTS AND CASH FOR MUSICAL INSTRUMENTS AND MATERIALS TO SCHOOLS AND COMMUNITY PROGRAMS AS WELL AS GRANTS TO MUSIC STUDENTS AND BEST MUSIC TEACHER AWARDS. MUSICOUNTS LEARN IS AN ONLINE PORTAL TO SUPPORT THE ADVANCEMENT OF MUSIC EDUCATION THROUGH INNOVATIVE LEARNING RESOURCES FOR TEACHERS AND STUDENTS
THE RBCXMUSIC FIRST UP PROGRAM POWERED BY MUSICOUNTS OFFERS A TRANSFORMATIONAL PROFESSIONAL DEVELOPMENT EXPERIENCE TO 30 EMERGING ARTISTS AND MUSIC PROFESSIONALS WHO REPRESENT THE NEXT GENERATION OF CANADA'S MUSIC INDUSTRY.
