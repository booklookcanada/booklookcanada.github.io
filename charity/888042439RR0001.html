VE'AHAVTA IS A SOCIAL SERVICE AGENCY OFFERING HIGH-IMPACT PROGRAMS FOR PEOPLE AT RISK OF HOMELESSNESS HELPING THEM MOVE FROM INSTABILITY TO SELF-SUFFICIENCY. OUR PROGRAMS FILL A SERVICE GAP BY EQUIPPING INDIVIDUALS NOT ONLY WITH THE SUPPLIES AND SERVICES THEY NEED BUT ALSO A SENSE OF COMMUNITY AND CARE THAT FOCUSES ON THE WHOLE PERSON AND THEIR UNIQUE NEEDS. CLIENTS DEVELOP THE SKILLS AND SELF-CONFIDENCE TO RE-ENTER THE WORKFORCE. WE WORK DIRECTLY BOTH WITH OTHER AGENCIES FROM WHICH WE ACCEPT REFERRALS AND REFER OUT TO INCLUDING THOSE THAT HANDLE HOUSING/SHELTER PLACEMENT HEALTHCARE LEGAL/FINANCIAL SERVICES JOB SKILLS TRAINING NEWCOMER SERVICES AND EMPLOYMENT.ONGOING PROGRAMS: - RELIEF AND REFERRAL: OUR MOBILE OUTREACH VANS PROVIDE HOT MEALS HARM REDUCTION KITS CLOTHING AND PROGRAM REFERRALS TO PEOPLE FACING HOMELESSNESS - WORK AND LIFE SKILLS TRAINING: THESE PROGRAMS COMBINE CLASSROOM LEARNING VOCATIONAL ASSESSMENTS AND CASE MANAGEMENT TO HELP PEOPLE ACHIEVE ACADEMIC AND EMPLOYMENT GOALS - PAID INTERNSHIPS: INDIVIDUALS LEARN JOB-SPECIFIC SKILLS IN EITHER THE WAREHOUSE OR KITCHEN INDUSTRIES WHILE EARNING MONEY IN PREPARATION FOR THE WORKFORCE - VOLUNTEER COMMUNITY MOBILIZATION: ALL VE'AHAVTA PROGRAMS ARE DESIGNED TO INCLUDE MEANINGFUL FACE-TO-FACE VOLUNTEER OPPORTUNITIES THAT SPARK TRANSFORMATIONAL CHANGE IN BOTH CLIENTS AND VOLUNTEERS
