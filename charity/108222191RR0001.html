What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Jun 30, 2024)

TEARFUND IS PASSIONATE TO SEE ALL PEOPLE FREED FROM POVERTY LIVING TRANSFORMED LIVES AND REACHING THEIR GOD-GIVEN POTENTIAL. FIRMLY AFFIRMING NATIONAL LEADERSHIP WE COLLABORATE WITH CHURCH-BASED PARTNERS ACROSS THE GLOBAL SOUTH TO FIX THE BROKEN RELATIONSHIPS AT THE ROOT OF POVERTY. 1. CHURCH AND COMMUNITY TRANSFORMATION: WE TRAIN AND EQUIP LOCAL CHURCHES TO LEAD LASTING CHANGE IN THEIR COMMUNITIES WITH LOCAL SOLUTIONS. 2. FOOD SECURITY AND LIVELIHOODS: WE HELP PEOPLE FEED THEIR FAMILIES IMPROVE DIETARY INTAKE AND GENERATE ADDITIONAL INCOME. 3. ECONOMIC EMPOWERMENT: POVERTY ALLEVIATION THROUGH SAVING GROUPS AND BUSINESS TRAINING WE HELP WOMEN AND THEIR FAMILIES BUILD EQUITY ACCESS CAPITAL AND START NEW BUSINESSES. 4. TRAUMA HEALING: RESTORING SURVIVORS OF SEXUAL VIOLENCE WHILE ENCOURAGING JUSTICE AND SOCIETAL CHANGE. 5. CLIMATE ADAPTATION: THROUGH LAND RESTORATION PROTECTION WATER MANAGEMENT TREE PLANTING AND CLIMATE-SMART AGRICULTURE WE HELP FAMILIES ADAPT TO A NEW REALITY. 6. EMERGENCY RELIEF: WE PROVIDE EMERGENCY FOOD AND SERVICES IN TIMES OF DISASTER. 7. WOMEN EMPOWERMENT: REDUCING GENDER DISPARITIES AND GENDER-BASED VIOLENCE AND ENSURING WOMEN'S EQUAL ACCESS TO ECONOMIC SOCIAL AND DECISION-MAKING PROCESSES. 8. RECONCILIATION: WE WORK TOWARDS RECONCILIATION WITH CANADA'S FIRST NATIONS BUILDING TRUST AND RESTORING RELATIONS. TEARFUND IS PART OF THE TEARFUND FAMILY ONE OF THE LARGEST NETWORKS OF CHURCH-BASED RELIEF AND DEVELOPMENT AGENCIES.
