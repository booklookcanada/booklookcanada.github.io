Who runs it — the people it pays

It reported 66 full-time positions and 55 part-time employees (year ended Mar 31, 2024). Total pay for all its positions: $4,338,520, as filed. The highest pay band it reported was “$80,000–$119,999”. 2 employees were reported in that band. Pay is reported in bands — ranges, not exact salaries. The top band is the highest range with anyone in it: it is not one person's salary, and it is not what everyone there is paid.

REXDALE WOMEN'S CENTRE DELIVERS PROGRAMS AND SERVICES WHICH INCREASE IMMIGRANT WOMENS KNOWLEDGE AND CONTACT FOR SERVICES RESCOURCES AND OTHER SERVICES.
