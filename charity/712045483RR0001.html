What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

THE PRIMARY OBJECTIVES OF THE RCO CHARITABLE DUND ARE TO: -MAKE GIFTS TO LOCAL COMMUNITY ORGANIZATIONS THAT MEET THE CRITERIA OF QUALIFIED DONEES UNDER THE INCOME TAX ACT FOR THE BETTERMENT OF THE COMMUNITY AND THE SUPPORT OF THE AGED YOUTH AND PERSONS WITH DEVELOPMENT OR PHYSICAL DISABILITIES; -ADVANCE EDUCATION BY GRANTING SCHOLARSHIPS AND BURSARIES TO STUDENTS IN NEED TO ALLOW THEM TO PURSUE POST-SECONDARY EDUCATION; AND -SUPPORT VARIOUS CHARITABLE PROJECTS GLOBALLY AIMED AT PROVIDING WATER SANITATION AND HUMANITARIAN AID IMPROVING HEALTH EDUCATION AND FOOD SECURITY AND ENHANCING ECONOMIC DEVELOPMENT THROUGH GIFTS MADE TO THE ROTARY FOUNDATION AND OTHER NON-GOVERNMENTAL ENTITIES
