What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Sep 30, 2024)

We offer day and overnight children's summer camps for recreation religious and environmental programs; integrating children with special needs into each week of camp. - We offer two weeks of overnight camp for Supported Young Adults - We provide summer camps for low-income single moms and their children who would otherwise not be able to afford summer vacation - We sponsor and host various retreats for personal leadership personal growth and renewal - We provide outdoor education and leadership opportunities for a variety of elementary and secondary schools as well as homeschooling groups. - We offer a program once a week for young adults with special needs geared to Community life and skills. providing a place were these young adults can be part of a safe community where friendships will flourish skills will be developed and a lot of fun will be had
We have expanded our programs for Young Adults with special needs. Offering 1 day per week as a LINC (Living IN Community) connect group and 1 day a week as a LINC skills group. We also offer a 'respite' program twice a year for participants to stay overnight for 3 days during the week.
