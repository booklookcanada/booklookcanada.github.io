What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

Mass Culture will oversee the development training and dissemination of information data and research about the Canadian arts and culture sector. Activities for Mass Culture are the following: 1. Developing and stewarding a network of stakeholders to exchange and create research; 2. Helping set conditions for cultural research discussions and collaborative work to take place; 3. Organizing events and communications to share and make cultural research accessible; 4. Creating and maintaining a publicly accessible database of cultural research; 5. Conducting workshops and training in research analysis; and 6. Publishing newsletters and other communications in electronic or other form.
