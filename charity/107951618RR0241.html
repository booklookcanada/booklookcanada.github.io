What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

WE ASSIST CLIENTS WITH PAYING THEIR RENT THROUGH THE HOUSING FIRST PROGRAM. WE RUN A FOOD BANK CLOTHING & FURNITURE ASSISTANCE FOR LOW INCOME CLIENTS. WE PREACH PROMOTE AND ADVANCE THE SPIRITUAL TEACHINGS OF THE SALVATION ARMY BY HOLDING WEEKLY WORSHIP SERVICES. WE ALSO PROVIDE MINISTRIES FOR MEN WOMEN AND CHILDREN ON A WEEKLY & MONTHLY BASIS. WE PROVIDE PASTORAL CARE. WE SEND CHILDREN & ADULTS TO CAMPS FOR RECREATIONAL & RELIGIOUS PURPOSES. WE DONATE FUNDS TO OUR TERRITORIAL HEADQUARTERS FOR OVERSEAS MISSIONARY ACTIVITIES. WE PROVIDE FOOD & PASTORAL CARE TO CLIENTS ON THE STREET FROM OUR MOBILE KITCHEN TRUCK. WE RUN A WALKING PROGRAM TO PROMOTE THE HEALTH & SPIRITUAL WELL-BEING OF WOMEN & MEN.
