What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

The church continues to support qualified charities as well as the United Church of Canada Mission. Seniors Euchre is provided on a biweekly basis the prayer shawl knitting group meets weekly except in the summer and the minister provides the 5 local retirement/long term care facilities with regular monthly in person services. A music program is provided as well. Bible study is offered during Lent. A 6 week children's camp Growing Youth is ran during July and August. The UCW meet on a monthly basis and provides luncheons dinners and yard sales to raise funds for their ministry as well as support the General Fund. We continue to meet the needs of those who approach us with gift cards for local food stores through our benevolent fund.
