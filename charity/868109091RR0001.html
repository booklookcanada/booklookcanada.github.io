What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

COME OVER AND HELP AID ORGANIZATION IS A CHRISTIAN ORGANIZATION THAT PARTNERS WITH THE REFORMED CHURCH COMMUNITY IN CENTRAL & EASTERN EUROPE. OUR GOAL IS TO GLORIFY GOD THROUGH THE PROCLAMATION OF THE GOSPEL OF JESUS CHRIST AND THROUGH THE MERCY MINISTRY OF THE CHURCH. OUR VISION IS TO SEE THRIVING BIBLICAL FAITHFUL CHURCHES SERVING IN THEIR LOCAL CONTEXT. OUR PROJECTS INCLUDE CHURCH PLANTING THEOLOGICAL INSTRUCTION LITERATURE MINISTRY ELDERLY CARE CHILDREN MINISTRY ADDICTIONS REHABILITATION MINISTRY AND HUMANITARIAN AID.
