MARKHAM CHINESE BAPTIST CHURCH (THE 'CHURCH') IS AN AUTONOMOUS AND INDEPENDENT LOCAL ORGANIZATION OF BELIEVERS IN THE CHRISTIAN FAITH WHOSE PURPOSE ARE TO PREACH THE GOSPEL TO BUILD UP FELLOW BELIEVERS AND TO SPREAD THE LOVE OF GOD TO OUR COMMUNITY OUR COUNTRY AND THE WORLD. THE CHURCH IS A REGISTERED CHARITY UNDER THE INCOME TAX ACT (CANADA).
