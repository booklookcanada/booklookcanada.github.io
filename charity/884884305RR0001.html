What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

Stratford and Perth County Community Foundation (Community Foundation) facilitates philanthropy and provides gifts and resources to community service organizations delivering vital programs and services to the residents in Stratford St. Marys and Perth County. Applications for funding are accepted and evaluated to Board-approved criteria with gifts made to charitable organizations and qualified donees. The Community Foundation funds a broad range of vital organizations without discrimination to deliver programs and services to enhance social services; support health and wellness programs; expand education and literacy; support children and youth; protect the environment; advance arts culture and heritage; care for seniors; and provide recreation and sports opportunities. The Community Foundation is an active local organization with staff serving on community-based committees and working in collaboration with other local funders to provide networking and development opportunities for the local not-for-profit sector.
