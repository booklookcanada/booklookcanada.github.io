What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

THE CHARITY MAINTAINS A DATABASE OF MEDICAL SCHOOLS AND RELATED RESEARCH FACILITIES IN THE FIELDS OF CARDIOLOGY ONCOLOGY AND PSYCHIATRY AND PRIMARILY IN CONNECTION WITH THE FUNDING OF STUDENT RESEARCH ASSESSES THE BASES UNDER WHICH THESE ORGANIZATIONS ENDEAVOUR TO PROMOTE EXCELLENCE IN THESE AREAS. THE MACH-GAENSSLEN FOUNDATION OF CANADA IS COMMITTED TO THE CARRYING-OUT THE SUPPORTING AND THE PROMOTION OF MEDICAL RESEARCH AND PROJECTS IN THESE ABOVE SUBJECT FIELDS.
