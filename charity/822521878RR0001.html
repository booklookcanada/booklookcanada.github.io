The highest pay band it reported was “$120,000–$159,999”. 2 employees were reported in that band. Pay is reported in bands — ranges, not exact salaries. What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

THE GREENBELT FOUNDATION IS INTERESTED IN CONDUCTING ACTIVITIES THAT PROMOTE AND SUSTAIN THE GREENBELT AS A BENEFICIAL VALUABLE AND PERMANENT FEATURE ENHANCING THE LIFE OF ONTARIANS. WE WILL SUPPORT A VARIETY OF ACTIVITIES IN THE FOLLOWING THREE PROGRAM AREAS: 1. SUPPORTING A VIABLE AGRICULTURAL AND VITICULTURAL SECTOR; 2. PROMOTING VIBRANT RURAL COMMUNITIES; AND 3. PROTECTING RESTORING AND ENHANCING THE NATURAL ENVIRONMENT.
