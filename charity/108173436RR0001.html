What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Apr 30, 2024)

1) PROVIDE EDUCATION TO STUDENTS PURSUING VARIOUS DISCIPLINES IN THE FACULTY OF ARTS AND SCIENCE AT THE UNIVERSITY OF TORONTO 2) PROVIDE RELIGIOUS EDUCATION TO STUDENTS PURSUING PROFESSIONAL AND ACADEMIC DEGREES IN THEOLOGY 3) PROVIDE FINANCIAL AID TO STUDENTS THROUGH SCHOLARSHIPS BURSARIES AWARDS PRIZES AND EMPLOYMENT TO ASSIST THEM IN COMPLETING THEIR STUDIES 4) PROVIDE HOUSING AND FOOD SERVICES TO STUDETNS ON CAMPUS 5) ASSIST TO PROMOTE AND ADMINISTER RESEARCH TO SOCIAL SCIENCES AND HUMANITIES. 6) VIC READY PROGRAM TO ASSIST STUDENTS WITH FUTURE PLANNING.
