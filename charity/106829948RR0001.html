What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

CALEDON MEALS ON WHEELS PROVIDES PROGRAMS AND SERVICES THAT PROMOTE THE HEALTH WELL-BEING AND INDEPENDENCE OF SENIORS AND PEOPLE WITH DISABILITIES INCLUDING: - NUTRITIOUS MEALS DELIVERED TO THEIR HOME WITH SECURITY CHECKS - FRIENDLY VISITS - SOCIAL RECREATIONAL AND HEALTH PROMOTION GROUPS - EQUIPMENT THAT SUPPORTS SEARCH AND RESCUE EFFORTS FOR THOSE AT RISK OF WANDERING.
