What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

JOHN HOWARD SOCIETY OF SIMCOE MUSKOKA PROVIDES SUPPORT TO BOTH YOUTH AND ADULT OFFENDERS THROUGH A NUMBER OF PROGRAMS. ALL PROGRAMS ARE FUNDED PRIMARILY THOUGHT ONTARIO MINISTRY FUNDING. THE MAIN PROGRAM IN THIS ORGANIZATION WHICH ACCOUNTS FOR 45% OF FUNDING IS THE NORTH SIMCOE ATTENDANCE CENTRE WHICH PROVIDES A PLACE FOR YOUTH TO GO FOR COUNSELLING VISITS AND IS ALSO A RESOURCE CENTRE FOR THESE CLIENTS. OTHER PROGRAMS INCLUDE THE ADULT AND YOUTH CSO PROGRAMS WHERE WE WORK WITH CLIENTS ON PROBATION AND HELP ENSURE COURT ORDERS FIR COMMUNITY SERVICES ARE SUPERVISED. THE ORGANIZATION ALSO PROVIDES SUPPORT TO INDIVIDUALS INCARCERATED IN FEDERAL AND PROVINCIAL INSTITUTIONS AS WELL AS INDIVIDUALS TRANSITIONING TO THE COMMUNITY FROM CORRECTIONS. ANGER MANAGEMENT PROGRAMMING IS ANOTHER PROGRAM BEING OFFERED TO THE COMMUNITY
