What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

Peter Pan Co-op Preschool provides a warm caring environment in which children can learn. Our programs introduce children to the world in which they live by encouraging a natural sense of curiosity and knowledge through play stories songs field trips and hands-on activities. It is a quality program which promotes physical emotional intellectual and social growth. It is a child-oriented approach of learning facilitated by certified Early Childhood Educators. The parent Board of Directors are responsible for all administrative functions of the program.
