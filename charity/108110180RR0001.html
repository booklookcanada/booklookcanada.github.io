What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Jun 30, 2024)

Timothy Eaton Memorial Church (the 'Church') provides worship services every Sunday and on select days of the Christian calendar. The church ministers to children youth and adults and provides pastoral care on-site and in the community. The Church supports the community through various programs and donations to other charitable organizations.
