What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

CANADA HOUSE - A COMMUNITY CENTER BUILT TO SERVICE JERUSALEM RESIDENTS OF ALL AGES. A SPACE WHERE JERUSALEM RESIDENTS CAN INTERACT WITH AND LEARN FROM EACH OTHER AS THEY BUILD THEIR LIVES IN JERUSALEM. CANADA PATHWAY AND CULTURE COMMUNITY FUND - ESTABLISHING AS A RESPONSE TO THE COVID-19 CRISIS WHICH SUPPORTS TWO CRITICAL AREAS IN JERUSALEM: ONGOING NEEDS FOR VULNERABLE POPULATIONS OF ALL AGES AND THE RECOVERY OF THE CULTURAL SECTOR WHICH HAS BEEN DECIMATED BY COVID. TOLERANCE COALITION AND SHARED LIVING HUB - A COALITION OF DOZENS OF ORGRANIZATIONS INSTITUTIONS AGENCIES AND INITIATIVES THAT WORK TO ADVANCE SHARED LIVING IN THE CITY. THEY FOCUS ON PEOPLE OF DIFFERENT FAITHS AND ORIENTATIONS AND ARE SEEKING TO MAKE JERUSALEM A CITY WHERE ALL GROUPS ARE INCLUDED AND LIVE TOGETHER HARMONIOUSLY.
EMERGENCY FUND IN RESPONSE TO EVENTS OF OCTOBER 7TH - JFC SUPPORTED PROGRAMS FOR VULNERABLE POPULATIONS DIRECTLY IMPACTED BY THE WAR AND EVACUEES HOUSED IN JERUSALEM PARTICULARLY SUPPORT FOR CHILDREN AND YOUTH AND AT-RISK POPULATIONS IN THE CITY. SHAI DORON LEADERSHIP FELLOWS PROGRAM SUPPORTING CIVIL SOCIETY LEADERS WORKING WITH ALL COMMUNITIES IN JERUSALEM
