Who runs it — the people it pays

It reported 26 full-time positions and 24 part-time employees (year ended Mar 31, 2024). Total pay for all its positions: $1,541,070, as filed. The highest pay band it reported was “$80,000–$119,999”. 3 employees were reported in that band. Pay is reported in bands — ranges, not exact salaries. The top band is the highest range with anyone in it: it is not one person's salary, and it is not what everyone there is paid.

What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

PROVIDE TRANSITIONAL AND PERMANENT HOUSING TO PERSON(S) AND FAMILIES WHO ARE HOMELESS. PROVIDING HOUSING FOR WOMEN AND CHILDREN WHO HAVE FLED DOMESTIC VIOLENCE FOR A BETTER LIFE. OFFERING SUPPORT PROGRAMS TO THESE PERSON(S) TO ASSIST WITH THEIR TRANSITION AND SECURE A BETTER FUTURE. FURNITURE BANK PROGRAMS PROVIDES AN EFFECTIVE SOLUTION FOR WASTE DEPOSAL WHILE OFFERING RECYCLED FURNITE TO LOW INCOME FAMILIES AND THOSE IN NEED OF FURNITURE TO HOUSE THEIR NEW BEGINNINGS.
