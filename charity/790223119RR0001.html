What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

Facilitated access to medical diagnostic testing for underserved communities enabling early detection and treatment of health conditions and improving long-term outcomes. - Distributed vital medications and critical medical equipment to individuals and families in need significantly enhancing their quality of life and access to care. - Provided fee subsidies to support the education of nursing students to address a critical gap in the healthcare system and contribute to long-term capacity building. - Targeted outreach and partnerships to raise awareness about urgent health issues and mobilized resources to fund critical medical treatments for vulnerable populations.
