What it says it does

VOICE FOUND IS A SURVIVOR LED CHARITABLE ORGANIZATION WITH A MISSION TO PREVENT SEXUAL ABUSE AND EXPLOITATION AND TO SUPPORT THE HEALING AND RECOVERY OF INDIVIDUALS AND COMMUNITIES FROM THE HEALTH FINANCIAL AND EMOTIONAL IMPACTS OF SEXUAL EXPLOITATION. WE PROVIDE SUPPORT AND SERVICES THAT HELP SURVIVORS TO HEAL AND REBUILD THEIR LIVES THROUGH THE FOLLOWING PROGRAMS; - HOPE FOUND PROJECT PROVIDES CRISIS AND FIRST STAGE CARE FOR PERSONS EXITING HUMAN TRAFFICKING/SEX WORK. - THE CLINIC IS A PRIMARY AND PREVANTATIVE HEALTH CLINIC FOR THOSE AT RISK OF BEING OR WHO HAVE BEEN TRAFFICKED - PATH FOUND PROVIDES LONGER TERM SUPPORT AND CARE TO HELP SURVIVORS AS THEY BUILD NEW LIVES - STRENGTH FOUND IS A PROGRAM TO SUPPORT ADULT SURVIVORS OF CHILDHOOD SEXUAL ABUSE WE ALSO DEVELOP AND DELIVER EDUCATION TO SHARE BEST PRACTICES SUPPORT OTHER SERVICE PROVIDERS AND AID IN THE PREVENTION AND TREATMENT OF SEXUAL EXPLOITATION. ALL PROGRAMS ARE INFORMED AND DEVELOPED WITH A TEAM THAT INCLUDES PERSONS WITH LIVED EXPERIENCE.
