What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

Organize and fund public forums and seminars on educational topics including the Ukrainian famine/Holodomor. Produce educational displays at public events. Participate in and organize events that facilitate the development of amateur arts groups. Financially support a Remembrance Day commemoration honoring fallen Canadian soldiers. Humanitarian aid and support for Ukrainian cultural and educational programming.

What this page can't tell you

  • Whether its work works. A return shows money, not results.
  • Whether anyone audited these figures. The CRA publishes returns as filed, without checking them. Some charities also post audited statements — BookLook compared the largest 200 charities' published statements with their returns (see “How to read this site”).
  • Anything after Mar 31, 2024, its newest return in the published data.
  • Who its directors are. The published data does not include them.
  • Questions worth asking it directly: Do you publish audited financial statements? Who sits on your board this year? What did last year's programs achieve, and how do you know?
