What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

PREACH TEACHINGS OF MUSLIM FAITH - PREACH AND ADVANCE THE TEACHINGS OF THE MUSLIM FAITH AND RELIGIOUS TENETS DOCTRINES OBSERVANCES AND CULTURE ASSOCIATED WITH THE FAITH. WE HAVE ESTABLISHED COURSES FOR MUSLIMS TO LEARN ABOUT THEIR FAITH. THIS INCLUDES OUR SUNRISE (FAJR) PROGRAM AS WELL AS THE SUNSET PROGRAM (MAGHRIB) AND WEEKLY CLASSES ON READING THE QUR'AN FOR WOMEN. HOUSE OF WORSHIP WITH SERVICES OF MUSLIM FAITH - WE HAVE ESTABLISHED 5 DAILY PRAYERS AT THE CENTRE. WE HAVE ALSO ARRANGED FOR IMAMS (RELIGIOUS SCHOLARS) TO LEAD PRAYERS. PROPAGATE MUSLIM FAITH - WE HELD NIGHTLY PROGRAMMING AND EVENING IFTARS DURING RAMADAN IN 2019 AND 2021. WE HOLD MULTIPLE EID PRAYERS WITH HUNDREDS IN ATTENDANCE SO THAT INDIVIDUALS CAN FULFILL THEIR FAITH REQUIREMENTS. WE REGULARLY SEND WORDS OF ENCOURAGEMENT TO THE COMMUNITY FOR THEM TO INCREASE THEIR VIRTUOUS DEEDS. WE HAVE ALSO ESTABLISHED AN OPEN DOOR POLICY IN RECRUITING OF VOLUNTEERS SO THAT MEMBERS FEEL LIKE THEY ARE PART OF A COMMUNITY THAT WILL NATURALLY INCREASE THEIR FAITH. RELIGIOUS SCHOOL OF INSTRUCTION - WE HOLD CLASSES FOR STUDENTS TO MEMORIZE THE QUR'AN CALLED OUR HIFZ PROGRAM WITH CONSISTENT STUDENTS ENROLLED IN THIS PART TIME PROGRAM WITH AN ESTABLISHED TEACHER. REGULAR QUR'AN CLASSES ARE HELD FOR WOMEN.
