What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

We hold twice weekly daily Mass Celebrate Mass Saturday and Sunday. We celebrate the sacraments of Baptism Communion Reconciliation weekly. We celebrate Ministry of the Sick and Matrimony when required. First Communion and Confirmation were celebrated with school children. We celebrate Funerals and provide care for the grieving. We prepare for the various sacrament: first Communion and confirmation. We hold retreats for the First Communion and Confirmation students to prepare. We provide Children's Liturgy each week at Sunday Mass. We visit the sick in homes and hospitals. We visit the nursing homes in our area when required for the sacrament. We hold Mass 4 times a year at Macassa Lodge for the residents. We collect food for the food bank. We donate to evangelizing caring and justice organizations throughout the Diocese and beyond. We offer a Youth Ministry which meets bi-weekly. Telephone conversations are still in affect to discuss funerals baptism wedding preparations . Social events for the seniors and volunteers.
