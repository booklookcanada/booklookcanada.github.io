What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

Religious organization that holds weekly services in person and via ZOOM. We provide pastoral care donate for overseas and national missionary activities and support local charitable causes. We work with First Nations organizations and Aboriginal Women's groups towards reconciliation goals. We work with other church groups to provide Messy Church activities for youth in our community. We provide a sitting area and outside meeting area for community members in our Peace Park area on our property.
