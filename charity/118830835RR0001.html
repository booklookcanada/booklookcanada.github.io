What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

Environmental Defence's activity in 2023-2024 was focused mainly on our work in clean economy promoting livable communities protecting Canadians from toxics protecting clean water and addressing plastic pollution. Our clean economy work highlighted the environmental social and economic risks of fossil fuels development and highlighted the benefits of taking action on climate change. We also focused on the government’s commitment to phase out fossil fuel subsidies. And we engaged with the federal government’s commitments to put a cap on oil and gas emissions and implement clean electricity regulations. We engaged with the government of Ontario on their plan to address the environment and encouraged Ontarians to state their opinion with elected representatives. We worked to raise awareness of the potential impacts of a new 400-series highway in the Greater Toronto Area. We also turned our attention to the growing threat of plastic pollution in the environment including plastic beverage containers in Ontario and worked to see Canada commit to addressing plastics at the national and international level.
