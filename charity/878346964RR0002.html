What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

TO IDENTIFY A DIVERSE RANGE OF INITIATIVES AND PROGRAMS FOR DISADVANTAGED VISIBLE MINORITY AND MARGINALIZED WOMEN AND THEIR FAMILIES IN THE AREAS OF HEALTH ENVIRONMENT SOCIAL BUSINESS AND COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT. TO FOCUS ON THE INITIAL ACTIVES AND PROGRAMS THAT PROVIDE OPPORTUNITIES TO IMPROVE THE SELF-ESTEEM INDEPENDENCE AND WELFARE OF THESE WOMEN THROUGH SELF-HELP AND COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT PROGRAMS. DEVELOP AND PROVIDE TRAINING EDUCATION SUPPORTIVE COUNSELING AND SERVICES FOR DISADVANTAGED VISIBLE MINORITY AND MARGINALIZED WOMEN TO IMPROVE THEIR SKILLS AND TO ASSIST THEM IN THEIR INTEGRATION WITHIN THEIR COMMUNITIES.
THE GOALS IS TO IMPROVE INTER-CULTURAL UNDERSTANDING AND DEVELOP STRATEGIES FOR MORE EFFECTIVE RESPONSE TI ISSUES SUCH AS SOCIAL ISOLATION UNREPORTED HATE CRIME AND RACE-BASED SCHOOL BULLING. IN YEAR OF 2024-2025 OUR ORGANIZATION CONTINUING WORKING MAY DIFFERENT COMMUNITY SUPPORT PROGRAMS WITH OUR MEMBERS GIVE SOME HOURS TO THE COMMUNITY SUPPORT WITH OUR SISTERS ORGANIZATIONS.
