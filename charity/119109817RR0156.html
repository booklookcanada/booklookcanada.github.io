What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

HOLY FAMILY RETREAT HOUSE IS A PLACE WHERE PEOPLE COME TO PRAY TO BE ALONE WITH GOD TO BE WITH OTHER PEOPLE AND TO SEEK SPIRITUAL DIRECTION. THE HOLY FAMILY RETREAT HOUSE PROVIDES AND ATMOSPHERE OF QUIET AND PRAYER. WE WELCOME GROUPS FROM ALL RELIGIOUS DENOMINATIONS AND WALK OF LIFE. HOLY FAMILY RETREAT HOUSE SPONSOR WEEKEND RETREATS FOR MARRIED COUPLES MEN WOMEN MEN AND WOMEN RECOVERY INNER HEALING GROUPS AND CHARISMATIC PRAYER GROUPS ALL USE OUR FACILITIES. PRIVATE RETREAT BOTH GUIDED/DIRECTOR OR UNDIRECTED ARE ALSO AVAILABLE. WE PROVIDE SPACE FOR ELEMENTARY AND HIGH SCHOOL STUDENTS TO PARTICIPATE IN A RETREAT DAY OR WEEKEND. PARISH TEAMS RCIA GROUPS KNIGHTS OF COLUMBUS CATHOLIC WOMEN’S LEAGUES SCHOOL BOARDS SCHOOL BOARDS TEACHERS PRINCIPALS SEMINARIANS AND OTHER GROUPS ALL USE OUR FACILITIES. THE HOLY FAMILY RETREATS HOUSE IS IN USE ON A DAILY BASIS.
