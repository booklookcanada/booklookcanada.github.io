What it says it does

JUBILEE FELLOWSHIP CRC IS A CONGREGATION OF THE CHRISTIAN REFORMED CHURCH. OUR VISION AND MISSION IS 'IN CHRIST WITH EACH OTHER TO THE WORLD'. JUBILEE HOSTS WEEKLY SUNDAY WORSHIP SERVICES AND IT PROVIDES A NUMBER OF DISCIPLESHIP AND MINISTRY PROGRAMS TO CHILDREN YOUTH YOUNG ADULTS ADULTS AND SENIORS. JUBILEE OFFERS A SUMMER CAMP PROGRAM FOR CHILDREN IN THE COMMUNITY. JUBILEE IS ACTIVE IN REFUGEE SETTLEMENT.
