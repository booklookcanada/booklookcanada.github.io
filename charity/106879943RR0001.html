What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

THE CATHOLIC CHARITIES OF THE ARCHDIOCESE OF TORONTO PROVIDES HEALTH AND SOCIAL SERVICES IN THE ARCHDIOCESE THROUGH ITS MEMBER AGENCIES AND AFFILIATED ORGANIZATIONS. SERVICES ARE DELIVERED IN A COMMITTED EMPOWERING WAY REFLECTING THE VALUES AND SOCIAL MISSION OF THE CHURCH. THESE AGENCIES ARE FUNDED AT VARYING LEVELS BY THE CHARITY AND THE BALANCE OF THE FUNDING COMES FROM PUBLIC AND PRIVATE SOURCES. CATHOLIC CHARITIES AND ITS MEMBER AGENCIES PROVIDE MANY OPPORTUNITIES TO VOLUNTEER IN AREAS SUCH AS: WORK WITH ELDERLY CHILDREN FAMILIES THOSE WITH SPECIAL NEEDS YOUNG MOTHERS IMMIGRANT AND REFUGEES ETC. AS WELL AS ON THE BOARD OF THESE AGENCIES.
