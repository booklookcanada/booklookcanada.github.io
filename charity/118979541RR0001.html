What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

KACL provides support & assistance to children and adults with developmental disabilities and to adults with mental health needs through programs designed to help these individuals achieve their full potential and enjoy a meaningful and satisfying life. Programs include: Infant Development; Children's Behavior Intervention autism and respite supports; Adult residential and vocational supports including individuals with complex support needs; Adult mental health supports; Full day and after-school child care services including special needs resourcing.
