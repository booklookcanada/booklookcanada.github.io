What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

WE PROVIDE SHELTER & SERVICES FOR HOMELESS MEN WE HOLD WEEKLY WORSHIP SERVICES. WE PROVIDE PASTORAL CARE WE PROVIDE CLOTHING AND PERSONAL SUPPLIES TO OUR HOMELESS POPULATION. WE PROVIDE A MEALS PROGRAM FOR OUR CLIENTS THE HOMELESS AND OUR COMMUNITY. WE PROVIDE FINANCIAL COUNSELING AND FINANCIAL MANAGEMENT. WE HOST AN INCOME TAX CLINIC. WE ASSIST WITH THE RESUME WRITING AND HOUSING & JOB SEARCHES. WE PROVIDE A SERVICE TO OUR COMMUNITY WE TAKE OUR EMERGENCY VEHICLE OUT IN DOWNTOWN HAMILTON 363 NIGHTS YEARLY AND GIVE OUT HOT SOUP & SANDWICHES TO THE HOMELESS. WE GIVE OUT AS AVAILABLE HATS GLOVES SLEEPING BAGS BLANKETS ETC. WE ALSO IN EMERGENCY OR DISASTER SITUATIONS USE OUR EMERGENCY VEHICLE TO SUPPORT FIREFIGHTERS POLICE & OTHER EMERGENCY SERVICE WORKERS TO SUPPLY THEM WITH COFFEE SANDWICHES AND A HELPING HAND THROUGH VERY DIFFICULT SITUATIONS.
