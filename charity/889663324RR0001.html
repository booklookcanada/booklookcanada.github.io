What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

Regeneration Outreach Community continues to support those experiencing homelessness those who are precariously housed and those living with food insecurity. Through our Meal Program we provide breakfast 365 days a year and lunch Monday to Friday. During our Meal Programs our guests have access to system navigation ID Clinic spiritual care and essential care services. The Regeneration Marketplace provides access to free groceries 5 days per week using a choice method. Our food procurement has continued to grow in capacity allowing us to meet the growing needs of our community with a 58% growth in visits to our Marketplace. Our food procurement also allows us to share with 40 other community food-related organizations.
