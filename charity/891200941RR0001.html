What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

WORD & DEED MINISTRIES CANADA (W&D) IS A CHRISTIAN ORGANIZATION DEDICATED TO HELPING ADDRESS THE SPIRITUAL AND PHYSICAL NEEDS OF PEOPLE IN THE DEVELOPING WORLD IN ACCORDANCE WITH BIBLICAL PRINCIPLES. W&D PARTNERS WITH CHRISTIAN ORGANIZATIONS IN THE DEVELOPING WORLD ON A WIDE VARIETY OF PROJECTS INCLUDING CHILD SPONSORSHIP CHRISTIAN EDUCATION ORPHAN CARE DISASTER RELIEF AGRICULTURE TRAINING MEDICAL CARE AND VOCATIONAL TRAINING WHILE PROVIDING ACCOUNTABILITY AND ENCOURAGEMENT FOR OUR PARTNERS AS WELL AS TIMELY UPDATES TO ALL SUPPORTERS. W&D EMPHASIZES THREE THINGS IN EACH OF OUR ONGOING PROJECTS: 1.) A GOSPEL FOCUS 2.) TARGETING SELF-SUFFICIENCY UNDER CHRIST 3.) A RELIANCE ON INDIGENOUS PARTNER ORGANIZATIONS.
