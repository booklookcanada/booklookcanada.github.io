What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

ADOPT-A-DOG/SAVE-A-LIFE INC. IS A REGISTERED CHARITABLE ORGANIZATION DEDICATED TO SAVING ABANDONED DOGS AND PLACING THEM IN LOVING AND CARING HOMES. WE ARE BASED IN TORONTO ONTARIO. WE ARE COMMITTED TO SAVING DOGS OF ALL AGES AND BREEDS. WE GIVE PRIORITY TO DOGS IN ANIMAL SHELTERS AND PUPPY MILLS. WHEN WE ARE ABLE WE ASSIST DOGS FROM PRIVATE OWNERS WHO ARE NO LONGER ABLE TO CARE FOR THEIR DOG. OUR GOAL IS TO WORK WITH OTHER ANIMAL RESCUES AND SHELTERS TO ENSURE THAT NO DOG IS LEFT AT RISK.
