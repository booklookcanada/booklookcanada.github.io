What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

WOMEN'S COLLEGE HEALTH RESEARCH WAS ESTABLISHED TO ASSIST WOMEN'S COLLEGE HOSPITAL AND OTHERS IN UNDERTAKING AND CARRYING OUT ANY AND ALL PHASES OF HEALTH RESEARCH PROJECTS; TO INITIATE AND PROVIDE THE FACILITY FOR SPECIALIZED SERVICES RELATED TO WOMEN'S HEALTH ISSUES NOT OTHERWISE READILY AVAILABLE TO THE HOSPITAL; TO PARTICIPATE DIRECTLY IN RESEARCH ACTIVITIES; TO PROVIDE FACILITIES AND SUPPORT FOR PATIENT CARE PROGRAMS OR SERVICES CARRIED OUT BY THE HOSPITAL; TO EDUCATE IN THE HEALTH SCIENCES FIELD PARTICULARLY RELATED TO ISSUES OF GENERAL PUBLIC INTERST CONCERNING WOMEN'S HEALTH AND WOMEN'S HEALTH ISSUES; AND FOR SUCH OTHER PURPOSES DEEMED TO BE IN THE INTEREST OF THE HOSPITAL AND RELATED TO THE ABOVE LISTED OBJECTIVES. WCHR OWNS REVENUE PRODUCING REAL ESTATE AND AN INVESTMENT PORTFOLIO AND FUNDS RESEARCH GRANTS.
