What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

We hold weekly worship services (On-line and in person)to provide ministry to men women and children. We provide ongoing pastoral care to those in need. We run a free summer religious program for children. We donate funds to the Presbyterian Church in Canada for mission and humanitarian work. We offer outreach programs. We support other registered charities including financial support food clothing and devotional programs. We provide weekly prayer meetings and group bible study programs. We make our premises available to two community outreach programs. We provide a weekly drop in centre for seniors.
