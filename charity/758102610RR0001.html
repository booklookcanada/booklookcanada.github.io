What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

Established with the vision of 'advancing excellence in education'. Its mission is to 'seek recognize promote and celebrate exceptional scholars and distinguished programs at leading institutions in study skill development learning and research'. This is achieved through the provision of scholarships bursaries awards grants and prizes to encourage achievements in post-secondary education.
