What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

Holds weekly Worship Services (Combined online & In-Person throughout 2024); Ministers offer counselling to families & individuals; Ministers visit the sick shut-ins & elderly; Give pastoral care; Donate funds to the Mission & Service Fund of the United Church of Canada; Provide meeting space for numerous community groups as allowed (Guides Scouts); Provide Pastoral Care support in several long-term care homes hospitals & hospice; Ministers officiate at weddings baptisms & funerals; Provide religious training & instruction; Provide Sunday school programs. Community Theatre Camp; provided Community Theatre Club & Community Children's Choir in-person; Offer Therapeutic Touch Treatments to struggling sick & dying patients free of charge; Support a Prayer Shawl Ministry for all requests; continue to partner with the Anishnabeg Outreach (AO) providing meeting space & storage space for several of the AO programs in Guelph; Support various socially responsible groups such as Guelph Neighbourhood Support Coalition North End Harvest Market; Sponsor a child with Plan Canada; Member of Guelph United Ministries; Support the University of Guelph Ecumenical Campus Ministry
