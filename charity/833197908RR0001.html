What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

The Charitable purpose of the Foundation is to receive or maintain a fund or funds and to apply all or part of the principal and income therefrom from time to time to Roman Catholic charities registered under the Income Tax Act (Canada). The Foundation will not be involved in any direct charitable activities beyond the distribution of funds to Roman Catholic registered charities.
