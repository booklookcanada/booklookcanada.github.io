What it says it does

Supporting qualified donees in Canada with emphasis on local hospitals cancer care religious institutions and organizations supporting sustainable development. To relieve poverty by providing clothing food housing and infrastructure to poor people within India. Advancement of education by providing facilities books transportation and educational aids to students in India. Creation of a scholarship program to fund tuition for the less fortunate in India.
