What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Jun 30, 2024)

STEM Fellowship is a Canadian charitable organization that empowers students and educators to become active and motivated partners in education. The goal is to foster digital citizens of the future. The organization connects students and foster future thought leaders across Canada through ambassadors university branches high school chapters and partnerships. The organization provides high school and undergraduate students with their first opportunity of scholarly publication while enabling them to develop scholarly writing skills through workshops and our Scholarly Writing Challenge. The organization fosters inclusion and diversity in STEM fields by creating mentorship and research opportunities for students from communities traditionally underrepresented in STEM. The organization delivers unique experiential learning programs in scholarly communication and data science for high school and undergraduate students.
