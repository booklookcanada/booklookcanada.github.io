What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

THE NOURISH AND DEVELOP FOUNDATION RELIEVES THE CONDITIONS ASSOCIATED WITH POVERTY FOR THE BENEFIT OF PERSONS WHO ARE POOR PERSONS OF LOW INCOME AND PERSONS IN NEED THROUGH OPERATING PROGRAMS THAT INCLUDE: A) PROVIDING FOOD SHELTER AND OTHER BASIC SUPPLIES TO SUCH PERSONS; B) PROVIDING TRAINING AND WORKSHOPS TO SUCH PERSONS ON TOPICS DESIGNED TO ENABLE THEM TO ACQUIRE GREATER SKILLD OF SELF RELIANCE SUCH AS PLANNING AND PREPARING NUTRITIOUS AND AFFORDABLE MEALS VEGTABLE GARDENING HOUSEHOLD BUDGETING STRATEGIES FOR SAVING ON ENERGY COSTS PRESERVING FOOD; AND C) DOING ALL SUCH THINGS AS ARE IDENTICAL OR ANCILLARY TO THE ATTAINMENT OF THE ABOVE PURPOSE TO PROVIDE EDUCATION PROGRAMS THAT WILL PROVIDE INSTRUCTION ON CREATING A SYSTEM OF SUSTAINABLE AGRICULTURE RENEWABLE ENERGY ALTERNATIVE BUILDING METHODS AND OTHER GREEN SUSTAINABLE CLEAN TECHNOLOGIES. TO RECEIVE AND MAINTAIN A FUND OR FUNDS AND TO APPLY ALL OR PART OF THE PRINCIPAL AND INCOME THEREFROM FROM TIME TO TIME TO CHARITABLE ORGANIZATIONS THAT ARE ALSO REGISTERED CHARITIES UNDER THE INCOME TAX ACT (CANADA)
