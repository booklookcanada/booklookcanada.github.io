What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended May 31, 2024)

THE EASTERN ONTARIO & WESTERN QUEBEC CHRISTMAS CHEER FOUNDATION'S PURPOSE IS TO RAISE FUNDS AT EVENTS HELD PRIMARILY AROUND THE CHRISTMAS SEASON. THE FOUNDATION USES THESE FUNDS TO MAKE DONATIONS TO OTHERS IN COMMUNITY CAUSES. DURING THE YEAR COMMUNITY GRANTS WERE PAID TO THE OTTAWA FOOD BANK THE CARING & SHARING EXCHANGE AND OTHER LOCAL ORGANIZATIONS. CONTRIBUTIONS IN-KIND ARE RECEIVED FOR SOME FUNDRAISING COSTS.
