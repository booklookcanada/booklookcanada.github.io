Who runs it — the people it pays

It reported 10 full-time positions and 17 part-time employees (year ended Mar 31, 2024). Total pay for all its positions: $1,200,730, as filed. The highest pay band it reported was “$80,000–$119,999”. 4 employees were reported in that band. Pay is reported in bands — ranges, not exact salaries. The top band is the highest range with anyone in it: it is not one person's salary, and it is not what everyone there is paid.
