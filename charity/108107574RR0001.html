What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

Provides pastoral care and spiritual leadership to members and attendees of the church and community. we support local food banks and local charities. We support international relief efforts through our associates CBOQ and TBM. We provide support to Live-in Caregivers (nannies). We also provide care and spiritual leadership to Christians from Iran who have come to Canada as refugees or other means. We also provide leadership and administrative services to a Filipino church that is not yet registered as a charity.
We continue to hold two services on Sunday and one service is posted electronically for home viewers. We also have a number of mid week church meetings.
