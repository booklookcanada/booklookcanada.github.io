What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

Producing and /or presenting theatre productions; Providing training and teaching of theatre and related arts to theatre professionals and practitioners; Developing and delivering student mentorship and community outreach programmes in theatre and related arts; Cooperating with and working in conjunction with artistic educational and charitable institutions organizations and facilities in the production development study and application of performance training and teaching of theatre and related arts; Producing programs and materials directly related to the charitable objects for presentation broadcast and distribution on any means of audio visual computer Internet electronic digital or other forms of communication as may become available from time to time; and Giving donations scholarships and bursaries for charitable and/or educational purposes in accordance with the objects.
