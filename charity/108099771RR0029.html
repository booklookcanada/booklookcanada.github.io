What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

ST ALBAN'S HAS BEEN PROVIDING SPIRITUAL LEADERSHIP COMMUNITY BUILDING AND INDIVIDUAL SUPPORT TO RESIDENTS OF ACTON AND THE WIDER GEOGRAPHIC AREA FOR OVER 150 YEARS. IN ADDITION TO REGULAR WORSHIP SERVICES CURRENT PROGRAMS INCLUDE A FOCUS ON MAINTAINING AND DEVELOPING OUTREACH PARTNERSHIPS. WE OFFER OUR CHURCH TO COMMUNITY ORGANIZATIONS WHO NEED MEETING SPACE FOR VARIOUS PROGRAMS SUCH AS: EXERCISE AND EDUCATION OPPORTUNITIES FOR THE ELDERLY MARGINALIZED PEOPLE SUCH AS THE LONELY AND THOSE IN GROUP HOMES AS WELL AS ELEMENTARY AND HIGH SCHOOL CHILDREN. WE HAVE A FULLY STOCKED COMMUNITY KITCHEN WHERE WE OFFER COOKING CLASSES FOR VARIOUS COMMUNITY GROUPS INCLUDING DIVERSE CULTURES.
