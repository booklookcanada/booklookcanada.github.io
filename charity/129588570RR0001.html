What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended May 31, 2024)

1. Provide suitable facilities for display restoration storage of records and other items relating to the history exploration settlement development and culture of the railways in the former Police Village of Komoka and area. 2. Actively discover seek out and acquire all manner of items related to the area and its history of the railways that passed through Komoka and the railways' influence on the area. 3. Accurately and properly catalogue all railway items and artifacts received so that they may be readily available for the viewing public and serious researchers. 4. Educate the public through the use of live steam demonstrations model displays slide shows web site mall displays library displays or lectures.
