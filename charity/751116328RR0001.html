What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

We give free coffee and snacks to seniors on a weekly basis. We give monthly card games to seniors. We financially support the ongoing maintenance and rehabilitation and improved accessibility of Sacred Heart of Jesus Church and the Hall in Batawa On. We provide free prayer shawls and Christmas Food Hampers to various clients. We support our local school and Batawa Lions Club with non-perishable items and donations. We provide two ongoing scholarships to our local school and Secondary school. We also provide support to the Esk-Omi Mission Canadian Red Cross Open Door Cafe (meals for hungry) Food Pantry items as well as bake days for seniors and retreats and protocol lessons for Students. We support the Hall and church with PPE equipment and donations for heating and digital equipment for in house retreats and meetings. We installed accessibility doors and thresholds plus button to help those with accessibility issues. Memorial tree replacement planting (Environmental) and a mediation garden for all ages and living fence as well are ongoing. We continue to help our local groups in collecting items for the homeless.
New for 2024/25 we are working on hearing sight and safety projects as well as our Solar Panel Environmental project.

  • Anything after Dec 31, 2024, its newest return in the published data.
