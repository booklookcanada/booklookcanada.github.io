What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

A daily program of spiritual discourses prayers meditation and devotional hymns is conducted at the Toronto and Abbotsford temples as well as temples located in the USA. Every Sunday and during special festive days the spiritual sermons and prayers are followed by a communal 'Lungar' (free food for all attending the temples). In addition meditation and prayers are regularly conducted thereby awakening the slumbering souls and guiding followers and devotees on the right path of humanity and spirituality. The activities of the organization are purely limited to spiritual upliftment of human. All the temples are open for devotees and followers to pray meditate and reflect.
Due to the construction and rebuilding of the Charity's main temple building during the last 3 years (the main temple building located at 260 Ingleton Blvd Scarborough) which is being rebuilt and thus temporarily inaccessible for use to carry on the aforementioned regular ongoing programs) the weekly prayers meditation and other spiritual programs are carried out in devotees homes. In addition special religious programs are celebrated by renting convention centers. Other temples located in BC and USA have been carrying out the above programs and activities on a regular basis.
