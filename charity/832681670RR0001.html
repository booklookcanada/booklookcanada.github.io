What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

ST.JOSEPH'S HOSPICE RESOURCE CENTRE OF SARNIA LAMBTON PROVIDES MEDICAL HEALTH AND SUPPORT SERVICES INCLUDING COUNSELLING TO TERMINALLY ILL INDIVIDUALS AND THEIR FAMILIES. THEY OPERATE A TEN-BED HOSPICE FACILITY WITH AN EMPHASIS ON PROMOTING WHOLE PERSON CARE IN SARNIA LAMBTON IN A MANNER WHICH MAINTAINS AND RECOGNIZES THE HEALING MINISTRY REFLECTION OF CHRISTIAN VALUES IN THE ROMAN CATHOLIC TRADITION. IN ADDITION THE HOSPICE ALSO PROVIDES EMOTIONAL AND SPIRTUAL SUPPORT FOR THE TERMINALLY ILL FREE OF CHARGE TO ALL RESIDENTS OF SARNIA LAMBTON REGARDLESS OF FAITH CULTURE OR RACE.
