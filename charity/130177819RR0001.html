What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

WE ARE AN ALL INCLUSIVE CHRISTIAN CONGREGATION PROVIDING WEEKLY SERVICES AND PASTORAL CARE FOR OUR MEMBERS ADHERENTS AND THE COMMUNITY AT LARGE INCLUDING HOSPITALS LONG TERM CARE AND RETIREMENT HOMES. WE DONATE FUNDS TO OUR NATIONAL CHURCH MISSION AND SERVICE FUND TO QUALIFIED DONEES TO ASSIST WITH THEIR PROGRAMMES AND TO THE NEEDY. WE PROVIDE FREE ACCOMODATION FOR A SEASONAL MEDICAL CLINIC ASSISTING OFFSHORE WORKERS IN OUR LOCAL COMMUNITY AND FREE ACCOMODATION TO A GROUP FOR IT'S ADULT RECOVERY PROGRAMS. WE CONTINUE TO ASSIST FAMILIES ARRIVING FROM THE UKRAINE TO SETTLE AND FEEL WELCOME IN OUR LOCAL AREA.
