What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

The charity held weekly worship services provided pastoral care and held Bible study groups for men women and youth. Charitable donations were received from parishioners and various other sources. These donations were used to pay the pastors pay the retired pastors fund the operating expenses of the church assist needy families in the community and to make contributions to other charitable organizations.
