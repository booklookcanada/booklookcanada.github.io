What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

THE PURPOSES OF THE ORGANIZATION ARE: 1. TO ESTABLISH AND OPERATE A RESIDENTIAL TREATMENT SUPPORT CENTRE FOR PERSONS DIAGNOSED WITH A SERIOUS MENTAL ILLNESS AND WHERE PRESENT CO-OCCURING SUBSTANCE USE ISSUES; 2. TO PROVIDE MENTAL HEALTH AND ADDICTION COUNSELLING AND OTHER SUPPORT SERVICES TO ASSIST THESE INDIVIDUALS TO AQUIRE DEVELOP AND SUSTAIN SKILLS NECESSARY TO RECOVERY AND INDEPENDENT LIVING IN THE COMMUNITY; 3. TO ASSIST THESE INDIVIDUALS TO DEVELOP LIFE AND WORK SKILLS THAT WILL ENABLE THEM TO SEEK MEANINGFUL EMPLOYMENT AND TRANSITION BACK TO THE COMMUNITY SUCCESSFULLY; AND 4. OTHER COMPLEMENTARY PURPOSES CONSIST WITH THSES OBJECTIVES.
