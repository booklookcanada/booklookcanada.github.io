What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

The purposes of the organization are to provide poverty relief for persons in Canada who are in conditions of need hardship or distress by reason of their economic (and social) circumstances by equipping and empowering local churches to deliver support and education services.
