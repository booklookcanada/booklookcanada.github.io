What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

Provides long-term and ongoing counselling advocacy and support to women and girls over the age of 12 that have been or are being abused. This includes women and girls abused by their intimate partners exploited in prostitution trafficked and/or subjected to sexual harassment or torture through individual counselling and group support . Provides support and counselling to family members of women and girls who have been prostituted/sex trafficked . Provided public education to the community province and country
