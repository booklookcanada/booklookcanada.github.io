What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

TORONTO PEOPLE WITH AIDS FOUNDATION ('PWA') ENGAGES PEOPLE LIVING WITH HIV/AIDS IN ENHANCING THEIR HEALTH AND WELL BEING THROUGH PRACTICAL AND THERAPEUTIC SUPPORT SERVICES AND BROADER SOCIAL CHANGE AND INSPIRES INDIVIDUALS TO LIVE INTO THEIR DREAMS AND DISCOVERIES. PROGRAMS INCLUDE PRACTICAL SUPPORT (FOOD BANK FINANCIAL ASSISTANCE) HEALTH & THERAPEUTIC CARE (MASSAGE REIKI YOGA) DREAMING & ENGAGMENT (PEER OUTREACH POWER PROGRAM) AND COMMUNITY ACCESS (INCOME ACCOMODATION FOOD SUPPORT).
