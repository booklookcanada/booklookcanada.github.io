What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Aug 31, 2024)

THE SISTER OF SERVICE IS A RELIGIOUS INSTITUTE. IT PROVIDES FOR THE DAILY NEEDS OF ITS MEMBERS INCLUDING THE CARE OF THE MEMBERS IN THEIR LATER YEARS. THE MEMBERS GIVE ALL THEIR INCOME TO THE INSTITUTE. SOME MEMBERS OF THE INSTITUTE PROVIDED PASTORAL SERVICES AND RETREAT SERVICE FOR MEN AND WOMEN. COUNSELING SERVICES ARE ALSO PROVIDED AND MEMBERS VOLUNTEER IN VARIOUS CAPACITIES IN THE COMMUNITY. THE INSTITUTE DONATES FUNDS TO OTHER CHARITABLE ORGANIZATION TO ENABLE THEM TO CARRY ON THEIR WORK.
