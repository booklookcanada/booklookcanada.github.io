What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

1).Sunday Worships We have been practiced Sunday Worship Services every Sunday in Oakville Ontario since 2015. We moved to King's Christian Collegiate as our Sunday Worship services location in 2019 our service hour has been changed to 10am every Sunday. During the Covid-19 lockdown period we have kept our Sunday worship services online by live streaming broadcast on YouTube and Zoom. In our Sunday services we focus on worship Lord our trinity God such as singing the praises songs prayer biblical preaching respond song and benediction etc. 2). Bible Study and Trainings The Bible defines the nature and character of God. God’s inherent nature is the way of peace justice mercy happiness and joy radiating outward toward those He has created. The character of both God the Father and Christ the Son is spiritual holiness righteousness and absolute perfection. The Holy Spirit is the key ingredient that enables human beings to begin to manifest some of the very character of God. It is the power by which God imparts His nature. Righteous holy godly character is the possession and practice of love joy peace patience mercy kindness goodness faith gentleness humility and self-restraint. We believe that the Bible is the spiritual food of our Christian life it guides us our behaviors and lead us to live our life in godly way From the very first day LBCO is determined to build our church in accordance with the teachings of the Bible. In order to help our brothers and sisters lay a good foundation of the truth of the Bible our church has opened various weekly Bible study classes. The sermons for Sunday worship are expository preaching Sunday school classes and prayer meetings are all prepared according to the Bible and pray according to God’s will. we have Bible studies every week and trainings every other week on an ongoing basis. 3).Prayer Meetings Prayer meetings are on weekly basis every Wednesday prior to 2019; and during Covid -19 the prayer meetings have been changed to every morning of the week from Monday to Saturday and going forward. 4). Small Group & Fellowship Activities: Every two weeks as always; 5). Evangelism within church communities;
1). Family Ministry: At LBCO through family ministry the relationship between husband and wife is adjusted to a care and loving relationship and the skills of raising children are cultivated by the godly teachings so that children can grow up in a care and loving environment and learn to be grateful thankful responsible with care and love towards others. 2). World Mission: In 2024 LBCO donated $5 000 to Joint Mission and we sent sisters to participate in short-term missions to Germany. In 2024 LBCO donated $3 000 to the SAGOS Canada. 3). Sponsorship of Theological students: In 2024 several co-workers in our church began to study and equip themselves in the seminary. Our church not only supports them in prayer but also establishes a theological education fund to support their study and equipping in the seminary.

What this page can't tell you

  • Anything after Dec 31, 2024, its newest return in the published data.
