What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

Youth Circles: Restorative justice program funded by Justice Canada. To divert youths at risk from: joining gangs criminal justice system disciplinary processes of Safe School Act Peace circles in school: restorative discipline approach designed to train teachers staff & students to participate in circles lead circles and applications in the school setting. City of Toronto crisis response intervention & prevention. Bounce back: Host weekly skill and experiential based supportive training and group work for youth community leaders preparing them to become local ambassadords for their communities. Youth community ambassadors develop creative events community engagement activities online programs school based activities and reclaim community space located in the City of Toronto's NIA neighbourhoods.
