What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Jun 30, 2024)

The Foundation in collaboration with Montcrest School is in the process of developing Katie's Corner a curated collection of books focused on sharing stories and/or personal experiences that enlighten children of all ages about the diversity that exists in this world the subsequent equity challenges faced and the need to create a more inclusive world where everybody feels they belong. We believe that educating children who represent our future with engaging books featuring diversity equity and inclusiveness will help contribute to a more compassionate world. We chose to defer gifts to subsequent years because the programming wasn't at a stage to scale it to other schools by the end of Fiscal Year 2024.
