What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Jul 31, 2024)

THE OBJECTIVE OF THE FOUNDATION IS TO: A) TO PROMOTE THE RELIEF AND TREATMENT OF PHYSICAL DISFIGUREMENT HOWEVER CAUSED IN ANY AND EVERY PART OF THE WORLD; B) TO ORGANIZE PROMOTE FUND AND DELIVER THE SERVICES OF SURGEONS AND ASSOCIATED MEDICAL PERSONNEL FOR THE RELIEF OF PHYSICAL DISFIGUREMENT IN ANY AND EVERY PART OF THE WORLD; C) TO FOSTER PROMOTE AUGMENT DEVELOP AND ENCOURAGE INVESTIGATIVE KNOWLEDGE PF PLASTIC AND RECONSTRUCTIVE SURGERY RELATED DISCIPLINES; D) TO ENCOURAGE THE TEACHING AND EDUCATION OF THIS MEDICAL SPECIALITY; E) TO TRANSFER SKILLS TECHNIQUES TECHNOLOGY AND EQUIPMENT NECESSARY FOR THE TREATMENT OF PHYSICAL DISFIGUREMENT TO THOSE PARTS OF THE WORLD WHERE THEY ARE NEEDED; AND F) TO RAISE FUNDS BY A VARIETY OF MEANS TO FULFILL ALL OF THE AFORESAID PURPOSES.
