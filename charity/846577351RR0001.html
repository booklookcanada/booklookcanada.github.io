What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

WE STRIVE TO ENGAGE ALL MICAH MEMBER CHURCHES IN ONGOING INITIATIVES. WE HAVE FIVE STRATEGIC PRIORITIES: OPERATING AN AFFORDABLE HOUSING PROJECT; INITIATING AND COMPLETING ANOTHER AFFORDABLE HOUSING PROJECT; STRENGTHENING MEMBERSHIPS; ENGAGING AND PURSUING PARTNERSHIPS; AND EDUCATING THE BROADER COMMUNITY.
WE CONTINUED OUR ADVOCACY ACTIVITIES BY ENGAGING WITH THE BROADER COMMUNITY AND ASSISTING OTHER COMMUNITY MEMBERS (RICHMOND HILL) TO INCORPORATE A NEW ORGANIZATION TO PROMOTE THE DEVELOPMENT OF AFFORDABLE HOUSING. IN ADDITION WE HAVE RECEIVED COMMITMENTS FROM A LOCAL DEVELOPER FOR A PARCEL OF LAND IN MARKHAM AND WE ARE NEGOTIATING AN AGREEMENT WITH THE CITY OF MARKHAM FOR HOUSING ACCELERATOR FUNDS TO SUPPORT A PROPOSED 161 UNIT RENTAL HOUSING PROJECT
