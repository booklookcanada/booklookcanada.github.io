What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

THE OTTAWA REGIONAL CANCER FOUNDATION IS A SUPPORTIVE CANCER CARE CHAMPION. WE HARNESS THE POWER OF OUR COMMUNITY TO DELIVER LIFE CHANGING RESULTS FOR THOSE AFFECTED BY CANCER. WE OFFER A COMMUNITY CANCER HUB A SINGLE PORTAL FOR LOCAL CANCER PATIENTS TO ACCESS COMMUNITY BASED NONMEDICAL CARE THEY NEED. THE CANCER FOUNDATION ALSO INCREASES ACCESS TO INNOVATIVE PERSONALIZED THERAPIES BY FUNDING LOCAL RESEARCH. THE COMMUNITY CANCER HUB OFFERS A CANCER SYSTEM NAVIGATION PROGRAM TO ACT AS A CONDUIT BETWEEN MEDICAL CARE AND COMMUNITY BASED NON-MEDICAL CARE (MENTAL HEALTH NUTRITION FITNESS) FOR CANCER PATIENTS AND THEIR FAMILIES.
