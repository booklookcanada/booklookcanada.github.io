What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Jun 30, 2024)

Educate Canadians on traditional Chinese culture and host workshops on skills training in media production in Canada. Educate seniors on using the internet internet security & cultural activity. Provide career fairs mentoring programs and skills training programs for youth and immigrants.
Our comprehensive youth job placement services go beyond merely connecting young job seekers with available job openings. We are committed to empowering young individuals by providing them with a range of valuable resources and support to help them embark on successful career journeys. Our services include but are not limited to the following: Job Matching: Our priority is to connect young job seekers with opportunities that match their skills and career goals. We carefully assess individual profiles to ensure the best job fit. Career Support: We offer comprehensive support including resume reviews interview preparation and career counselling to equip young individuals with the skills and confidence needed for successful job searches. Networking and Community: We provide access to valuable networking opportunities and foster a supportive community where young job seekers can connect with professionals and peers creating a platform for growth and collaboration. Our goal is to empower youth to not only secure employment but also to thrive in their chosen careers. We are dedicated to providing a holistic approach to job placement ensuring that young individuals have the tools and support they need to achieve their career aspirations.
