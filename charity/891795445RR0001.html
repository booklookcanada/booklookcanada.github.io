What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

CBBAG GAVE ONLINE AND IN-PERSON WORKSHOPS AND COURSES ACROSS CANADA CBBAG PRODUCES A FULL-COLOUR MAGAZINE ON THE BOOK ARTS IN ENGLISH W/FRENCH ARTICLES ISSUED SEMI-ANNUALLY. CBBAG OFFERS A COMPREHENSIVE DISTANCE LEARNING PROGRAMME IN THE BOOK ARTS. CBBAG MAINTAINS A WEBSITE PROVIDING RESOURCE INFORMATION FOR BOOK ARTISTS. REGIONAL ACTIVITIES INCLUDING LECTURES OUTREACH ACTIVITIES EXHIBITIONS. AWARDS CHOLARSHIPS TO SUPPORT STUDENTS PARTICIPATING IN A CORE CURRICULUM BOOKBINDING COURSE.
LAUNCHED A MAJOR EXHIBITION TO CELEBRATE THE 150TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE BIRTH OF LUCY MAUD MONTGOMERY WHICH IS CURRENTLY TOURING CANADA.
