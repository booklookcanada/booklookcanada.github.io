What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

United Way Waterloo Region Communities is dedicated to helping people live better lives in every one of the seven communities we serve. We are the largest funder of social programs in Waterloo Region second only to government. We are locally focused informed connected and deeply invested in helping people reach their full potential in the seven communities that comprise Waterloo Region: Cambridge Kitchener Waterloo Wilmot Woolwich Wellesley and North Dumfries.
