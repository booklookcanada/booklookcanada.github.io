What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Jun 30, 2024)

Through God's transformational power we build loving relationships to bring hope to children and families impacted by poverty. As Christians we demonstrate God's love in tangible ways by living among the families of our city's poor communities & partnering with others to achieve positive change. We provide weekly children & youth recreational meetings tutoring to at-risk children and youth women's social programs for those marginalized in society day camps in March break & summer break Christmas assistance and other programs as needed.
