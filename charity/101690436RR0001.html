Who runs it — the people it pays

It reported 43 full-time positions and 49 part-time employees (year ended Dec 31, 2024). Total pay for all its positions: $4,210,524, as filed. The highest pay band it reported was “$80,000–$119,999”. 5 employees were reported in that band. Pay is reported in bands — ranges, not exact salaries. The top band is the highest range with anyone in it: it is not one person's salary, and it is not what everyone there is paid.

What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

ESTONIAN RELIEF COMMITTEE (ERC) IN CANADA IS A CANADA-WIDE ESTONIAN AID ORGANIZATION. ITS OBJECTIVE IS TO DRAW TOGETHER ESTONIANS AND THEIR ORGANIZATIONS IN CANADA TO PROVIDE AID FOR BENEFIT OF THE ESTONIAN COMMUNITY GIVE OR ORGANIZE AID TO NEEDY ESTONIANS AND TO ESTABLISH AND MAINTAIN OR SUPPORT CHILDREN'S SUMMER CAMP NURSING AND RETIREMENT HOMES AND OTHER SOCIAL SERVICE AND COMMUNITY ORGANIZATIONS.
