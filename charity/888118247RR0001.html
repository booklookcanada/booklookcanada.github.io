THE FOUNDATION HAS DEDICATED ITSELF TO HEALTHY LIVING AND IMPROVING THE LIVES OF COMMUNITY RESIDENTS THROUGH ITS EFFORTS TO FUND HEALTH CARE INITIATIVES IN PARTNERSHIP WITH PETERBOROUGH FAMILY HEALTH TEAM. THE FOUNDATION WILL WORK WITH FAMILY HEALTH TEAMS SPECIALISTS AND COMMUNITY HEALTH SERVICE PARTNERS TO DEVELOP PROGRAMS WITH PATEINT-CENTERED FOCUS AND A GOAL TO ENHANCE COMMUNITY HEALTH SERVICES.
