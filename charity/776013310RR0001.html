What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

Women's Transitional Homes Mental Health Foster Care The People's Market (food program). The organizations purpose is to assist those affected by spousal abuse through counselling and treatment programs. It also provides affordable and secure housing for women who have been emotionally and physically abused and their children. Foster program helps recruit and train foster families and then connect foster children to these families. The People's Market provides food provision for those facing abuse and poverty.
