What it says it does

THE ARCHDIOCESE IS ENTRUSTED WITH THE PROVISION OF SERVICES OF THE ROMAN CATHOLIC CHURCH TO MEMBERS OF THE PARISHES AND COMMUNITY. IN PARTICULAR THIS INVOLVES: - ESTABLISHMENT OF NEW PARISHES MISSIONS AND CHAPLAINCIES IN THE ARCHDIOCESE TO SERVE THE CATHOLIC COMMUNITY. - PROVIDING FOR RELIGIOUS EDUCATION OF THE CATHOLIC FAITH COMMUNITY THROUGH RELIGIOUS SERVICES COURSES RETREATS AND SEMINARS. - DISSEMINATION OF CATHOLIC SERVICES AND DOCTRINE USING ALL TYPES OF MEDIA INCLUDING PRINT AND BROADCAST MEDIA. - ASSISTING IN THE OPERATION OF CATHOLIC SEMINARIES AND COLLEGES ENTRUSTED WITH THE TRAINING OF CLERGY DEACONS AND LAITY. - WORKING WITH AND ASSISTING OTHER CATHOLIC ORGANIZATIONS INCLUDING RELIGIOUS ORDERS AND SOCIAL AGENCIES. - RESPONDING TO AND ASSISTING EFFORTS IN ADDRESSING DISASTER RELIEF AND SOCIAL CRISES. - PROVIDING FUNDS AND SERVICES IN THE EVANGELIZATION OF THE CATHOLIC FAITH
