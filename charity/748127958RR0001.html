What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

Weekly Community Meal Program – Provides over 1 200 free vegetarian meals weekly to seniors adults and children prepared off-site and distributed by volunteers. Faith-Based Community Programs – Weekly satsang meditation and spiritual gatherings open to the public at no cost. Senior Support Services – Priority assistance with mobility seating and meal access for seniors attending programs. Community Welfare Assistance – Case-by-case support such as essential items emergency meals and referrals. Seasonal Community Food Drive – Collection and distribution of non-perishable food items to families in need. Volunteer Engagement – Large volunteer base of 140 plus volunteers contributing significant weekly service hours for program delivery setup organization and crowd/parking management.
Guruji Mandir Scholarship Awards – New educational initiative providing two annual scholarships of $8 000 each renewable up to four years for students enrolled in accredited colleges or universities.
