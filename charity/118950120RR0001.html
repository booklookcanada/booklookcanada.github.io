What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

Habitat for Humanity Canada' brings communities together to help families build strength stability and independence through affordable homeownership. In fulfilling this mission we provide resources training and ongoing support to help increase the capacity and growth of our local Habitat for Humanity organizations located across Canada. We also partner with international organizations to support international projects designed to provide shelter for low-income families.
