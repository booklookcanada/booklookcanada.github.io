What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

1. Financial Empowerment Champions: We animate support & work with a national network of 14 local non-profit Financial Empowerment Champion (FEC) organizations that are Canada's leaders in providing high quality free financial help to people with low incomes. 2. Prosperity Gateways: Our Prosperity Gateways program helps public service providers identify the financial help needs of their service users design and pilot interventions refine them and build them sustainably into their services to improve service outcomes and client financial health. 3. Technology-enabled Financial Help Services: We work with partners to build test and scale innovative digital solutions that enhance community financial empowerment services and enable people with low incomes to take independent action to build their financial wellbeing. 4. Solutions Lab: We work with partners from all sectors to collaboratively explore and define priority financial health issues develop solution frameworks that leverage the contributions of each sector and advance implementation of these solutions. 5. Government Policy Change: We work with governments to remove barriers and promote policies regulations and programs that enhance financial wellbeing for people with low incomes - conducting research mobilizing new knowledge & insights problem solving and providing formal and informal feedback and advice.
