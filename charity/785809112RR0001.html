What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

Spiritual Mental and Emotional ministry: -These services are carried out on a weekly basis throughout the year. It includes: -Teaching the principles of the Christian faith for salvation spiritual growth enlightenment understanding and overall wellbeing. -Pastoral counseling for married couples singles youth and adults of all ages. This includes emotional support for those going through challenging or pressing situations stress and other life challenges. - Blessing/christening of babies and children. Couples relationship building program - This includes: - marital conferences and seminars inside and outside the organization. Community Outreach program: - Education Athletics and Arts program. This includes: - our tutoring program for students in grades JK-8. This service is geared towards helping students enhance their academic achievements and release their potential. - Athletics programs are used to strengthen youths' self-esteem further athletics and develop the art of working in a team environment. - Community media spotlight presentation with discussion of important topics arising from the presentation. - House visits for prayer and counselling. Talent and Art Events: This involves a showcase of musical/oratory talents and Art display of pieces created by people in the church and community. This event is held throughout the year and is ongoing. Missions Donations: - helping families in need by giving benevolence gifts and aids. - donation collection for 'The Open Door' charity. Items that can be donated include: clothing food items that are nonperishable donated throughout the year.
Along with continuing the ongoing programs we will work to establish the following programs. Mentorship program: leadership and entrepreneurial skills development we endeavor to be apart of creating A generation of leaders through this program. Mental and Physical health program: This program will be geared towards seniors adults teens and children creating workshops and athletic events to promote a healthy mind and body lifestyle.
