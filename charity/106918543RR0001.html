What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

Children Believe is a global charity dedicated to removing the barriers to education that steal children’s choices one by one until they have none. For sixty years the organization has brought together brave young dreamers caring supporters and partners and unabashed idealists joined by a common belief that access to education means a lifetime of choices and is the most powerful tool for children to change the world. We currently operate in six countries with programs that focus on five interconnected pathways of change: access to education health and nutrition child protection and participation gender equality and social inclusion youth employability and women’s economic empowerment. And we won’t stop until every child can grow up to live the life they choose to live
The Championing Nutrition and Gender Equality (CHANGE) project funded by Global Affairs Canada is Children Believe Canada’s new flagship grant. The five-year project engages with local national and global partners to improve women’s leadership and control over vital resources while helping to strengthen their access to proper health services and nutrition. CHANGE focuses on reaching the poorest and most marginalized especially women adolescent girls and their children. Through this work we will help bring positive impact for 385 000 people in Ethiopia and Ghana.
