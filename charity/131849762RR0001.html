What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

Hold weekly worship services for men women youth and children. Provide pastoral and deacon care. Donate funds to denomination for overseas and domestic activities. Once a month community members in need can receive from the Foodbank Truck (provided by the Huron County Distribution Centre) food and various other items as available (ex. clothing winter coats toiletries). Weekly educational programs for youth; weekly prayer and fellowship programs for adults. Community meal provided for members of church and community twice a month.
