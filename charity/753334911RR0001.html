What it costs to raise a dollar

Across its 2021, 2022, 2024 returns pooled together, it spent $1.30 on fundraising for every $100 of cash donations it received, as filed. Pooling three returns smooths out one unusual campaign. Gifts of goods are taken out of the donation side only where the CRA publishes that breakdown (the most recent year), so read this as approximate.

Who runs it — the people it pays

It reported 9 full-time positions and 19 part-time employees (year ended Dec 31, 2024). Total pay for all its positions: $659,971, as filed. The highest pay band it reported was “$40,000–$79,999”. 6 employees were reported in that band. Pay is reported in bands — ranges, not exact salaries. The top band is the highest range with anyone in it: it is not one person's salary, and it is not what everyone there is paid.
