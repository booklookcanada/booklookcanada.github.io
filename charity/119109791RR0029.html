What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

THE ROMAN CATHOLIC EPISCOPAL CORPORATION FOR THE DIOCESE OF ST. CATHARINES IN CANADA – ST. ALEXANDER EXISTS FOR THE PURPOSE OF THE ADVANCEMENT OF THE ROMAN CATHOLIC FAITH THROUGH EVANGELIZATION. 1. THE CHURCH PROVIDES DAILY WORSHIP SERVICES FOR PRAYER AND SPIRITUAL GROWTH THAT MODEL CHRIST’S TEACHINGS; TEACHING CORE VALUES PRINCIPLES AND BELIEFS OF THE ROMAN CATHOLIC RELIGION. 2. RELIEVING POVERTY THROUGH COMMUNITY OUTREACH AND DONATIONS TO OTHER REGISTERED CHARITIES. 3. TEACHING SOCIAL CONSCIOUSNESS AND AWARENESS BY ENCOURAGING VOLUNTEERISM AND TEACHING PARISHIONERS HOW TO MEET SOCIETAL NEEDS THROUGH VOLUNTEER AND CHARITABLE ACTIVITIES. 4. COMFORTING THE BEREAVED VISITING THE SICK AND THE ELDERLY THROUGH PASTORAL CARE TEAMS. 5. PROMOTING VOCATIONS TO THE PRIESTHOOD AND RELIGIOUS LIFE.
