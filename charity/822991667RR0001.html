What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

1.REFUGE SERVICE: AT MATTHEW HOUSE OTTAWA WE PROVIDE A SUPPORTIVE FAMILY -LIKE ENVIRONMENT THAT OFFERS MUCH MORE THEN JUST SHELTER. EACH REFUGEE CLAIMANT IN OUR PROGRAM RECEIVES COMPREHENSIVE WRAP-AROUND SUPPORT THAT INCLUDES A BED FOOD CULTURAL ORIENTATION AND PRACTICAL SKILLS DEVELOPMENT AIMED AT FOSTERING INDEPENDENCE AND SELF-SUSTAINABILITY. IN 2024 ALONE WE EMPOWERED 414 REFUGEE CLAIMANTS TO BEGAN THEIR NEW LIVES IN CANADA WITH DIGNITY AND HOPE. 2.FURNITURE BANK; WE HELP HOUSES FEEL LIKE HOMES .OUR FURNITURE BANK PROGRAM SUPPORTS NEWCOMERS REFUGEE AND LOW- INCOME FAMILIES BY PROVIDING ESSENTIAL GENTLY-USED FURNITURE. THIS IMITATIVE PROVIDES COMFORT AND A SENSE OF STABILITY HELPING INDIVIDUALS AND FAMILIES BUILD WELCOMING ENVIRONMENTS WHERE THEY CAN THRIVE. IN 2024 WE FURNISHED 1 818 HOMES – A SIGNIFICANT IMPACT ON THE WELLBEING OF OUR COMMUNITY. AT MATTHEW HOUSE OTTAWA EVERY CONTRIBUTION SUPPORTS OUR MISSION TO PROVIDE ESSENTIAL SERVICES AND FOSTER COMMUNITY INTEGRATION FOR REFUGEE AND VULNERABLE POPULATIONS.
