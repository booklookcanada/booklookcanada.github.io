THE BARRIE COLTS FOUNDATION ['BCF'] IS A CHARITY CREATED BY BARRIE COLTS JUNIOR HOCKEY LTD. OF THE ONTARIO HOCKEY LEAGUE. FUNDS ARE TO BE CONTRIBUTED EACH YEAR THROUGH THE PROCEEDS OF THE BARRIE COLTS ANNUAL GOLF TOURNAMENT JERSEY AUCTIONS THE 50/50 PROGRAM AND PERIODIC CONTRIBUTIONS FROM THE HORSEPOWER SPORTS & ENTERTAINMENT GROUP INC.
