What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

CHRISTIAN CHARITY THAT SEES THE HOPE AND POTENTIAL IN EVERY YOUNG PERSON. AS AN CHAPTER OF YOUTH FOR CHRIST CANADA NIPISSING YFC IS EQUIPPED TO REACH LOCAL YOUTH THROUGH NATIONALLY APPROVED STRATEGIES. THE MISSION OF YOUTH FOR CHRIST CANADA IS 'TO PARTICIPATE IN THE BODY OF CHRIST IN RESPONSIBLE EVANGELISM OF YOUTH PRESENTING THEM WITH THE PERSON WORK AND TEACHINGS OF CHRIST AND DISCIPLING THEM INTO THE CHURCH.' NIPISSING YFC ASPIRES TO LIVE OUT THIS MISSION BY CREATING A SAFE PLACE WHERE YOUTH CAN BE IMPACTED BY THE GOSPEL AND GROW IN RELATIONSHIP WITH THE CHURCH.
