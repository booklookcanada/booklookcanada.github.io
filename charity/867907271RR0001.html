What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

THE CHARITY IS A CO-ED CAMP FOR CHILDREN LOCATED IN PARRY SOUND ONTARIO CANADA WHOSE PURPOSE IS TO PROVIDE SCHOOL AGED CHILDREN WITH RELIGIOUS AND EDUCATIONAL EXPERIENCES AS WELL AS TO MAINTAIN AND CONDUCT EDUCATIONAL PROGRAMS AND SEMINARS DESIGNED TO INCREASE AWARENESS OF JEWISH VALUES AND BELIEFS. THE CHARITY ALSO PROVIDES SUBSIDIES AND OTHER FINANCIAL ASSISTANCE TO FAMILIES WHICH ARE FINANCIALLY CHALLENGED.
