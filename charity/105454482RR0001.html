What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

UNITED WAY PETERBOROUGH & DISTRICT PROVIDES FUNDING TO 20 PARTNER AGENCIES ENABLING THEM TO PROVIDE MULTIPLE PROGRAMS AND SERVICES TO THE COMMUNTIY. MOVING PEOPLE FROM POVERTY TO POSSIBILITY AND BUILDING STRONG & HEALTHY COMMUNITIES ARE TWO OF THE PRIMARY FOCUS AREAS. THE ORGANIZATION ALSO COMPILES RESEARCH ON HOMELESSNESS HOUSING AND LIVING WAGE.
