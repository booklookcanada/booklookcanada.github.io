What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

PLAST IS COMMITTED TO CARRYING OUT THE PLAST MISSION STATEMENT WHICH STATES: 1. TO CONTRIBUTE TO EDUCATION OF YOUNG PEOPLE THROUGH VALUES BASED ON THE PLAST OATH & PROMISE; 2. TO HELP BUILD A BETTER WORLD WHERE PEOPLE ARE SELF-FULFILLED AS INDIVIDUALS AND PLAY A CONSTRUCTIVE ROLE IN SOCIETY; 3. TO EDUCATE YOUTH ABOUT UKRAINIAN CANADIAN HERITAGE. WE DEVELOP LEADERSHIP SKILLS THROUGH TRAINING AND SELF-EDUCATION PROGRAMS AND ACTIVITIES.
