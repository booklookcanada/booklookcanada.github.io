What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Aug 31, 2024)

SAT PROGRAM: HOSTS 350 CHILDREN A PLACE FOR FRIENDS MEALS & OPPORTUNITY TO BE TAUGHT POSITIVE LIFE CHOICES & ENCOURAGEMENT. JUNIOR LEADERSHIP: HELPS OUT WITH SAT. PROGRAM. JUNIOR LEADERS ARE MENTORED BY LEADERS WITH LESSONS THAT ARE RELEVANT TO THEIR LIVES. YOUTH PROGRAM: HOSTS UP TO 250 YOUTH AGES 12-15 GIVING THEM A SPACE TO HAVE FUN WITH FRIENDS HAVE A MEAL & PROVIDE THEM WITH LEADERS THAT INTENTIONALLY INVEST IIN THEM WALKING BESIDE THEM AS THEY ARE DEALING WITH SO MUCH. YOUTH MEET ON TUESDAY AND THURSDAY EVENINGS. EACH CHILD RECEIVES A HOME VISIT TO SEE IF THEY HAVE PERSONAL NEEDS & TO BUILD RELATIONSHIPS. AFTER SCHOOL TUTORING: OFFERED TO 30 PLUS CHILDREN PER WEEK. THIS HELPS KIDS TO BETTER THEIR GRADES BUILD RELATIONSHIPS CONFIDENCE & HAVE A BETTER OUTLOOK AT SCHOOL. SUMMERS: HAVE WEEKLY GIRLS & GUYS NIGHTS FOR GAMES SPORTS & CRAFTS. SUMMER CAMP: RUNS 4 WEEKS IN JULY & AUGUST FOR KIDS & YOUTH INCLUDES CRAFTS GAMES SINGING & WORSHIP OUTINGS IE. NIAGARA WATERPARK LUNCH & SNACKS SPORTS COURT: IS USED FOR OUR YOUTH NIGHTS & SUMMER CAMPS IT IS USED FOR BASKETBALL SOCCER IE. AND CONVERTED TO A SKATING RINK IN THE WINTER. WE HAVE A COUNSELLING RPGRAM FOR KIDS & YOUTH GOING THROUGH ANXIETY & TRAUMA IN THEIR DAILY LIVES. HOPING TO EXPAND THESE PROGRAMS IN THE FUTURE. ALL PROGRAMS ONGOING AND NEW ARE PROVIDED FREE OF CHARGE TO ALL ROSE CITY KIDS.
