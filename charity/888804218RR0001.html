What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

THE CORPORATION IS A GLOBAL ORGANIZATION THAT PROTECTS EDUCATES AND EMPOWERS CHILDREN TO RISE ABOVE ADVERSITY USING THE POWER OF PLAY. WE REACH MILLIONS OF CHILDREN EACH YEAR IN SOME OF THE MOST DIFFICULT PLACES ON EARTH HELPING THEM TO STAY IN SCHOOL AND GRADUATE RESIST EXPLOITATION OVERCOME PREJUDICE PREVENT DISEASE AND HEAL FROM THE TRAUMA OF WAR. FOR MORE THAN 20 YEARS WE HAVE DELIVERED PROGRAMS WITH IMPACT IN BOTH DEVELOPMENT AND HUMANITARIAN CONTEXTS. HEADQUARTERED IN TORONTO CANADA THE CORPORATION OPERATES PROGRAM OFFICES IN 14 COUNTRIES ACROSS AFRICA ASIA THE MIDDLE EAST AND CANADA AND HAS FUNDRAISING OFFICES IN CANADA GERMANY NORWAY THE NETHERLANDS SWITZERLAND THE UNITED KINGDOM AND THE UNITED STATES. OUR PROGRAMS ARE FACILITATED BY MORE THAN 500 INTERNATIONAL STAFF AND 100 000 TEACHERS AND COACHES.
