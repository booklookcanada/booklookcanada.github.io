What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

WE HAVE CONTINUED TO SUPPORT THE BROCK COMMUNITY FOOD BANK AND ALSO DONATED TO OTHER CHARITIES BEFORE OUR CLOSURE ON MARCH 31 2024. SOME OF THESE CHARITIES WERE LOCAL SCHOOL FOOD PROGRAMS; WE WANTED TO ENSURE THAT CHILDREN WERE BEING FED. LAKEVIEW MANOR LADIES AUXILLIARY ALL FOOD BANKS WITHIN BROCK TOWNSHIP CURVE LAKE NORTH HOUSE AND WOMEN'S RESOURCE CENTRE. WE ALSO HELD WEEKLY WORSHIP SERVICES AND PROVIDED PASTORAL CARE UNTIL OUR BUILDING CLOSED AND WE AMALGAMATED WITH ANOTHER CONGREGATION. AS OF MARCH 31 2024 WE HAVE CEASED TO BE IN EXISTENCE.
NO NEW PROGRAMS WERE INTRODUCED.
