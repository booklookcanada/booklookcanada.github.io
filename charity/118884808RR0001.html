What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

FARM RADIO INTERNATIONAL CONTINUES TO OFFER ONGOING PROGRAMS TO OUR CONSTITUENTS AND STAKEHOLDERS INCLUDING THE FOLLOWING: WE ANIMATE AND SERVE A NETWORK OF SOME 1 400 RADIO STATIONS AND 5 000 INDIVIDUAL BROADCASTERS IN 40 COUNTRIES IN SUB-SAHARAN AFRICA WITH TEXT- AND AUDIO-BASED CONTENT RESOURCES AND TRAINING MATERIALS AND COURSES. ENGAGEMENT OF BROADCASTERS IN THE NETWORK CONTINUES TO GROW. FARM RADIO ALSO CONTINUES TO IMPLEMENT PROJECTS WORKING WITH A WIDE RANGE OF AFRICA CANADIAN AND OTHER PARTNERS. THESE PROJECTS INCLUDE PROGRAM CONTENT ON NATURE-BASED SOLUTIONS NATIONAL RADIO-BASED AGRICULTURAL EXTENSION SEXUAL AND REPRODUCTIVE HEALTH AND RIGHTS FOR ADOLESCENTS GIRLS’ EDUCATION AND MORE. OUR EVALUATIONS CONTINUE TO SHOW THAT LISTENERS TO PROGRAMS SUPPORTED BY FARM RADIO ARE POPULAR AND INCREASE KNOWLEDGE AND POSITIVE CHANGE IN RESPECT OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTIVITY HEALTH OUTCOMES AND GENDER EQUALITY.
IN 2023 FARM RADIO EXPANDED ITS WORK USING INTERACTIVE RADIO INCLUDING MAJOR PROJECTS ON UNPAID CARE IN EIGHT COUNTRIES WORKING WITH LEADING WOMEN’S RIGHTS ORGANISATIONS AND COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND RESILIENCE IN THE DEMOCRATIC REPUBLIC OF CONGO. FARM RADIO ALSO INTRODUCED NEW NETWORK-BASED PROGRAM APPROACHES WHICH HAVE INCREASED BROADCASTERS’ ENGAGEMENT WITH THE NETWORK.
