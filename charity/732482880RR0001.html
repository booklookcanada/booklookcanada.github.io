What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

PEGASUS ANIMAL SANCTUARY IS AN ANIMAL SANCTUARY AND REHABILITATION CENTRE FOR ABUSED NEGLECTED OR ABANDONED FARM ANIMALS THAT CARES FOR THE ANIMALS AND WILL PROVIDE EDUCATION ON ANIMAL WELFARE TO MEMBERS OF THE COMMUNITY IN PORT PERRY AND SURROUNDING AREAS INCLUDING LOCAL YOUTH COMMUNITY GROUPS AND CORPORATE GROUPS VIA WORKSHOPS AND INTERACTIVE TOURS THAT TAKE PLACE AT THE SANCTUARY. THE PURPOSES OF PEGASUS ANIMAL SANCTUARY INCLUDE ADVANCING EDUCATION BY THE PROVISION OF CLASSES IN ANIMAL WELFARE AND BENEFITING THE COMMUNITY IN PORT PERRY AND SURROUNDING AREAS BY PROVIDING SANTUARY AND REHABILITATION SERVICES FOR FARM ANIMALS NEEDING CARE AND ATTENTION AND HUMAN ASSISTANCE AN THEREBY PROMOTING THE MORAL AND ETHICAL DEVELOPMENT OF THAT COMMUNITY.

What this page can't tell you

  • Whether its work works. A return shows money, not results.
  • Whether anyone audited these figures. The CRA publishes returns as filed, without checking them. Some charities also post audited statements — BookLook compared the largest 200 charities' published statements with their returns (see “How to read this site”).
  • Anything after Dec 31, 2024, its newest return in the published data.
  • Who its directors are. The published data does not include them.
  • Questions worth asking it directly: Do you publish audited financial statements? Who sits on your board this year? What did last year's programs achieve, and how do you know?
