What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

WORSHIP SERVICES CONDUCTED UP TO TWICE A WEEK. PROVIDE MINISTRIES FOR YOUTH SENIORS YOUNG ADULTS ON A WEEKLY/MONTHLY BASIS. PROVIDE PASTORAL CARE. DO FUNERALS BAPTISM'S AND WEDDINGS. SHARE IN RUNNING A SUMMER CAMP FOR RECREATION AND RELIGIOUS PROGRAMS AS WELL AS A VACATION BIBLE SCHOOL. PROVIDE FINANCIAL SUPPORT TO ELCIC FOR MISSIONS WORK AND SUPPORT LOCAL FOOD BANKS CRISIS CENTERS HELP PROGRAMS AND CHARITIES. A REGULAR SUNDAY SCHOOL BIBLE STUDY AND CONFIRMATION CLASSES ARE PROVIDED. A LARGE NUMBER OF VOLUNTEERS HELP OUT IN FUNDRAISING EVENTS TO SUPPORT LOCAL CHARITIES AND PROGRAMS AT THE CHURCH. PROVIDE SPACE FOR EXERCISE CLASSES FOR SENIORS. PROVIDE SPACE FOR A MUSIC HEALING VETERANS PROGRAM FOR ARMED FORCES AND EMERGENCY RESPONSE PERSONNEL WHO SUFFER FROM PDSD AND RELATED ISSUES. GIVE OUT BAGS OF FOOD TO NEEDY WHO COME TO OUR DOORS. HAND OUT MEAL VOUCHERS AT THE SOUP KITCHEN FOR A FREE MEAL AT THE CHURCH SUPPERS. PROVIDE A SPACE FOR A DRUG ADDICTION PROGRAM. HAVE THE LEGAL CLINIC WORK OUT OF OUR BUILDING.
