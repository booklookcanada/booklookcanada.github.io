What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Mar 31, 2024)

1. TRAUMA RESILIENCE PROGRAM (TRP)- THE TRP IS A CLINICALLY FACILITED GROUP- BASED TRAINING PROGRAM THAT COMBINED THE POWER OF A TEAM APPROACH WITH EFFECTIVE TRAUMA-RESILIENT SKILL DEVELOPMENT. 2. COPE (COUPLES OVERCOMING PTSD EVERYDAY)- COPE IS CANADA'S LEADING CLINICALLY FACILITATED COUPLES-BASED TRAUMA PROGRAM FOR THOSE AFFECTED BY OPERATIONAL STRESS INJURY. 3. PTSD SERVICE DOG PROGRAM- THE PTSD SERVICE DOG PROGRAM PROVIDES FUNDING TO SUPPORT THE TRAINING OF APPOXIMATELY 100 DOGS PER YEAR. 4. COUPLES BASED EQUINE THERAPY- A COMBINATION OF EQUINE ASSITED LEARNING PSYCHOLOGICAL INTERVENTION COMBINED WITH TOOLS TO REDUCE CONFLICT MANAGE AGER AND IMPROVE COMMUNICATION. 5. BEFORE OPERATIONAL STREE (BOS)- BOS IS A GROUP BASED INTERVENTION PROGRAM PROVIDING PARTICIPANTS THE OPPORTUNITY TO RECEIVE AND OFFER SUPPORT IN A GROUP CONTEXT WITH ISSUES RELATED TO OPERATIONAL STREE. 6. FIRST RESPONDER ASSIST- THIS PROGRAM PROVIDES A CLINICAL TEAM DEDICATED TO SERVINC THE PSYCHOLOGICAL NEEDS OF FIRST RESPONDERS IN THE PROVINCE OF ONTARIO AND OFFERS PREVENTION SERVICES CRISIS SUPPORT INDIVIDUAL TREATMENT AND CLINICAL TRAINING. 7. WARRIOR KIDS- THE PROGRAM WAS DESIGNED TO SUPPORT CHILDREN WHO ARE LIVING WITH A PARENT WHO HAS OPERATIONAL STRESS INJURIES. 8. SPOUSAL RESILIENCY PROGRAM- DESIGNED TO PROVIDE SUPPORT TO THE SPOUSES OF VETERANS AND FIRST RESPONDERS SUFFERING FROM OPERATIONAL STRESS INJURIES. 9.WARRIOR HEALTH - A CENTRALIZED PLATFORM CONNECTING INJURED VETERANS FIRST RESPONDERS AND THEIR FAMILIES WITH OCCUPATIONALLY AWARE CLINICIANS.
