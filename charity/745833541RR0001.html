What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

Ongoing programs is to fight against poverty and social economic inequalities in order to ensure sustainable development through the renewal of the mind by providing spiritual awakening and concrete material assistance to the less fortunate and to all those who experience difficulties in life. We support local food bank and shelter to address pressing issue of food insecurity that affects many families and individuals. With these aids we want to reduce these socio-economic gaps by encouraging and empowering the less fortunate to overcome their challenges so that they can successfully achieve their dreams with new strategies that will change their lives.
The various activities are related to health care clothing education spiritual support and motivation by focusing on children teens in vulnerable situation teens moms and/or orphaned and minorities immigrants who are deprived. The goal is to offer them support and guidance for them to be able to navigate their challenges and improve their well-being. Furthermore to create a supportive environment that fosters growth resilience and empowerment for those in need.
