What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

We are the national association of Canadian Unitarian Universalist congregations. Programs to further our charitable purpose include: 1) providing resources and support to congregations; 2) fostering growth according to Unitarian Universalist principles and beliefs; 3) working with other groups for the benefit of the community; 4) organizing educational opportunities for lay leaders religious professionals members youth and young adults; and 5) providing funding and grants to qualifies donees and individuals for growth initiatives leadership development and to further our charitable purposes.
New programs are planned and created to further our charitable purposes and these include virtual programming and trainings.
