What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Dec 31, 2024)

We presented 11 live performances in various communities across Southern Ontario during the 2024 fiscal year. We also produced virtual performances and educational chats for the general public free of charge. Our singers were presented mainly as a full choir but also as step-out soloists duos and trios in these live and virtual events. We finished up our 2023/2024 season in April and May of 2024 with 4 live performances and began our 2024/2025 in the Fall of 2024 with 6 live performances and a virtual educational video chat. We are preparing for a new season (2025/2026) of 10 live concerts and we hope to finally deliver educational and community workshops in the summer of 2026.
