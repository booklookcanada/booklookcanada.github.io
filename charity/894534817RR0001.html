OUR HEALTHCARE PUBLIC FOUNDATION IS INVOLVED IN RAISING FUNDS FOR 3 HEALTHCARE CHARITIES (QUALIFIED DONEES) LOCATED HERE IN BARRY'S BAY ONTARIO - 1) THE ST. FRANCIS MEMORIAL HOSPITAL 2) THE MADAWASKA HOSPICE PALLIATIVE CARE AND 3) THE VALLEY MANOR INC. WE HAVE 65+ VOLUNTEERS CONTRIBUTING UPWARDS OF 2 000 HOURS IN TOTAL EACH YEAR ASSISTING US IN OUR FUNDRAISING GOALS. FRANCIS CIRCLE GIVING CLUB - MEMBERS JOIN A MONTHLY/ANNUAL GIVING CLUB. 7) GUARDIAN ANGEL PROGRAM - GRATEFUL PATIENTS RECOGNIZE APPRECIATION OF A CAREGIVER 8) ROCKATHON 9) BUTTERFLY RELEASE EVENT - IN SUPPORT OF HOSPICE/PALLIATIVE CARE SERVICES IN THE COMMUNITY 10) HOSPITAL EMERGENCY ROOM RE-DEVELOPMENT CAMPAIGN 11) CATCH THE ACE LOTTERY - TO SUPPORT THE EQUIPMENT NEEDS OF THE HOSPITAL 12) THIRD PARTY EVENT SUPPORT 13) PLANNED GIVING.
