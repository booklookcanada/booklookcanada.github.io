What it says it does

In its own words, as filed with CRA (from its return for the year ended Jun 30, 2024)

SUPPORTS ACCESS TO EFFECTIVE EDUCATION TO UNDERPRIVILEGED CHILDREN WORLDWIDE TO SUPPORT SCHOOLS AND PROVIDE AN EXCELLENT STANDARD IN EDUCATION AND TO REDUCE BARRIERS TO SUCCESS FOR YOUTH AT RISK. IN CARRYING OUT ITS MANDATE THE CHARITY: 1) OPERATES A DROP IN CENTRE AND SKILLS TRAINING CENTRE FOR YOUTH AT RISK. 2) WORKS WITH PARTNER INDIGENOUS SCHOOLS ACROSS THE COUNTRY TO HELP INCREASE GRADUATION RATES AND PROVIDE EDUCATIONAL PROGRAMS THE SCHOOL DOES NOT HAVE ADEQUATE RESOURCES TO PROVIDE. A SPECIAL EMPHASIS IS PLACED ON STEM PROGRAMS. 3) OPERATES A NATURE EDUCATION FACILITY FOR ELEMENTARY AGE SCHOOL CHILDREN. 4) OPERATES A NATURE RETREAT PROPERTY FOR YOUTH AT RISK. 5) SUPPORTS SCHOOLS INTERNATIONALLY WITH BASIC NEEDS 6) PROVIDES OPPORTUNITIES FOR YOUNG PEOPLE OF VARYING ETHNIC BACKGROUNDS TO LEARN TOGETHER CELEBRATE DIVERSITY AND BREAK DOWN STEREOTYPES AND BARRIERS.
NO NEW PROGRAMS AT THIS TIME
