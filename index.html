BookLook covers Ontario: 39,699 registered charities and 487,398 annual returns, filing years 2009–2024. Every figure is as filed with the Canada Revenue Agency. There are no scores, grades, or rankings here.

The newest return the CRA has published is for a year ended December 31, 2024 — the published files run about two years behind, for every charity.
